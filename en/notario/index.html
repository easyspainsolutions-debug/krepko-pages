      We take care of the whole process

      When you come to Krepko, you get not just an appointment with a notary but full preparation of the signing and support at every stage of the procedure.

      Most difficulties arise not during the signing of the documents but much earlier. The type of notarial act has to be correctly identified, the documents prepared, the information checked, the translation arranged, the details agreed with the notary, and it has to be confirmed that the finished documents will be accepted exactly where you plan to use them.

      That is precisely what we do.

      We study your situation in advance, find the right solution, prepare the necessary documents, agree all the details and support the procedure until it is fully completed.

      Where necessary, we also arrange:

      • notarised translations;
      • apostilles on documents;
      • legalisation;
      • sending documents to other countries;
      • dealings with banks, public authorities, lawyers and other organisations.

      How it works

      1. Analysis of your situationWe study your task and determine what will be required in your particular case.
      2. Preparing the documentsWe check the information, prepare the necessary texts, arrange the translation and agree all the details in advance.
      3. Signing the documentsWe prepare and organise the signing before the notary and make sure everything goes as quickly as possible and without mistakes.
      4. Follow-upWhere necessary, we arrange the apostille, translation, legalisation, sending of the documents and further support.

      Can't find the document you need?

      That is not a problem.

      Most notarial acts do not have a standard form. Many documents are drawn up individually, based on the requirements of a specific country, bank, public authority or private organisation.

      We will prepare the text of the document in the language you need, agree it with the notary and arrange the whole procedure from start to the receipt of the finished documents.

      Frequently asked questions

      How is the cost calculated?
      The cost depends on the type of notarial act, the language of the document, the number of parties and whether a translation, an apostille or legalisation is needed. Krepko reviews your situation and agrees the cost and timeframe before we start.
      What does Krepko's work include?
      An analysis of your situation, preparing the text of the document directly in the language you need, translation, agreeing the wording with the notary, full preparation of the signing, accompanying you with translation at the appointment and coordinating the apostille.
      Can I have a document for another country drawn up before a Spanish notary?
      Yes. We prepare a power of attorney, consent or statement for any country other than Spain directly in the language you need, have it apostilled and send the original. More details — power of attorney in your language; for Ukraine — power of attorney for Ukraine.
      In which cities does Krepko accompany you to the notary?
      Throughout Spain — in the largest cities and their surroundings. Krepko has permanent representatives in Alicante, Valencia, Murcia, Madrid, Barcelona and Málaga. If you are somewhere else, tell us your city — we will tell you where we will hold the signing.
      Can the document be in English or Ukrainian?
      Yes. We prepare the document directly in the language you need — Russian, Ukrainian, English or another language. We check in advance which language the organisation where the document will be used will accept.
      I don't know what the document I need is called — can I just explain my situation?
      Yes, that is how we work: you describe your situation in your own words, and we identify the right document and prepare the text and your signing before the notary. All you will need to do is come with your passport.
      I don't speak any Spanish — how will the visit to the notary go?
      We prepare the document directly in the language you need and accompany you at the signing, translating the notary's explanations. You know the content in advance and sign understanding every clause.
