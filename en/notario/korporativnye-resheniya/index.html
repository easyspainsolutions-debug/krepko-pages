    Corporate resolutions in notarial form

    • a specialist will reply within 15 minutes
    • terms agreed before we start
    • in Russian, Ukrainian and Spanish
    Message us on WhatsApp The first consultation is free

    We prepare a certificate of the resolutions of your company's governing bodies, agree the wording with the notary and arrange the escritura (notarial deed) — elevación a público de acuerdos sociales (formalising corporate resolutions as a public deed).

    Resolutions of the general meeting of shareholders or of the board remain, on their own, internal company documents. For the Registro Mercantil (Commercial Registry), a bank or a counterparty to accept them, the resolutions are "raised" to notarial form: the notary draws up an escritura on the basis of the minutes or a certificate of resolutions signed by an authorised person.

    How the process works

    1. Reviewing your situationWe study the articles of association, the shareholder structure and the registry's requirements for the specific resolution.
    2. Preparing the documentsWe prepare the minutes and the certificate of resolutions and agree the wording with the notary.
    3. Signing the documentsWe prepare and conduct the signing before the notary. Your presence will be needed for just 30 minutes.
    4. Receiving the finished documentsWe arrange the filing with the registry, the translation, the apostille and the delivery of the documents.

    Timeframes and payment

    Preparation usually takes from a few days to a few weeks — depending on the type of resolution, the number of shareholders and whether documents of a foreign company are needed.

    Processing times at the Registro Mercantil depend on the registry itself, so we allow a margin for this stage and keep you informed of the status.

    One all-inclusive payment: we quote the price after reviewing your case, you pay once the document is signed, no advance payment. The consultation and review are free.

    When notarial form is needed

    Typical situations:

    • a change of company director — appointing or replacing the administrador;
    • a change of the company's registered address;
    • amendments to the articles of association;
    • approval of a transaction by the shareholders' meeting;
    • granting powers to sign specific documents.

    If one of the shareholders is a foreign company, its corporate documents and confirmation of its representative's powers will also be needed — as a rule, with an apostille and a translation.

    Why the registry sends documents back

    The requirements for a certificate of resolutions are strict: who exactly signs it, how the meeting is described, whether the quorum was met, whether the wording matches the articles of association. A formal defect in any of these parts — and the documents are sent back for correction, and the procedure starts all over again.

    We go through the articles of association and the shareholder structure before the visit to the notary and prepare the certificate so that it goes through the first time.

    The signing takes minutes. The preparation takes experience. We take care of both.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which resolutions must be entered in the Registro Mercantil?
    Those that change the information about the company itself: a change of director or of the form of management, the registered address, the company name, the articles of association, the share capital, restructuring and liquidation. Internal resolutions — distribution of profits, approval of internal rules, appointment of employees — do not go to the registry. If you are unsure which category your resolution falls into, we will work it out before preparing the documents.
    Who signs the certificate of resolutions?
    Usually the director or the secretary of the governing body — depending on what the company's articles of association provide. We check the powers before the visit, so that the signature does not raise questions at the registration stage.
    Can resolutions be formalised without coming to Spain?
    Often — yes, through a representative. This requires a power of attorney for companies with an exact list of powers, and the resolution itself is signed by the company's authorised person.
    Why are documents sent back by the registry?
    Most often because of formal defects: the certificate was signed by the wrong person, the quorum is not described, the wording differs from the articles of association. Less often — because of an incomplete set of documents for a foreign shareholder.
    In what form are a foreign shareholder's documents needed?
    As a rule — with an apostille and a translation into Spanish, and the information on the representative's powers must be current as of the date of signing. We clarify the exact requirements before preparing the documents.
    We are moving the company's office to another city. What needs to be formalised?
    A change of address is formalised by a shareholders' resolution and a notarial deed, with an entry in the Registro Mercantil. We will prepare the minutes and the certificate, accompany you at the signing before the notary and file the documents with the registry.
    The company is registered to me alone. Do I also need minutes of meetings?
    Yes, the sole shareholder records their decisions in writing, and the registry needs them in the same form as minutes of meetings. We will draw up the documents and take them through the notary and the Registro Mercantil.
