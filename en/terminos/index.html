    Términos y Condiciones / Terms of Service

    In force since 2026-05-19 · Última actualización: 28 de octubre de 2026
    In brief. Krepko is the trading name of WEONERENT, S.L. (Spain); it coordinates and provides support with administrative procedures (notary, bank, documents). The price is agreed in writing before work begins. The services of third-party providers — the notary, the translator, public authorities — are paid for at their own rates.

    1. Parties and subject matter of the contract

    Service provider: WEONERENT, S.L., CIF B22809552, Avinguda de la Costa Blanca 93, 03540 Alicante, España (hereinafter “Krepko”). Full details are in the Aviso Legal.

    Client: a natural person over 18 years of age who has requested a service through the website krepko.es, messengers (WhatsApp, Telegram) or other agreed channels.

    Subject matter of the service: administrative support, translation support, and organising performance with the involvement of the relevant specialists (notary, bank, tax specialist, immigration lawyer) within the scope of the request chosen by the client. The specific scope is agreed in each case before work begins.

    Krepko is a support service. Krepko does not provide legal services within the meaning of Ley 2/1974 de Colegios Profesionales: notarial, legal (lawyers’) and tax acts are performed by the relevant specialists engaged to carry out the order, under their own professional liability.

    2. Conclusion of the contract

    The contract is deemed concluded after:

    1. The scope of work and the full price have been agreed in writing (WhatsApp, Telegram, email)
    2. The client has confirmed that they are ready to provide the necessary documents (passport, NIE, originals, etc.)
    3. The client has confirmed that they are ready to pay for the services of third-party providers — the notary, the translator, government fees — at their rates

    Payment for Krepko’s work is made after the estimate has been confirmed in writing — work does not begin before then. All arrangements are recorded in the correspondence. At the client’s request, a separate written contract (presupuesto formal) may be drawn up.

    3. Payment

    Minimum age

    Clients are natural persons over 18 years of age (mayor de edad under Spanish law).

    Currency

    All payments are in euros (EUR). Prices shown on the website include applicable taxes (IVA — where applicable).

    Payment procedure — two models

    Model A: Krepko services in Spain — notary, bank, translation support, coordination of residence permits through partners.

    • Payment for Krepko’s work (drafting the document, liaising with the notary, reserving the signing date) — after the estimate has been confirmed in writing, before work begins.
    • Services of third-party providers (notarial fee, apostille, interpreter at the signing, etc.) — paid by the client upon signing and registration, at the rates of those providers.

    Model B: documents and legal services from Ukraine — certificates, apostilles, duplicate certificates, notarial powers of attorney, representation before Ukrainian authorities by the Krepko team in Ukraine.

    • Payment is made when the order is confirmed, before processing begins. This is because the Krepko team in Ukraine incurs real non-refundable costs (government fees, apostilles, notarial fees, courier) from the moment work starts.
    • The price is final, with no hidden extras — it is fixed before the start.
    • After payment, the process starts immediately, without delay.

    Payment methods for both models: bank transfer (SEPA), Bizum, cash in person, Spanish card. Cryptocurrency is not accepted.

    Full estimate before the start

    Krepko states the full expected cost (Krepko’s fee + estimated official costs) before work begins. The price is not revised unilaterally. If circumstances come to light during the process that substantially increase the scope (for example, additional documents, requirements of the notary), this is agreed with the client in writing before work continues.

    “Process launch” — the point of no return for official costs

    “Process launch” means the moment when a document has been sent for preparation, translation or to the notary, or when Krepko has already paid a government fee (for example, for an apostille) on the client’s behalf. After this moment, the client reimburses Krepko for the costs actually incurred, regardless of the client’s eventual withdrawal from the service — these are the actual costs of the notary, apostille and translation, not payment for Krepko’s work.

    4. Service timeframes

    Estimated timeframes are given in each specific case when terms are agreed. Krepko makes reasonable efforts to meet them, but the final timeframe may depend on:

    • The availability of the partner (notary, bank, gestor)
    • The speed of public authorities (BOE, AEAT, Extranjería, Hacienda)
    • The completeness and quality of the documents provided by the client
    • Force majeure circumstances

    Delays caused by the client (failure to provide documents, rescheduling an appointment) do not give grounds for a refund.

    5. Cancellation and refunds (Cancelaciones y reembolsos)

    The cancellation policy differs for the two payment models (see §3).

    5.1. Model A: Krepko services in Spain — cancellation before process launch

    If the client withdraws from the service before process launch (see §3 “Process launch”), the client has no financial obligations towards Krepko. Krepko does not charge for the consultation and agreeing the terms.

    5.2. Model A: Krepko services in Spain — cancellation after process launch

    If the client withdraws after process launch:

    • Official costs already incurred by Krepko on the client’s behalf (apostille, notarial fee, translation, etc.) — the client reimburses the amount actually incurred.
    • Krepko’s fee for the part of the work actually completed is paid proportionally — this is discussed individually, based on the amount of preparation done.

    5.3. Model B: documents from Ukraine — refund specifics

    Services from Ukraine are paid for when the order is confirmed (see §3). Refunds depend on the moment the Krepko team in Ukraine starts work:

    • Before the order is passed to the Krepko team in Ukraine (as a rule, within 24 hours of payment) — a 100% refund minus bank charges for processing the payment.
    • After the order has been passed to the Krepko team in Ukraine — no refund is possible for costs already incurred (government fees, notarial acts, apostilles, payment for work in Ukraine and for the courier). A refund is possible for the part of the amount that has not yet been put to work.
    • After the document has been delivered to Spain — no refund is provided, as the service has been rendered in full. If the document contains a factual error through Krepko’s fault, it is redone at Krepko’s expense.

    5.4. Accompaniment to the notary / bank / public authorities

    When the cancellation was notifiedFinancial consequences
    ≥ 48 hours before the agreed appointment No penalty. Only official costs already incurred by Krepko are reimbursed.
    24–48 hours before the appointment Official costs + 50% of Krepko’s fee (compensation for the reserved time).
    Less than 24 hours before the appointment Official costs + 100% of Krepko’s fee.
    “No Show” (the client did not attend without notice) Official costs + 100% of Krepko’s fee.

    5.4. Cancellation by Krepko

    If, for objective reasons (illness, force majeure, problems on the partner’s side), Krepko is unable to perform the service, the client receives:

    • Full cancellation of all financial obligations towards Krepko, or
    • Rescheduling to another date at no extra charge, or
    • Replacement of the service with an equivalent one at the client’s request

    If Krepko has already incurred official costs (apostille, notarial fee) that cannot be refunded to the client, Krepko reimburses the client the full amount of those costs.

    5.5. Consumer’s right of withdrawal (derecho de desistimiento)

    Under Real Decreto Legislativo 1/2007 (LGDCU), you have 14 calendar days from the conclusion of the contract to withdraw from the service without giving any reason, if work has not yet begun.

    If work has already begun (process launch under §3), the proportional part of the work actually completed is payable, plus reimbursement of official costs.

    To withdraw, write to hello@krepko.es with the subject “Desistimiento” and the name of the service.

    6. Client’s obligations

    For the service to be provided properly, the client undertakes to:

    • Provide accurate and complete information about their situation
    • Provide the necessary documents in good time (passport, NIE, certificates, etc.)
    • Arrive on time for agreed appointments or give advance notice of being late
    • Follow Krepko’s recommendations on preparing documents
    • Pay official costs in good time at the moment of the act (to the notary at signing, for the apostille on submission, to the translator when ordering)
    • Pay Krepko’s fee after receiving the result

    If the client has deliberately misled Krepko about their situation (for example, concealed a criminal record when applying for a residence permit, or provided forged documents), Krepko is entitled to terminate the contract immediately, with no obligation to provide further support; official costs already incurred by the client remain the client’s responsibility.

    7. Krepko’s liability

    Krepko is liable for:

    • The quality and timeliness of work it performs itself (translation, coordination, support)
    • The safekeeping of documents handed over by the client while they are in Krepko’s possession
    • The confidentiality of client data (see Política de Privacidad)

    Krepko accepts no liability for:

    • Decisions of public authorities (refusal of a residence permit, a bank’s refusal to open an account, a notary’s refusal)
    • The actions of partners (notaries, banks, gestores, lawyers) — they are responsible for their work within the scope of their licence
    • Changes in Spanish legislation while the service is being provided
    • The consequences of incomplete or false information provided by the client
    • The client’s late arrival at appointments and the related consequences

    Krepko’s financial liability limit for a specific service is restricted to the amount actually received from the client for that service.

    8. Services involving third parties

    When coordinating services that involve partners (notary, bank, immigration lawyer, gestor), Krepko acts as an intermediary/coordinator, not as the person providing the service itself. The contractual relationship for the service itself (notarial certification, opening an account, keeping tax records) arises between the client and the relevant partner.

    Krepko does not guarantee:

    • Specific outcomes of procedures (a positive decision on a residence permit, approval of a bank account)
    • Exact processing times for cases at public authorities
    • That partners’ current rates and terms will remain unchanged

    9. Force majeure (Fuerza mayor)

    Neither party is liable for failure to perform its obligations due to circumstances beyond its reasonable control: natural disasters, epidemics, strikes, military action, changes in legislation, infrastructure failure (electricity, internet), acts of the authorities.

    In the event of force majeure, the parties act in accordance with Article 1105 of the Código Civil (Spain) — obligations are suspended for the duration of the circumstances.

    10. Confidentiality

    Information that Krepko receives from the client in the course of providing the service is confidential and is processed in accordance with the Política de Privacidad, the GDPR (RGPD) and the LOPDGDD. Krepko does not disclose information to third parties without the client’s consent, except in cases expressly provided for by law.

    11. Intellectual property

    Prepared documents, templates, translations and instructions developed by Krepko for a specific client are transferred to the client for full use once the client has received the result and paid Krepko. Templates and materials originally developed by Krepko may be reused for other clients.

    12. Governing law and jurisdiction

    These terms are governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Spain, including:

    • Código Civil — civil-law relations
    • RDL 1/2007 (LGDCU) — consumer protection
    • Ley 34/2002 (LSSICE) — information society services
    • GDPR (RGPD) and LOPDGDD — processing of personal data

    All disputes arising from these terms shall be heard by the courts of the place where Krepko is established (Alicante, Spain), unless the applicable consumer protection legislation provides otherwise.

    13. Out-of-court dispute resolution

    If you have a claim or complaint, please first contact Krepko directly at hello@krepko.es — most issues are resolved within a few days.

    If no solution is found, the consumer has the right to apply:

    • To the Junta Arbitral de Consumo at the consumer’s place of residence
    • To the Oficina Municipal de Información al Consumidor (OMIC)
    • To the EU online dispute resolution platform: ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr

    14. Changes to the terms

    Krepko reserves the right to make changes to these terms. Changes apply to new contracts from the moment a new “Última actualización” date is published. Contracts already concluded are governed by the version in force at the time of conclusion, unless the parties have agreed otherwise.
