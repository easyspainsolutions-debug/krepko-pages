    How things work in Madrid

    What works differently here from the neighbouring city.

    Municipal registration (padrón)

    Municipal registration (empadronamiento) is handled by Línea Madrid — twenty-six offices in the city's twenty-one districts, strictly by appointment. An important detail few people use: you can book at any office, not only the one for where you live. Free dates are unevenly spread across the districts, and a trip to the neighbouring district can sometimes save weeks of waiting. We prepare your application for the office with the earliest slot.

    Consulate

    The Embassy of Ukraine is in Madrid, on Ronda de la Abubilla — there is no need to travel to another region, consular appointments take place here. Opening hours vary by day of the week. At the same time, some tasks do not require the consulate at all: a power of attorney, travel consent for a minor or a statement is drawn up before a Spanish notary, in your language. We will tell you which is faster in your case.

    Notary

    Madrid has its own College of Notaries. A document signed here is valid throughout Spain. We prepare the text in advance, agree it with the notary's office and are present at the signing — the meeting is spent on signing, not on clarifying the wording.

    Ukrainian passport

    Since February 2026, the DP “Dokument” centre in Madrid has been located in the Valdebernardo shopping centre, in the Vicálvaro district. It issues both the passport and the ID card; the embassy issues only the passport. We prepare the supporting documents and plan the timing so that your passport does not get in the way of renewing your TIE.

    Frequently asked questions

    Will someone from Krepko be with me at appointments in Madrid?
    Yes. In Madrid and its suburbs, at every appointment where someone needs to be present — at the notary's, the bank, the city council or the immigration office — a Krepko representative is there with you. They translate, keep track of the documents and speak to the staff in Spanish, while the preparation, correspondence, documents from other countries and tasks in other Spanish cities are handled remotely.
    Can I book a municipal registration (empadronamiento) appointment outside my own district?
    Yes, and this is the main thing worth knowing about Madrid. Municipal registration is handled by Línea Madrid — twenty-six offices in twenty-one districts, and you can book at any of them regardless of where you live. People usually look at the office in their own district, see that the dates are taken and wait. Yet a free slot can often be found in the neighbouring district — and that is the office we prepare your application for.
    Is there a Ukrainian consulate in Madrid?
    Madrid has the Embassy of Ukraine, and consular appointments take place there — there is no need to travel to another region. Opening hours vary by day of the week. At the same time, powers of attorney, consents and statements can also be drawn up before a Spanish notary in Russian or Ukrainian: your text in your language goes into the document alongside the Spanish one. Sometimes this is noticeably faster than waiting for a consular appointment — we will review your situation and tell you plainly.
    Why does getting a residence permit drag on, even though the documents have been submitted?
    Most often the issue is the second step. The permit is granted by the Oficina de Extranjería (the immigration office), while the TIE card is issued by the National Police, and these are two separate queues. People receive a positive decision, breathe a sigh of relief and find out about the second step too late, when the nearest dates have already been taken. We plan the second step in advance and prepare the documents for it.
    Where is the apostille affixed to a notarial document in Madrid?
    At the College of Notaries of Madrid. Apostilles on notarial documents are affixed only by Colleges of Notaries, but any of them will do, wherever the document was signed. We arrange the apostille ourselves together with the document; no separate trip is needed.
