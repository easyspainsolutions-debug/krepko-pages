    Criminal record certificate from Russia

    • a specialist will reply within 15 minutes
    • terms agreed before we start
    • in Russian, Ukrainian and Spanish
    Message us on WhatsApp The first consultation is free

    In short. Spanish authorities need a paper certificate from the Russian Interior Ministry (MVD) with an apostille on the original: an electronic certificate from Gosuslugi (the Russian public services portal) cannot be apostilled. Under the regulation, the certificate is issued within up to 30 calendar days, with a possible extension of up to 30 more, and the apostille within up to 5 working days, or up to 30 if specimen signatures and seals have to be requested. Validity is counted from the date of issue: for visas, three months; for Spanish nationality, six; for arraigo, the rules do not set a period. Sources: MVD Order No. 660, 330-FZ. Checked on 5 November 2026.

    We handle a criminal record certificate from Russia without you travelling: you send us your details, and we obtain the certificate from the Russian Interior Ministry, have it apostilled and deliver the original to Spain. Here, we arrange a sworn translation and check the document against your passport.

    We check two things first: the certificate must be on paper, with an apostille on the original, and valid on the day you submit it. We check your authority's requirements and the dates before we start.

    What we need from you

    Everything is sent by message:

    • photos of your Russian internal passport: the pages with your personal details, registration and deregistration, marriage and divorce;
    • all your previous surnames, first names and patronymics and the documents confirming the change — for example, a marriage certificate;
    • the regions of Russia where you lived, including where you got married;
    • the spelling of your full name in Latin letters — exactly as in your passport and TIE;
    • where you are submitting the certificate, in which city in Spain and by what date.

    Internal passport — check it before we start. For a Russian citizen, the MVD regulation names the internal passport or a temporary identity card as the document for requesting the certificate (MVD Order No. 660, para. 14.2). People who have lived abroad for a long time often do not have one: it was not replaced at 20 or 45, it was lost, or it was never issued. If this is your case, tell us straight away — we check the route before payment, not after a refusal.

    How the process works

    1. You tell us where you are submitting the certificate and by what date you need it.
    2. We check your authority's requirements: from which countries certificates are needed, for what period and how long the certificate must still be valid on the day you submit it.
    3. We agree the scope of work and the cost before we start — nothing is added along the way.
    4. We obtain the paper certificate from the Russian Interior Ministry and have the MVD apostille placed on it.
    5. We send you a scan before dispatch and check the form, the apostille and your full name against your passport. The original travels to Spain with a tracking number.
    6. We arrange a sworn translation of the certificate and the apostille, check the spelling of your name and hand you the finished set.

    Timeframes and payment

    Under the MVD regulation, the certificate is issued within 30 calendar days of the application being registered, and this period may be extended by up to 30 more days (MVD Order No. 660, paras. 10–11). The apostille is placed within up to 5 working days, or, if specimen signatures and seals need to be requested, within up to 30 working days (330-FZ, art. 6). How long delivery of the original to Spain takes depends on the carrier. A sworn translation usually takes about two weeks; from Russian it is faster.

    The certificate's validity is counted from the date of issue, so we plan the order from your filing date. Tell us that date straight away — before we start, we will tell you how to plan the timing.

    We quote the price as a single amount before we start, with no hidden extras. The consultation and review are free.

    Why it is needed in Spain

    Its official name is the “certificate of the existence (absence) of a criminal record and (or) of the fact of criminal prosecution or of the termination of criminal prosecution”. It is issued by the Russian Interior Ministry based on the records of the GIAC (the MVD's Main Information and Analytical Centre) and the regional information centres. Spanish authorities ask for it in the following cases:

    • arraigo (residence on the grounds of ties to Spain) — sociolaboral, social, socioformativo, familiar: certificates from all countries where you lived during the five years before entering Spain; the office requests the Spanish certificate itself;
    • the 2026 regularisation — for applications filed before 30 June 2026, the requirement is the same; if you have confirmed that you requested the certificate and it was not issued within a month, the Spanish Ministry of Justice may request it through diplomatic channels; if the certificate does not arrive within three months, the office will give you 15 days to submit it, otherwise the application will be deemed withdrawn;
    • Spanish nationality — the certificate from your country of origin;
    • residence visa — including for a family member applying from Russia — and a student visa for more than six months: certificates from the countries where the person lived for the last five years;
    • digital nomad visa — certificates from the countries of residence for the last two years and a declaration covering five years;
    • work with children — the certificate from the country of origin is also submitted by Spanish or EU citizens of foreign origin or with another nationality, for example naturalised people born in Russia.

    To renew a residence and work permit — as an employee or as an autónomo (self-employed) — and for a change of status (modificación), a Russian certificate is not required. If you are not sure whether you need it, describe your situation: we will tell you before you order.

    The MVD apostille — on a paper certificate only

    Russia and Spain are parties to the Hague Apostille Convention, so a certificate for Spain is legalised with an apostille. It is placed by the MVD: a regional information centre — on the certificates it issued itself, the GIAC — on any certificate (MVD Order No. 625, para. 16; Russian Government Resolution No. 479). The apostille is a stamp on the original itself.

    The MVD does not place an apostille on an electronic certificate from Gosuslugi or on its printout from an MFC (multifunctional public services centre). That is why we order a paper certificate. We do not use a notarised copy of the certificate, even one with an apostille: the Consulate of Spain in Moscow states plainly that it does not accept such copies.

    What the certificate will show

    The certificate covers more than a current criminal record (MVD Order No. 660, para. 76):

    • convictions, even if the record has been expunged or lifted — with the date and the grounds;
    • criminal cases closed on non-exonerating grounds: due to the statute of limitations, under an amnesty, through reconciliation of the parties, with a court fine;
    • information about being on a wanted list.

    Administrative offences are not included in the certificate. For arraigo, Extranjería (the immigration office) takes into account convictions for acts that are also crimes under Spanish law (RD 1155/2024, art. 130.2), and assesses the certificate itself. If you have had a criminal case, even a closed one, tell us before you order: we will have the certificate translated in full, and before you submit it, it is worth showing it to an immigration lawyer.

    Which translation will be accepted

    For Extranjería and a nationality application, we arrange a sworn translation — it is done by a traductor jurado, a translator accredited by the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs. Both the certificate and the apostille are translated: the MVD apostille is filled in in Russian (330-FZ, art. 8). A translation certified by a notary in Russia is not suitable for Extranjería or for a nationality application.

    Your full name in Latin letters in the translation must be exactly as in your passport and TIE. We check the spelling before the translation is finalised, and if the certificate shows a different surname, we will tell you which document confirming the change to attach.

    Frequently asked questions

    Do I need to travel to Russia?
    No — neither to Russia nor to the consulate. You send us your details and photos of your passport, and we obtain the certificate from the MVD, have it apostilled, deliver the original to Spain and arrange the sworn translation.
    Will an electronic certificate from Gosuslugi do?
    For submission in Spain — no. The MVD stamps an apostille only on a paper certificate from an information centre or the GIAC, not on an electronic certificate or its printout from an MFC. That is why we order a paper certificate.
    How long is the certificate valid and when should I order it?
    The period is counted from the date of issue, not from the date of the apostille. The Consulate of Spain in Moscow states three months; for nationality, a certificate without a stated validity period is valid for six months (FAQ of the Spanish Ministry of Justice). For arraigo the rules do not set a period, so we check your office's requirement before you order, and plan the order itself from your filing date.
    I do not have an internal passport, or it has expired. Can I still order the certificate?
    This needs to be checked before we start. For a Russian citizen, the MVD regulation names the internal passport or a temporary identity card. Describe which documents you have — before payment, we will tell you which route is possible.
    I lived in several cities in Russia. Do I need a certificate from each region?
    No, one is enough. The check is run against the records of the GIAC and the information centres by place of birth, residence, stay and conviction, and registration does not matter. Send us all the regions where you lived.
    I used to have a different surname. Will that be taken into account?
    Yes. The check covers all previous surnames, first names and patronymics, so send us all of them. If the name in the certificate differs from the one in your passport or TIE, a document confirming the change — for example, a marriage certificate — is attached to the application package.
    Before Spain, I also lived in another country. Is a Russian certificate enough?
    For arraigo — no: you need certificates from all countries where you lived during the five years before entering Spain, not before applying. For nationality, a certificate from your country of origin is enough. Describe where and when you lived — we will tell you which certificates you will need.
    I am a Spanish citizen, but I was born in Russia. Do I need a certificate to work with children?
    If you obtained Spanish nationality by naturalisation or kept your Russian citizenship — yes: you need an MVD certificate with an apostille, and a Spanish passport does not exempt you from it. To work with minors, the certificate from the country of origin is submitted by citizens of foreign origin or with another nationality, including Spanish and EU citizens (RD 1110/2015, art. 9.3). If you are Spanish by birth and have no other citizenship, describe your situation — before you order, we will tell you whether you need the certificate.
    I am not a Russian citizen, but I lived there before moving to Spain. Do I need a Russian certificate?
    For arraigo — yes, if Russia falls within the five years before you entered Spain. The MVD also issues the certificate to foreign nationals (MVD Order No. 660, para. 2). We will tell you how to arrange it in your case before we start.
