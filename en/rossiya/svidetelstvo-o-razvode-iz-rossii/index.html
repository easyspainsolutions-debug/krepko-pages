    A divorce document from Russia, ready to submit in Spain: we obtain a duplicate divorce certificate without you travelling to Russia, have it apostilled and arrange a sworn translation. It is most often requested before a new marriage in Spain.

    Which document will confirm your divorce depends on how it was processed — at the civil registry (ZAGS) or through a court — and whether you received your own divorce certificate after the court decision. We work this out before you order, so that you do not order anything you do not need.

    What we need from you

    • a photo of your passport;

    If the Registro Civil (the Spanish civil registry) or a notary in Spain has already named the document they need, forward the wording exactly, even in Spanish.

    How the process works

    1. You tell us how and when the divorce was processed and where you are submitting the document.
    2. We identify the document — a duplicate divorce certificate, a marriage registration certificate with a note of its dissolution or a copy of the court decision — and check your authority's requirements.
    3. We agree the scope of work and the cost before we start.
    4. We obtain the document from the ZAGS.
    5. We have the apostille placed: for a ZAGS document, by the ZAGS authority of the same region; for a copy of a court decision, by the Russian Ministry of Justice.
    6. We send you a scan, check the names and dates against your passport and send the original to Spain with a tracking number.
    7. We arrange a sworn translation of the document together with the apostille, if one is needed, and hand you the finished document.

    Timeframes and payment

    If the record is in the electronic ZAGS register, the law says the duplicate certificate is issued on the day you apply (143-FZ, art. 9). If the record is not there, the ZAGS checks the paper record, and the timeframe depends on the office. By law, the apostille is placed within no more than 5 working days; if the authority needs to request information on the signature or seal, the period may be extended to 30 working days (330-FZ, art. 6). Then the original travels to Spain — the timeframe depends on the delivery service. A sworn translation usually takes about two weeks; from Russian it is faster.

    If, after a court divorce, you did not receive your own certificate, it is first issued on the basis of the court decision — we check this timeframe separately. We give you the overall timeframe for your case before we start.

    We quote the price as a single amount before we start, with no hidden extras. The consultation and review are free.

    Which document confirms a divorce

    It depends on how the marriage was dissolved:

    • through the ZAGS — it is confirmed by a divorce certificate. A duplicate is issued from the record in the unified ZAGS register, so you can obtain it not only in the city where the divorce was processed;
    • through a court, and you received the certificate from the ZAGS yourself — we order a duplicate divorce certificate;
    • through a court, but you did not receive your own certificate — even if your former spouse registered the divorce at the ZAGS, you do not have a certificate, and there will be no duplicate. It is issued on your application on the basis of the court decision (143-FZ, art. 35). What this will require and how long it will take, we check before we start.

    A copy of the court decision itself is needed less often. If your authority asks specifically for it, you need a copy certified by the court with a note that the decision has entered into force; the apostille on it is placed by the Russian Ministry of Justice (Russian Government Resolution No. 479, para. 8), and we check where to apply before you order. Whether a Spanish authority will accept a Russian court decision and not ask for it to be recognised in Spain depends on the authority and the purpose — we check this in advance too.

    After a divorce, a duplicate marriage certificate is not issued (143-FZ, art. 9). Instead, the ZAGS issues a certificate of marriage registration with a note of its dissolution — it shows both the marriage and its dissolution.

    What is requested for a marriage in Spain

    The Registro Civil offices in Madrid and the Valencian Community (Alicante, Valencia) ask divorced people for a certificado literal of the previous marriage with a note of the divorce. Valencia states explicitly that it does not accept a court divorce decision. The closest thing to such a document is the marriage registration certificate with a note of its dissolution together with the divorce certificate, but whether your Registro Civil will accept them is not officially set out. So we first check the requirements of your Registro Civil or notary, and then order.

    A divorce document does not say that you have not entered into a new marriage. If the authority asks you to confirm your current status as well, that is a separate document — a marital status certificate.

    Apostille and translation

    The apostille on ZAGS documents is placed by the regional ZAGS authority (Russian Government Resolution No. 479). In practice, it is the authority of the region where the document was issued, so we obtain the duplicate certificate and have it apostilled in the same region. In Spain, an apostille cannot be placed on a Russian document. For Spain, we prepare a paper document with an apostille on the original, not on a notarised copy.

    The Registro Civil and Extranjería (the immigration office) accept a sworn translation (traductor jurado). The Russian apostille is filled in in Russian, so it is translated too: if only the certificate is translated, the authority may return the documents. We arrange a sworn translation of the document together with the apostille and check the spelling of names against your passport and TIE.

    Frequently asked questions

    The divorce was processed in another city. Do I need to go there?
    No. A duplicate certificate is issued from the record in the unified ZAGS register, so you can obtain it not only in the city where the divorce was processed — we have the apostille placed in the same region. You do not need to travel to Russia.
    We divorced through a court. Why is there no divorce certificate?
    Each former spouse receives a divorce certificate on their own application to the ZAGS. If you did not apply to the ZAGS after the court decision — even if your former spouse did — you do not have your own certificate. It is issued on your application on the basis of the court decision (143-FZ, art. 35); we will tell you what is needed for this in your case before we start.
    I need a marriage certificate, but I am divorced. What should I order?
    Divorced people are not issued a duplicate marriage certificate — this is set out in the law (143-FZ, art. 9). Instead, we will order a certificate of marriage registration with a note of its dissolution: it confirms both the marriage and the divorce.
    I still have the old certificate. Can I submit it?
    It is usually simpler to order a duplicate. In practice, the apostille is placed in the region where the document was issued — which means the old form would have to be sent there. We obtain the duplicate certificate and have it apostilled in the same region. If the form is laminated, worn or hard to read, that is in itself grounds for a duplicate (143-FZ, art. 9).
    Can I download the divorce details from Gosuslugi (the Russian public services portal)?
    Electronic data from the ZAGS register are not suitable for Spain: you need a paper document with an apostille. That is why we order a paper duplicate certificate.
    Is an apostille needed?
    We have an apostille placed on every divorce document, and it is part of the service: the Registro Civil, for example in the Valencian Community, requires an apostille or legalisation on foreign documents, and an apostille can only be placed in Russia.
    Can I order it together with other documents?
    Yes: we order a birth certificate and marriage and divorce documents together and send them in one shipment. Describe the full list — we will price it all together.
