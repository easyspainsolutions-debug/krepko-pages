    What we need from you

    • a photo of your passport;

    How it works

    1. You tell us where you are submitting the document and send us the marriage details.
    2. We check whether the marriage is in force and what your authority requires: the certificate or, after a divorce, a marriage registration certificate and a divorce certificate.
    3. We agree the scope of work and the cost before we start.
    4. We obtain the document from the ZAGS and have the apostille placed on it straight away.
    5. We send you a scan to check and send the original to Spain with a tracking number.
    6. We arrange a sworn translation of the document together with the apostille, check the spelling of names and hand you the finished document.

    Timeframes and payment

    If the marriage record is in the Unified State Register of Civil Status Records, the law says the duplicate document is issued on the day you apply to the ZAGS (143-FZ, art. 9, para. 4). The timeframes for the apostille, delivery from Russia and the translation depend on the authority, the post and the translator, so we give you the overall timeframe for your case before we start.

    If you need the document for an application, tell us the date straight away. To correct an application, a Spanish authority usually gives 10 working days; at your request, this may be extended by up to 5 more if the document is difficult to obtain (art. 68 Ley 39/2015). A document from Russia needs more time, so it is safer to order it before you apply rather than after you are asked for it.

    We quote the price as a single amount before we start, with no hidden extras. The consultation and review are free.

    Where in Spain a marriage certificate is requested

    • for family reunification (reagrupación familiar) — together with birth certificates;
    • at the Registro Civil (the Spanish civil registry) — when a Russian marriage is entered in the Spanish register;
    • at a notary's office and at the Land Registry (Registro de la Propiedad) — when a transaction requires proof of the marriage.

    If you are getting married in Spain and have been married before, the Registro Civil will ask you to confirm that the previous marriage has ended. Which documents are issued for this in Russia — see the next section.

    If the marriage has been dissolved

    The Russian ZAGS does not issue a duplicate marriage certificate to divorced people (143-FZ, art. 9, para. 3). Instead, they are issued a certificate of marriage registration with a note of its termination — in Ministry of Justice Order No. 200, this is form No. 6. The divorce itself is confirmed by a divorce certificate, which is also issued as a duplicate. More about it on the divorce certificate from Russia page.

    For a new marriage in Spain, the Registro Civil offices in Madrid and the Valencian Community ask divorced people for a certificado literal of the previous marriage with a note of the divorce, and Valencia states explicitly that it does not accept a court divorce decision. There is no Russian document with that name: the closest equivalent is the marriage registration certificate with a note of its termination together with the divorce certificate. Whether your Registro Civil will accept them is not officially set out, so we check the requirement of your Registro Civil or notary before you order.

    If the divorce was granted by a court but was not registered at the ZAGS, there is no divorce certificate: first, the divorce is registered at the ZAGS. We check how to do this in your case before we start.

    Marriage, divorce and marital status — three different documents

    A marriage certificate confirms that the marriage was registered. It does not say whether the marriage is still in force.

    A divorce certificate confirms that a specific marriage has ended. It does not say that you have not entered into a new one.

    A certificate of no marriage registration confirms that no marriage of yours is registered in Russia. Someone who has been married is issued it only for the period after the divorce. More about this certificate on a separate page.

    If you do not know which of the three you need, tell us where you are submitting it. We will work it out and tell you plainly.

    Apostille and translation

    We have an apostille placed on every document — it is part of the service. On ZAGS documents, the apostille is placed by the ZAGS authorities of the Russian regions (Government Resolution No. 479), and only on the paper original: in Spain, an apostille cannot be placed on a Russian document. A copy of the old certificate, even a notarised one, will not replace the original, so we order a new duplicate certificate.

    The apostille is written in Russian, and the sworn translator translates it together with the certificate. The translation must be sworn — by a traductor jurado accredited by the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs; a Russian notarised translation is not suitable for Extranjería (the immigration office) or for a nationality application. We arrange a sworn translation and check the spelling of names: in the certificate they are written in Cyrillic, and in the translation they must match your passport, TIE and NIE.

    Frequently asked questions

    The marriage has been dissolved. Can I get a duplicate marriage certificate?
    No: after a divorce, the Russian ZAGS does not issue a duplicate marriage certificate. Instead, there is the marriage registration certificate with a note of its termination, and the divorce is confirmed by a divorce certificate. By default, we order both documents, and we check what your authority requires before you order.
    Do I need to travel to Russia?
    No — neither to Russia nor to the consulate. You send us the marriage details and a photo of your passport, and we obtain the document from the ZAGS.
    The certificate is intact, but it is laminated or worn. Do I need a duplicate?
    Yes. Lamination, wear and illegibility are grounds for a duplicate certificate under the law (143-FZ, art. 9, para. 1). We do not have the old form apostilled: we order a new document.
    We got married back in the USSR. What should we do with the old certificate?
    For Spain, we order a duplicate certificate in the current format based on the same record and have it apostilled. If the marriage was registered not in the RSFSR but in another Soviet republic, the record is kept in that country, not in Russia, and this service does not cover it — tell us about this straight away.
    Can I order the certificate through Gosuslugi (the Russian public services portal)?
    For Spain — no. Electronic data from the register are not suitable: the apostille is placed only on the paper original. We obtain the paper document.
    My wife's surname changed. Does that matter?
    Yes. A Spanish authority checks the full name along the whole chain of documents: birth, marriage, change of name. Send us all spellings — the maiden name and the current one, as in the passport and TIE. A discrepancy of even one letter in the translation is grounds for returning the document.
    Is an apostille needed?
    We have it placed on every document: for submission, the document comes with an apostille and a sworn translation, and the apostille itself is translated too. The apostille is part of the service.
    Can I order it together with a birth certificate?
    Yes. We order ZAGS documents for you and your family members together, and they travel from Russia in one shipment. Describe the full list — we will price it all together.
