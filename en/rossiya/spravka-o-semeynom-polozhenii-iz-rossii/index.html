    Marital status certificate from Russia (form No. 15)

    • a specialist will reply within 15 minutes

    Confirmation that you are not currently married. It is most often requested before registering a marriage in Spain.

    In Russia, this document is the civil registry (ZAGS) certificate of the absence of a state registration of marriage, form No. 15. First, we find out which document your Registro Civil (the Spanish civil registry) or notary needs, then we obtain the certificate in Russia, have it apostilled and deliver the original to Spain. You do not need to travel to Russia.

    How the process works

    1. You tell us where you are registering the marriage and whether you have been married before.
    2. We check the requirement of your Registro Civil or notary: which marital status document is needed and what else is needed from Russia.
    3. We agree the scope of work and the cost before we start.
    4. We obtain the certificate from the ZAGS and have it apostilled.
    5. We send you a scan, then send the original to Spain with a tracking number.
    6. We arrange a sworn translation of the certificate together with the apostille and check the spelling of names.

    Timeframes and payment

    The timeframe is made up of several parts: issue of the certificate by the ZAGS, the apostille, shipping from Russia and the translation. Each part depends on the authority and the delivery service, so we give you an estimate for your case before we start.

    The law sets no single validity period for the certificate: it is set by the authority handling the marriage file. Catalonia accepts a marital status document no older than six months, unless it states a different period; Madrid and Valencia do not state a period for it on their pages. For a marriage, the Registro Civil offices in Madrid and Valencia ask for a birth certificate no older than one year. So we order the documents for your filing date: not too early, and with time to spare for shipping.

    We quote the price as a single amount before we start, with no hidden extras. The consultation and review are free.

    What this certificate is

    The certificate of the absence of a state registration of marriage is issued on form No. 15 from Russian Ministry of Justice Order No. 200. It is generated from the unified electronic register of civil status records, so any ZAGS office in Russia can issue it, not only the one at your place of residence.

    Someone who has been married is issued the certificate only for the period when they were not married (Order No. 200, annex 2, para. 8): for example, from the date of the divorce, the death of the spouse or the annulment of the marriage. This means that people who have been married also need a document relating to the previous marriage — more on this below.

    The certificate reflects the records of the Russian register. If the marriage was registered in another country, the Spanish authority may ask about it separately.

    What is required for a marriage in Spain

    The marriage file is handled by the Registro Civil (expediente matrimonial) or a notary (acta matrimonial), and each authority has its own document requirements. The Registro Civil offices in Madrid and Valencia publish the following:

    • a marital status document: Madrid asks foreign nationals for a certificado de soltería, divorcio o viudedad; it is not on the Valencia list;
    • foreign documents — with an apostille and a sworn translation;
    • a birth certificate — no older than one year: we also order it from Russia — birth certificate;
    • for divorced people — the record of the previous marriage with a note of the divorce; Valencia does not accept a court divorce decision instead of it;
    • for widowed people — the record of the previous marriage and of the spouse's death (Valencia);
    • a certificate of capacity to marry (certificado de capacidad matrimonial) — only from citizens of the countries on their lists; Russia is not on them.

    In Russia, divorced people are not issued a duplicate marriage certificate. Instead, the ZAGS issues a certificate of marriage registration with a note of its dissolution, and the divorce is confirmed by a divorce certificate. We check which of these documents your authority will accept before you order — more details on the divorce certificate from Russia page.

    Apostille and translation

    For a Spanish authority, we prepare the certificate with an apostille. It is placed by a ZAGS authority in Russia and only on the paper original: it cannot be placed in Spain. Electronic data from the ZAGS register on Gosuslugi (the Russian public services portal) are not suitable for a Spanish authority.

    The apostille is filled in in Russian, so the sworn translator translates it together with the certificate. The Registro Civil offices in Madrid and Valencia ask specifically for a sworn translation (traductor jurado). We arrange it and check the spelling of names against your passport and TIE.

    Frequently asked questions

    How is certificate No. 15 different from a divorce certificate?
    A divorce certificate confirms that a specific marriage has ended. Certificate No. 15 confirms that no marriage is registered; someone who has been married is issued it only for the period outside marriage. A person can be divorced and already remarried, so the authority handling the marriage file needs your current status. If you have been married, both documents are usually requested.
    I have never been married. Do I still need a document?
    In most cases, yes. The Registro Civil offices in Madrid and Catalonia ask foreign nationals for a marital status document, while the published Valencia list does not include it. The Russian document for this is certificate No. 15; we check whether your authority needs it and will accept it before you order.
    Can I get the certificate without travelling to Russia?
    Yes — you do not need to travel either to Russia or to the consulate. You send us your details and photos of your passport, and we obtain the certificate from the ZAGS.
    I am divorced. What will the certificate show?
    Only the period after the divorce: that is how the form works. That is why divorced people usually also need a document relating to the previous marriage — a certificate of marriage registration with a note of its dissolution or a divorce certificate. We check what exactly your authority will accept before you order.
    Is an apostille needed?
    Yes. For a Spanish authority, we prepare the certificate with an apostille: it is placed by a ZAGS authority in Russia and is part of the service. The apostille is filled in in Russian, so it is translated together with the certificate.
    Will an extract from Gosuslugi do?
    No. Electronic data from the ZAGS register are not suitable for a Spanish authority: the apostille is placed on the paper certificate issued by the ZAGS.
    Do I need a certificado de capacidad matrimonial?
    The Registro Civil offices in Madrid and Valencia do not ask Russian citizens for it: this certificate is required only from citizens of the countries on their lists, and Russia is not on them. We check your authority's requirement before you order.
    How long is the certificate valid?
    The law sets no single period; it is set by the authority handling the marriage file. Catalonia accepts a marital status document no older than six months, unless it states a different period. So we order the certificate for your filing date, with time to spare for shipping from Russia.
    Can I order it together with a birth certificate?
    Yes, it is more convenient: we obtain both documents and have them apostilled, and they travel to Spain in one shipment. Describe the full list — we will price it all together.
