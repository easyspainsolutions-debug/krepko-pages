    IRPF — annual tax return for residents

    • a specialist will reply within 15 minutes
    • terms agreed before we start
    • in Russian, Ukrainian and Spanish
    Message us on WhatsApp The first consultation is free

    IRPF is the annual personal income tax return, the main tax obligation of a Spanish resident. The campaign takes place every spring, and every year brings the same questions: do I have to file, what do I do with income from another country, and which deductions am I entitled to.

    What sets our clients apart is income in several countries: a salary or freelance work here, rent or a pension there, accounts both here and there. Where these overlap, the return stops being routine and becomes a real task: double taxation agreements, credit for tax paid abroad, currency conversions.

    We review your situation and prepare the data; the calculation and filing are done by our tax specialist.

    Do you need to file

    The obligation depends on the amounts and types of income for the year, and the thresholds differ from one situation to another. Important cases from practice:

    • working for two employers — the threshold for mandatory filing is lower than with one, if the smaller of the two salaries for the year exceeds €1,500;
    • foreign income — rent, dividends, freelance work from abroad almost always mean you have to file;
    • benefits and payments — some of them also count as income;
    • voluntary filing — sometimes pays off: with deductions and withholdings, the tax office refunds money.

    We give you the answer "you must / you need not / it pays to file voluntarily" in a free review.

    Half the value of a good tax return is the deductions you did not know about. The other half is the double taxation that did not happen.

    Frequently asked questions

    I moved in the middle of the year. Am I already a resident?
    Residence is determined on the basis of the full year — by the days you spend in the country and by your centre of vital interests. We will go through your case and tell you which year is your first tax year.
    I have a salary in Spain and rent from a flat in Ukraine. What about it?
    Foreign rental income is included in the return; double taxation is avoided through the agreement between the countries. You will need documents on the income and on the tax paid there. If your flats, accounts or securities abroad exceed €50,000 in any one block, a separate Modelo 720 is filed.
    My employer already withholds tax. Why do I need a return?
    Withholdings are an advance payment. The return settles the whole year: sometimes there is an extra payment, and quite often a refund, especially with deductions.
    I have heard about a deduction for renting a home. Am I entitled to it?
    Deductions are divided into state and regional ones — the set depends on your autonomous community. We will check your region during the review.
    What happens if I do not file even though I was obliged to?
    Surcharges that grow over time, and a fine. It is better to close a missed year voluntarily — it is noticeably cheaper.
    During the year I worked in two places. I've been told I now have to file. Is that true?
    Yes, if all your salaries except the largest one together exceed €1,500 for the year, the income threshold for filing is lower and the return often becomes mandatory. We will check your data, work out the result and prepare the filing.
    My husband does not work, only I do. Should we file jointly or separately?
    Both options are possible — joint filing sometimes saves money. We calculate both options using your figures and file the one that works out better for the family.
