    Modelo 720 for 2026 — from 1 January to 31 March 2027

    Assets abroad as at 31 December 2026 are declared if any one block exceeds €50,000. You cannot file before 1 January, so we collect the data in advance.

    In short. Modelo 720 is a declaration by a Spanish tax resident of assets held abroad. It is filed if, as at 31 December, any one of three blocks — accounts, securities and insurance, property — abroad exceeds €50,000 (for accounts, the average balance for the last quarter is also counted). The deadline is from 1 January to 31 March: for 2026, by 31 March 2027. The declaration does not create any tax, but failing to file leads to a fine. Cryptocurrency abroad is declared separately, in Modelo 721, with the same threshold and deadline. We check whether this applies to you and collect the data, and the filing is done by our tax specialist. Sources: Orden HAP/72/2013, art. 7; RD 1065/2007, art. 42 bis, 42 ter, 54 bis.

    You moved and have lived in Spain for more than half the year — which means you have become a tax resident. From that point on, the tax office expects from you not only the annual IRPF return but also information about what you have left abroad: accounts, deposits, a flat, shares.

    Many people find out about this obligation when the bank or the tax office is already asking questions. Since 2022, fines for Modelo 720 have become ordinary rather than ruinous, but it still has to be filed on time.

    Who has to file

    The declaration is filed by a Spanish tax resident — someone who spent more than 183 days in the country during the calendar year or whose main centre of economic interests is here (art. 9 LIRPF). Assets are counted in three blocks, each one separately:

    • bank accounts abroad — the balance on 31 December and the average balance for the last quarter: you have to file if at least one of these amounts exceeds €50,000;
    • securities, shares, insurance policies and annuities abroad — the value on 31 December;
    • property abroad — the acquisition value, not today's value: the purchase price together with the costs and taxes of the transaction, and in the case of an inheritance or gift, the real value at the time of acquisition. If the amount is not in euros, it is converted at the exchange rate on 31 December of the reporting year.

    If no block exceeds €50,000, you do not need to file. If one does, all the assets in that block go into the declaration. A joint account or a flat held in shared ownership is counted in full, not by your share.

    Cryptocurrency on foreign exchanges and in services that hold it for you is a separate declaration, Modelo 721: the threshold is also €50,000 as at 31 December, and the deadline is the same.

    Modelo 720 is information, not a tax. A resident reports income from assets abroad — rent, interest, a sale — in the annual IRPF return.

    What happens if you do not file

    Until 2022, Modelo 720 carried special consequences: large fixed fines, a 150% penalty and additional tax assessments with no limitation period. On 27 January 2022, the Court of Justice of the EU (case C-788/19) found them to breach the free movement of capital, and Ley 5/2022 abolished them.

    The general rules now apply (art. 198 LGT): for failing to file — €20 for each item or set of data, from €300 to €20,000 for each block. If you file late on your own initiative, before the tax office asks, it is half as much: from €150 to €10,000.

    Frequently asked questions

    My flat in Ukraine was bought a long time ago and cheaply. Do I have to declare it too?
    For property, the acquisition value counts, not today's value: the purchase price together with the costs and taxes of the transaction, and if the flat was inherited or received as a gift, its real value at the time of acquisition. If the amount is not in euros, it is converted at the exchange rate on 31 December of the reporting year, not at the rate on the date of purchase. You do not need to file for this block if the value of all your properties abroad, calculated in this way, does not exceed €50,000 in total. If the flat is in shared ownership, its full value is counted, not just your share. Accounts and securities are counted separately, in their own blocks.
    I filed Modelo 720 last year. Do I have to file every year?
    No. You file again only if the amount of a block has grown by more than €20,000 since the last declaration, another block has exceeded €50,000 for the first time, or you have sold an asset or closed an account that has already been declared. We keep track of this for you.
    I missed the deadline. What should I do?
    File as soon as possible, without waiting for a letter from the tax office: if you file late on your own initiative, the fine is half as much — from €150 to €10,000 (art. 198 LGT). We will work out for which years a declaration is needed and file it.
    I am under the Beckham Law regime. Do I have to file too?
    While the regime applies — according to the tax office's (AEAT) guidance, no: under this regime, tax is not calculated on all worldwide income. The obligation may arise from the first year after the regime ends — if any one block exceeds €50,000. More details on the Beckham Law regime page.
    I am under temporary protection. Does this apply to me?
    Immigration status and tax residence are different things. If you lived in Spain for more than 183 days during the year, you are a tax resident, and the Modelo 720 rules for you are the same as for everyone else.
    What is Modelo 721?
    A declaration of cryptocurrency held abroad — on foreign exchanges and in services that hold it for you. The threshold and deadline are the same: more than €50,000 as at 31 December, filing from 1 January to 31 March. If you hold cryptocurrency, we file both declarations.
