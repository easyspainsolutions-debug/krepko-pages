    Transferring money to Spain

    • from Ukraine and Russia
    • documents for the bank in advance
    • a specialist will reply within 15 minutes

    In short. Money is transferred to Spain from your own account to a Spanish one, by bank transfer. By law, a Spanish bank checks the source of large and unusual incoming payments and may hold a transfer until the check is complete. Since 29 January 2026 Russia has been on the EU list of high-risk countries for money laundering: banks apply enhanced due diligence to transactions linked to Russia. The €100,000 ceiling on the accounts of Russian citizens at a single EU bank does not apply to those who hold a residence permit in an EU country. We prepare the documents for the bank and accompany you at the branch; we do not accept or transfer the money itself. Sources: Ley 10/2010; Regulation (EU) 2026/46.

    You have sold a flat, received an inheritance or are simply moving with your savings — and you want the money to reach your Spanish account without being blocked. Most often a transfer gets stuck not when it is sent, but at the check in the Spanish bank. That is why we start with the documents: we prepare them before the transfer, not after a letter from the bank.

    How the process works

    1. Route reviewYou describe where the money is coming from and where it is going. We tell you which restrictions apply in the sending country and what the Spanish bank will ask.
    2. DocumentsWe draw up a list for your situation and obtain what is missing: certificates, statements, notarial documents with an apostille and translation.
    3. BankWe prepare the set of documents for the bank before the transfer. If the transfer has already been held, we reply to the bank's request and accompany you at the branch.
    4. TransferYou transfer the money yourself, from your own account to your own account: we do not accept or transfer it.

    The cost depends on the set of documents — we agree it before work begins, with no hidden extras.

    From Ukraine

    The National Bank of Ukraine (NBU) allows money to be transferred abroad only in a closed list of cases: payment for goods and services, housing rent, medical treatment, education. Most of them are subject to monthly limits; medical treatment and education have no limit on the amount. As a rule, the payment goes to a specific recipient for a specific service; card-to-card transfers to private individuals abroad are possible only within the monthly limit.

    Transferring a large sum to your own account abroad — for example, money from the sale of a flat — is not expressly named among these cases. That is why we plan the route of the money before the transaction, not after: the sale contract with an apostille and translation will be needed by both the bank and the Spanish tax office.

    The NBU changes its limits several times a year — in 2026, in August and September. Before your transfer we check the version currently in force.

    From Russia

    • The €100,000 ceiling. An EU bank does not accept deposits from a Russian citizen if their accounts at that bank hold more than €100,000. The ceiling does not apply to those who hold a temporary or permanent residence permit in an EU country: with a Spanish TIE, it does not affect you.
    • Sanctioned banks. Transactions with banks on EU sanctions lists are prohibited — the transfer goes from a bank that is not on these lists.
    • Enhanced due diligence. Since 29 January 2026 Russia has been on the EU list of high-risk third countries for money laundering. The bank is required to apply enhanced checks to transactions linked to Russia — including the source of funds and the client's source of wealth.
    • Third countries. Transferring through your own account in a third country does not lift the ceiling: it is calculated by citizenship, not by the sending country. We do not advise this route.

    What the Spanish bank will ask

    Banks are required to check the source of funds for large and unusual transactions. They usually ask for:

    • a supporting document: sale contract, certificate of inheritance, deed of gift or loan agreement;
    • a statement from the sender's account;
    • proof of income, if the money is savings.

    By law the bank does not explain why a transaction is being checked, and without documents it may refuse to carry it out or close the account. Documents on the source of the money will also be useful for the tax office: it taxes an unexplained increase in assets. We put together the set of documents for the bank as part of the proof of the source of funds service.

    Cash across the border

    From €10,000, cash is declared at the EU external border and separately on entry to Spain — even from another EU country. Without a declaration the money is temporarily seized. Within Spain itself you cannot pay €1,000 or more in cash if one of the parties is a company or a business owner.

    What we do not do

    We do not accept or transfer clients' money. We do not advise on how to get around limits, sanctions or bank checks: no splitting of amounts, no other people's accounts and no workaround routes. We work only with legal channels and documents.

    Frequently asked questions

    The bank has held my transfer and is asking for documents. What should I do?
    Reply without waiting for a second request: if the check cannot be completed, the bank is entitled to refuse the transaction or close the account. Send us the bank's letter — we will tell you what to collect, prepare the documents and accompany you at the branch.
    I have a residence permit in Spain and a Russian passport. Does the €100,000 limit apply?
    No. The ceiling does not apply to those who hold a temporary or permanent residence permit in an EU country. The bank will still check the source of funds — it is better to prepare the documents in advance.
    Can I transfer money from the sale of a flat in Ukraine to Spain?
    NBU restrictions on such transfers are strict, and the rules change several times a year. That is why we plan the route of the money before the transaction and prepare the sale contract with an apostille and translation straight away — both the bank and the tax office in Spain will ask for it.
    How much cash can I bring into Spain?
    The amount is not limited, but from €10,000 cash must be declared — at the EU border and on entry to Spain, including from another EU country. Without a declaration the money is temporarily seized.
    Do you transfer the money yourselves?
    No. You transfer the money yourself, from your own account to your own account. We prepare the documents for the bank, explain the restrictions and accompany you at the branch.
    Why does the bank not explain the reason for the delay?
    The law prohibits the bank from telling the client that their transaction is being checked. That is why the bank's reply usually comes as a general request for documents — and we prepare the set of documents based on that request.
