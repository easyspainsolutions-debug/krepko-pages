    Política de Cookies / Cookie policy

    In force since 2026-05-12 · Última actualización: 26 octubre 2026
    In brief. The website uses only analytics cookies — Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity — and only if you have clicked “Accept all”. They are needed to understand which materials people read and which parts of the website are hard to use. No advertising or marketing cookies. You can disable them in your browser — the website will continue to work as usual.

    1. What cookies are

    Cookies are small text files that websites store in your browser on your first visit. They serve various purposes: remembering your settings, helping the website work correctly, collecting anonymised traffic statistics.

    2. Which cookies krepko.es uses

    The website uses only analytics cookies from two services: Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and Microsoft Clarity. They are set only after you have clicked “Accept all” in the banner. Separately, the website counts anonymised statistics without cookies — this is described in the privacy policy.

    CookiePurposeDurationType
    _ga User session identifier for GA4 (anonymised) 2 years Analytics
    _ga_8F3TB9QMLY Session state for a specific GA4 property 2 years Analytics
    _clck Anonymised visitor identifier for Microsoft Clarity 1 year Analytics
    _clsk Combines page views into a single Clarity session recording 1 day Analytics
    CLID, MUID Microsoft identifiers for the operation of Clarity (third-party) 1 year Analytics

    GA4 property ID: G-8F3TB9QMLY. Microsoft Clarity project ID: z3npvw3og1.

    3. What these cookies collect

    GA4 analytics cookies collect:

    • Traffic source (where the visitor came from — search, direct visit, link)
    • Which pages were visited, in what order and for how long
    • Device type (mobile / desktop), browser, screen resolution
    • Approximate geolocation (at country/city level, without an exact address)
    • Anonymised IP address (Google anonymises IP addresses by default in the EU)

    The data is aggregated and used only to understand which materials help users and which need improving. Krepko cannot identify a specific person from this data.

    For more on how Google processes this data, see Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

    Microsoft Clarity shows how people use a page: clicks, scrolling, cursor movement, as well as session recordings that show where a person got confused. Clarity masks the text you type into fields. More details: Microsoft Privacy Statement.

    4. What the website does NOT have

    • Advertising cookies — no Facebook Pixel, Google Ads, TikTok Pixel or similar
    • Marketing trackers — no retargeting, no profiling for advertising
    • Social media cookies — social media buttons are not loaded automatically
    • Partner cookies — no third parties other than Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity
    • “Dark patterns” in the consent banner — no pressure to accept

    5. Legal basis

    In accordance with the GDPR (RGPD) and the Spanish law LSSICE, the use of analytics cookies for aggregated statistics is permitted on the basis of the website Owner’s legitimate interest (legítimo interés), provided that:

    • IP anonymisation is used
    • Specific users are not identified
    • Data is not passed to advertising networks
    • The option to opt out is provided

    All of these conditions are met.

    6. How to disable cookies

    Through your browser settings

    Any browser lets you block, delete or restrict cookies. Instructions for the main browsers:

    • Google Chrome
    • Mozilla Firefox
    • Safari (Mac)
    • Microsoft Edge
    • Opera

    Disable Google Analytics only

    Google provides an official browser add-on that blocks only GA cookies without affecting anything else:
    tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

    Incognito mode

    In private browsing mode, most browsers do not keep cookies after the tab is closed. This is the simplest way to visit the website “without a trace”.

    7. If you disable cookies

    The website will continue to work exactly the same. All articles, prices, forms and contact details are available without cookies. The only thing that changes is that Krepko will not see that you visited (for analytics, this is +1 unknown visitor in the statistics).

    8. Changes to the policy

    If new services appear on the website (for example, a chat widget or a payment form) that require additional cookies, this page will be updated with a new date. If the new cookies are not solely analytical, a consent banner will be added.
