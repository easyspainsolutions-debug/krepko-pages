    Spanish citizenship after 10 years: how to count the period and which documents to prepare in advance

    Krepko · updated 12 November 2026 · ~9 min read
    In short. The general period for Spanish nationality by residence is 10 years of lawful and continuous residence immediately before the application (art. 22 of the Civil Code). They are counted from the first residence permit, not from arrival. Besides the period, you need the DELE exam at A2 level or higher and the CCSE, a birth certificate and a criminal record certificate from your country of origin, legalised and translated. By law, a decision is made within a year, silence means refusal, and at the oath you declare that you renounce your previous citizenship; the rules were checked on 1 November 2026.

    Anyone who received their first residence permit in Spain in 2016 reaches ten years in 2026 — the general period for nationality by residence. It is counted from the first lawful permit, not from arrival, and you apply only once the period has been completed in full. What takes longest to prepare is not the application but the documents from your country of birth: the birth certificate and the criminal record certificate with a translation and, as a rule, an apostille, and the criminal record certificate is not valid for long.

    Below we look at how to count the period, whether the years under temporary protection count, what happens to Ukrainian citizenship and in what sequence to order documents from Ukraine and Russia. We checked the rules on 1 November 2026 against the BOE and the websites of the Spanish and Ukrainian ministries.

    The application is submitted electronically on the Ministry of Justice portal. What takes longest is preparing the papers from your country of birth. We order them from Ukraine and Russia without you having to travel. We prepare the documents for Spanish nationality for your application date, while the application itself is handled by an immigration lawyer.

    From which date the 10 years are counted

    The Civil Code (art. 22.3) requires residence to be lawful, continuous and immediately preceding the application. In practice this means:

    • Only lawful residence counts. Time as a tourist or without documents does not count towards the period. If you received your first residence permit through arraigo (residence on the grounds of ties to Spain), the years before it do not count.
    • The count starts from the date your first residence permit takes effect, not from the date of arrival or municipal registration.
    • You can apply only once the period has been completed in full: with a first permit from March 2016 — not before March 2026.
    • Continuity is assessed over the whole period. The law does not specify a permissible length of trips for nationality. Long absences and gaps between cards, for example due to a late renewal, are best reviewed before applying.

    Besides the period, the law requires good civic conduct and integration (art. 22.4), and the minister may refuse on grounds of public order or national interest (art. 21.2).

    Who needs less than 10 years. Five years — refugees; two years — nationals by birth of Latin American countries, Andorra, the Philippines, Equatorial Guinea and Portugal, and Sephardic Jews; one year — for example, those born in Spain and those who, by the time they apply, have been married for a year to a Spanish national (art. 22.1–22.2). These cases are covered in the articles on nationality for a child and on nationality through marriage.

    Do the years under temporary protection count

    Years under temporary protection count towards the five years for long-term residence (larga duración nacional) — this is stated expressly in SEM instruction 2/2026. You cannot apply for larga duración-UE from a protection card. For nationality, the question has not been officially decided: the Civil Code does not mention protection, the SEM instruction is silent on nationality, and we had found no position from the Ministry of Justice as of 1 November 2026.

    Even if the years are counted, ten years under protection cannot be completed before 10 March 2032: the regime has applied in Spain since 10 March 2022, when Orden PCM/169/2022 entered into force.

    Temporary protection is not refugee status. It is a separate regime (RD 1325/2003), not international protection, so the five-year period for refugees does not apply to it.

    What you can already plan for is moving to long-term residence. You apply for it directly from a protection card once five years have passed since the date of your protection decision; the very first beneficiaries of protection will reach them no earlier than 10 March 2027. Years on long-term residence are already indisputably lawful residence. Details are in the article on permanent residence after temporary protection, and other routes in the overview of residence permit types in Spain.

    What happens to Ukrainian citizenship

    Spain is unequivocal: at the oath you declare that you renounce your previous citizenship (art. 23.b of the Civil Code). The exceptions are for natives of Latin American countries, Andorra, the Philippines, Equatorial Guinea, Portugal and for Sephardic Jews; they do not apply to Ukrainians and Russians. There is also a follow-on rule: a Spaniard who is not Spanish by birth loses Spanish nationality if for three years they use exclusively the citizenship they renounced (art. 25.1.a).

    On the Ukrainian side, Law No. 4502-IX on multiple citizenship has been in force since 16 January 2026 (State Migration Service of Ukraine, DMSU). Under it, Ukraine recognises multiple citizenship when a Ukrainian citizen acquires the citizenship of a state on the Cabinet of Ministers' list, and EU membership is taken into account when drawing up the list (Verkhovna Rada). The list was approved by Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 1412 and was amended in 2026.

    Caveat. We were unable to open the current texts of the law and the list on zakon.rada.gov.ua on 1 November 2026, so we make no claim as to whether Spain is on the list. There is also no official clarification of how Ukraine treats the renunciation made at the Spanish oath. Check the texts before the oath. For Russian citizens the Spanish requirement is the same; find out the consequences under your country's law in advance.

    Which documents are needed

    The list is set by the annex to Orden JUS/1625/2016. For an adult applicant, the core is as follows:

    • a full, valid passport of your country of origin — the one stated on your residence card;
    • a birth certificate from your country of origin, legalised and translated;
    • a criminal record certificate from your country of origin, also legalised and translated;
    • a marriage certificate from the country where the marriage took place, if you are married;
    • DELE A2 and CCSE certificates or consent to the results being checked with the Instituto Cervantes;
    • proof of payment of the fee (tasa) — €104.05 (Modelo 790, code 026; the amount on the Ministry of Justice website as of 1 November 2026).

    The ministry checks the Spanish criminal record certificate, municipal registration and residence permit details itself if you have given consent in the application. All documents must be valid on the day of application.

    How we prepare the documents

    A Soviet birth certificate

    If your certificate is of the Soviet type, an apostille will not be affixed to the original in Ukraine: the rules for affixing apostilles do not allow it on original documents of authorities of the former Soviet republics of the USSR (point 4). The way out is a reissued certificate of the modern type from DRACS: an apostille is affixed to it.

    The place of birth in the reissued certificate is written according to the civil registration record, so Soviet names of cities are, as a rule, retained. And a Ukrainian apostille is not affixed to a reissued certificate from the Embassy of Ukraine in Madrid (point 3 of the same rules), so it is best to find out before applying whether the Ministry of Justice will accept it for nationality; a DRACS form with an apostille does not raise this question. Check the spelling of names against your passport and residence card.

    How to get it without travelling is explained in the article on a birth certificate from Ukraine; we order it with an apostille without you having to travel — reissued birth certificate.

    Russia: what to bear in mind

    Russia. The criminal record certificate is needed for the whole country, not for a region: the annex to the order lists Russia among federal states. The apostille is affixed to it in Russia. For ZAGS certificates, the exemption under the 1984 exchange of notes applies (see the table); we will tell you during the review of your case whether it applies to your document. Details are on the Documents from Russia page.

    The order: exams, documents, application

    1. Count the period
    The date of your first permit, trips and gaps between cards.
    2. Take the exams
    DELE at A2 level or higher and the CCSE (a test on the Constitution and life in Spain) are run by the Instituto Cervantes, as a general rule in person (art. 6 of RD 1004/2015, art. 10 of Orden JUS/1625/2016). The CCSE certificate is valid for four years, so it is convenient to take the exams first. Minors do not take them.
    3. Order the certificates
    Birth and, if you are married, marriage certificates — legalised (as a rule, with an apostille; for Russian ZAGS certificates, see the table) and translated. Their validity is determined by the document itself, so they are prepared in advance.
    4. Check your passport
    Full and valid on the day of application. If it is expiring, a Ukrainian passport can be replaced in Spain.
    5. The criminal record certificate — last
    If no validity period is stated on the certificate itself, it is valid for six months from the date of issue, and the apostille and translation fall within those six months, so the certificate is ordered to match the application date. We order the Ukrainian one without you having to travel — criminal record certificate from Ukraine; details are in the article on the two criminal record certificates.
    6. Submit the application
    Electronically on the Ministry of Justice portal, with the fee paid.
    7. Decision and oath
    By law — within a year of the application reaching the Ministry of Justice; without a reply it is deemed rejected (art. 11 of RD 1004/2015). After notification of a favourable decision you have 180 days to take the oath and declare that you renounce your previous citizenship, otherwise the decision lapses (art. 21.4 of the Civil Code). According to the Ministry of Justice, the oath is taken at the Registro Civil (the Spanish civil registry) or before a notary.

    We count the period from your documents, order the birth certificate and the criminal record certificate from Ukraine and Russia without you having to travel and make sure nothing has expired by the time you apply. The application is handled by an immigration lawyer, and the decision is made by the Minister of Justice.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many years does it take to get Spanish citizenship?
    Usually after 10 years of lawful and continuous residence immediately before the application (art. 22 of the Civil Code). Refugees need five years, nationals by birth of Latin American countries, Andorra, the Philippines, Equatorial Guinea and Portugal — two. One year is enough, for example, for those born in Spain and those who, by the time they apply, have been married to a Spanish national for a year.
    Does temporary protection count towards the 10 years for nationality?
    The question has not been officially decided. Years under protection count towards the five years for long-term residence (larga duración nacional) — this is stated in SEM instruction 2/2026, while you cannot apply for larga duración-UE from a protection card. The instruction is silent on nationality, and there is no position from the Ministry of Justice. Even if they are counted, ten years under protection cannot be completed before 10 March 2032.
    Will I have to renounce Ukrainian citizenship?
    Spain requires you to declare at the oath that you renounce your previous citizenship (art. 23 of the Civil Code), and there is no exception for Ukrainians. On the Ukrainian side, Law No. 4502-IX on multiple citizenship has been in force since 16 January 2026, but how Ukraine treats a renunciation declared at a Spanish oath has not been officially clarified. It is worth checking the current texts of the law and the list of states before the oath.
    I have a Soviet birth certificate. Will it be accepted?
    Not in that form: apostilles are not affixed to original Soviet documents in Ukraine, and for nationality the certificate must be legalised. You need a reissued certificate of the modern type from DRACS with an apostille. We order it without you having to travel to Ukraine.
    Will a notarised translation made in Ukraine be accepted?
    Not for nationality. The Spanish Ministry of Justice guidance note names a sworn translation (traductor jurado) or a consular translation legalised by the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs; a notarised translation from Ukraine is not on this list.
    How long does it take to get a decision?
    By law — up to a year from the moment the application reaches the Ministry of Justice; if there is no reply, it is deemed rejected (art. 11 of RD 1004/2015). After notification of a favourable decision you have 180 days to take the oath and declare that you renounce your previous citizenship, otherwise the decision lapses (art. 21.4 of the Civil Code).
