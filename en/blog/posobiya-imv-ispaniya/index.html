    Benefits in Spain in 2026: what you are actually entitled to with temporary protection

    Krepko · updated 13 November 2026 · ~9 min read
    In short. The national minimum income (IMV) in 2026 is up to €733.60 a month for one adult. You can apply after a year of legal residence in Spain, and there is no exception for temporary protection. The old-style child benefit has been replaced by the CAPI supplement — up to €115 a month per child. Regional income, as a rule, is paid on top of the IMV. The exception is Andalusia: there, RMISA is granted only after the IMV has been refused.

    Many figures about benefits in Spain get passed around, and most of them are out of date: the minimum income rose noticeably in 2026, the old-style child benefit was closed to new applications back in 2020, and Catalonia's PIRMI has not existed since 2017. If you live in Spain with temporary protection or have recently obtained a residence permit, the answer to the question “am I entitled to it?” depends above all on one date.

    Below are the 2026 amounts according to the Seguridad Social and the BOE, the key condition of a year of residence, how to apply, the child supplement, and how the regional payments of Catalonia, Valencia, Madrid and Andalusia combine with the national one.

    IMV minimum income: how much is paid in 2026

    The Ingreso Mínimo Vital is a national payment for households with an income below a guaranteed level. Its amount is linked to the non-contributory pension, not to the IPREM: the guaranteed income of one adult equals this pension divided by 12 (Ley 19/2021, art. 13). In 2026 the pension was raised to €8,803.20 a year (RDL 3/2026), and the IMV rose by about 11%.

    Number of people in the household Guaranteed monthly income If there is one adult raising children
    1€733.60—
    2€953.68€1,115.07
    3€1,173.76€1,335.15
    4€1,393.84€1,555.23
    5 or more€1,613.92 (cap)€1,775.31

    Each household member from the second onwards adds 30% to the base; the cap is 220%. A single-parent household gets a further 22%, and the same applies if the household includes a person with a disability of 65% or more.

    These figures are a cap, not the payment. INSS pays the difference between the guaranteed income and the household's income for the previous year, and this difference must be at least €10 a month. Example: a single adult earned €4,800 last year, that is, €400 a month. The payment is 733.60 − 400 = €333.60. The average payment is also noticeably below the cap: in December 2025, still based on the 2025 amounts, it was €483.1 a month per household of any size (government data).

    The asset ceiling for one person is €26,409.60, not counting the home you live in; for households the scale is higher.

    The key condition: a year of legal residence

    The law requires legal and actual residence in Spain continuously for at least a year before applying (art. 10.1.a). The one-year requirement is waived only for children who joined the household through birth, adoption, reunification, guardianship with a view to adoption or permanent foster care, for victims of human trafficking and for victims of gender-based violence. Temporary protection is not on this list.

    Temporary protection is a permit to reside and work, and the law accepts an “autorización de residencia en cualquiera de sus modalidades” (art. 21.2). Hence our reading: the year counts from the date of the first permit. We have not found a direct clarification from INSS specifically on temporary protection, so we put it cautiously: it makes sense to apply after a year of legal residence, with no guarantees for any specific case.

    The padrón does not replace the year. The municipal registration certificate (certificado de empadronamiento) proves only your address (art. 21.3). Twelve months on the padrón with nine months of a permit means nine months, not a year. An NIE is mandatory. How to register is explained in our article on the padrón.

    Other conditions:

    • age: a single applicant must be 23 or over. Between 23 and 29 you need two years of living independently and 12 months of work with alta (registration) in the Seguridad Social; from 30, a year at an address separate from your parents. The head of a household with children may be under 23 if they are an adult;
    • household: its members must have lived together for at least 6 months before applying. Exceptions: birth, adoption, reunification, violence.

    How and where to apply for the IMV

    1. Check two dates: whether a year has passed since your first permit and what the household's income was for the previous year. If the need arose this year (for example, you lost your job), from 1 April to 31 December you can apply on the basis of the current year's income, provided the previous year's income did not exceed the set limits.
    2. Gather the documents: NIE, the temporary protection card or decision, a municipal registration certificate listing everyone who lives with you, marriage documents and your children's birth documents.
    3. Submit the application at imv.seg-social.es or through the Seguridad Social electronic office. There is a route there without a digital certificate or Cl@ve. INSS decides, but in the Basque Country, Navarre and, from 1 October 2026, Catalonia the region itself decides, and the application is submitted there (Lanbide, Navarre's social services, the Generalitat via tràmits.gencat.cat); other regions and municipalities accept applications only under an agreement with INSS. The information line is 020.
    4. Wait up to 6 months for a decision. The right to the payment starts on the 1st of the month following the application (art. 14.1). If there is no answer within 6 months, it is a refusal (art. 28.3).

    The recipient must report changes in income and household composition to INSS within 30 days and file an IRPF tax return every year. You can work: since 2023 there has been a work incentive (RD 789/2022), and RD 240/2026 revised it from 27 March 2026. An increase in earnings of up to €6,000 a year is not counted at all in the annual recalculation, and only half of the amount above €6,000 is counted (45% for households with disability or single-parent supplements).

    The CAPI child supplement instead of the old child benefit

    The benefit for a child without a disability or with a disability below 33% (asignación por hijo a cargo) has been closed to new applications since 1 June 2020, when RDL 20/2020 came into force. Those who received it before keep receiving it until they lose the entitlement.

    In its place there is the complemento de ayuda para la infancia (CAPI), a monthly amount for each child according to their age on 1 January:

    • under 3 — €115;
    • from 3 to 6 — €80.50;
    • from 6 to 18 — €57.50.

    CAPI can be received without the IMV itself: the household's income for the previous year must be below 300% of the guaranteed income. For two adults and two children this is about €50,178 a year — our own calculation using the formula; there is no official example. The year of residence is required here too. The supplement is applied for in the same application at imv.seg-social.es (in the Basque Country, Navarre and Catalonia, through the region); the tax office (AEAT) has nothing to do with it.

    The strand for children with disabilities continues: for a child under 18 with a disability of 33% or more, €1,000 a year; from 18 with a disability of 65% or more, €5,962.80 a year.

    Regional income: usually a top-up to the IMV

    The order is the same almost everywhere: first you apply for the IMV, then the region tops it up to its own level.

    Region and payment Maximum for one person Residence in the region
    Catalonia — Renda Garantida de Ciutadania (RGC)€801.852 years
    Valencia now — Renta Valenciana de Inclusión (RVI)by the formula of Law 19/201712 months
    Valencia from 20.10.2026 — RVI under the new law€793.803 years; no minimum period for temporary protection
    Madrid — Renta Mínima de Inserción (RMI)€469.931 year
    Andalusia — Renta Mínima de Inserción Social (RMISA)€733.601 year

    Catalonia. PIRMI was abolished in 2017 (Llei 14/2017); RGC now applies: €733.60 IMV + €68.25 RGC = €801.85. You need two years of living in Catalonia; there is a shorter period for refugees, but temporary protection is not named in the list. As a rule, RGC cannot be combined with work. Applications are made in person at the Oficina de Treball for your place of residence, by appointment (phone 012).

    Valencia. Law 19/2017 currently applies: 12 months on the padrón, the IMV is deducted and RVI pays the difference. The new Law 2/2026 comes into force on 20 October 2026: up to €793.80 a month for one person plus €60 per child (if the household does not receive CAPI or other child payments), but no more than €180 per household, and it expressly exempts people under temporary protection from the residence period (art. 9.2.b). In August 2026 the law was amended (Ley 5/2026): the general residence period became 3 years instead of 15 months, and the exemption for temporary protection was kept. The same amendment wrote the principle of “national priority” into the law and instructed the regional government to set out the access conditions in a separate decree; how this will affect temporary protection is not yet known. Conditions: first apply for the IMV, not be in official employment, be aged 23 or over (from 18 if you have children in your care, with a disability of 33% or more and in other special cases, art. 4.2.b).

    Madrid. RMI for one person is €469.93, less than the IMV: if a single person is awarded the IMV in full, RMI will add nothing. Age 25–65; applications go to the municipal social services centre.

    Andalusia. The logic is the reverse: RMISA is paid to those who were refused the IMV in the same year — €733.60 for one person plus €220.08 for each additional household member. You must be registered with the SAE (the Andalusian employment service) as a jobseeker. Applications go to the servicios sociales comunitarios (community social services) or through the Junta portal.

    Temporary protection: what you have from day one and how benefits relate to a residence permit

    From day one, temporary protection gives you a permit to reside and work, and access to social services and healthcare. People without means can enter the state reception system (sistema de acogida) — this is the route for those who do not yet have a year of residence. The payment of €400 plus €100 per child, which was given for six months in 2022, has ended: in Madrid, applications are officially closed.

    Temporary protection cards have been extended until 4 March 2027, and the status itself at EU level until 4 March 2028. When switching to your own residence permit, one detail matters. If you have lived in Spain for a year and your permit already gives you the right to work, when switching under art. 191.3 RD 1155/2024 the renewal requirements of art. 80 are checked. One of the grounds is receiving a public payment aimed at social or labour integration (art. 80.2.c, referring to LOEX, art. 38.6.c). In our view, the IMV and regional income fit this description by their purpose. We have not found an official instruction that says so directly, so before applying check with an immigration lawyer. The switching routes are collected on the page Switching from temporary protection.

    Common mistakes

    • Counting the year from the padrón. What counts is legal residence, that is, the permit.
    • Trusting old amounts. “About €580 for one person” and “PIRMI up to €730” are out of date. Current amounts are published on seg-social.es.
    • Reporting changes to the tax office. Changes in income and household composition are reported to INSS, within 30 days.
    • Leaving for a long time without telling anyone. Notify INSS in advance of trips abroad longer than 90 days a year. Bear in mind that more than 90 days outside Spain in a year (except for a confirmed illness) puts the right to the IMV itself at risk.

    If you do not qualify yet

    • You do not have a year of residence. Contact the municipal social services and the reception system. Note the date when a year will have passed since your first permit, and apply the next day. In Valencia, from 20 October 2026, people with temporary protection are exempt from the residence period for RVI.
    • Your income is above the IMV threshold. Check CAPI: its threshold is three times higher.
    • You worked and lost your job. Look at unemployment benefit: you need 360 days of contributions in the last 6 years; it pays 70% of the base for the first 180 days and 60% after that. The application must be made within 15 working days of dismissal.

    Frequently asked questions

    I have had temporary protection for six months. Can I apply for the IMV?
    Not yet. The IMV requires a year of legal residence in Spain before applying, and temporary protection is not named among the exceptions. It is reasonable to count the year from the date of your first permit — there is no direct clarification from INSS on temporary protection, so this comes without guarantees. Until then, contact the municipal social services and the reception system.
    How much IMV will a single person get in 2026?
    Up to €733.60 a month. The actual payment is the difference between this amount and your income for the previous year, and it must be at least €10 a month. If you earned €400 a month last year, the IMV will be about €333.60.
    Can I receive the IMV and regional income at the same time?
    Yes: regional income, as a rule, tops up the IMV to its own level. In Catalonia, RGC brings the amount up to €801.85 for one person; in Valencia, RVI pays the difference. In Madrid, RMI for one person is €469.93, which is less than the IMV, so with the full IMV there will be no top-up. In Andalusia it is the other way round: RMISA is granted after the IMV has been refused.
    Where do I apply for child benefit?
    The old benefit for a child without a disability or with a disability below 33% has been closed to new applications since 1 June 2020. It has been replaced by the CAPI child supplement — €115, €80.50 or €57.50 a month depending on age. It is applied for in the same application as the IMV, at imv.seg-social.es, and in the Basque Country, Navarre and Catalonia through the region. The tax office has nothing to do with it.
    How long does it take to get a decision on the IMV?
    By law, up to 6 months. If there is no decision within that time, it counts as a refusal. If approved, the payment accrues from the 1st of the month following the application.
    Can the benefit help when switching from temporary protection to a residence permit?
    It can. If you have lived in Spain for a year and your permit already gives you the right to work, when switching under art. 191.3 RD 1155/2024 the renewal requirements of art. 80 are checked. One of the grounds is receiving a public payment aimed at social or labour integration (art. 80.2.c, referring to LOEX, art. 38.6.c). We have not found an official instruction that names the IMV directly, and different routes have their own income requirements, so check with a lawyer before applying.
    Do you arrange benefits?
    No. You submit the application for the IMV and regional payments yourself or through social services; the INSS information line is 020. We prepare Ukrainian documents for the file: duplicate birth and marriage certificates, the apostille and translation into Spanish.
