    How to accept an inheritance in Ukraine while living in Spain

    Krepko · updated 13 November 2026 · ~9 min read
    In short. To accept an inheritance in Ukraine, as a general rule you have six months from the date of death (Art. 1270 of the Civil Code of Ukraine). The application is filed in person or sent to the notary by post with the signature authenticated by a notary, and it is not accepted under a power of attorney (Art. 1269 CC, para. 3.6, Ch. 10 of Procedure No. 296/5). From Spain, the signature is authenticated by a Spanish notary, the document is apostilled and the original is sent to the notary at the deceased's last place of residence; we agree the form in advance with that notary. If you still do not have the formal application by the end of the period, the deadline is preserved by an application sent before it expires by post without signature authentication or electronically with a qualified electronic signature (KEP): the notary will open the case and ask you to send the formal one. Rules checked on 1 November 2026.

    A parent or another person close to you has died in Ukraine, and you live in Spain and cannot or do not want to travel there. You can accept the inheritance from here too, but on one condition: you sign the application to accept it yourself; it is not filed under a power of attorney. The signature on it can be authenticated before a Spanish notary, and the remaining steps in the case can be entrusted to a representative in Ukraine.

    Below we look at how much time you have, who does not need an application at all, how to arrange it in Spain and send it to Ukraine, and what to do if the deadline has almost run out or has already passed. We checked the rules on 1 November 2026 against the Civil Code of Ukraine and the Procedure for Performing Notarial Acts.

    How much time you have

    The deadline for accepting an inheritance is six months. It starts on the day the inheritance opens, that is, the date of death (Arts. 1220 and 1270 of the Civil Code of Ukraine). An heir who has not filed an application within this period is deemed not to have accepted the inheritance (Art. 1272), unless the law deems them to have accepted it without an application (more on this below).

    That is why you should start with the application, not with the power of attorney. The signature on it will be authenticated by a Spanish notary in your city: this is signature authentication on a document. The notary certifies; we prepare the text and accompany you at the signing.

    There are two exceptions to the general rule. If the right to the inheritance arose for you only because other heirs did not accept it or renounced it, you have three months from that moment to apply (Art. 1270 Part 2). And during martial law and for two more years after it ends, a special counting rule applies: if the death was registered more than a month after the date of death or the day from which the person was declared dead, the six months are counted from the date of state registration of the death. The day the inheritance opens remains the date of death (para. 20 of the Final and Transitional Provisions of the CC).

    Deadlines are not suspended. The advice that "during martial law the inheritance deadlines are suspended" is out of date: the current version of Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 164 on notarial practice contains no such rule. There is now only one special rule on deadlines during martial law: counting from the date the death was registered.

    Why the application cannot be filed under a power of attorney

    The Civil Code of Ukraine requires the application to accept an inheritance to be filed by the heir personally (Art. 1269 Part 2). The Procedure for Performing Notarial Acts specifies: applications to accept an inheritance, to renounce it and to withdraw such applications that are drawn up by a representative under a power of attorney are not accepted by the notary (para. 3.6, Ch. 10).

    "In person" does not mean "travel there". If you come to a Ukrainian notary yourself, the signature does not need to be authenticated: the notary will establish your identity on the spot (para. 3.4). From abroad, the application is sent by post, and then the authenticity of the signature on it must be certified by a notary (para. 3.5).

    Everything else can be handled by a representative under a power of attorney, so at the notary's office in Spain it is convenient to sign two documents in one visit: the application and the power of attorney.

    Who does not need an application, and when a renunciation is needed

    The law deems some heirs to have accepted the inheritance without an application (Art. 1268 CC):

    • those who lived permanently with the deceased on the date of death, if they have not declared a renunciation within six months;
    • young children and minors, as well as heirs who lack legal capacity or have limited legal capacity.

    The notary checks cohabitation primarily from the documents on registration of the place of residence (paras. 3.19–3.20, Ch. 10 of the Procedure). If you live in Spain but in Ukraine you are still registered at the same address as the deceased, check this with the notary: you may be deemed to have already accepted the inheritance. But it is still safer to file an application within six months: then the acceptance will not depend on a dispute about where you lived.

    If such an heir does not want to inherit, they need a renunciation, within the same six months (Art. 1273). The application to renounce is drawn up in the same way as the application to accept: in person or by post with an authenticated signature, not under a power of attorney (paras. 3.3–3.6, Ch. 10). For a child under 14, the parents may renounce only with the permission of the guardianship authority; a teenager aged 14 to 18 renounces with the consent of the parents and that authority (Art. 1273 Parts 3–4).

    A renunciation, like an acceptance, applies to the whole inheritance and cannot be conditional (Art. 1268 Part 2, Art. 1273 Part 5 CC, para. 3.12, Ch. 10 of the Procedure). You can change your mind while the deadline for accepting the inheritance has not expired: both the application to accept and the renunciation can be withdrawn within this period (Art. 1269 Part 5, Art. 1273 Part 6).

    If the inheritance was left in Spain, the procedure is different: we explained it in the article on inheritance in Spain.

    How to arrange the application in Spain and send it to Ukraine

    1. Identify the notary in Ukraine
    The application is filed with the notary at the place where the inheritance opens, which is the deceased's last place of residence (Art. 1221 CC). If another notary has already opened the case, for example on the application of another heir, as a rule only that notary will accept the application (para. 2.4, Ch. 10 of the Procedure), so ask your relatives who is handling the case. If the deceased lived where active hostilities are taking place, or in a temporarily occupied territory, and the date on which hostilities or occupation ended has not been determined, then for the period of martial law and six months after it the application can be filed with a notary anywhere in Ukraine. The same applies to settlements where the authorities temporarily do not exercise their powers in full. For occupied territories this rule applies for the whole period of occupation and six months after it (para. 21 of the Final and Transitional Provisions of the CC). Territories of "possible hostilities" and territories where hostilities have already ended are not covered: the notary checks the status of the settlement against the Ministry for Development's List of Territories at the time the first application in the case is filed (para. 2.1, Ch. 10 of the Procedure). So check the status with the notary before sending.
    2. Prepare the text
    We prepare the application in Ukrainian from the start and agree the text in advance with the notary handling the case in Ukraine.
    3. Sign before a Spanish notary
    In person, with a valid passport or TIE. The notary authenticates the signature, and a Krepko representative at the appointment translates every clause. In the same visit you can also sign the power of attorney. If your Ukrainian passport has expired, read our separate guide.
    4. Get the apostille
    On notarial documents the apostille is affixed by the College of Notaries (Real Decreto 1497/2011, Art. 3), and only on the paper original (Orden JUS/1207/2011, Art. 11). Spain and Ukraine are parties to the 1961 Hague Convention, so consular legalisation is not needed. We arrange the submission for the apostille ourselves.
    5. Translate into Ukrainian
    If the application is in Ukrainian from the start, usually only the Spanish notary's certification and the apostille are translated. The final scope of the translation is determined by the notary handling the case. The Krepko team in Ukraine takes care of the translation. If little time is left before the deadline, send the original by post directly to the notary handling the case: an application is deemed filed on time if it was sent to the notary, not forwarded to us (para. 3.21, Ch. 10). We agree in advance with that notary how to attach the translation.
    6. Send the original by post
    An application sent by post to the notary before the six months expire is deemed filed on time, even if it arrives later: the notary files the envelope in the case (para. 3.21, Ch. 10). Keep the postage receipt.
    What is not expressly regulated. The Law of Ukraine "On Notaries" allows a Ukrainian notary to accept documents drawn up under foreign law (Art. 98), and documents from abroad without legalisation if an international treaty provides for this (Art. 100); for Spain this is the apostille under the Hague Convention. There is no separate rule specifically on an application to accept an inheritance with the signature authenticated by a foreign notary. That is why we agree the form and text with the notary in Ukraine before signing, not after.

    The signature can also be authenticated by a Ukrainian consulate (Art. 38 of the Law "On Notaries"). Men aged 18–60 currently need a valid electronic military registration document for consular services (Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 981 of 29 July 2026). A Spanish notary does not ask for such a document. More on this in the article on consular services for men.

    How not to miss the deadline if the application is not ready yet

    Signing, the apostille and posting take time. If little of it is left before the six months run out, the Procedure provides a safety net (paras. 3.5 and 3.21, Ch. 10):

    • an application by post without notarial authentication of the signature: if it was sent before the deadline expires, it is deemed filed on time, even when it arrives later;
    • an application in electronic form, signed with a qualified electronic signature (KEP), if it was sent no later than 23:59 on the last day of the period. It is safer to count by Kyiv time, not Spanish time, and not to leave sending it until the last evening.

    The notary accepts such an application, opens the inheritance case and tells you that you need to send an application with a notarially authenticated signature or come in person. This is a safety net, not a substitute: you will still need the formal application, and you should not delay it.

    Power of attorney: what to entrust to a representative

    Everything except the application itself, the renunciation of the inheritance and the withdrawal of such applications can be done by a representative in Ukraine under a power of attorney: gathering documents, obtaining the certificate of the right to inheritance, registering the property and, if necessary, selling it. The certificate is issued, as a rule, at the place where the inheritance opens and no earlier than six months later (Art. 1298 CC, Art. 66 of the Law "On Notaries"), so it is convenient to sign the power of attorney in advance, during the same visit as the application.

    The power of attorney is drawn up before a Spanish notary directly in the language you need, with an apostille, and the original is sent to the representative. More details on the page on a power of attorney for Ukraine, and what to write in the text so that it is accepted, in a separate article.

    A husband's or wife's consent to selling inherited property is usually not needed: under the Family Code of Ukraine, property received during marriage by inheritance is the heir's personal property (Art. 57). Consent will be needed if you are selling jointly acquired property; see a separate article on this.

    If the six months have already passed

    There are two ways to claim the inheritance after the deadline (Art. 1272 CC).

    1. With the consent of the other heirs. If everyone who has already accepted the inheritance agrees in writing, you file an application with the notary at the place where the inheritance opened, without going to court (Art. 1272 Part 2). Their consent must reach the notary before the certificate of the right to inheritance is issued (para. 3.16, Ch. 10 of the Procedure).
    2. Through the court. If the reason for missing the deadline is valid, the court may set an additional period for filing the application (Art. 1272 Part 3). Whether the reason is valid is assessed by the court; the claim is handled by a lawyer in Ukraine, brought in by the Krepko team in Ukraine.

    Before assuming the deadline has been missed, check the date the death was registered: if it was registered more than a month later, the six months may be counted from that date (para. 20 of the Final and Transitional Provisions of the CC).

    Frequently asked questions

    Can an inheritance in Ukraine be accepted under a power of attorney?
    No. The application to accept an inheritance is filed by the heir personally, and the notary does not accept applications from a representative under a power of attorney (Art. 1269 of the Civil Code of Ukraine, para. 3.6, Ch. 10 of Procedure No. 296/5). The signature on the application can be authenticated before a Spanish notary and the document sent to Ukraine by post. Everything else in the case, except renouncing the inheritance and withdrawing such applications, is handled by a representative under a power of attorney.
    How long do I have to claim an inheritance in Ukraine?
    Six months from the date of death (Art. 1270 of the Civil Code of Ukraine). During martial law and for two years after it, if the death was registered more than a month later, the period is counted from the date the death was registered (para. 20 of the Final and Transitional Provisions of the CC). If the right to the inheritance arose because other heirs did not accept it or renounced it, the period is three months from that moment.
    Do I need to travel to Ukraine to accept an inheritance?
    No. The signature on the application is authenticated by a Spanish notary, then the document is apostilled and the original is sent by post to the notary at the deceased's last place of residence. Ukraine has no separate rule on an application with the signature authenticated by a foreign notary, so we agree the text and form in advance with the notary handling the case.
    I am registered at my late father's flat but live in Spain. Do I need to file an application?
    Possibly not. Anyone who lived permanently with the deceased is deemed to have accepted the inheritance if they have not renounced it within six months (Art. 1268 of the Civil Code of Ukraine), and the notary checks cohabitation primarily from the documents on registration of the place of residence. But since you actually live in Spain, it is still safer to file an application within six months: then the acceptance will not depend on a dispute about where you lived. If you do not want to inherit, you need a renunciation within six months.
    How do I renounce an inheritance in Ukraine if I live in Spain?
    In the same way as accepting it: with an application to renounce within six months, in person or by post with the signature authenticated by a notary, but not under a power of attorney (Art. 1273 of the Civil Code of Ukraine, paras. 3.3–3.6, Ch. 10 of Procedure No. 296/5). The signature is authenticated by a Spanish notary, followed by the apostille and sending it to the notary in Ukraine. For a child under 14, the parents may renounce only with the permission of the guardianship authority.
    What should I do if the six months have already passed?
    You can file an application with the written consent of all the heirs who have already accepted the inheritance; it is submitted to the notary before the certificate is issued. If there is no consent, the court may set an additional period if the reason for missing the deadline is valid (Art. 1272 of the Civil Code of Ukraine). First check the date the death was registered: in some cases the period is counted from that date.
