    Citizenship for a child born in Spain: when to apply and where to start

    Krepko · 1 November 2026 · ~9 min read
    In short. A child of foreigners is Spanish by birth only in special cases, for example if the law of neither parent gives the child their nationality (art. 17 of the Civil Code); this does not apply to children of Ukrainian citizens. The usual route is citizenship after one year of legal residence (art. 22.2.a), and the safest way is to count that year from the child's residence permit under art. 159 of the Immigration Regulation, which is issued for five years. Families on temporary protection may apply for this residence permit at any time without leaving protection (SEM Instruction 2/2026). Once the child becomes a citizen, the father or mother can apply for a residence permit as the parent of a Spanish minor (art. 94.1.f). Rules checked on 1 November 2026.

    A child born in Spain does not become Spanish automatically, and a certificate from the Registro Civil (civil registry) is not enough for citizenship. There is a short route: an application for citizenship can be filed after one year of legal residence. But it is safer to count that year not from birth but from the child's own residence permit, so every month without a residence permit pushes back the whole chain.

    Below are the steps for a family of foreigners, including those on temporary protection: which residence permit to obtain for the child, who applies for citizenship and when, and which residence permit the parents can then apply for. We checked the rules on 1 November 2026 against the BOE, the instruction of the immigration authority and the guidance of the Spanish Ministry of Justice.

    Why a child does not become Spanish at birth

    Spain passes on nationality through the parents, not through the place of birth. Art. 17.1 of the Civil Code treats as Spanish by birth the children of Spaniards and, only in a few cases, children of foreigners born in Spain:

    • if at least one parent was themselves born in Spain (art. 17.1.b);
    • if the parents are stateless or the law of neither of them gives the child their nationality (art. 17.1.c);
    • if the child's parentage has not been established (art. 17.1.d).

    Art. 17.1.c does not apply to children of Ukrainian citizens: Ukrainian law gives them Ukrainian citizenship wherever they are born (art. 7 of the Law “On Citizenship of Ukraine”). For families from Russia, the answer also depends on the law of their country.

    So the route that works is citizenship by residence. For those born in Spain, the law shortens the usual ten years to one year (art. 22.2.a), and the safe starting point for legal residence is the child's own residence permit. We handle this step together with an immigration lawyer; more on the page Residence for minor children.

    Step 1. A residence permit for the child under art. 159 — the count starts here

    A child born in Spain is granted a residence permit under art. 159 of the Immigration Regulation (RD 1155/2024): straight away for five years from the date of the decision and without a visa. Under art. 159.1, the father or mother files the application within six months of the child's birth or of one of the parents obtaining a residence permit, if that happened later.

    SEM Instruction 2/2026 (point tercera) extended art. 159 to families with temporary protection, and the rules for them are more lenient:

    • you can apply at any time: the six months only start to run once a parent obtains a residence permit other than temporary protection;
    • you can apply even if neither parent has yet switched to another residence permit.

    These rules apply now; whether they will change after the elections on 29 November 2026 is not known.

    In July 2026, the Supreme Court removed from art. 159.1 the condition that the child must not have left Spain since birth; the court did not abolish the six-month period. The office is still entitled to check that the child has in fact lived in Spain since birth, but temporary and justified trips do not by themselves take away the right to use this article (judgment of 8 July and order of 1 September 2026).

    Under art. 159.3, the application must be accompanied by:

    • a full copy of a valid passport, travel document or cédula de inscripción (registration document for those without a passport), for both the child and the parent filing the application;
    • a copy of the Spanish birth certificate;
    • for children of school age, a document showing that the child is enrolled in school (art. 159.2).

    So the application is filed by whichever parent holds such a valid document. If the office finds a gap in the documents, it will send a request: you have ten working days from notification to reply, otherwise the application is deemed withdrawn and the case is closed (art. 159.4; working days under art. 30.2 of Law 39/2015).

    The other parent's consent is not on the list in art. 159.3: in certain cases it is required by art. 160, for children born outside Spain. If the office still asks for consent, we have covered how to respond in the article on this kind of request, and the deed of the other parent's consent itself is authorised by a notary: we prepare the text and accompany you at the signing.

    By law, the decision is made within one month of receipt of the application; if there is no answer, the application is deemed rejected (art. 159.4). Within a month of being notified of the approval, the child and a parent apply in person for the TIE card at the police (art. 159.5). If the child has their own temporary protection card, it is renounced only after the residence permit has been approved, and the receipt for the renunciation is shown when applying for the TIE (SEM 2/2026, séptima). What is important to do before renouncing is covered in our article on renouncing temporary protection.

    Step 2. A year later — the citizenship application

    For someone born in Spain, one year of residence is enough for citizenship (art. 22.2.a). Residence must be legal, continuous and immediately before the application (art. 22.3). A residence permit under art. 159 is valid from the date of the decision, so it is safer to count the year from that date.

    How to count. If the decision on the child's residence permit is dated 15 December 2026, a year of legal residence is reached on 15 December 2027. Apply no earlier than that, ideally with a margin of a few days.
    Will time on temporary protection count? Years on protection count towards the five years for long-term residence (larga duración nacional); this is stated expressly in SEM Instruction 2/2026 (point sexta). You cannot apply for larga duración-UE (EU long-term residence) from a temporary protection card. For citizenship, the question has not been officially decided: the instruction is silent on it, and we have not found an official position of the Ministry of Justice. So the basis to rely on is the residence permit under art. 159 and a year lived on it.

    Who applies. For a child under 14, the parents apply as legal representatives; from the age of 14, the child applies personally together with them (art. 21.3 of the Civil Code). According to the Ministry of Justice guidance of 3 September 2021, prior authorisation from the Registro Civil is not needed if the parents agree: both sign the form. One parent's signature is enough if it is a single-parent family, if the other parent has been deprived of parental rights by a court decision, or if they are absent and have executed a notarial deed of authorisation. If the parents disagree, a court decides. In Spain, such a deed is authorised by a notary: we prepare the text and accompany you at the signing.

    What you need. Under the procedural regulation (RD 1004/2015, art. 5) and the Ministry of Justice guidance:

    • the child's passport: if the residence permit was issued on the basis of a cédula de inscripción, you will still need a passport for this application;
    • the Spanish birth certificate, which also proves birth in Spain;
    • a certificate from the school or childcare centre: compulsory for children of school age, optional for pre-school children;
    • the parents' identity documents: DNI, NIE or passport;
    • the receipt for payment of the fee of €104.05 (Modelo 790, code 026; amount on the Ministry of Justice website as of 1 November 2026).

    The regulation requires a criminal record certificate only from the age of 18 or after emancipation (art. 5.3), and minors do not sit the DELE and CCSE exams (Law 19/2015, Seventh Final Provision). The procedure is electronic (same provision).

    By law, the decision is made within a year of the application reaching the ministry; if there is no answer, it is deemed rejected (RD 1004/2015, art. 11.3). After notification of the approval, there are 180 days to make the required declarations at the Registro Civil and request entry of the citizenship (art. 21.4 of the Civil Code, art. 12.1 of the regulation). The oath is taken only from the age of 14; at that point the child also declares personally that they renounce their previous nationality (art. 23.a–b). How the procedure works for adults is described on our page on Spanish nationality.

    If the child is already 16–17. The one year for those born in Spain does not depend on age, but the exemption from the exams applies only until 18. Older children brought to Spain, on the other hand, are granted a residence permit under a different article, 160, and the application must be filed while the child is under 18: this route is covered on the page Residence for minor children.

    Step 3. Once the child is a citizen — residence permits for the parents

    The father, mother or guardian of a minor Spanish citizen is entitled to apply for a residence permit as a relative of a Spaniard (art. 94.1.f RD 1155/2024) if the child is in their care and lives with them, or if they fulfil their obligations towards the child. The residence permit is issued for five years, with the right to work as an employee and as self-employed (art. 95), and you can apply in Spain (art. 97.1.c).

    This route is expressly open to holders of temporary protection (SEM 2/2026, quinta.2). The order is as follows:

    1. wait until the child's citizenship has been entered in the Registro Civil: without that entry, citizenship is not considered acquired (art. 23.c of the Civil Code);
    2. check your passport: you need a valid one before applying, and a full copy of it is attached to the application (art. 96);
    3. apply for the residence permit while remaining on protection;
    4. after approval, file the renunciation of temporary protection and show the receipt when the card is issued (SEM 2/2026, séptima).

    Renouncing protection is irreversible, so it is not filed before the approval. More about this permit on the page Residence for relatives of Spanish citizens.

    The whole chain consists of the child's year of residence, the time for the decision on citizenship (by law up to a year) and the entry in the Registro Civil. It does not offer a quick way out of temporary protection: if you need a status sooner, compare it with changing your status from temporary protection (modificación).

    The child's Ukrainian documents

    A child needs a passport for citizenship. A first passport can be issued at the Ukrainian consulate on the basis of the Spanish birth certificate with an apostille and a translation into Ukrainian. We have covered how to do this step by step in the article on documents for a child born in Spain, and we arrange the apostille on the Spanish certificate.

    Where families most often lose time

    • They wait for “a year after birth”. Without the child's residence permit, the year may not count, and the citizenship application is pushed back.
    • They renounce protection too early. The renunciation is filed only after the new residence permit has been approved; this applies to both the child and the parents.
    • They miss the ten working days to reply. Without a reply to the office's request, the residence permit case is closed (art. 159.4), and you have to apply again.

    Frequently asked questions

    My child was born in Spain. Are they automatically Spanish?
    No. Spain passes on nationality through the parents. A child of foreigners is Spanish by birth only in special cases, for example if the law of neither parent gives the child their nationality (art. 17.1.c of the Civil Code). Under Ukrainian law, a child of Ukrainian citizens is a citizen of Ukraine (art. 7 of the Law “On Citizenship of Ukraine”), so their route is citizenship after one year of residence.
    Can we apply for citizenship one year after birth?
    The year is counted not from birth but from legal residence, and it must run without interruption immediately before the application (art. 22.2.a and 22.3 of the Civil Code). The safe starting point is the date of the decision on the child's residence permit under art. 159. Whether time on temporary protection counts has not been officially decided.
    We are on temporary protection. Does this prevent us from getting citizenship for our child?
    It does not prevent you from starting: you can apply for the child's residence permit under art. 159 at any time without leaving protection, as SEM Instruction 2/2026 states. The issue is a different one: whether time on protection counts towards citizenship has not been officially decided, so it is better to count the year from the child's residence permit. How we arrange a child's residence permit.
    Does a child have to sit the citizenship exams?
    No. Everyone under 18 is exempt from the DELE and CCSE exams (Law 19/2015, Seventh Final Provision). For children of school age, a certificate from the school is attached to the application (RD 1004/2015, art. 5).
    The child's father stayed in Ukraine. Can we apply for citizenship without his signature?
    Yes, if the father executes a notarial deed authorising the application: this is stated in the Spanish Ministry of Justice guidance on applications on behalf of children under 14. The father can execute such a deed before a Ukrainian notary, which the Krepko team in Ukraine arranges. If he comes to Spain, the deed is authorised by a Spanish notary: we prepare the text and accompany you at the signing. If the parents disagree with each other, a court decides.
    When do the parents become entitled to a residence permit through the child?
    Once the child's citizenship has been entered in the Registro Civil. The father or mother can then apply for a residence permit as a relative of a Spanish citizen (art. 94.1.f RD 1155/2024): for five years, with the right to work, applying from within Spain. This is allowed for holders of temporary protection (SEM 2/2026, quinta.2), and protection is renounced only after approval; this step is irreversible. More on the page Residence for relatives of Spanish citizens.
