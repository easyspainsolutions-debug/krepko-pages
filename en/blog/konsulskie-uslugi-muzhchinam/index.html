    The consulate refused a man aged 18–60: what to do about your documents

    Krepko · updated 12 November 2026 · ~9 min read
    In short. The rule applies to consular acts for men who are Ukrainian citizens aged 18–60. A power of attorney, a statement, a consent and other notarial acts can be done before a Spanish notary: with an apostille and a notarised translation, such a document is valid in Ukraine. You do not need a military registration document for this, because a Spanish notary does not ask for it.

    What exactly has changed

    Resolution No. 981 of 29 July 2026 approved two procedures: one for keeping the Unified State Register of Conscripts, Persons Liable for Military Service and Reservists, and one for checking the validity of military registration documents by a consular officer of a Ukrainian diplomatic mission abroad.

    The wording of the rule: Ukrainian citizens of the male sex aged from 18 to 60, when applying for consular services, submit together with the required documents a military registration document in electronic form, including a printed one. The consul checks it through the e-Consul system, which queries the Register directly.

    The resolution came into force on the day of its publication. The exceptions are points 12 and 13 of the Checking Procedure: they take effect six months after the date of entry into force, that is, around February 2027.

    What counts as a military registration document. The electronic document from the Reserve+ app, including a printed one: the consul reads its two-dimensional barcode. A paper military ID or temporary certificate is accepted only from those registered for military service with the Security Service of Ukraine (SBU) or an intelligence agency. The check runs against the Register, so an expired or invalid entry does not solve the problem.

    Who the rule does not apply to

    Point 2 of the Checking Procedure lists the cases where the rule does not apply. There are few of them, and the wording is narrow:

    • issuing an identity certificate for return to Ukraine;
    • consular acts concerning children of Ukrainian citizens whose other parent is a foreign national or a stateless person;
    • acts on applications from citizens who are under arrest, detained or deprived of liberty abroad.

    The rule does not apply to women: the text expressly states the male sex. Nor does it apply to men over 60 or under 18.

    Check your situation before booking an appointment. Summaries of the rule on social media differ: some extend it to all Ukrainians, others reduce it to passports only. Neither follows from the text. If you are not sure whether your request falls under the restriction, write to us and we will look at the wording for your task.

    Which documents can be done before a Spanish notary

    The Ministry of Justice of Ukraine explains that a power of attorney abroad can be arranged in two ways: before a consul or before a notary of the country where you are staying. The second route is a full one, not a fallback. The document is drawn up under the law of the country where it is signed, and is then legalised for Ukraine.

    Through a Spanish notary you can arrange:

    • general powers of attorney and special ones: to sell property, for representation in court, for transactions with a bank account, to collect documents;
    • consents: for a child's travel, for a transaction, for actions of the other spouse;
    • statements: on marital status, on a renunciation, confirming facts;
    • renouncing an inheritance and statements in inheritance cases;
    • signature authentication on a document drawn up in your language.

    A Spanish notary draws up such documents as a notarial record under Article 207 of the Reglamento Notarial (the Notarial Regulations). The record includes the text in your language and its Spanish version, so the document can be read by both sides: by you when signing, and by the Ukrainian notary later.

    What a Spanish notary will not do. A passport, a criminal record certificate from the Ukrainian register, registration of civil status records: these are state functions and remain with the Ukrainian authorities. Some of these documents are handled differently: a criminal record certificate or a duplicate birth or marriage certificate we obtain in Ukraine and send to Spain ready, with an apostille.

    What is needed for the document to be accepted in Ukraine

    There are three requirements, and all of them can be met in Spain:

    1. Signing before a notary
    The text is prepared in advance and agreed with the notary. Your part takes about 30 minutes: you come, listen to the translation of every clause and sign.
    2. Apostille
    Spain and Ukraine are parties to the Hague Convention, so an apostille is affixed instead of consular legalisation. On notarial documents it is affixed by the College of Notaries.
    3. Notarised translation into Ukrainian
    Both the document itself and the apostille are translated. The translation is certified by a notary. We arrange this, and the document arrives in Ukraine ready to be submitted.

    Note the order: the apostille is affixed before the translation, otherwise the translation will have to be redone, because the Ukrainian notary will require a translation of the stamp as well.

    Common mistakes

    Waiting until “everything is clarified”. Points 12 and 13 of the Procedure take effect in six months, and this is not a cancellation but an extension of the procedure. Tasks that have a deadline, such as a transaction, a court case or an inheritance case, gain nothing from waiting.

    Arranging a power of attorney “for everything at once”. A Ukrainian notary or registrar looks at the scope of powers in specific terms. Wording that is too general raises questions, and wording that is too narrow does not cover the action needed. The text is written for a specific task and a specific authority.

    Translating before the apostille. The most common and most expensive mistake: a translation without the apostille stamp will have to be redone.

    Assuming any translation will do. For Ukraine you need a notarised translation into Ukrainian. For the reverse route, when a Ukrainian document is submitted in Spain, the requirements are different: there you need a sworn translation or the combination “notarised translation plus apostille”.

    How long it takes

    Preparing the text and agreeing it with the notary usually takes one or two working days, depending on how complex the document is. The signing takes about half an hour. In Spain the apostille on a notarial deed is arranged quickly: if the document is signed in the morning, you can collect it with an urgent apostille on the same or the next working day. Translation and delivery to Ukraine come on top of that.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does the rule apply only to passport matters or to all consular services?
    The rule is worded broadly, “when applying for consular services”, without listing the types. The exceptions are named separately: the identity certificate for return to Ukraine, acts concerning children whose other parent is a foreign national or a stateless person, and applications from citizens who are under arrest, detained or deprived of liberty.
    Does it apply to women and to men over 60?
    No. The text names Ukrainian citizens of the male sex aged from 18 to 60. Outside this group the military registration document requirement does not apply.
    Is a power of attorney from a Spanish notary sure to be accepted in Ukraine?
    The Ministry of Justice of Ukraine expressly names arranging it before a notary of the country where you are staying as one of the two ways. The conditions: an apostille on the document and a notarised translation of the document and the apostille into Ukrainian. It is stated separately that a consul does not certify a power of attorney for an action which, by its content, can only be performed in person.
    Will a Spanish notary ask for a military registration document?
    No. A Spanish notary establishes your identity from a passport or another valid document and works under Spanish law. The military registration document requirement relates to consular acts of Ukraine.
    What should I do if I need a passport specifically, not a power of attorney?
    A passport is issued only by a Ukrainian authority, and a notarial document cannot replace that. What remains here is putting your military registration data in order. That is outside our work; we handle the document side: powers of attorney, consents, statements, certificates and duplicates from Ukraine.
    How long is such a power of attorney valid?
    The term is stated in the text itself. If it is not stated, under Ukrainian law the power of attorney is valid until it is revoked, but some authorities and registrars ask for the term to be named expressly. We set it based on the task the document is made for.
    Can I arrange the document if I don't live in Alicante?
    Yes. We work in six cities in Spain: Alicante, Valencia, Murcia, Madrid, Barcelona and Málaga. A Krepko representative attends the signing in each of them.
