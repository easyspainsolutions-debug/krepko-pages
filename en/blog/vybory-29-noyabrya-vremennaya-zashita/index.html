    The 29 November elections and temporary protection: which rules only the EU changes and which Spain does

    Krepko · 18 November 2026 · ~8 min read
    In short. The 29 November 2026 elections do not in themselves change the rules of temporary protection. The term of protection until 4 March 2028 was set by the Council of the EU, and only the Council can change it. TIE cards have been extended until 4 March 2027 by an order of the Spanish Ministry of the Interior. Applying for a residence permit from within Spain is allowed by the regulation as amended by RD 316/2026, which the government changes; the change of status (modificación) and full counting of years under protection are named explicitly by instruction SEM 2/2026, which the issuing body itself changes.     Elections to the Spanish parliament have been called for 29 November 2026. In themselves they do not change a single rule of temporary protection: each rule stays in force until it is changed by whoever has the power to do so. The term of protection set by the EU decision can be changed only by the Council of the EU, a law by the Spanish parliament, the regulation by the government, the order on cards by the minister, and an instruction by the body that issued it.

    Below we sort the term of temporary protection, the validity of TIE cards (foreigner identity cards), the right to switch to an ordinary residence permit and the counting of years under protection into these levels: what may change and by which act. We make no forecasts: the text contains only rules and dates that we checked on 18 November 2026 against Spain's Official State Gazette (BOE), the EU law database EUR-Lex and the website of the Ministry of Inclusion.

    What the elections change in themselves and what they do not

    The elections were called for 29 November 2026 by Royal Decree (Real Decreto, RD) 806/2026, and the first session of the new parliament (Cortes Generales) for 23 December. After the elections and until the new government takes office, the outgoing one acts in a caretaker capacity (en funciones): it handles ordinary business, takes other measures only in urgent cases or for duly justified reasons of general interest, and cannot submit bills to parliament (Art. 101 of the Constitution, Art. 21 of Ley 50/1997).

    The switching routes open now are described on the page on switching from temporary protection to a residence permit.

    Term of protection: the Council of the EU decision

    Temporary protection was extended until 4 March 2028 by the Council of the EU in Decision 2026/1912 of 30 July 2026. Such decisions apply directly, without individual extension decisions; the Spanish Ministry of the Interior also notes this. Only the Council of the EU can extend protection or end it early, by qualified majority, on a proposal from the European Commission (Art. 4.2 and 6.1 of Directive 2001/55/EC).

    The date is not final: in the same decision the Council stated that in the event of a lasting ceasefire it is ready to act under the rule on early termination of protection (recital 23).

    The condition on military obligations (Art. 2 of the decision) does not apply to those who were under protection in Spain (or another EU country where they obtained it) up to and including 4 August 2026 and have not interrupted it there: more on this in the article on temporary protection. Ukrainian citizens who were in Spain before 24 February 2022 and cannot return were included in protection by the Spanish Council of Ministers agreement of 8 March 2022 (Orden PCM/170/2022): for them the ground for protection is a Spanish act. In the preamble to Orden INT/96/2026 the Spanish Ministry of the Interior stated that the Council of the EU extensions of 2023 and 2024 also apply to them.

    TIE cards: a ministerial order

    The Directive obliges countries to provide people under protection with residence documents for its entire term (Art. 8.1). Spain does not reissue cards already issued, but extends their validity by an order of the Minister of the Interior: Orden INT/96/2026 of 12 February 2026 extended them until 4 March 2027.

    As of 18 November 2026 there is no order in the BOE for the period after that date. The orders of 2024, 2025 and 2026 were published on 27 February, 1 March and 17 February, 3–15 days before the day the cards expired. The term of protection itself does not depend on the order.

    Switching to a residence permit from within Spain: royal decree and law

    In February 2026 the Ministry of the Interior wrote that the switch from protection to other permits had not yet been regulated (preamble to Orden INT/96/2026). It was regulated by RD 316/2026, which amended the Immigration Regulation (RD 1155/2024). Under the new wording of additional provision 19 (DA 19ª), in force since 16 April 2026, holders of a temporary protection permit may apply for permits whose procedure can be started in Spain, including arraigo (Art. 125).

    The regulation is changed by the government: by a royal decree of the Council of Ministers, without a vote in parliament (Art. 97 of the Constitution, Art. 24 of Ley 50/1997), but a decree has its own procedure: RD 316/2026 was adopted after a public consultation and an opinion of the Council of State. Individual provisions can be annulled by a court: in 2026 the Supreme Court annulled several provisions of the regulation, but not Art. 191 and not DA 19ª.

    Above the regulation stands the Immigration Law (LO 4/2000). It sets out the basics: the administration may grant an arraigo residence permit (Art. 31.3), and the right to long-term residence is given by five years of continuous temporary residence (Art. 32.2). The law is changed by parliament. An urgent decree-law of the government takes effect immediately, but within 30 days the Congress must approve or revoke it (Art. 86 of the Constitution).

    Change of status and counting of years: the body's instruction

    Instruction SEM 2/2026 was issued on 22 June 2026 by the State Secretariat for Migration (Secretaría de Estado de Migraciones). Its purpose is a uniform interpretation and application of DA 19ª. The following are named explicitly in it, and not in the regulation:

    • change of status under Art. 191: it is not named in DA 19ª, and Art. 191.7 still closes it to temporary protection permits; the instruction regards DA 19ª as a special rule that overrides this prohibition (point one);
    • arraigo while holding a valid permit: DA 19ª names arraigo explicitly, but the instruction (point two) does not apply to protection holders the regulation's general condition of not holding a stay or residence permit (Art. 126.h, added by the same RD 316/2026), again citing DA 19ª as a special rule;
    • counting of time: the regulation requires two years of continuous stay for arraigo (Art. 126.b) and five years of legal residence for national long-term residence (larga duración nacional, Art. 183.1); the instruction states explicitly (points two and six) that years under protection count towards both periods, and towards the five years in full;
    • exit procedure: protection is renounced after the new permit has been approved (point seven).

    An instruction is neither a law nor a regulation. Under Art. 6 of Ley 40/2015, bodies use instructions to direct the work of their subordinate units, and a breach of an instruction does not in itself make a decision invalid. It can be amended or revoked by the body that issued it, without parliament or the Council of Ministers. The text sets no period of validity: the date of 4 March 2027 appears in the preamble as the term of protection on the day of signature. As of 18 November 2026 the instruction is published on the ministry's website, and there is no information there about its revocation.

    The routes themselves are covered in the articles on changing temporary protection status and on long-term residence after protection.

    What this means if you are thinking about switching

    • Protection is not renounced in advance. An express renunciation ends it automatically (Art. 24.1 of RD 1325/2003), so it is filed after the new permit has been approved. The procedure is in the article on renouncing temporary protection.
    • The lower the level of the act, the shorter the path to changing it. A law goes through parliament, the regulation through the royal decree procedure, and the body changes an instruction itself. Whether and when these rules will change is unknown as of 18 November 2026.

    Part of the preparation does not depend on the instruction: these requirements are set out in the laws, the regulation and the ministry's information sheet on arraigo.

    • a valid passport: a copy is required both for arraigo (Art. 130.1) and for long-term residence (Art. 184.3);
    • municipal registration (empadronamiento) without gaps: the first example of a document proving your stay in Spain in the ministry's information sheet; non-EU foreign nationals without long-term residence renew their entry in the register of residents (padrón) every two years, otherwise the town hall may cancel it (Art. 16.1 of Ley 7/1985);
    • a criminal record certificate from every country where you lived in the last five years before entering Spain (from Ukraine and, if applicable, from other countries): for arraigo it is required by Art. 130.2 if you have not lived in Spain for five consecutive years; an apostille or legalisation and a translation by a sworn translator for foreign documents are required by the ministry's information sheet. How long the certificate is valid is covered in the article on the validity of certificates.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can temporary protection in Spain be cancelled after the elections?
    For those who obtained protection under the EU decision, Spain cannot do this on its own. The term of protection until 4 March 2028 is set by Council of the EU Decision 2026/1912, and only the Council of the EU can extend it or end it early, by qualified majority on a proposal from the European Commission (Art. 4.2 and 6.1 of Directive 2001/55/EC). The Spanish authorities control their own acts: the order on cards, the Immigration Regulation and instructions.
    My card is valid until 4 March 2027. What happens after that date?
    Protection itself has been extended until 4 March 2028 and does not depend on the card. Spain extends the validity of the cards by an order of the Ministry of the Interior: the current one, Orden INT/96/2026, extended them until 4 March 2027, and as of 18 November 2026 there is no order for the next period in the BOE. The orders of 2024, 2025 and 2026 were published on 27 February, 1 March and 17 February.
    Can the counting of years under protection towards permanent residence be cancelled?
    The five years for long-term residence are set out in the law (Art. 32.2 of LO 4/2000) and the regulation (Art. 183.1). That years under temporary protection count in full towards this period for national long-term residence (larga duración nacional) is stated in point six of instruction SEM 2/2026, and the instruction can be changed by the issuing body itself. As of 18 November 2026 it is published on the ministry's website, and there is no information about its revocation. How to count your five years is covered in the article on long-term residence after protection.
    If I apply and the rules change later, will my application be decided under the old ones?
    There is no answer in advance: this is determined by the transitional provisions of the new act itself. There is a known example: when the current regulation was introduced, applications filed earlier were decided under the rules in force on the day of filing, unless the applicant asked for the new ones to be applied (transitional provision 2 of RD 1155/2024). Instruction SEM 2/2026 contains no transitional rules.
    Should I renounce protection now, while the current rules are in force?
    No. Under point seven of instruction SEM 2/2026, protection is renounced after the new permit has been approved, and an express renunciation ends it automatically (Art. 24.1 of RD 1325/2003). A renunciation filed in advance would leave you without status until a decision on your application. In addition, the right to apply under DA 19ª belongs to holders of a temporary protection permit, and a change of status under Art. 191 is requested by those who are in Spain with a temporary residence permit (Art. 191.1). The procedure is in the article on renouncing temporary protection.
    When will the new government be able to change anything?
    There is no exact date. The first session of the new parliament has been called for 23 December 2026 (Art. 5 of RD 806/2026). Until the new government takes office, the outgoing one acts in a caretaker capacity: it handles ordinary business and takes other measures only in urgent cases or for duly justified reasons of general interest (Art. 21 of Ley 50/1997). The Constitution does not set a deadline by which a government must be formed; if within two months of the first vote on a candidate no one wins the confidence of the Congress, new elections are called (Art. 99). A law is changed by parliament, the regulation by the Council of Ministers by royal decree, and an instruction by the body itself.
