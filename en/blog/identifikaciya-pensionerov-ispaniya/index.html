    Identification for a Ukrainian pension: how to complete it from Spain by 31 December

    Krepko · updated 18 November 2026 · ~7 min read
    In short. Identification is completed every year by everyone who receives a Ukrainian pension and spends at least 183 days a year abroad (Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine Resolution No. 299). It is valid for a calendar year: identification completed in 2025 covers 2026, and for your pension to keep arriving in 2027, complete it again by 31 December 2026.

    If you receive a Ukrainian pension and live in Spain, once a year the Pension Fund of Ukraine (PFU) has to make sure that you are who you say you are. The deadline is 31 December; otherwise, payments are stopped from 1 January. The good news: you do not need to travel anywhere, and the quickest route takes fifteen minutes on your phone.

    Below are all three methods that work, step by step, with honest timeframes for each, the main points where things go wrong and what to do if you have already missed the deadline. The procedure is run by the state and is free of charge.

    Method 1: Diia.Signature — fifteen minutes

    The quickest route: logging in to the Pension Fund's personal account with Diia.Signature (Дія.Підпис) counts as identification in itself, with no applications and no waiting. The most convenient way is through the mobile app “Pension Fund of Ukraine” (Пенсійний фонд України): you log in, and the mark is there.

    What you will need:

    • a biometric document, i.e. a passport with a chip or an ID card: since February 2026, Diia.Signature can only be activated by reading the chip;
    • a smartphone with NFC, the same feature you use when you tap your phone to pay at a till;
    • the Diia app with Diia.Signature activated.

    Activating the signature: in Diia, open the menu → Diia.Signature → scan the document code → hold the document against the back of your phone → pass the photo check → create a signature code.

    The main point where things go wrong is the photo check. Hold the phone at eye level, use even light without shadows and a plain background, preferably take off your glasses, and try to look as you do in the photo in your document. If the check fails again and again, do not keep going round in circles: there is a second method.

    An old-style passport, the “booklet” without a chip? Diia.Signature will not work at all. Either use the video call (method 2) or, if you want to settle the matter for years ahead, an urgent biometric passport can be made in Spain, without travelling to Ukraine.

    Would you rather not work it all out yourself?

    We will go through the whole process with you: set up Diia.Signature on your phone, book the video call and prepare you for it, and check the mark. We work with older people patiently and in plain language — identification for the PFU from Spain.

    Method 2: a video call with the Pension Fund

    For those who do not have a biometric document or a smartphone with NFC. No electronic signature is needed at all, only your passport and a device with a camera, even a grandchild's tablet.

    How to book:

    • on the PFU portal (portal.pfu.gov.ua), the tab “Ідентифікація у режимі відеоконференцзв'язку” (identification by video conference); no login is required. Since October 2025 the form has a box “Тимчасово проживаю за межами України” (I am temporarily living outside Ukraine): tick it;
    • or by calling the contact centre: 0 800 503 753, from Spain +380 44 281 08 70 or +380 44 281 08 71.

    You choose the platform yourself: Zoom, WhatsApp, Viber, Telegram, Google Meet or Signal. The date and time will arrive by email. The session itself takes about twenty minutes: a fund employee will compare you with the document you show to the camera.

    Keep an eye on the timeframes. The session is scheduled no earlier than 10 working days and no later than 30 calendar days after the request. If you apply in December, you risk getting a date in January. The rule is simple: send your request no later than the end of November.

    Method 3: the consulate — the fallback route

    The Ukrainian consulate certifies that the person is alive, and this document, together with an application to continue payments, is sent by registered post to the PFU office where you are registered (or filed through the portal, but that requires an electronic signature).

    The route works, but it is the hardest: getting an available appointment slot at the consulate is a task in itself, and from Alicante and Valencia the consular district is Barcelona, hundreds of kilometres away. We keep this option for cases where the first two really do not fit.

    Has your pension been stopped? It is not lost

    If identification has not been completed by 31 December, payment stops from 1 January. But the reinstatement rule is lenient: complete the identification within a year of the date payment was stopped, and payment will be resumed from that very date, with back pay for all the missed months. According to the Pension Fund's guidance, the main condition is to complete the identification. Some fund offices also ask for an application for reinstatement: if payment has not resumed, file it through the PFU portal or by post.

    How to make sure everything has been counted

    In the personal account on the PFU portal there is a service called “Моя ідентифікація” (My identification), which shows the date of completion and the status. After using any of the methods, check there: it is better to know than to hope.

    Beware of “helpers” who call you. The Pension Fund does not ask for your card number, CVV or codes from text messages, and does not charge money for identification. A call “from the fund” asking you to read out your card details is from fraudsters: hang up.

    Frequently asked questions

    I completed identification in December 2025. Do I need to do it again in 2026?
    Yes. Identification is valid for a calendar year: the one from December 2025 covered 2026, and for payments in 2027 you need to complete it again by 31 December 2026.
    I have a booklet passport without a chip. Can I do it online?
    Not through Diia.Signature: it can only be activated with a biometric document. Book a video call with the PFU, where no electronic signature is needed at all. For the future, a biometric passport can be issued in Spain, at the passport service.
    Will I make it if I request a video call in December?
    It is risky: the session is scheduled within 30 calendar days of the request, and a December request may get a January date. There is no official guidance on whether such a case counts, so it is safer to apply before the end of November.
    Can I complete the identification on behalf of my mother?
    No: the fund has to see the pensioner themselves, by video, through their own Diia.Signature or in person. But you can be at their side and run the process for them: set up the app, submit the request, hold the phone.
    Is there a charge for identification?
    No, the procedure is run by the state and is free of charge through Diia.Signature and by video; at the consulate, a consular fee may be charged for certifying that the person is alive. Support services may also be chargeable if you want someone to go through the process with you.
