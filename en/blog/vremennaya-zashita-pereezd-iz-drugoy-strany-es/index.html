    From Poland or Germany to Spain for temporary protection: what changed in 2026

    Krepko · updated 12 November 2026 · ~9 min read
    In short. Protection is tied to a country: moving from Poland or Germany to Spain means a new application, and since 5 August 2026 new applicants have had to prove a lawful exit from Ukraine (EU Decision 2026/1912). According to the European Commission's guidance, the exit stamp should, as a rule, be no older than 90 days on the date of the application, but this rule is not in the text of the decision, and whether Spain applies it has not been confirmed. If you left in 2022, a DPSU certificate will show an old date and may not settle the matter. For those on the military register, the main document becomes the one from Reserve+. As of 1 October 2026, Spain had not clarified how everyone else should prove their exit: there is no list of accepted documents.

    If you were granted temporary protection in Poland or Germany and are now moving to Spain, what awaits you here is not a transfer of your status but a new application. Since 5 August 2026 new applicants have had to prove that they left Ukraine lawfully and in compliance with their military obligations. The simplest proof, according to the European Commission's guidance, is a Ukrainian exit stamp, in Ukrainian «штамп про перетин кордону» (border crossing stamp). Most people do not have one: for a long time Ukrainian citizens have been stamped only at their own request, and this is not a border guard's mistake.

    Below we look at what has changed for those who are changing country, why the 90-day guideline hits those who left in 2022, when a certificate from the State Border Guard Service of Ukraine (DPSU), that is, a «довідка про перетин кордону» (border crossing certificate), is useful, and when the Reserve+ app matters more. We checked the rules on 1 October 2026 against the text of the EU decision, the European Commission's guidance and the laws of Ukraine.

    Do you need to apply for protection again if you already have it in Poland or Germany?

    Yes. Moving to Spain means a new application and a check under the new rules. The European Commission says so plainly in its guidance on Decision 2026/1912 (section III): anyone who seeks protection in a country other than the one through which they entered the EU and where they requested it must prove that they left Ukraine lawfully and in compliance with their military obligations.

    The exemption for those already under protection does not apply when you move. Article 2 of the decision, as amended by the corrigendum of 5 August, does not require proof from those who enjoyed protection in the given country on 4 August 2026 and keep it there without interruption. The exemption is tied to the country: a status in Poland or Germany does not bring you under it in Spain.

    What changed for new applicants on 5 August 2026?

    Since 5 August 2026 Council of the EU Decision 2026/1912 grants protection to new applicants only on condition that they have fulfilled their military obligations in Ukraine. Recital (18) names the evidence: a lawful border crossing on exit or an exit stamp in the passport. If the exit cannot be proved that way, an easily verifiable official document is needed, for example one from Reserve+. The burden of proof is on the applicant. Under recital (20), military obligations may exist regardless of age and sex for those who are not subject to conscription but have enlisted in military service or are connected with military activity.

    Who the condition does not apply to: under Art. 2 of the decision (as corrected), those who were under protection in that country on 4 August 2026 inclusive and keep it without interruption. The European Commission (section IV of the guidance) adds those who were already in the country and applied before the rules entered into force but had not yet received a status. If you have been under protection in Spain without interruption since 4 August 2026 or earlier, you most likely do not need a border crossing certificate.

    Why is there no border crossing stamp («штамп про перетин кордону») in the passport?

    Ukrainian citizens do not get a border crossing mark in their passport unless they ask for it themselves. This is not news from 2026: it is set out in the Law “On Border Control” No. 1710-VI (Art. 12), and since 1 January 2020 the procedure for marks has been governed by Order No. 714 of the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine (MVS). Foreign nationals always get a mark: in the passport or, at their request, on an immigration card. In August 2026 the DPSU confirmed that a stamp cannot be added retroactively and that only the certificate remains, and the European Commission acknowledges that Ukrainians' passports are systematically not stamped.

    Since 10 April 2026 a second gap has been added: the European Entry/Exit System (EES) has become fully operational, and Schengen stamps are no longer placed in passports on entry into the EU. Those who entered the EU after that date, as a rule, have no marks in their passport from either the Ukrainian or the European side.

    I left in 2022: how does the 90-day rule work?

    The European Commission divides the check into two levels. In countries bordering Ukraine, such as Poland, the lawful crossing at the border checkpoint is enough in itself. In the others, including Spain and Germany, a Ukrainian exit stamp is enough. But the date of the stamp should, as a rule, be no earlier than 90 days before the application for protection; exceptional circumstances are taken into account. This rule is not in the text of the decision, and whether Spain applies it has not been officially confirmed.

    For those moving, this is the main question. For anyone who left Ukraine in 2022–2025, the exit date is older than 90 days by months or years, both in the stamp and in the DPSU certificate. Section III of the guidance refers to point B of section I, where this guideline is set out, and does not explain how to apply it to those who lived in another EU country. So, to be honest: if Spain applies the rule literally, a certificate of an old exit may not help, and the second-level document will be decisive.

    When is Reserve+ decisive rather than a DPSU certificate?

    If the first level is not enough or the authority has doubts, the European Commission names a certificate from Reserve+ as the basic document: it contains the full name, date of birth, the fact of exemption and its period of validity. For this document the Commission recommends a sworn translation into English or the language of the country, ordered from the Ukrainian authorities or through the consulate. The DPSU certificate is not named explicitly: each country accepts or rejects such “other documents” at its own discretion. The guidance says nothing about an apostille.

    A DPSU certificate confirms the fact of exit, not its grounds. It shows the date, time, border checkpoint and direction but, according to the available descriptions, does not state on what grounds the person left, for example a deferment or an exemption. So it does not replace Reserve+ or documents on a deferment or exemption.

    How is this checked in Poland, Germany and the Czech Republic?

    The experience of your previous country does not carry over to Spain: according to the Commission's guidance, each country decides for itself which additional documents to accept. For comparison:

    • Poland. The country borders Ukraine, so according to the Commission's guidance a lawful crossing at the border checkpoint is enough there. According to the Polish business press, holders of PESEL UKR with continuous status do not need new documents.
    • Germany. On 21 August 2026 the Federal Ministry of the Interior (BMI) wrote that new applicants prove compliance with their military obligations, for example with a Ukrainian exit stamp or documents on exemption or deferment. The list is open, and the DPSU certificate is not named in it explicitly.
    • Czech Republic. On 4 August 2026 the Ministry of the Interior named confirmation through Reserve+. On 20 August 2026 the refugee aid organisation OPU wrote that the rules mainly concern men aged 18–60 and that they need a paper eVOD, the electronic military registration document from Reserve+. OPU does not mention the DPSU certificate. The certificate is unlikely to replace the eVOD: according to the available descriptions, it does not state the grounds for exit.

    Which documents are accepted in Spain as of 1 October 2026?

    There is no official list. The Spanish portal Ucrania Urgente only says that since August 2026 the fulfilment of military obligations must be proved. On 12 August 2026 the Embassy of Ukraine in Spain officially requested a list of documents from the Spanish side, and as of 1 October no answer had been published.

    Practice, according to media reports, is contradictory. On 11 August Focus.ua reported that men had been refused in Alicante, where an extract from Reserve+ with a translation and a DPSU exit mark were required. On 14 August TSN reported refusals in Madrid and approvals in Alicante, Barcelona and Valencia. These are secondary sources, and they cannot be relied on as a rule. No one can currently guarantee that a DPSU certificate will be accepted, and we do not promise it.

    Who among those moving will benefit from a border crossing certificate («довідка про перетин кордону»)?

    The certificate is issued by the DPSU Main Centre for Processing Special Information (GTsOSI) in Kyiv; border checkpoints do not issue such certificates. According to descriptions by the DPSU in the media and by practising lawyers, it contains the full name, date of birth, citizenship, passport details and, for each crossing, the date, time, border checkpoint and direction. We have not found a channel through Diia, an administrative service centre (TsNAP) or a consulate.

    The certificate makes sense if:

    • you left Ukraine recently, there is no stamp, and your application in Spain falls within the 90-day guideline;
    • the authority explicitly asks for a DPSU exit mark;
    • you need to confirm the date and checkpoint of a lawful exit in addition to Reserve+.

    On its own, the certificate will most likely not help if you left in 2022–2025 and Spain applies the 90-day guideline, or if you are expected to confirm your military obligations. Foreign nationals usually do not need it: they always get a border crossing mark. For a change of residence permit status (modificación), arraigo (residence on the grounds of ties to Spain) and long-term residence, the rules do not require such a certificate, and we do not obtain it for these purposes.

    How to get a DPSU certificate: yourself for free or through Krepko?

    You can request information about yourself on your own and free of charge. The DPSU page describes a written application with a scan of the first page of your passport: in person, by post to 26 Volodymyrska St, Kyiv, 01601, or by e-mail to gcosi-dpsu@dpsu.gov.ua as a scan with a handwritten signature or a file with a qualified electronic signature (KEP). The e-mail address is given on the page dated 18 December 2025, while a later version of the page mentions only filing in person and by post. Under the law on the protection of personal data, the information is issued within 30 calendar days (Art. 16). It is free under Art. 19 of the same law: a person's access to data about themselves is not charged for.

    The second route is a lawyer's request. The law on the bar (Art. 24) allows up to 5 working days for a reply, and if there is a lot of information, the deadline can be extended to 20 working days by notifying the lawyer in writing. But the same article does not oblige the authority to provide restricted information in response to a request, and information on border crossings is confidential personal data. The DPSU provides it to a lawyer only with your written consent, so the statutory deadlines are a guide, not a guarantee: in practice at the moment some people get a reply in 5 working days, others in 15 or 20. We quote the timeframe with a margin.

    A lawyer does not open access to any other information: it is the same as with a request made on your own. According to the experience of lawyers who handle such requests, the DPSU replies with a certificate in the client's name. We check before ordering whether it will arrive as a PDF file with a KEP or on paper. How such a document will be assessed by a Spanish authority has not been officially confirmed.

    What to do before applying in Spain?

    1. Check your passport: if there is a Ukrainian exit stamp, you most likely do not need the certificate.
    2. Count how many days will pass from your exit from Ukraine to your planned application in Spain.
    3. If you have military obligations, prepare the document from Reserve+ and allow time for translation.
    4. If you left recently and there is no stamp, order the DPSU certificate straight away: the reply, delivery and translation take weeks.
    5. Find out in what form your authority asks for the documents: paper, translation. A general overview of the status is in the article on temporary protection in Spain.

    Frequently asked questions

    I am under temporary protection in Poland. Can I simply transfer it to Spain?
    No. In Spain you file a new application, and since 5 August 2026 new applicants have had to prove a lawful exit from Ukraine and compliance with their military obligations (EU Decision 2026/1912, European Commission guidance, section III). The exemption for those who were under protection on 4 August 2026 applies only in the country where they had protection.
    I left Ukraine in 2022 without a stamp. Will a DPSU certificate help?
    The certificate will confirm the date and checkpoint of your exit, but according to the European Commission's guidance the exit date should, as a rule, be no earlier than 90 days before the application; exceptional circumstances are taken into account. This rule is not in the text of the decision, and whether Spain applies it has not been confirmed. If it does, an old date may not help, and the second-level document will be decisive: for those on the military register, first of all Reserve+.
    I have been under protection in Spain since 2022. Do I need the certificate?
    Most likely not. The new condition does not apply to those who were under protection in Spain on 4 August 2026 and keep it without interruption. For a change of residence permit status (modificación), arraigo and long-term residence, the rules do not require a DPSU certificate either.
    Can I get a DPSU certificate myself?
    Yes, free of charge (Law No. 2297-VI, Art. 19). A written application and a scan of the first page of your passport are sent to the DPSU Main Centre for Processing Special Information in person, by post or by e-mail (the e-mail address is given on the DPSU page dated 18 December 2025; a later page mentions only filing in person and by post). The information is issued within 30 calendar days. Through a lawyer the statutory deadline is shorter: up to 5 working days, but the DPSU has the right to extend it to 20 working days, and then the difference from a request made on your own almost disappears. For confidential information the law does not expressly guarantee this deadline, and your written consent is needed for the request.
    Does a DPSU certificate replace Reserve+?
    No. The certificate confirms the fact, date, border checkpoint and direction of the crossing, but, according to the available descriptions, it does not state the grounds for exit. Recital (18) of Decision 2026/1912 treats a lawful exit as evidence, but whether Spain will accept the certificate as such evidence has not been confirmed. It does not replace Reserve+ documents on a deferment or exemption.
    Does the certificate need an apostille and a translation for Spain?
    An apostille: no, it is not placed on a DPSU certificate. A translation: whether one is needed is not officially known: Spain has not published a list of documents, and the European Commission recommends a sworn translation for the Reserve+ document but says nothing about the DPSU certificate. If the authority asks for a translation, we arrange a sworn translation from Ukrainian.
    Do women also have to prove their exit?
    Every new applicant must prove a lawful exit, and under recital (20) of Decision 2026/1912 military obligations may exist regardless of sex and age for those who have enlisted in military service or are connected with military activity. According to media reports, in some cities women were also asked for Reserve+; in August 2026 the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Ukraine (MZS) confirmed such isolated cases in some EU countries, mainly among those who could not prove a lawful exit.
