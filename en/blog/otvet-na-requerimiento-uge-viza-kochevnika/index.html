    UGE request on a digital nomad visa: how to reply within 10 days

    Krepko · updated 12 November 2026 · ~9 min read
    In short. A UGE request (requerimiento) on a digital nomad residence permit must be answered within 10 working days, counting from the day after notification (arts. 68.1 and 30 Ley 39/2015). Before the deadline expires you can ask for up to 5 more working days if a document is hard to obtain (arts. 68.2 and 32.3), but the office is not obliged to extend it. If you do not reply to a request under art. 68, your application is deemed withdrawn, and the extension of your stay that the application gave you ends together with the procedure (art. 76.1 Ley 14/2013). While the request is open, the office may suspend the 20-day deadline for a decision (art. 22.1.a Ley 39/2015). Rules checked on 1 November 2026.

    After you apply from Spain for a digital nomad residence permit, a letter may arrive in your electronic inbox from the UGE (Unidad de Grandes Empresas y Colectivos Estratégicos, the unit for large companies and strategic groups) listing what is missing from your file. This is a requerimiento: the office is not refusing you but asking you to fill the gaps. You have 10 working days to reply, and some of them go on making sense of the Spanish wording.

    Below we explain how to count the deadline, what happens if you do not reply, how to ask for 5 more days and how to cover the items that often come up in 2026 requests. We checked the rules on 1 November 2026 against the BOE and UGE documents.

    What a requerimiento is and why you received one

    A requerimiento is a letter in which the UGE lists what is missing from your file or what needs clarifying. The UGE guide for applicants states it plainly: if the documents submitted are not sufficient, the authority may request additional ones at any time. A request is a working stage, not a refusal: your file is waiting for your reply.

    Besides the list of items and the deadline, look in the letter for a reference to an article of the law: what happens if you do not reply depends on it.

    We handle the digital nomad visa from checking the requirements through to the card, and if you applied yourself, we also step in at the request stage: we go through the letter item by item and gather the missing documents, including certificates from Ukraine. The reply is filed by an immigration lawyer.

    How to count the 10 working days

    The counting rules are set out in art. 30 Ley 39/2015:

    • only working days count: Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays are excluded;
    • the count starts on the day after notification;
    • a public holiday where you live or where the authority is based counts as a non-working day, but for electronic filing only the calendar of the electronic registry applies (art. 31.3);
    • if the last day of the period is a non-working day, the deadline moves to the next working day.

    The UGE runs the procedure electronically (art. 76.1 Ley 14/2013), and the request usually arrives in your electronic notifications inbox. It is deemed received when you open it. If electronic notifications are mandatory for you or you chose them yourself, and you do not open the letter within 10 calendar days of it being made available, it is deemed rejected: the notification counts as served and the procedure moves on (arts. 43.2 and 41.5). In other words, the reply deadline may start running even if you never read the letter. A similar guide for Extranjería (the immigration office) is in the article on a request for the other parent's consent.

    Art. 68 or art. 73: what happens if you do not reply

    Check which article the letter refers to. The law provides for two kinds of request, and their consequences differ.

    Article in the letter When it applies If you do not reply
    Art. 68 Ley 39/2015 The application lacks a mandatory document or information The application is deemed withdrawn (desistimiento) by a separate decision, and the procedure ends
    Art. 73 Ley 39/2015 The office asks you to correct or complete a specific step of a procedure already under way The office may declare that you have lost the right to that step (decaído en su derecho al trámite) and decide on what is in the file, and without the requested document a requirement may remain unproven

    Under art. 73.3, a document submitted late is still accepted if it arrives before the notification that the deadline has been missed or on the same day. This is a last-resort safety net, not a plan: the text of art. 68 contains no such provision.

    Filing the application extends your current stay or residence until a decision is made (art. 76.1 Ley 14/2013). If the procedure ends with the application being withdrawn (art. 84.1 Ley 39/2015), that extension ends too. And you can only apply again from Spain while you are in the country lawfully (arts. 62.3.a and 74 quinquies.1 Ley 14/2013). For those who applied within their visa-free period or the period of their visa, this is the main risk. For holders of temporary protection, the lawfulness of their stay rests on the protection itself.

    While the request is open, the office may suspend the 20-day deadline for deciding on the application (art. 76.1): from notification until your reply or until the end of the period for replying (art. 22.1.a Ley 39/2015). If the letter mentions the suspension, the period after which the application is approved by administrative silence will continue to run after your reply or after the period for replying ends.

    How to ask for 5 more days

    Art. 68.2 Ley 39/2015 allows the deadline to be extended by up to 5 working days at your request or on the office's own initiative if the documents are difficult to obtain (dificultades especiales). The conditions are the same for all extensions (art. 32.3): the request must be made and decided on before the deadline expires, an expired deadline cannot be extended, and a refusal to extend cannot be appealed separately. That is why it is better to ask in the first days of the period.

    The request is a short letter in Spanish: the file number, which item of the request you cannot meet in time, why (for example, a certificate from another country with an apostille and sworn translation) and when the document will be ready. Attach proof that you have ordered it. If the document is being obtained abroad, also cite art. 32.2: for procedures that involve a step abroad, the law provides for an extension for the maximum period in any case. Whether this rule applies to the deadline under art. 68.2 has not been officially clarified. The office may refuse, so the documents are gathered in parallel.

    What 2026 requests often ask for

    The items below often come up in UGE requests in 2026. This is practice, not an official list; what each item requires is set out in the law and the UGE guide.

    Lawful stay without passport stamps

    Only someone who is here lawfully can apply from Spain (arts. 62.3.a and 74 quinquies.1 Ley 14/2013). Since 10 April 2026, passport stamps have been replaced by the EES (Entry/Exit System): your entry date cannot be seen from your passport, and the request may ask you to prove it. Spanish authorities can see EES records themselves. If you arrived from another Schengen country and made an entry declaration (declaración de entrada), attach it: it is made on entry or within the first three working days in Spain. Tickets and boarding passes are indirect evidence, and the office decides how much weight to give them. More on this in the article on proving your entry date without a stamp. For holders of temporary protection, lawful stay is proven by a valid temporary protection permit; on switching from it, see the article from temporary protection to the nomad visa.

    Income: a bank certificate, not a statement from the app

    The UGE guide requires payslips (nóminas) or invoices (facturas) for the three months before you apply and a certificate from the bank in your name, stamped or signed by the bank, for the same three months. The payments in the certificate must match the invoices or payslips and be marked separately. A statement from the app without the bank's stamp or signature will not do. If your income falls short of the threshold, you need a certificate of savings that cover the difference for the whole period of the permit. More in the section on proving your income.

    Translation: sworn, on paper or with an electronic signature

    Documents not in Spanish are submitted with a translation by a sworn translator authorised by the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The guide also accepts a translation made or checked by a consulate, provided it is then legalised by the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and for a CV a plain translation is enough. Foreign public documents must carry an apostille or be legalised. The apostille is part of the document, so the translator is given the document with the apostille already on it.

    A sworn translator may sign the translation with a qualified electronic signature. The signature covers the whole file, the original together with the translation, and is verified with the VALIDe service (Orden AUC/213/2025, art. 3). A paper sworn translation with the translator's signature and stamp is also valid (art. 3.7 of the same Orden). If the translation is signed electronically, submit the PDF itself from the translator: a scan of a printout no longer carries that signature.

    Criminal record certificate

    You need a certificate from every country where you have lived in the last two years, and a declaration made under your own responsibility (declaración responsable) that you have had no criminal convictions in the past five years (art. 62.3.c Ley 14/2013). The certificate is not required if you already hold a permit for residence or stay in Spain of more than six months and it was already submitted for that permit (UGE guide). A foreign certificate needs an apostille and a sworn translation. The guide does not say how long the certificate remains valid; if the request asks for a more recent one, it is quicker to order a new one. We order the certificate from Ukraine remotely, with an apostille; for documents from Russia, see the separate section.

    Regulated profession

    If your profession is regulated in Spain (for example, a doctor or a lawyer), the UGE guide requires homologation of your degree or a statement before a notary that you will not work in that profession in Spain. The second option makes it easier to meet the request deadline: homologation is a separate procedure, while the statement is signed before a Spanish notary in Spanish and needs no apostille or translation. A notarial declaration is one of our services: the notary certifies it, we prepare the text and accompany you at the signing.

    If the request concerns family members, see the article on the nomad visa with your family, and for what the UGE checks at renewal, the article on renewing a nomad residence permit.

    If a document will not arrive in time

    This is practice, not a rule of law. If one document is missing by the end of the period, the reply is still filed on time: everything that is ready, plus proof that the missing document has been ordered (a receipt for the certificate order or a letter from the translator with the completion date). Asking for an extension on the last day is too late: the office must decide on the request before the period ends (art. 32.3). The UGE guide itself advises attaching an explanatory letter (escrito explicativo), especially when the documents do not exactly match what was requested. As soon as the document arrives, it is sent on with the file number. For a request under art. 68, the office is obliged to accept a late document if it arrived before the decision on withdrawal of the application (as the Supreme Court has ruled), and under art. 73, if it arrives before the notification that the deadline has been missed or on the same day. That is why silence is worse than any partial reply.

    How to file your reply

    1. Go through the letter. Write down each item, the deadline and the article of the law the office refers to.
    2. Gather the documents item by item. Name the files after the item numbers in the request.
    3. Write a covering letter in Spanish. For each item: what you are attaching, and if a document is not ready yet, why and when it will be.
    4. File the reply electronically. You need the digital certificate of the applicant or the representative. If a lawyer filed the application as your representative, check with them who receives notifications on the case and who replies.
    5. Keep the filing receipt (justificante) with the date and time: it proves that the reply was filed on time.

    If the permit is still refused after your reply, the decision can be appealed through a recurso de alzada (art. 76.1 Ley 14/2013) within one month (art. 122.1 Ley 39/2015). The lawyer decides whether to appeal or apply again based on the text of the refusal.

    Frequently asked questions

    The UGE has sent me a requerimiento. Does that mean I will be refused?
    No. A request asks for missing documents; it is not a decision on your case: the UGE guide expressly reserves the authority's right to request additional documents if those submitted are not sufficient. A full reply on time keeps your application in progress. The decision is made by the authority, and no one can guarantee it in advance.
    From which day do the 10 working days count?
    From the day after notification. Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays are not counted (art. 30 Ley 39/2015). An electronic notification is deemed received when you open it. If electronic notifications are mandatory for you or you chose them, and you do not open the letter within 10 calendar days, it is deemed rejected and the procedure moves on (arts. 43.2 and 41.5). So it is worth checking your notifications inbox until your case is decided.
    Can the deadline for replying be extended?
    Yes, by up to 5 working days if the documents are hard to obtain (art. 68.2 Ley 39/2015). You need to ask in advance: both the request and the decision on it must come before the deadline expires, the office is not obliged to agree, and a refusal cannot be appealed separately (art. 32.3). That is why documents are gathered in parallel with the request for an extension.
    What happens if I do not reply in time?
    If the request was issued under art. 68, the application is deemed withdrawn and the procedure ends. With it ends the extension of your stay that the application gave you (art. 76.1 Ley 14/2013), and you can only apply again from Spain while you are in the country lawfully. A new application means a new fee of €73.26 (UGE questions and answers, checked on 1 November 2026). If the request was issued under art. 73, the office may decide on what is already in the file.
    My criminal record certificate from Ukraine will not be ready by the deadline. What should I do?
    File on time what is ready, together with proof that the certificate has been ordered, and send the certificate itself with its apostille and sworn translation as soon as it arrives. It is better to ask for an extension of 5 working days in the first days of the period. Sending documents later is practice, not a guarantee: for a request under art. 68, the office is obliged to take a late document into account only if it arrived before the decision on withdrawal of the application (as the Supreme Court has ruled). We order the certificate from Ukraine remotely, with an apostille.
    I have a medical degree. Why does the UGE need a notary?
    For regulated professions, the UGE guide requires either homologation of your degree or a statement before a notary that you will not work in that profession in Spain. The statement is signed before a Spanish notary in Spanish, and it needs no apostille or translation. A notarial declaration is one of our services: the notary certifies it, we prepare the text and accompany you at the signing.
