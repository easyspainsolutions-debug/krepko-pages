    A car with Ukrainian plates in Spain: how long you can drive it and when to register it

    Krepko · 24 November 2026 · ~8 min read
    In short. A resident of Spain may not drive a car with foreign plates: it must be registered with the DGT, and the tax law allows 30 days for this from when the car starts being used in Spain, or 60 days when moving here with an exemption from the registration tax (Ley 38/1992, Article 65.1.d). For holders of temporary protection the Ministry of Inclusion gives 213 days (183 + 30) from the date of the decision on protection: if protection was granted in spring 2022, this period expired long ago. The route for registering a car from Ukraine: customs, ITV with a Spanish technical data sheet, the registration tax and the vehicle tax, then the DGT (fee €99.77). While the DGT processes the registration, you can get green temporary plates for 60 days: they are issued against the full set of registration documents.

    You came to Spain in your own car with Ukrainian plates and have been living here for months or years. The main question is how long you can keep driving it and what you need to register it in Spain. The answer depends on whether you have temporary protection and on when the car entered Spain.

    Below: the deadlines for holders of temporary protection and for everyone else, the registration route from customs to Spanish plates, the conditions for the exemption from the registration tax when moving here, and what official sources say about selling such a car. We checked the rules and the authorities' pages on 24 November 2026; the links are in the list of sources.

    How long can you drive with temporary protection?

    The Ministry of Inclusion states on its “Ucrania urgente” portal: people from Ukraine who have been granted temporary protection by a decision of the Spanish Ministry of the Interior are given 213 days (183 + 30) to register in Spain the cars they brought from Ukraine or from another country. The period is counted from the date of the decision granting protection. The page is dated 7 June 2022; as of 24 November 2026 we did not find a separate DGT document with this period.

    What this means in practice:

    • The period is tied to the decision, not to the card. If protection was granted on 15 March 2022, the 213 days ended on 14 October 2022. The ministry's page does not say that renewing the TIE card restarts the count.
    • The tax deadlines run in parallel. Ley 38/1992, as worded on 30 September 2026, still makes the registration tax payable on a car used in Spain if no application for registration is filed within 30 days (60 days when moving here with an exemption). The law takes as the start date the day the car was brought into Spain or, if that is not proven by a document, the later of two dates: the purchase of the car or the start of residence in Spain. If the car was under temporary admission, the count runs from the date it left that regime or the regime ended.
    • Consequences. If the obligation to register the car has not been met, the tax office or the traffic authorities give 5 days to register it or to provide security for payment of the tax (a bank guarantee or a surety insurance bond), after which the car is impounded until the matter is settled (Ley 38/1992, first additional provision, paragraph 4).

    The same ministry page confirms: if a person with temporary protection registers a car in their own name or transfers a car to themselves, the application is accepted provided they have authorised residence, an NIE and a proven address.

    What is the deadline if you do not have temporary protection?

    The tax office (AEAT) puts the rule plainly: residents of Spain may not drive cars with foreign plates, an imported car is registered on arrival, there are 30 days for this, and for the duration of the process the DGT issues temporary plates. The obligation to register is set out in the first additional provision of Ley 38/1992: vehicles used here by residents are registered in Spain. The deadlines and the rule on the start date are the same as described above.

    How do you register a car in Spain?

    1. Customs. Ukraine is outside the EU, so the car first goes through customs, and you pay the applicable duty and taxes or arrange an exemption. Afterwards an H1 import certificate is issued, which is needed for registration (DGT). If you are claiming the customs relief for moving here, the declaration is filed within 12 months of the start of your habitual residence in Spain (AEAT).
    2. ITV and the Spanish technical data sheet. To the ITV station you bring the registration document from the country of origin and proof of homologation: a European certificate of conformity (CoC), a reduced technical data sheet signed by an engineer (ficha reducida), and for cars from before 2002, a homologation of that specific car by an engineer (by equivalence or individual), which is done before the ITV. If the car does not meet Euro VI (Euro V for mixed-use and commercial vehicles) or has no CoC, the DGT warns of modifications and additional procedures.
    3. Registration tax. It is paid once using Modelo 576, or the exemption is confirmed with Modelo 06 or 05. For a used car the tax base is its market value on the date the tax becomes chargeable, and the law allows average prices from tables approved by the Ministry of Finance to be used (Article 69). The rate depends on CO2 emissions: see the table below.
    4. Vehicle tax (IVTM). An annual tax charged by the town hall where you live; for registration you need a receipt for payment or for the exemption.
    5. Registration with the DGT. You apply at a Tráfico office by appointment (on the website or on 060) or online with Cl@ve or a digital certificate. You need the car's original documents, the H1 certificate (if the import is not noted on the ITV technical data sheet), the technical data sheet, the tax receipts and a document with your address: a TIE or a passport with an NIE. The fee (tasa 1.1) is €99.77. The maximum time for a decision is 3 months, and no answer means refusal. Then the plates are made at a specialised workshop, and the car is insured.

    up to and including 120 g/km, electric cars0%
    over 120 and under 160 g/km4.75%
    from 160 to 200 g/km (not including 200)9.75%
    200 g/km and over, or emissions not proven14.75%

    These are the rates of Article 70.2.b of Ley 38/1992 that apply if the autonomous community has not approved its own. Regions are entitled to change them (Article 70.2.a), so the exact rate should be checked for the place of registration.

    What can you drive while the car is being registered?

    The DGT states plainly that a car brought in from outside the EU may not be driven until it is registered in Spain, and offers temporary green plates (placas verdes). The conditions on the DGT website:

    • they are valid for 60 days; the fee (tasa 1.4) is €20.61;
    • the owner or their representative applies, online or at a Tráfico office by appointment;
    • the application must be accompanied by the full set of registration documents: receipts for both fees (1.4 and 1.1), the registration tax, the municipal tax, and for a car registered in another country, the complete original documents from the country of origin;
    • you may drive only with insurance and a valid ITV: many insurers sell a temporary policy;
    • if the car could not be registered within 60 days for reasons beyond your control, you ask for an extension before the period expires: it is free, but you need a document stating the reason for the delay, usually from the ITV station.

    When is no registration tax due if you move to Spain?

    Ley 38/1992 (Article 66.1.n) exempts from the tax a car registered in connection with its owner moving their permanent residence to Spain. The conditions of the law:

    • before the move you lived outside Spain for at least 12 consecutive months;
    • the car was bought or imported under the general tax conditions of the country of origin, without a tax refund on export;
    • you used the car in your previous country for at least six months before leaving;
    • registration was requested within the period of Article 65.1.d: 60 days;
    • for 12 months after registration the car may not be transferred to another person, otherwise the tax is charged on the date of transfer.

    This exemption does not require a prior decision by the tax office: it is claimed with Modelo 06 using code ET4 before registration with the DGT. According to the AEAT instructions, the form must be accompanied by a certificate from the consular authority confirming residence outside Spain for at least 12 consecutive months and the invoice for the purchase of the car. It is filed in person at the AEAT office for your tax address; online only through a gestor. The exemption applies if your case meets all the conditions, and this can only be proven with documents.

    The customs relief for moving here has similar conditions: 12 months of living outside the EU and six months of using the car. After it is applied, the car may not be lent, rented out or transferred to others for 12 months without notifying the authorities in advance (AEAT).

    Can you sell a car with Ukrainian plates in Spain

    On the DGT, AEAT and Ministry of Inclusion websites we did not find a Spanish procedure for selling a car that is registered in Ukraine and not registered in Spain. The official sources say something else:

    • a resident buyer is also obliged to register the car in Spain (Ley 38/1992, first additional provision);
    • after the customs relief and after the exemption from the registration tax, 12-month bans on transferring the car apply;
    • after registration with the DGT, the car is sold like any ordinary Spanish car, with a change of owner at the DGT.

    If you are planning a sale, it is best to work out the deadlines and procedure before customs and before applying for the exemption: this determines whether you will have to pay extra tax.

    Frequently asked questions

    How long can I drive a car with Ukrainian plates in Spain if I have temporary protection?
    The Ministry of Inclusion on its “Ucrania urgente” portal gives 213 days (183 + 30) from the date of the decision granting temporary protection. If protection was granted in 2022, this period has already expired. The tax law, meanwhile, counts its own deadline: 30 days from when the car starts being used in Spain (Ley 38/1992, Article 65.1.d).
    Can I drive on Ukrainian plates while the car is being registered?
    The DGT states that a car brought in from outside the EU may not be driven until it is registered in Spain. For the duration of the process the DGT issues temporary green plates for 60 days for a fee of €20.61 against the full set of registration documents; they can be extended free of charge before the period expires if the delay is not your fault. You may drive on them only with insurance.
    How much is the registration tax on a car from Ukraine?
    The tax is calculated on the market value of the used car, and the rate depends on CO2 emissions: under the law's general scale, 0% up to 120 g/km, 4.75%, 9.75% and 14.75% from 200 g/km (Ley 38/1992, Article 70). Regions are entitled to approve their own rates. If you move your permanent residence here, you may be exempt from the tax if the conditions of Article 66.1.n are met.
    Who qualifies for the registration tax exemption when moving to Spain?
    Someone who before the move lived outside Spain for at least 12 consecutive months, used the car there for at least six months and bought it under the general tax conditions. Registration must be requested within 60 days, and for 12 months afterwards the car may not be transferred to others. The exemption is claimed with Modelo 06 using code ET4 before registration with the DGT.
    Can I sell a car with Ukrainian plates in Spain?
    There is no Spanish procedure on the DGT, AEAT or Ministry of Inclusion websites for selling a car that is registered in Ukraine and not registered in Spain. A resident buyer is also obliged to register it, and after the customs relief and the tax exemption, 12-month bans on transfer apply. After registration with the DGT, the car is sold like any ordinary Spanish car.
    Do I need to exchange my Ukrainian driving licence along with registering the car?
    No. Registering the car and exchanging the licence are different procedures: the car is registered with the DGT as a vehicle, while the licence is exchanged with a separate application, and their deadlines are counted under different rules. We covered how long you can drive on a Ukrainian licence with temporary protection and without it in the article on Ukrainian driving licences in Spain, and the exchange procedure in the article on exchanging a driving licence.
