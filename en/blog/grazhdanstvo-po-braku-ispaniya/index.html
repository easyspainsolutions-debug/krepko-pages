    Spanish citizenship by marriage: a 5-year residence permit for your spouse and applying after one year

    Krepko · updated 12 November 2026 · ~9 min read
    In short. You apply for Spanish citizenship by marriage when, by the date of your application, you have been married to a Spanish citizen for a year, you are not living apart, and you have lived in Spain legally and continuously for a year immediately before applying (art. 22.2.d and 22.3 of the Civil Code). Until then, you can live and work on a residence permit for a relative of a Spanish citizen: it is issued for five years with the right to work as an employee and as self-employed (art. 95.1 and 95.3 RD 1155/2024), and holders of temporary protection apply for it from within Spain (SEM Instruction 2/2026). Unmarried partners can also get this residence permit, but they have no shortened period for citizenship. Whether time on temporary protection counts towards the year of residence for citizenship has not been officially decided, so it is safer to apply once you have a year on the residence permit behind you (rules checked on 1 November 2026).

    Marriage to a Spanish citizen shortens the path to Spanish nationality from the general ten years to one year. But that year does not start automatically on the wedding day: by the date of your application you need both a year of marriage and a year of legal residence in Spain. Legal residence comes from a separate residence permit for relatives of Spanish citizens. It is granted straight away for five years, with the right to work.

    Below we explain who is entitled to this residence permit, how marriage differs from a domestic partnership, how to switch to it from temporary protection, when to apply for citizenship and what happens to the residence permit on divorce. We checked the rules on 1 November 2026 against the BOE and documents of the immigration authority.

    What marriage to a Spanish citizen gives you

    Marriage by itself gives you neither a residence permit nor citizenship: these are two separate applications. The residence permit for a relative of a Spanish citizen is applied for at Extranjería (the immigration office), citizenship at the Ministry of Justice, and citizenship requires a year of legal residence, which is easiest to build up on this residence permit.

    Since 20 May 2025, families of Spanish citizens have been processed under the national regime, under Chapter VII of Title IV of the Immigration Regulation (RD 1155/2024). Previously, relatives of Spaniards were issued an EU citizen's family member card or a residence permit on the grounds of family ties (arraigo familiar). Anyone whose permit of this kind was valid on 20 May 2025 keeps their residence under the terms of the new chapter without a new application (Transitional Provision 3). What we do and how the application works is described on the page Residence for the spouse and relatives of Spanish citizens.

    Residence permit for a spouse: conditions, work and timeframes

    The permit is available to a spouse over 18, provided the marriage has not been dissolved or declared null and you live together (art. 94.1.a). The marriage must be genuine: the regulation excludes marriages entered into en fraude de ley (in fraud of the law), and the Immigration Act classes a marriage of convenience for a residence permit as a serious offence (art. 53.2.b Ley Orgánica 4/2000).

    • Where to apply. If you are both in Spain, the application is filed here, at the Extranjería office of your province, without travelling for a visa (art. 97.1.c). Either spouse can file it.
    • Work. Once the application has been accepted for processing, you can live and work until the decision (art. 97.5). The permit itself gives the right to work as an employee and as self-employed throughout Spain (art. 95.1).
    • Duration. Five years from the date of approval or, if the spouse intends to live in Spain for less time, for that period (art. 95.3).
    • Decision. By law, within two months of filing; silence means refusal (art. 97.6). The application is processed as a priority and without a fee (art. 97.8).

    Documents under art. 96 and 97.4: a copy of all pages of your passport, valid for at least another year; your spouse's passport or DNI and their statement that no other spouse or partner lives with them in Spain; the marriage certificate; a criminal record certificate from the countries where you have lived in the last five years (if Ukraine is among them, a Ukrainian one too); a medical certificate is not required when applying from within Spain (DGGM criterion 5/2025). If your Spanish spouse has been married before, attach the ruling on the previous divorce that settles the family home and payments to the former spouse and children: without it, the new spouse is not granted the residence permit (art. 94.1.a). The authority accepts foreign documents with an apostille and a translation into Spanish, either sworn or consular.

    Marriage or domestic partnership (pareja de hecho)

    The residence permit is also granted without marriage, but for citizenship the difference is fundamental: only marriage gives you one year instead of ten.

    You cannot claim both grounds at once: the regulation treats them as mutually exclusive. How to get married before a notary or at the civil registry is covered in our article on marriage for foreigners in Spain. The notary formalises the marriage; we prepare the documents and accompany you at the signing — getting married before a notary. Without marriage, a couple can be registered as a domestic partnership (pareja de hecho). With an entry in the register you apply for the residence permit under art. 94.1.b; without one, under art. 94.1.c, if you prove 12 months of living together or have a child in common. For a registered partnership you do not need to prove how long you have lived together, and after separation the residence permit based on it can be kept (art. 99.1 and 99.4). For both options, you will usually need a marital status certificate from Ukraine.

    If you are on temporary protection

    Holders of temporary protection apply for the residence permit for a relative of a Spanish citizen directly from within Spain, under the general rules of Chapter VII (SEM Instruction 2/2026, point quinta.2; Additional Provision 19 of RD 1155/2024 as worded by RD 316/2026). These rules apply now; whether they will change is not known.

    1. Before applying, check your passport: it must be valid, with at least a year left. If it is about to expire, you can replace your passport in Spain.
    2. You file the application while remaining under protection.
    3. You receive the approval.
    4. You file an express renunciation of temporary protection.
    5. Within a month of being notified of the approval, you apply for the TIE card (art. 97.7 RD 1155/2024) and show the receipt for the renunciation (SEM 2/2026, point séptima).
    Renouncing protection is irreversible: protection ends automatically (art. 24.1.d RD 1325/2003). That is why you file it only after the new residence permit has been approved. What to do before this step is covered in our article on renouncing temporary protection.

    For long-term residence (larga duración nacional), years on protection count towards the five years. For citizenship, the question has not been officially decided: neither the Civil Code nor the SEM instruction says anything about it. So it is safer to count the year of residence for citizenship from the start of this residence permit.

    Citizenship after one year: what has to line up

    Under art. 22.2.d of the Civil Code, one year of residence is enough for someone who, by the date of the application, has been married to a Spanish citizen for a year and is not separated from their spouse either legally or in fact. Residence must be legal, continuous and immediately before the application (art. 22.3). This means two periods have to come together on the same date: a year of marriage and a year of residence.

    Example. The marriage took place on 10 March 2026, and the residence permit for a relative has been valid since 20 May 2026. A year of marriage is reached on 10 March 2027, a year on the residence permit on 20 May 2027. The safe date to apply is no earlier than 20 May 2027.

    There are two more general requirements: good civic conduct and integration (art. 22.4). Integration is proven through the Instituto Cervantes exams: DELE at level A2 or above and CCSE, a test on the Constitution and life in Spain (art. 6 RD 1004/2015). Nationals of Spanish-speaking countries are exempt from DELE; Ukrainians and Russians need both exams.

    If the marriage is dissolved or you separate before applying, the shortened period through marriage no longer applies. For widows and widowers, one year of residence is enough if you were not living apart at the time of the spouse's death (art. 22.2.e). Who else gets citizenship after one year, and who after ten, is covered in the article on Spanish citizenship after 10 years.

    Documents for citizenship: what to order in advance

    The list is set by the annex to Orden JUS/1625/2016:

    • a full, valid passport of your country of origin, the one shown on your residence card;
    • a birth certificate with an apostille and a sworn or consular translation;
    • a criminal record certificate from your country of origin, also with an apostille and a translation; if it states no validity period, it is valid for six months from the date of issue;
    • extracts from the Spanish civil registry (Registro Civil) of your spouse's birth and of the marriage;
    • a certificate of joint municipal registration or of living together with your spouse;
    • the DELE and CCSE diplomas, or consent in the application for them to be checked;
    • the receipt for payment of the fee on Modelo 790, code 026.

    If you married abroad, the marriage is first entered in the Spanish Registro Civil: the ministry asks for the marriage extract from there specifically. Documents from your country of origin take the longest. From Ukraine, we obtain them without you having to travel and have them apostilled: the birth certificate, the criminal record certificate and, for the residence permit, if you married in Ukraine, the marriage certificate. The full set is on the page documents for Spanish nationality.

    Statutory timeframes: the whole path

    1. Residence permit for a relative — up to two months
    Silence means refusal (art. 97.6 RD 1155/2024).
    2. TIE card — within a month
    In person at the police after being notified of the approval (art. 97.7).
    3. A year of marriage and a year of residence
    You apply for citizenship once both periods have been met (art. 22.2.d and 22.3 of the Civil Code).
    4. Decision on citizenship — up to a year
    Counted from when the application reaches the Ministry of Justice; silence means refusal (art. 11.3 RD 1004/2015).
    5. Oath — within 180 days
    After notification: the oath, a declaration renouncing your previous nationality and a request for entry in the Registro Civil; otherwise the decision lapses (art. 21.4 and 23 of the Civil Code). Natives of Ibero-American countries, Andorra, the Philippines, Equatorial Guinea and Portugal, and Sephardic Jews, are exempt from this declaration.

    Renewing the residence permit and what happens on divorce

    The residence permit remains valid as long as the grounds for it remain: if the spouses stop living as one family, the permit may be revoked (art. 98.3). A change of address, nationality or marital status must be reported to Extranjería or the police within two months (art. 98.2). If the permit was issued for less than five years, it is renewed on the same terms: the application is filed within two months before expiry or three months after. Filing after expiry may lead to a fine (art. 95.4), so it is safer to file earlier.

    For reporting a divorce, an annulment of the marriage or the cancellation of a partnership registration, art. 99.4 allows six months, but art. 98.2 only two, so it is safer to report within two months. Six months are allowed for the application for independent residence. If you are keeping the residence permit on the basis of three years of marriage, the period is counted not from the divorce decision but from the notification that the court has admitted the petition for divorce, separation or annulment; for a registered couple, from the notification of the cancellation of the entry (art. 99.4.a and 99.6). That is why we advise preparing the application immediately after the petition is filed. You can keep the residence permit in one of these cases (art. 99.4 and 99.5):

    • the marriage or registered partnership lasted at least three years before the proceedings began, one of them in Spain;
    • you have been granted custody of the Spanish citizen's children;
    • you have contact rights with a minor child who lives in Spain;
    • you have suffered gender-based, sexual or domestic violence or human trafficking at the hands of your Spanish spouse, or family abandonment. In that case the length of the marriage does not matter, but you need a court protection order, a police or public prosecutor's report or another official document confirming it (art. 99.5).

    Art. 99.1 excludes unregistered partners (art. 94.1.c) from this rule, although art. 99.4 mentions them; we review such cases individually.

    Frequently asked questions

    After how many years of marriage do you get Spanish citizenship?
    You can apply when, by the date of your application, you have been married to a Spanish citizen for a year, you are not living apart and you have lived in Spain legally and continuously for a year immediately before applying (art. 22.2.d and 22.3 of the Civil Code). By law, the Ministry of Justice decides within a year; if there is no answer, the application is deemed rejected.
    We are not married but live together. Will I get citizenship after one year?
    No: the law gives one year instead of ten only to spouses (art. 22.2.d of the Civil Code). The partner of a Spanish citizen can get a residence permit for a relative if the couple is entered in a register of an EU or EEA country or Switzerland, or if you prove 12 months of living together; with a child in common, that period is not required (art. 94.1.b–c RD 1155/2024). For citizenship, the general period of 10 years applies to a partner.
    I am on temporary protection and have married a Spaniard. What should I do about my protection?
    Apply for the residence permit for a relative of a Spanish citizen directly from within Spain: SEM Instruction 2/2026 expressly allows this. You renounce protection only after the new permit has been approved, and you show the receipt for the renunciation when applying for the TIE card. The renunciation is irreversible, so it is not filed in advance. Before applying, check your passport: it must be valid for at least another year.
    Will years on temporary protection count towards citizenship by marriage?
    The question has not been officially decided: neither the Civil Code nor SEM Instruction 2/2026 says anything about it with regard to citizenship. For long-term residence (larga duración nacional), years on protection do count, but that is a different procedure. It is safer to apply for citizenship once you have a year on the residence permit for a relative of a Spanish citizen behind you.
    Can I work while my application for a spouse's residence permit is being processed?
    Yes, if you applied from within Spain: once the application has been accepted for processing, you are entitled to live and work as an employee and as self-employed until the decision (art. 97.5 RD 1155/2024). By law, the decision itself is made within two months.
    What happens to the residence permit if we divorce?
    It can be kept as an independent permit if the marriage lasted at least three years, one of them in Spain, if you have been granted custody of the children or contact rights with a child who lives in Spain, or if you have suffered violence, human trafficking or family abandonment and this is confirmed by a court protection order, a police or public prosecutor's report or another official document (art. 99.4 and 99.5 RD 1155/2024). The application is filed within six months. If the grounds are three years of marriage, the period is counted from the notification that the court has admitted the divorce petition, not from the date of the divorce itself (art. 99.4.a and 99.6 RD 1155/2024). After a divorce, you can no longer apply for citizenship after one year through marriage: the law requires you to be married on the date of the application.
