    Exchanging a Ukrainian driving licence for a Spanish one in 2026: a step-by-step guide

    Krepko · updated 11 November 2026 · ~6 min read
    In short. Ukrainian licences for motorcycles and cars are exchanged for Spanish ones without driving or theory exams — Ukraine and Spain have a mutual recognition agreement (canje directo); for lorries and buses a practical exam is required. You need a residence permit or temporary protection status, a medical certificate from a drivers' medical examination centre and an appointment at DGT for “Canje de permiso extranjero”: the original is taken from you at the appointment, and you are allowed to drive on a temporary permit. After 6 months of living in Spain, foreign licences are not valid without an exchange — except Ukrainian licences held by people with temporary protection, while it remains in force.     Do you need to exchange your licence, and when to do it

    Under Spanish law, if you live in Spain for more than 6 months, you must exchange your foreign driving licence for a Spanish one. Driving beyond this period without starting the exchange means risking a fine at the very first roadside check.

    For Ukrainians with temporary protection status the situation is more lenient. While temporary protection is in force, Ukrainian licences are recognised in Spain as valid for driving, provided they are valid: first licences issued for two years that expired after 31 December 2021 remain valid throughout martial law and for one more year after it (DGT note of 4 August 2025). The measure is temporary, and we do not advise building long-term plans on it.

    We advise exchanging your licence within the first 6–12 months after arriving: while the licence is valid, the exchange follows the direct procedure — without fines and without being refused a hire car.

    The agreement between Ukraine and Spain on mutual recognition

    Canje directo (direct exchange) is what makes it worth hurrying. Spain recognises Ukrainian licences as equivalent to Spanish ones, so category A and B licences are exchanged without a driving test and without a theory exam on the rules of the road.

    This is possible only if:

    • You have a valid Ukrainian driving licence (in force)
    • You hold a residence permit in Spain (temporary protection or resident status)
    • You have not been disqualified from driving by a court, and your licence is not suspended

    Russian licences are more complicated: there is no licence exchange agreement between Russia and Spain, and most often there is only one route — taking the exams in Spain as a new driver. Before you sign up with a driving school, write to us on WhatsApp: we will look at your case and tell you whether there are grounds for an exchange.

    Who can exchange a licence without an exam

    • Ukrainians with a valid licence for motorcycles and cars — exchange without exams, by the direct procedure
    • Ukrainians with a licence that is no longer valid — it cannot be exchanged under the agreement; what remains is taking the exams in Spain
    • All other foreigners — it depends on the country that issued the licence. Some countries have an agreement with Spain and the exchange is direct; the rest have to take the theory and driving tests

    Step-by-step guide: how to exchange your licence

    Step 1: Booking at DGT

    DGT (Dirección General de Tráfico) is the Spanish traffic authority. Appointments can be booked on the website www.dgt.es or by phone; in the list of procedures choose “Canje de permiso extranjero” — exchange of a foreign driving licence.

    Slots open 30 days ahead and are snapped up quickly: in large cities the real wait reaches 2–3 months. So it makes sense to check the calendar from the very first day booking opens.

    Recent on this topic: “Ukrainian driving licence in Spain: how long you can drive on it and how to exchange it without mistakes”

    Step 2: Preparing documents

    You need a complete set for the appointment: if anything is missing, the visit is rescheduled, and you will again have to wait months for a new appointment. Here is what to gather:

    • Original Ukrainian driving licence
    • Passport or ID card (original and copy)
    • NIE/TIE (foreigner identity number in Spain) — original and copy
    • Proof of address in Spain — not needed separately: DGT checks your registered address (padrón) itself in its database, and may ask for a registration certificate if the data has not been confirmed
    • Psychophysical medical certificate (certificado de apto psicofísico) — issued only at a drivers' medical examination centre (Centro de Reconocimiento de Conductores) in Spain. The price is set by the medical examination centre itself (DGT does not regulate it); it is issued in a single visit
    • A certificate confirming that your Ukrainian licence is valid is not needed: DGT requests verification of the licence from the Ukrainian side itself
    • A photo is not needed separately: the medical examination centre sends it to DGT itself together with its report
    • Form Mod. 03 “Trámites de conductores” (the exchange form) — handed out at the appointment, or download it in advance from the DGT website
    Important: requirements for copies differ between DGT offices — some accept a plain photocopy, others ask for a certified one. Bring the originals in any case: without them the appointment will not go ahead. We find out which format your office expects before booking.

    Step 3: Medical certificate

    Without the certificate, the DGT appointment will not take place. The examination checks your eyesight, hearing and reactions and confirms that there are no contraindications to driving. Check the certificate's validity period with the medical examination centre.

    Medical examination centres are found by place of residence — there is one in every town. On arrival, say straight away that the certificate is for a canje de permiso at DGT: then it will be issued in the right format and will not have to be redone.

    Step 4: Attending DGT

    Come at the appointed time with originals and copies. The appointment itself takes 10–20 minutes: the documents are checked, the Ukrainian licence is taken permanently (under the agreement it is returned to the Ukrainian side) and you are given a temporary Spanish permit — you can drive with it while the plastic card is being made; for lorries and buses the licence is taken and the permit is issued only after the practical exam.

    At the appointment you pay the tasas (government fees) — €28.87 for an exchange without an exam (DGT fee 2.3; if the category requires a driving test, €94.05); the DGT catalogue has no separate fee for the temporary permit.

    Timeframes and receiving the plastic card

    A Spanish driving licence is a plastic card the size of a bank card. The plastic card arrives by post in about a month and a half, and all that time you drive on the temporary permit (permiso temporal): in terms of driving rights it is equivalent to the permanent licence.

    You do not need to collect the card at a DGT office or a police station: DGT sends it by post to your home address.

    What to do if your Ukrainian licence has expired

    DGT will not accept a licence that is no longer valid as grounds for a canje: under the agreement only valid licences are exchanged. A certificate from the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine (MVS) stating that the licence was issued to you and was valid will not change this — DGT requests verification of the licence from the Ukrainian side itself.

    The alternative is to take the full exam in Spain, theory and practical. The route is longer and more expensive, but it works.

    Cost of the procedure

    • DGT fees (exchange) — €28.87 (for lorries and buses — €94.05)
    • Temporary permit — no separate fee
    • Medical certificate — €30–50
    • Photo — not needed separately
    • Copies and notary (if needed) — €10–30
    • Total — roughly €70–110 (for lorries and buses — more)

    For comparison: a full driving school course with exams costs hundreds of euros and takes months. An exchange under the agreement is the shortest and cheapest route while it is available to you.

    Typical mistakes and how to avoid them

    Not booking in time

    DGT booking opens 30 days ahead, and in Madrid, Barcelona and other large cities slots go within the first hours. Do not put it off: a free slot is caught on the day booking opens; after that only inconvenient dates remain.

    Forgetting to apostille the certificate from Ukraine

    Spain accepts a certificate from the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine only with an apostille. But it is not needed for a licence exchange: DGT requests verification of the licence from the Ukrainian side itself.

    An expired Ukrainian licence

    A licence that is no longer valid is not exchanged under the agreement, and a certificate stating that it used to be valid will not change this. Check the expiry date on the licence in advance, before you catch a slot at DGT.

    No municipal registration in Spain

    For an exchange you need a residence permit — temporary protection or resident status. If you have only just arrived and have not yet registered with the padrón (empadronamiento), we first see your NIE/TIE application through: this takes 1–2 months, and without them DGT will not accept the documents.

    2026 update: Spain has extended the validity of Ukrainian driving licences for holders of temporary protection while temporary protection remains in force (EU Regulation 2022/1280, art. 3). For now you can still drive on Ukrainian licences, but the extension is finite and the DGT queue is long — we do not advise starting the exchange in the last month.

    After the exchange: what has changed

    Spanish licences are valid throughout the EU and are recognised in many countries outside it. With one, the question of translating the document and disputes over whether your licence is valid in a particular country go away.

    Car hire companies rent to you on a Spanish licence without extra conditions, and at a roadside check the document is read straight away — without translations or clarifications.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does a Ukrainian need to exchange their licence for a Spanish one?
    Yes: under Spanish law, if you live in Spain for more than 6 months, you must exchange your foreign licence for a Spanish one. Ukrainians with temporary protection do not have to exchange their licence while the protection remains in force (EU Regulation 2022/1280, art. 3).
    Can I exchange a Ukrainian licence without an exam?
    Yes, Ukraine and Spain have a mutual recognition agreement (canje directo). Ukrainian licences for motorcycles and cars are exchanged without an exam if you have a residence permit or temporary protection status and have not been disqualified from driving by a court; for lorries and buses a practical exam is required.
    What should I do if my Ukrainian licence has expired?
    If the licence is no longer valid, a direct exchange is not possible. You will have to take a full driving school course and pass the theory and driving exams at DGT — as a new driver.
    How long does it take to get the plastic Spanish licence card?
    The temporary permit (autorización temporal) is issued straight away when you hand in your original licence at DGT (for lorries and buses — after the practical exam), and the plastic card arrives by post at your padrón address within 30–60 days.
