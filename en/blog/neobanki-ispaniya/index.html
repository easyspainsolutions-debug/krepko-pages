    Neobanks in Spain 2026: Wise, Revolut, N26 for non-residents and Ukrainians

    Krepko · updated 13 November 2026 · ~6 min read
    Wise, Revolut and N26 can be opened in an evening with a passport, without an NIE (except N26) and without going to a branch, which is why it seems that you need not open a Spanish account at all. We handle account opening for non-residents and see where this logic breaks down: salary, renting, benefits, mortgages. Below: what each neobank really covers, where verification trips up Ukrainians, and which combination of accounts works without surprises.

    Why do you need a neobank if you have a Spanish account?

    Neobanks attract Ukrainians in Spain with low fees, fast opening and the ability to send money from Ukraine. They do not become a full replacement for a Spanish bank: with our clients this comes up with the very first salary payment or the signing of a lease. Each tool has its own role, and below we look at which one.

    What a neobank really gives you: opening without an NIE, fast verification (sometimes a passport is enough), low fees on transfers, a convenient mobile app and a virtual card within minutes. This is enough to get through your first weeks in Spain, but not enough to meet your obligations to an employer and a landlord.

    Wise (TransferWise) — the king of transfers from Ukraine

    How to open an account

    Wise can be opened in 5 minutes in the app or on the website. You need a passport and a phone number. Verification usually goes through instantly; sometimes Wise asks for additional documents, most often proof of address. No NIE is required, and this is exactly the case where you set up the account yourself, without us.

    Main features

    • IBAN: a Belgian IBAN (BE); Wise does not have a Spanish one
    • Cash withdrawals: free at ATMs in Europe (1–2 times a month)
    • Transfer fee: the real exchange rate plus a small percentage (usually 0.4–1% depending on the amount)
    • Card: a physical Visa card is delivered to Spain
    • Limits: the basic account is limited; with verification you can send up to €20 million in a single transfer

    Using it instead of a Spanish account

    Wise is not always suitable for a salary: under EU rules an employer is not entitled to require an account specifically in Spain, but many Spanish employers still will not pay into a Wise IBAN. A landlord may also refuse and require a Spanish account. Wise is convenient for personal payments, transfers and cash withdrawals, but it will not cover an employment contract or a lease: for those you need an account at a Spanish bank.

    Revolut — for travel and currency exchange

    Opening for Ukrainians

    Revolut can be opened with an international passport (the Ukrainian passport for travel abroad) and a selfie. Verification takes from a few minutes to 24 hours. For 2026 Revolut has adjusted the process for Ukrainians: the international passport is accepted without problems, and no certificates are requested at the start.

    Main features

    • IBAN: a Spanish IBAN is available on all plans, including the free one
    • Currency exchange: built-in exchange at a good rate, but on the basic plan it is fee-free only up to €1,000 a month (above the limit and at weekends, 1%); on Premium and Metal there is no limit and no fee
    • Card: a physical and a virtual Visa or Mastercard
    • Transfers: SEPA transfers are free, international ones are charged
    • Plans: the basic plan is free, Premium €9.99/month, Metal €15.99/month

    Verification problems

    Revolut often asks Ukrainians for additional documents: proof of address in Spain, a bank statement, a copy of the residence permit. If you have been refused, update your profile photo, upload the documents again and write to support via the chat. When the refusal is repeated, the reason is usually the same: there is nothing to prove your address with. Then we start with municipal registration (empadronamiento) and an account at a Spanish bank, and add the neobank on top.

    Need an account that will be accepted when you submit your documents?

    Not every account is suitable for a residence permit, a mortgage or a property transaction. Tell us what you need it for, and we will tell you what will work. We provide support at the bank.

    N26 — the minimalist German bank

    N26 operates in Spain, but with restrictions. To open an account you need a Spanish phone number, as well as an NIE and a residential address in Spain. Verification for a non-resident is harder than with Wise and Revolut.

    • IBAN: a Spanish IBAN (ES); a German one (DE) only for accounts opened before 17 April 2019
    • Card: Mastercard, delivered to Spain
    • Fees: transfers between N26 accounts are free, and so are SEPA transfers
    • Limits: a few free cash withdrawals a month; the number depends on the plan
    • Downsides: older accounts (opened before 17 April 2019) may have a German IBAN, and then clients sometimes require a Spanish one

    Conclusion: there is no point opening N26 for Spain: its IBAN is Spanish, but verification for a non-resident is harder and there is no gain in convenience. Wise and Revolut cover the same tasks more simply.

    Can you use a neobank instead of a Spanish account?

    The short answer: not completely. Here is where this hits a wall in practice; the list is put together from the situations people come to us with:

    What a neobank will not cover for you

    • Mortgage: banks only grant mortgages to Spanish accounts
    • Salary: many employers require a Spanish IBAN
    • State benefits: public authorities usually pay subsidies, child benefits and pensions into a Spanish account
    • Some contractual obligations: insurance and utilities may require a Spanish account
    • Autónomo: if you are self-employed, a Spanish account is needed for contributions and tax payments

    The best option: a neobank plus a Spanish account

    We advise clients not to choose, but to keep both accounts and split the tasks between them:

    • Wise or Revolut for receiving money from Ukraine, transfers and travel
    • A Spanish bank for salary, bills, a mortgage and taxes

    This combination removes the main headache: money from home arrives cheaply and quickly, while salary, rent and taxes go through a Spanish IBAN that nobody will dispute.

    How to transfer money from Ukraine through Wise

    The most common route for 2026 is five steps, all of which you do yourself:

    1. Open a Wise account in the app
    2. Get the account details in euros (Wise's IBAN is Belgian, BE) or take the details for a SWIFT transfer
    3. Send money from Ukraine from a foreign currency card, within the monthly limit of the National Bank of Ukraine (NBU), about €2,000 (the NBU does not allow a transfer to yourself to this IBAN or via SWIFT); the Wise rate is usually better than at Ukrainian banks
    4. Wait for the money to arrive: usually 1–2 days
    5. Transfer the amount to your Spanish account or withdraw cash
    If you need an account at a Spanish bank and all you have is an international passport: we handle the opening. What we need from you: a passport, a residential address and one visit to the branch. The first review of your situation is free; we discuss the cost once we understand the scope.

    Conclusions

    Choose Wise if the main thing is receiving money from Ukraine and paying when travelling. Revolut if you need currency exchange and a convenient app. Skip N26 for Spain: its IBAN is Spanish, but it is harder for a non-resident to open an account than with Wise and Revolut.

    Open a Spanish account in parallel and without delay: without one you cannot be paid a salary, take out a mortgage or receive benefits.     Frequently asked questions

    Can a Spanish account be replaced with a neobank such as Wise or Revolut?
    No, not completely. Spanish employers and landlords often require a Spanish IBAN specifically, and they may not pay a salary into a neobank. We advise a combination: a Spanish bank plus a neobank.
    Do you need an NIE to open Wise or Revolut?
    Not to register: with Wise and Revolut a passport and a phone number are enough, but Revolut, as a bank in Spain, may ask for an NIE within 15 days of opening. This is the main advantage of neobanks for non-residents and Ukrainians who have not yet received an NIE.
    Which neobank is best for transfers from Ukraine?
    Wise is the most convenient for transfers from Ukraine to Spain: it does not support transfers from hryvnia (only to hryvnia), but it uses the real interbank rate and charges a transparent fee of 0.4–1%. Bear in mind the NBU limits on international transfers.
    How much does Revolut cost in Spain?
    Revolut's basic plan is free, Premium is €9.99 a month, Metal is €15.99 a month. A Spanish IBAN is given on all plans, including the free one.
