    How to revoke a power of attorney if you live in Spain: a Spanish one and a Ukrainian one

    Krepko · 24 November 2026 · ~8 min read
    In short. A Spanish power of attorney is revoked by a notarial deed — escritura de revocación de poder; the representative's consent is not needed (art. 1733 of the Spanish Civil Code), and the revocation can even be signed by video call, unless the power of attorney is a general preventive one. Revocation does not protect you against transactions with those who did not know about it (arts. 1734 and 1738), so the representative, the bank and the other party to the transaction are notified straight away. A Ukrainian power of attorney, except an irrevocable one, can also be cancelled at any time (Article 249 of the Civil Code of Ukraine), but the cancellation must be entered in the Unified Register of Powers of Attorney: from Spain this is done through the Ukrainian consulate. A Spanish notarial deed for Ukraine is apostilled — only on the paper original.

    A power of attorney to sell a flat, for a bank account or a general one is no longer needed: the deal fell through, relations with the representative have gone sour or you are handing the matter to someone else. As a rule, it can be revoked at any time and without the representative's consent. But the revocation still has to reach the bank, the buyer or the notary preparing the transaction: until they know about it, the representative may manage to sign something that binds you.

    Below we explain how a Spanish power of attorney is revoked, how to cancel a Ukrainian one if you live in Spain, and whom to notify so that the old document can no longer be used. The rules were checked against primary sources; the dates of checking and the versions used are in the list of sources.

    Can a power of attorney be revoked without the representative's consent?

    Yes, both in Spain and in Ukraine this decision belongs to the principal alone.

    • Spain. The principal revokes the power of attorney at their own will and may demand that the representative return the document (art. 1733 of the Spanish Civil Code). Revocation is the first on the list of grounds on which representation ends; the same list includes the representative's renunciation and the death of either of them (art. 1732). If a new representative is appointed for the same matter, the previous power of attorney is deemed revoked from the day the previous representative was informed of this (art. 1735).
    • Ukraine. A power of attorney can be cancelled at any time, except an irrevocable one, and a waiver of this right is void (Article 249 of the Civil Code of Ukraine). Irrevocable powers of attorney are issued for a fixed term and only where the law allows it (ibid., part 4). Once representation has ended, the representative must return the power of attorney immediately (Article 248, part 3).

    A term written into the power of attorney does not prevent revocation: it can be cancelled even before that term expires.

    How do you revoke a Spanish power of attorney?

    A Spanish notarial power of attorney is an escritura (notarial deed) in the notary's protocol (official archive): the original (matriz) stays with the notary, and the representative acts on the copy issued to them. It is revoked by a notarial deed too.

    1. Deed of revocation. You will need an identity document with a photo and the details of the power of attorney: the notary, the date, the number in the protocol. A foreign national living in Spain proves their identity with a passport or a Spanish residence permit (Reglamento Notarial, art. 161).
    2. Possible by video call. The Notarial Act allows the revocation of powers of attorney to be authorised by videoconference, except for general preventive powers of attorney (art. 17 ter.1.d). Access is through the notaries' electronic platform with electronic identification (ibid., part 2).
    3. Note on the matriz. A document that cancels an earlier escritura takes effect against third parties when it is noted in the competent public register or in the margin of the matriz and of the copy on which the third party acted (art. 1219 of the Civil Code). So when signing, ask the notary how the note will appear on the matriz of the power of attorney.
    4. The representative's copy. The copy held by the representative will not receive the note by itself. That is why its return is demanded (art. 1733) and, in parallel, those to whom the representative may present it are notified.

    Whom should you notify of the revocation, and what do you risk if you stay silent?

    A signed revocation does not yet mean that everyone who may be presented with the power of attorney knows about it. The law protects those who did not know about the revocation:

    • if the power of attorney was granted for dealings with specific persons, the revocation does not affect them until they have been informed of it (art. 1734 of the Spanish Civil Code);
    • what the representative did without knowing that the power of attorney had ended is valid for third parties who entered into a transaction with them in good faith (art. 1738);
    • in Ukraine the principal must immediately notify the representative and the third parties known to the principal before whom the representative was to act, and transactions concluded before the representative learned or could have learned of the cancellation bind the principal if the third party did not know and could not have known that the power of attorney had ended (Article 249 of the Civil Code of Ukraine, parts 2–3).

    As a rule, we notify the representative themselves; the banks where they could have operated the account; the buyer, seller or tenant, if a transaction had already been discussed; the notary with whom a signing is scheduled. How to do this with proof is set out in the Reglamento Notarial: the notary draws up a notarial record of notification (acta de notificación) and sends it by registered post with acknowledgement of receipt or delivers it in person at the given address (art. 202); the addressee has two working days to reply in the same record (art. 204). The notary may also accept urgent notifications, including of the revocation of a power of attorney, on the basis of a letter with an authenticated signature (art. 205).

    How do you cancel a Ukrainian power of attorney while in Spain?

    A Ukrainian notarial power of attorney is entered in the Unified Register of Powers of Attorney. The termination of powers of attorney must be registered in it (Procedure for Performing Notarial Acts, Section II, Chapter 4, para. 7), and a notary in Ukraine checks the validity of a Ukrainian notarial power of attorney precisely against the register (Law “On Notaries”, Article 44). Until the cancellation is in the register, such a check shows the power of attorney as valid.

    • Who enters the cancellation. The registrars of the register are notaries of Ukraine, as well as diplomatic and consular missions of Ukraine with access to the register; the application to terminate the power of attorney is submitted by the principal (Regulation on the Unified Register of Powers of Attorney, paras. 1.5 and 2.3).
    • What a notary in Ukraine does. On receiving an application for cancellation, the notary makes a note on the copy of the power of attorney in their files and in the register of notarial acts, and if you have brought your own copy, on that too (Procedure, Section II, Chapter 4, paras. 6.2–6.3). The notary can notify the representative and third parties by passing them the relevant application (para. 6.4).
    • The consulate in Spain. According to the explanation of the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine, a consul who has access to the register through the Ministry of Justice network or through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs registers the cancellation of the power of attorney in the register themselves. The consul will also attest the signature on the application (Law “On Notaries”, Article 38). Ask whether your consulate has access when booking an appointment.

    Men aged 18 to 60 also need a military registration document for consular acts under martial law (Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 981, Verification Procedure, para. 3); more on this in the article about where to arrange a document for Ukraine.

    And a Spanish notary? Neither the regulation on the register nor the explanations of the Ministry of Justice describe how a cancellation of a Ukrainian power of attorney drawn up before a foreign notary is entered in the register. So the confirmed route from Spain is the consulate. If you cannot get there, ask a notary in Ukraine in advance whether they will accept such a document for entry in the register.

    How do you revoke a Spanish power of attorney that is used in Ukraine?

    A power of attorney signed before a Spanish notary for a transaction in Ukraine is revoked in the same way as any Spanish one: by a deed of revocation before a notary in Spain. An important difference: powers of attorney certified by authorities of foreign states, including by a Spanish notary, are not checked by a notary in Ukraine against the Unified Register (Procedure, Section I, Chapter 4, para. 4). This means the register will show nothing about the revocation, and in Ukraine you need to notify directly: the representative, the notary preparing the transaction, the bank.

    • Apostille. A Ukrainian notary accepts documents from abroad with legalisation, or without it where an international treaty provides for this (Law “On Notaries”, Article 100). Spain and Ukraine are parties to the Hague Apostille Convention, so the Spanish deed of revocation is apostilled. The apostille is issued by the dean of the notarial association or a notary to whom the dean has delegated this (RD 1497/2011, art. 3), and only on the paper original (Orden JUS/1207/2011, art. 11).
    • Translation. For a power of attorney from a foreign notary, the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine specifies a translation into Ukrainian, notarised once the document is in Ukraine. Check with whoever will be accepting the deed of revocation which translation it needs.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can a power of attorney be revoked if the representative objects?
    Yes. In Spain the principal revokes the power of attorney at their own will and may demand the return of the document (art. 1733 of the Spanish Civil Code). In Ukraine a power of attorney, except an irrevocable one, can be cancelled at any time, and a waiver of this right is void (Article 249 of the Civil Code of Ukraine). The representative's consent is not needed; they are only notified.
    From what moment does a revoked power of attorney stop being effective for a bank or a buyer?
    For them, what matters is whether they knew about the revocation. In Spain a transaction by a representative who did not know that the power of attorney had ended is valid for third parties acting in good faith (art. 1738 of the Spanish Civil Code), and the revocation does not affect those for dealings with whom the power of attorney was granted until they have been informed (art. 1734). So the bank, the buyer and the notary with whom the transaction is scheduled are notified straight away, preferably through a notarial record of notification.
    Can a Spanish power of attorney be revoked without going to the notary?
    The Notarial Act allows the revocation of powers of attorney to be authorised by videoconference, except for general preventive powers of attorney (art. 17 ter.1.d). Access is through the notaries' electronic platform with electronic identification. The note of revocation on the matriz of the power of attorney and the notification of the representative are still worth checking separately.
    How do I cancel a Ukrainian power of attorney if I live in Spain?
    Through the Ukrainian consulate: according to the explanation of the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine, a consul with access to the Unified Register of Powers of Attorney registers the cancellation in the register themselves. Check whether your consulate has access when booking an appointment. Men aged 18 to 60 need a military registration document for consular acts under martial law (Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 981). After the cancellation, immediately notify the representative and the third parties known to you (Article 249 of the Civil Code of Ukraine).
    Will a revocation before a Spanish notary work for a Ukrainian power of attorney?
    Neither the regulation on the Unified Register of Powers of Attorney nor the explanations of the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine describe how to enter in the register a cancellation drawn up before a foreign notary. The registrars of the register are notaries of Ukraine and consular missions with access to it, so the confirmed route from Spain is the consulate. If you cannot get there, ask a notary in Ukraine in advance whether they will accept such a document.
    Does a Spanish deed of revocation need an apostille to be shown in Ukraine?
    Yes. A Ukrainian notary accepts documents from abroad with legalisation or under an international treaty (Law “On Notaries”, Article 100), and Spain and Ukraine are parties to the Apostille Convention. The apostille on a notarial document is issued by the dean of the notarial association or a notary authorised by the dean, and only on the paper original (RD 1497/2011, art. 3; Orden JUS/1207/2011, art. 11). Check with whoever will accept the document which translation is needed.
