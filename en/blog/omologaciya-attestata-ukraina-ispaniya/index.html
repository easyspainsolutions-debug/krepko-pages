    Homologation of a Ukrainian school-leaving certificate in Spain: how to apply and what the volante gives you

    Krepko · 24 November 2026 · ~8 min read
    In short. A Ukrainian certificate of complete general secondary education (11 grades) is recognised as equivalent to the Spanish título de Bachiller under Real Decreto 104/1988; the new RD 889/2022 only concerns university degrees. You need the marks for grades 9, 10 and 11, an apostille from the Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine (MON) on each document and an official translation into Spanish. The fee, paid on Modelo 790 with code 079, is €49.76 in 2026. A volante is submitted together with the application: for six months from the date of registration it lets you enrol conditionally at an educational institution or for an exam without waiting for the decision.

    Your child has finished school in Ukraine or is completing it remotely, and plans to continue studying in Spain. For a Spanish university or exam to accept a Ukrainian school-leaving certificate, it is recognised as equivalent to the Spanish título de Bachiller: this procedure is called homologación.

    Below we explain which rule governs it, how Ukrainian school years correspond to Spanish ones according to the Ministry of Education's information sheet, which documents to gather, how much the fee is and how to enrol with a volante de inscripción condicional while there is still no decision. Everything was checked against the BOE and the ministry's website on 24 November 2026.

    Which law applies: RD 104/1988 or RD 889/2022?

    Two numbers come up in second-hand accounts, and they must not be confused. Recognition of school (non-university) education is governed by Real Decreto 104/1988 of 29 January 1988. The BOE publishes it as a consolidated text in force, last amended on 30 March 2022. The details of the procedure are set by the ministry's order of 14 March 1988 as amended by Orden ECD/3305/2002: among other things, the rules on the volante come from it. Real Decreto 889/2022 of 18 October 2022 governs the homologation and equivalence of university degrees and has nothing to do with the school-leaving certificate.

    Under RD 104/1988, homologation means that a foreign document is recognised as equivalent to a current Spanish qualification (título). There is also a second procedure — convalidación: crediting foreign years of study against Spanish courses so as to continue studying at a Spanish school (art. 3). To go on to further study after finishing school, what you need is the título de Bachiller, that is, homologation.

    If it is a degree from a Ukrainian university, that is a different procedure and a different law; on the apostille for a diploma, see the page “Diploma with an apostille from Ukraine”.

    Which Spanish school years does Ukrainian school correspond to?

    The Spanish Ministry of Education (Subdirección General de Ordenación Académica) has a separate information sheet on Ukraine with a table of equivalences. The key point: grade 11 of Ukrainian school covers both years of the Spanish Bachillerato, so a twelfth year of study is not needed.

    Ukrainian school Spanish system What to apply for
    Grades 1–9 Primary school and 1º–3º ESO Nothing: up to and including 4º ESO, a child is admitted to school without homologation
    Grade 10 4º ESO Homologation to the título de Graduado en ESO (no fee)
    Grade 11 1º and 2º Bachillerato Homologation to the título de Bachiller (fee €49.76)

    What the final document is called depends on the year of graduation. Until 2019 it was the “Атестат про повну загальну середню освіту” (certificate of complete general secondary education); from 2019 it is the “Свідоцтво про здобуття повної загальної середньої освіти” (certificate of attainment of complete general secondary education). The ministry accepts both as documents for homologation to the Bachiller. The same information sheet gives two Ukrainian grading scales: a 5-point scale (the minimum pass mark is 3) and a 12-point scale (the minimum pass mark is 4). If the scale is stated in the document itself, the average mark is calculated on that scale.

    If the child is still at school and is moving to a Spanish school, the procedure is different, and up to 4º ESO there is none at all. How school enrolment works is covered in our article on enrolling a child in school in Spain.

    Which documents are needed?

    The list is set by the procedure page on the ministry's website and the information sheet on Ukraine:

    • Application and volante. Both are generated in the ministry's sede electrónica, printed out and signed by the applicant or their legal representative.
    • Proof of payment of fee 079. Without it the case does not move: the ministry will request payment, and if it is not made, it will consider that the applicant has withdrawn the application.
    • Identity document. A copy of your passport or NIE; you need not attach a copy of your NIF if in the application you consent to your data being checked through the verification system.
    • The school-leaving certificate (atestat or svidotstvo) of complete general secondary education.
    • Marks for the final grade and the two before it — for Ukrainian school, these are grades 9, 10 and 11: subjects, marks and academic years. All these marks must be confirmed by official documents.
    • Power of attorney, if a representative applies: the applicant's written authorisation and the representative's identity document.

    The ministry may also request other documents if it considers that without them the equivalence of the curricula is not clear. You submit not the originals but copies checked against the original (copia verificada or compulsada) — usually at the registry when you apply. The copy is made only once the original already bears the apostille.

    Which apostille and which translation do you need?

    The ministry accepts educational documents from a country outside the EU and the European Economic Area only if they are legalised, and for parties to the Hague Convention, including Ukraine, an apostille is enough. What is legalised is the signature of the education authority on the original, not the certification of a copy or a translation.

    In Ukraine, the apostille on educational documents is affixed by the Ministry of Education and Science (Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 61 of 18 January 2003). Documents for this are accepted by the MON's state enterprise “Інформаційно-іміджевий центр” (Information and Image Centre); you can submit them without visiting in person. The centre specifically warns: a notarial affidavit with an apostille from the Ministry of Justice does not replace the MON apostille on an educational document.

    The translation must be official, into Spanish (castellano). The ministry accepts four options:

    • a sworn translator (traductor jurado) authorised in Spain;
    • a Spanish diplomatic or consular mission abroad;
    • the embassy or consulate in Spain of the country of which you are a national or which issued the document;
    • an official translator abroad, if their signature is legalised.

    The timeframe and cost of a sworn translation depend on the volume, so they are given once the documents are in hand.

    How much is fee 079 and how do you pay it?

    The fee is paid to open the case, on Modelo 790 with code 079. The 2026 form issued by the ministry's sede gives these amounts (checked on 24 November 2026):

    • homologation to the título de Bachiller — €49.76;
    • convalidación of individual courses (for example, 1º Bachillerato) — €24.88;
    • homologation to Graduado en ESO — no fee.

    In Spain, the form is paid at a bank that takes part in tax collection (entidad colaboradora; this is almost all banks), in cash or by direct charge to your account. The bank keeps the third copy; the first and second, stamped as paid, are submitted together with the application. From abroad, you pay by transfer to the Treasury's account at the Bank of Spain, with the details given on the fee page; bank charges must not reduce the amount that reaches the account. The form is downloaded afresh each time: each one has its own number, and photocopies are not accepted.

    Where to apply and who decides?

    Applications are open all year round. The application is filled in in the ministry's sede electrónica, printed out together with the volante and submitted with the documents to an official registry: the ministry's general registry in Madrid, the registry of a Delegación or Subdelegación del Gobierno, the registry of a Spanish embassy or consulate abroad, or any other registry under art. 16.4 of Law 39/2015. The decision takes the form of a ministerial order, and you are handed an individual credencial: it has the same effect as a Spanish título de Bachiller.

    If the application gives an address in Catalonia, Galicia or the Basque Country, homologation of school documents is handled by that region's education authority, not the ministry.

    How to enrol with a volante while there is no decision?

    The volante de inscripción condicional is a document on the form in Annex II of Orden ECD/3305/2002, signed by the applicant or their legal representative. The basis is art. 15.2 of RD 104/1988: educational institutions may conditionally admit those whose homologation case is open and still undecided at the end of the admission period. For the volante to work, it must bear the stamp of the registry where the application was submitted.

    • Validity. For conditional enrolment — six months from the date of the registry stamp.
    • After enrolment. The volante is valid only in the academic year in which you enrolled with it.
    • What it gives you. You can enrol at an educational institution or for official exams as if you already had the homologation, but conditionally.
    • Risk. The volante is issued on the applicant's own responsibility and does not prejudge the outcome. If homologation is not granted in the form in which it was requested, the conditional enrolment and the results of exams taken on its basis are cancelled.

    Hence the practical conclusion: apply so that the six months of the volante's validity cover the admission period of the university you need or the exam. Each Spanish university has its own admission rules. For foreign applicants, the ministry refers to UNEDasiss — a UNED service that helps people gain access to Spanish universities; its requirements and deadlines need to be checked separately.

    Can you start without documents if you have temporary protection?

    In 2022 a transitional provision was added to RD 104/1988 for those who have received temporary protection (RD 1325/2003, arts. 2 and 11). As an exception, in order to start the procedure, such an applicant may replace the documents with a declaration (declaración responsable) on the ministry's form, valid for a limited time. While it is valid, the time limits for correcting deficiencies and for closing the case under art. 8 do not apply.

    The declaration does not replace the documents for good: before the decision, you must submit the documents required by the homologation authority, that is, the same certificate with an apostille and a translation. This route helps you open a case if the certificate was left behind in Ukraine, but it does not exempt you from the apostille.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which law governs the homologation of a school-leaving certificate in Spain?
    Real Decreto 104/1988 of 29 January 1988, with the details in the ministry's order of 14 March 1988 as amended by Orden ECD/3305/2002. Real Decreto 889/2022 only concerns university degrees. The decision is made by the Ministry of Education, or, for an address in Catalonia, Galicia or the Basque Country, by the region's education authority.
    Are 11 grades of Ukrainian school recognised as equivalent to the Spanish Bachillerato?
    Yes. According to the table of equivalences published by the Spanish Ministry of Education, grade 11 of Ukrainian school corresponds to 1º and 2º Bachillerato, and the Ukrainian school-leaving certificate (from 2019, the certificate of complete general secondary education) is homologated to the título de Bachiller. The application requires the marks for grades 9, 10 and 11.
    How much does homologation of a school-leaving certificate cost in Spain?
    The state fee on Modelo 790 with code 079 in 2026 is €49.76 for homologation to the título de Bachiller (according to the form from the ministry's sede). The MON apostille in Ukraine and the official translation into Spanish are paid for separately, if you do not have them yet.
    What is a volante de inscripción condicional and how long is it valid?
    It is a document submitted together with the homologation application; once stamped by the registry, it lets you enrol conditionally at an educational institution or for an official exam without waiting for the decision. For enrolment it is valid for six months from the date of the stamp, and after enrolment only in that academic year. If homologation is refused, the enrolment and the exam results are cancelled.
    Does a Ukrainian school-leaving certificate need an apostille for Spain?
    Yes. The ministry accepts documents from countries outside the EU and the EEA in legalised form, and for Ukraine an apostille is enough. On educational documents it is affixed by the MON of Ukraine; documents are accepted by the state enterprise Information and Image Centre, and you can submit them without visiting in person. A notarial affidavit with an apostille from the Ministry of Justice does not replace the MON apostille.
    Can you apply for homologation without the original certificate if it was left behind in Ukraine?
    RD 104/1988 allows holders of temporary protection to start the procedure with a declaration (declaración responsable) on the ministry's form instead of the documents. But before the decision you still have to submit the certificate and marks with an apostille and an official translation, so it is worth ordering them straight away.
