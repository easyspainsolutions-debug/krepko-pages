    Spouse's consent to sell a flat in Ukraine: how to arrange it in Spain

    Krepko · 1 November 2026 · ~7 min read
    In short. Since late May 2026 the other spouse's consent to the sale of a flat or house in Ukraine must be notarially certified (Family Code of Ukraine, Article 65, part 3, as amended by Law No. 4824-IX); witnessing the signature alone is not enough under the new rule. From Spain, the consent is drawn up before a Spanish notary as a full notarial document, an apostille from the notarial association is added, and the paper original is sent to Ukraine. Consent is not needed if the documents show that the property belongs personally to one of the spouses, for example if it was bought before the marriage or inherited. The notary certifies the consent; we prepare the text with the details the Ukrainian notary expects and accompany you at the signing; we check the requirements of the notary in Ukraine before the appointment. The rules were checked on 1 November 2026.

    A flat in Ukraine was bought during the marriage and registered in the name of one spouse, while the family lives in Spain. To sell it, the other spouse has to take part as well: a notary in Ukraine will certify the contract only if presented with a notarially certified consent. Since late May 2026 the law specifically requires certification, and the former option, where the notary merely witnessed the signature on a statement, is not enough for this.

    We look at when consent is needed, what to write in it, how to arrange it before a Spanish notary and how to sign it together with a power of attorney to sell in a single visit. Separately: what to bear in mind if you are selling a car.

    What changed in 2026

    Under the Family Code of Ukraine, spouses dispose of joint property by mutual consent (Article 65). If one of them enters into a contract that requires notarial certification or state registration, the other's consent is given in writing. The sale of a flat or house is exactly such a contract: it is subject to notarial certification (Civil Code of Ukraine, Article 657). Law No. 4824-IX of 25 March 2026 amended this rule: previously the consent had to be notarially “witnessed”, now it must be notarially “certified”. The requirement has applied since late May 2026.

    For a notary these are different acts. By witnessing a signature, the notary confirms only that it was made by this person and does not certify what the document says. On documents that by law are subject to notarial certification, a signature is not witnessed at all (Law of Ukraine “On Notaries”, Article 78). In June 2026 the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine added to the Procedure for performing notarial acts a separate point on certifying consent to a transaction, with a list of mandatory details (Order No. 1483/5).

    Before a Spanish notary, a consent for Ukraine is drawn up as a full notarial document: the notary establishes the identity and checks the legal capacity of the person giving consent and that the consent is given freely. We prepare such a consent as part of the service “Spouse's consent before a notary”: the notary certifies it, we prepare the text and accompany you at the signing.

    When consent is needed and when it is not

    Property acquired during the marriage belongs to the spouses jointly by default, even if one of them had no earnings of their own for a valid reason (Family Code of Ukraine, Article 60). What matters next is in whose name the ownership is registered.

    • The flat is registered to one spouse. The contract is signed by that spouse or their representative, and the other spouse gives a notarially certified consent (Procedure for performing notarial acts, Section II, Chapter 1, point 4.2). This is the case we look at here.
    • The flat is registered to both. The contract is signed by all co-owners or their representatives (ibid., point 4.1). So the spouse in Spain will need not a consent but a power of attorney to sell.
    • The property is personal. Property acquired before the marriage, received as a gift or inheritance, bought with personal money, or privatised housing belongs to one spouse (Family Code, Article 57). Consent is not needed, but the notary must be able to see this from the documents: the document of title, an agreement between the spouses, the marriage certificate (point 4.4).
    • The marriage has been dissolved. Divorce does not end joint ownership of what was acquired during the marriage (Family Code, Article 68). If the property has not been divided, the sale requires the written, notarially certified consent of the former spouse as a co-owner (Civil Code of Ukraine, Article 369).

    If the seller has no husband or wife, the notary will ask for their statement that the flat is not jointly owned. When a representative is selling, the right to make such a statement must be written into the power of attorney (point 4.5). If the flat was inherited and the inheritance has not yet been formalised, you need to start with that: we explain how to do it from Spain in our article on inheritance in Ukraine.

    What the text of the consent must contain

    What goes into a consent certified by a Ukrainian notary is now set out in the Procedure (Section II, Chapter 5, point 5). We prepare a consent from Spain with the same details:

    • the place and date of signing;
    • details of the spouse giving consent: full name, date of birth, taxpayer identification number (RNOKPP) and the record number in the demographic register (UNZR), if they have them, passport series and number, place of residence;
    • details of the spouse to whom consent is given: full name, RNOKPP and UNZR, if they have them;
    • a clear description of the actions, for example the sale of a flat at a specific address;
    • the period of validity, if one is stated, in words.

    The buyer and the price do not have to be written in. But if they are named, the notary in Ukraine is obliged to check that the contract matches them (point 4.2). Such conditions protect the person giving consent, but if the buyer or the price changes, the consent will have to be drawn up again. If no buyer has been found yet, it is enough to describe the flat and the action, the sale, precisely.

    We collect the details in advance and check them against the flat's documents so that the consent is not sent back because of a mistake in a code or an address.

    How to arrange the consent in Spain: step by step

    1. A text for the specific transaction
    We will need the details of both spouses and the documents for the flat. We prepare the text in two columns, in Spanish and in the language you need: at a foreigner's request, a Spanish notary can draw up the document in this form (Reglamento Notarial, Article 150). For Ukraine, Ukrainian is more convenient. It is worth showing the draft in advance to the notary in Ukraine who will certify the contract: that way their requirements for the text are known before the signing.
    2. Signing before a Spanish notary
    In person, with a valid passport or TIE. Your part takes about 30 minutes; a Krepko representative accompanies you at the signing and translates every clause. If your Ukrainian passport has expired, first read our guide to this situation.
    3. Apostille
    On notarial documents, the apostille is added by the notarial association (Real Decreto 1497/2011, Article 3). Spain and Ukraine are both parties to the 1961 Hague Convention, so consular legalisation is not needed.
    4. Translation, if requested
    An apostille does not translate the text. If the notary in Ukraine asks for a translation, as a rule it is done in Ukraine and certified by a Ukrainian notary. If necessary, the Krepko team in Ukraine arranges this.
    5. The original goes to Ukraine
    The Ukrainian side will not accept a scan: the paper original with the apostille is needed. It is sent by courier with a tracking number to whoever is handling the transaction.
    Through the Ukrainian consulate. A consul can also certify a consent to a transaction: such a point was added to the Regulation on notarial acts in consulates in August 2026 (point 3.20). The document is Ukrainian from the outset and no apostille is needed, but you have to sign it in person at the consulate, by appointment and with a Ukrainian passport. Men aged 18–60 currently need a valid electronic military registration document with confirmed data for consular services (Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 981 of 29 July 2026).

    Consent and power of attorney in a single visit

    If both spouses live in Spain and the transaction in Ukraine is handled by a relative or acquaintance, two documents are needed, and it is convenient to sign them before the same notary on the same day:

    • the owner spouse grants a power of attorney to sell to a representative in Ukraine;
    • the other spouse gives consent to that sale.

    We prepare the texts together so that the property and the actions in the power of attorney and the consent match. We arrange the apostille on both documents, and they go to Ukraine in a single shipment.

    If the owner is in Ukraine and signs the contract in person, only the consent is needed from Spain. If it is the other way round, the owner grants a power of attorney here, and the other spouse has the consent certified by a notary in Ukraine. The general rules on a power of attorney for Ukraine are collected in our article on a power of attorney from Spain.

    If you are selling a car

    For a car bought during the marriage, the same Article 65 applies: consent to a contract involving valuable property is given in writing, and it must be notarially certified if the contract requires notarial certification or state registration. If the car is sold through a service centre of the Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVS), it is better to find out before signing which form of consent they will ask for.

    A power of attorney for a car has its own limitation. A representative cannot conclude a transaction on your behalf in their own interest (Civil Code of Ukraine, Article 238, part 3), and the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine specifically explains that you cannot re-register a car to yourself under a power of attorney. If the car is being bought by the person you wanted to entrust with the sale, you will need another representative or your own signature at the service centre.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I give consent to the sale of a flat in Ukraine without leaving Spain?
    Yes. The consent is drawn up before a Spanish notary, an apostille from the notarial association is added to the document, and the paper original is sent to Ukraine. We prepare the text to meet the requirements of the notary in Ukraine and accompany you at the signing. The other route is the Ukrainian consulate: there the consent is signed in person, by appointment and with a Ukrainian passport, and men aged 18–60 also need a valid electronic military registration document with confirmed data.
    The flat was bought before the marriage. Do I need my wife's consent to sell it?
    As a rule, no. Property acquired before the marriage is personal property (Family Code of Ukraine, Article 57). The notary in Ukraine must be able to see this from the documents: the document of title and the marriage certificate.
    We are divorced. Do I need my ex-husband's consent?
    If the flat was bought during the marriage and has not been divided, yes. Divorce does not end joint ownership (Family Code of Ukraine, Article 68), and selling joint property requires the notarially certified consent of the co-owner (Civil Code of Ukraine, Article 369).
    I have a consent on which a notary witnessed the signature. Will it do?
    Since late May 2026 the law requires a notarially certified consent, and witnessing a signature confirms only the signature (Law of Ukraine “On Notaries”, Article 78). It is best to ask the notary before the transaction how they will treat a consent drawn up earlier. If the transaction is still ahead, a new consent under the current rules removes the question.
    Do the buyer and the amount have to be written into the consent?
    Not necessarily. If the buyer or the price is named, the notary in Ukraine will check that the contract matches them, and if the buyer or the price changes, the consent will have to be drawn up again. As long as no buyer has been found, it is enough to describe the flat and the action, the sale, precisely.
    Can the consent and the power of attorney be signed on the same day?
    Yes. If both spouses are in Spain, the owner signs the power of attorney to sell and the other spouse signs the consent, before the same notary in a single visit. We prepare the texts together so that the property and the actions match, and arrange the apostille on both documents.
