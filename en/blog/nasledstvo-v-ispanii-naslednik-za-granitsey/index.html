    Inheritance in Spain when the heir lives abroad: what to arrange and by when

    Krepko · 18 November 2026 · ~8 min read
    In short. An inheritance in Spain can be arranged from abroad, without travelling: the case is handled by a representative under a power of attorney. The heir needs an NIE and a power of attorney; one drawn up in another country needs an apostille and a translation. The documents gathered relating to the deceased are the death certificate, a certificate from the Registry of Wills and the will or a notarial deed declaring the heirs. The inheritance tax return is filed within six months of the date of death; an extension of another six months is requested within the first five months (Arts. 67–68 RD 1629/1991).     What you can do without travelling to Spain

    Certificates, signing before the notary, the tax, the registry and the bank can be entrusted to a representative under a power of attorney. This is how representing heirs works: we become your point of contact for the inheritance case in Spain.

    A notarial deed is needed almost always: to register real estate, the shares and assets are determined in a notarial deed or a court decision; the exception is a sole heir, if nobody has a right to a forced share (Art. 14 of the Mortgage Law, Ley Hipotecaria). Renouncing an inheritance is also done before a notary in a public document (Art. 1008 of the Civil Code, hereinafter CC).

    What is needed from the heir: NIE and a power of attorney

    NIE. Every heir without a Spanish DNI needs a tax identification number; for a foreigner this is, as a rule, the NIE (foreigner identity number). A notarial deed on real estate states the tax numbers of the parties and of those they represent (Art. 23 of the Notaries Act, Ley del Notariado); otherwise the deed will not be registered (Art. 254 of the Mortgage Law). The NIE is requested in Spain or through the consular section of Spain in the country where you live, in person or through a representative whose power of attorney expressly names this right.

    Power of attorney. A power of attorney for an act that is executed as a notarial deed must itself be a public document (Art. 1280 CC). General wording about representation only gives the right to manage property (Art. 1713), so the powers are listed: to accept the inheritance or renounce it, to sign the partition, to obtain an NIE, to file the return and pay the tax, to represent you before the tax office, to register the property. Each heir arranges their own power of attorney for an inheritance; all of them can be granted to the same representative.

    You can sign it before a notary in Spain or in your own country. A Spanish notary accepts a foreign power of attorney if three conditions are met:

    • apostille: Spain and Ukraine are parties to the 1961 Hague Convention;
    • translation into Spanish: we choose the type of translation to suit the authority that accepts the document;
    • equivalence: the foreign authority performs the same functions as a Spanish one (Art. 60 Ley 29/2015); whether the powers are sufficient is assessed by the Spanish notary (Art. 98 Ley 24/2001), so the text is agreed with them in advance.
    If you are in Ukraine. The power of attorney is drawn up before a Ukrainian notary, the apostille on notarial documents is affixed by the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine, and then a translation into Spanish is made; whether the powers are sufficient is decided by the Spanish notary, so the text is agreed with them in advance. The second route is the consular section of the embassy: the consul performs the functions of a notary, the text is sent in advance in Spanish, it is signed in person, and a foreigner needs an NIE. If you do not speak Spanish, the embassy requires you to come with an official or sworn interpreter. Because of the war, the section deals primarily with urgent matters.

    Which documents relating to the deceased are gathered

    Document Where to get it What to bear in mind
    Death certificate The Civil Registry (Registro Civil) in Spain; if the person died in another country, the authority of that country If issued outside the EU, for example in Ukraine, with an apostille and a translation
    Certificate from the Registry of Wills (certificado de actos de última voluntad) The Spanish Ministry of Justice, fee paid with Modelo 790, code 006 The request can be made no earlier than 15 working days after the death. The tax office requires the Spanish certificate, even a negative one
    Will The notary who certified it: they are named in the certificate A copy may be obtained by those who may be entitled to the inheritance
    Deed declaring the heirs (declaración de herederos abintestato), if there is no will A notary at the deceased's last place of residence, the place where most of the property is or the place of death in Spain; otherwise, at the applicant's place of residence Documents proving the family relationship, information from the Registro Civil and the Registry of Wills, two witnesses; the conclusion is issued no earlier than 20 working days after the application (Arts. 55–56 of the Notaries Act)

    For the state tax agency (AEAT), the translation requirement is stated expressly: according to its guidance, documents from foreign authorities are submitted with the inheritance tax return with an apostille and a sworn translation (traducción jurada); the exception is documents from EU countries with a multilingual standard form. For the notary and the bank, we choose the type of translation to suit the authority that accepts the document.

    Birth and marriage certificates proving the family relationship are also submitted with an apostille and a translation; we explained how to obtain Ukrainian ones in the article on the birth certificate.

    Which law governs the division of the inheritance

    For the estates of those who died on or after 17 August 2015, Spain applies EU Regulation No. 650/2012 (Art. 83). The whole inheritance is governed by the law of the country where the deceased habitually lived on the date of death, even if that country is outside the European Union (Arts. 20–21). The estate of a Ukrainian citizen who lived in Spain is, as a general rule, divided under Spanish law, including the forced shares; in regions with their own civil law, for example Catalonia, the law of the region may apply (Art. 36). The exception is a will choosing the law of the country of citizenship (Art. 22).

    If the deceased lived outside the EU, for example in Ukraine, the conflict-of-laws rules of that country are also taken into account (Art. 34), and the content of the foreign law is proven, for example, by an opinion from a notary or consul (Art. 36 of the Mortgage Regulation).

    The procedure before a Spanish notary and the deadlines

    The order: certificates, determination of the heirs by will or notarial deed, the deed of acceptance and partition of the inheritance (aceptación y adjudicación de herencia), then taxes, the registry and the bank. If the partition is made by the heirs themselves, the deed is signed by all of them or their representatives (Art. 1058 CC).

    The tax does not wait for the notarial deed. If the deed cannot be signed within six months, the state tax agency accepts a return with an inventory of assets and heirs (inventario de bienes y herederos), and after the deed is signed a new return is filed.

    What to do Deadline Rule
    File the inheritance tax return (Impuesto sobre Sucesiones y Donaciones) 6 months from the date of death Art. 67 RD 1629/1991
    Request an extension of the deadline Within the first 5 months; the extension is for 6 months, with interest Art. 68 RD 1629/1991
    File the return for the municipal tax on the increase in land value (plusvalía municipal) for inherited urban property 6 months; on request, up to a year Art. 110 of the Local Finance Act
    Respond to a notarial demand, if a creditor or another interested party has requested it: accept the inheritance without reservation, accept it with an inventory of assets (a beneficio de inventario) or renounce it 30 calendar days; silence is treated as acceptance without reservation, and you are then liable for the deceased's debts with your own assets as well Arts. 1003, 1005 CC

    A non-resident heir pays tax only on the property in Spain (Art. 7 Ley 29/1987) and files a tax self-assessment (autoliquidación) on Modelo 650 with the state tax agency, having already paid the tax if any is due; the Basque Country and Navarre have their own procedure. The rules and reliefs of the autonomous community where the deceased lived apply, and if they did not live in Spain, those of the community where the most valuable part of the property is located (additional provision 2 Ley 29/1987). An heir living outside the European Union, for example in Ukraine, must appoint a representative resident in Spain before the filing deadline expires (Art. 18 RD 1629/1991; there are exceptions for countries of the European Economic Area). The representative accepts the appointment in writing, and the powers cover all obligations relating to this tax. The tax can be paid from the inheritance money: the bank may issue a cheque from the deceased's account payable to the tax office, and this is not treated as a distribution of the inheritance (Art. 8 Ley 29/1987).

    If deadlines are missed. For a return filed after the deadline without a demand from the tax office, a surcharge is applied: 1% plus 1% for each full month of delay, after 12 months 15% plus interest (Art. 27 Ley 58/2003). Until payment of the tax or exemption from it is confirmed, the bank does not release money from the account and the registry does not register the real estate (Art. 32 Ley 29/1987, Art. 254 of the Mortgage Law).

    What we do

    We represent heirs in Spain before the notary, banks, the tax office, registries and co-heirs. We study the composition of the estate, who the heirs are and the documents available, and clarify the notary's requirements. We prepare the text of the power of attorney for each heir and agree the scope of the powers. Then we gather the documents, sign the deed of acceptance under the power of attorney, pay the tax from your funds and register the property in the Land Registry. If there is no NIE, we arrange for it to be obtained. The deeds are certified by the notary.

    If the co-heirs have not agreed on the partition and the deceased did not appoint a partition executor, heirs holding at least half of the inheritance may ask a notary to appoint one (contador-partidor dativo, Art. 1057 CC). Otherwise the dispute is resolved in court through a lawyer, and we will tell you honestly when this is the route you need.

    Frequently asked questions

    My father died in Spain, and I live in Ukraine and cannot come. Can the inheritance be arranged without me?
    Yes. All the steps (certificates, the deed of acceptance of the inheritance before a notary, the tax, registering the flat and dealing with the bank) can be taken on by a representative under a power of attorney. The case can be handled under a power of attorney that you arrange before a notary in Ukraine, without leaving the country: it is apostilled and translated into Spanish. The powers in it are listed expressly, including the right to accept the inheritance or renounce it: general wording only gives the right to manage property (Art. 1713 of the Spanish Civil Code).
    The notary is asking for an NIE, and I do not have one. How do I get an NIE if I am not in Spain?
    The NIE is requested in Spain or through the consular section of Spain in the country where you live, in person or through a representative with a power of attorney that expressly names the right to apply for an NIE. In practice, according to the Embassy of Spain in Ukraine, the NIE is issued in two weeks on average, and this may take longer. Without the heirs' tax numbers, the inheritance deed will not be registered in the Land Registry (Art. 254 of the Mortgage Law).
    Will a Spanish notary accept a power of attorney drawn up before a notary in Ukraine?
    As a rule, yes, if three conditions are met. The first is an apostille: Spain and Ukraine are parties to the 1961 Hague Convention. The second is a translation into Spanish; we choose the type of translation to suit the authority that accepts the document. The third is equivalence: for registration in a Spanish registry, the foreign authority must perform the same functions as a Spanish one (Art. 60 Ley 29/2015). Whether the powers are sufficient is decided by the Spanish notary who certifies the deed (Art. 98 Ley 24/2001), so the text of the power of attorney is agreed with them before signing.
    How long do I have to file the inheritance tax, and what happens if I miss the deadline?
    Six months from the date of death (Art. 67 RD 1629/1991). An extension of another six months is requested within the first five months; if there is no reply within a month, it is deemed granted, but interest is charged for the extension period (Art. 68). For a return filed after the deadline without a demand from the tax office, a surcharge is applied: 1% plus 1% for each full month of delay, and after 12 months 15% plus interest (Art. 27 Ley 58/2003). Until payment of the tax or exemption from it is confirmed, the bank does not release money from the account and the registry does not register the real estate.
    There is no will, and all the heirs live abroad. Who will determine who is entitled to what?
    If the deceased lived in Spain, died here or most of their property is in Spain, the heirs are determined by a Spanish notary at one of these places in a deed declaring the heirs (declaración de herederos abintestato, Art. 55 of the Notaries Act). If the deceased lived and died abroad, the procedure for determining the heirs is agreed in advance with a Spanish notary. The notary is given documents proving the family relationship, the death certificate and the certificate from the Registry of Wills, two witnesses are needed, and the notary issues the conclusion no earlier than 20 working days after the application (Art. 56).
    My mother was a Ukrainian citizen and lived in Spain. Under which law is the inheritance divided?
    As a general rule, under the law of the country where the person habitually lived on the date of death, that is, Spanish law, and this applies to the whole inheritance (Art. 21 of EU Regulation No. 650/2012). Citizenship does not matter for this rule. It will be different if she chose the law of her country of citizenship in her will (Art. 22). Ukraine is not a member of the European Union, and the Regulation is not binding on its authorities; on property left in Ukraine, see the article on inheritance in Ukraine.
