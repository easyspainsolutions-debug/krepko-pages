    Apostille on a Spanish document for Ukraine: who issues it and where to apply

    Krepko · 24 November 2026 · ~8 min read
    In short. Spain and Ukraine are parties to the 1961 Hague Convention (Spain since 25 September 1978, Ukraine since 22 December 2003), so a Spanish document for Ukraine only needs an apostille, without consular legalisation. For notarial documents — a power of attorney, a consent, an authenticated signature — the apostille is issued only by the notarial association (Colegio Notarial) and only on paper. For Registro Civil (civil registry) certificates and the criminal record certificate, it is issued by the Ministry of Justice, its territorial offices, the secretariats of the regional High Courts of Justice or the same notarial associations. The city where the document was issued does not matter, and an apostille issued in Spain after 2011 can be checked online in the Ministry of Justice register for 25 years from the date of issue.

    Who issues the apostille for which document?

    The list of authorities is set out in Real Decreto 1497/2011. The main conclusion: the authority depends on who issued the document, not on where you live. All the listed authorities apostille a document “regardless of the place in Spain” where it was issued (arts. 1–3), so a certificate issued in another region does not need to be taken back there.

    Document for Ukraine Who issues the apostille Legal basis
    Birth, marriage or death certificate from the Registro Civil The Ministry of Justice citizen information service, the territorial offices of the Ministry of Justice (Gerencias Territoriales), the secretariat of the regional High Court of Justice (TSJ) or the notarial association RD 1497/2011, art. 1 and the transitional provision: since the Civil Registry Act 20/2011 entered into force (30 April 2021), these documents are apostilled as administrative documents
    Criminal record certificate (certificado de antecedentes penales) and other certificates from public authorities The same four authorities Art. 1.2: documents of the State administration, the autonomous communities, municipalities, Seguridad Social
    Notarial power of attorney, travel consent for a minor, spouse's consent, statement before a notary Only the notarial association: its dean or a notary to whom the dean has delegated this Art. 3; Orden JUS/1207/2011, art. 11 — on paper only
    Private document: a letter, a receipt, a certificate from an employer Cannot be apostilled as such. First a notary authenticates the signature, then the association issues the apostille on paper RD 1497/2011, art. 6; Orden JUS/1207/2011, art. 11
    Court decision: divorce, maintenance, adoption The TSJ secretariat, the Ministry of Justice citizen information service or a territorial office of the Ministry of Justice; the notarial association is not on this list Art. 2. Decisions of the Supreme Court and the Audiencia Nacional — only their own court clerks (art. 4)
    Extract from the Registro de la Propiedad or the Registro Mercantil The same four authorities as for administrative documents Art. 1.2.e

    What cannot be apostilled: documents issued by diplomatic or consular officials, and administrative documents directly related to commercial or customs operations. These are exceptions set by the convention itself (art. 1), and the Spanish Ministry of Justice repeats them on its procedure page.

    Why is a power of attorney not apostilled at the Ministry of Justice?

    It is easy to make a mistake here if you rely on Ukrainian explanations. The Ministry of Justice of Ukraine, in its explanation on powers of attorney from abroad, writes that the apostille is “usually” issued by the bodies of the ministry of justice of the country where the document was drawn up. For Spain this is not the case: on its procedure page the Spanish Ministry of Justice states explicitly that the apostille on notarial documents is issued exclusively by the notarial associations. Taking a power of attorney to a territorial office of the Ministry of Justice is pointless.

    Two more features of the notarial apostille:

    • Paper only. A notarial document and a private document with a signature authenticated by a notary are apostilled only on paper (Orden JUS/1207/2011, art. 11). According to the Ministry of Justice 2024 guidance note, such an apostille is signed by hand, contains a CSV verification code and is issued as an attachment to the document, fastened with staples and seals. So the paper original travels to Ukraine; a scan will not replace it.
    • Through the notary. The apostille may be issued not only by the dean of the association, but also by notaries to whom the dean has delegated this (RD 1497/2011, art. 3). The Ministry of Justice of Ukraine advises asking the notary in advance whether they can order the apostille themselves.

    We covered what to include in a power of attorney for Ukraine and how to send the original in the article about a power of attorney from Spain for Ukraine.

    How are typical documents for Ukraine apostilled?

    • A child's birth certificate. It is issued by the Registro Civil, and the apostille is issued by any of the four authorities in the table. If the certificate was issued electronically, with a CSV code, the apostille may also be electronic and linked to the document through this code (Ministry of Justice guidance note, 2024). What to do with the certificate next — registering citizenship, a passport — is in the article “Child born in Spain”.
    • Power of attorney. A notarial document: only the notarial association and only the paper original.
    • Travel consent for a minor or spouse's consent. If it was drawn up by a Spanish notary, it is a notarial document with the same procedure as a power of attorney. A consent handwritten without a notary cannot be apostilled (art. 6).
    • Criminal record certificate. It is issued by the Spanish Ministry of Justice; it is a document of the State administration, so the apostille is issued by the authorities under art. 1. We covered how the Spanish certificate differs from the Ukrainian one and which one you need in the article about the criminal record certificate.

    What is an electronic apostille and how do you check it?

    According to the Spanish Ministry of Justice guidance note (February 2024), all apostilles issued after 2011 are processed in an electronic system and entered in the Ministry of Justice Electronic Register of Apostilles (RD 1497/2011, art. 8). The form depends on the document:

    • a paper document signed by hand — an apostille with an electronic signature on paper, as an attachment fastened to the document;
    • a document with an electronic signature and a CSV code — an apostille linked to the document through this code: electronic if the document is electronic, and on paper if the document is printed;
    • a notarial document — a paper apostille signed by hand and with a CSV, described above.

    For now, an application for an electronic apostille through the Ministry of Justice sede (online portal) can be made only for some certificates issued by the ministry itself; login is with Cl@ve. A finished electronic apostille can be downloaded for one year. Apart from online applications, the Ministry of Justice accepts apostille applications in person and by post.

    The check is available to anyone to whom the document is presented: in the register you enter the CSV code, the apostille number and the date of issue. The register works around the clock, and an apostille can be checked for 25 years from the date of issue (Orden JUS/1207/2011, art. 4). The sede also offers a check of the electronic apostille's signature and a check that the right document is attached to the apostille. It is worth passing this information to the Ukrainian notary or authority if they have any doubts.

    You can also submit a public document for an apostille to the Ministry of Justice yourself — in person, by post or online for some certificates; a notarial one — to the notarial association.

    Is anything else needed after the apostille?

    Under the convention, the apostille is the only formality a country may require instead of legalisation (art. 3), and the signature and seal on the apostille itself are not certified further (art. 5). So after the Spanish apostille the document does not need to be taken anywhere else: neither to the Ukrainian consulate nor to the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

    A document drawn up at the Ukrainian consulate in Spain does not need an apostille at all. According to the explanation of the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine, a consular power of attorney does not need legalisation or translation and is valid from the moment it is received.

    Is a translation into Ukrainian needed?

    The convention allows the apostille to be drawn up in the language of the issuing authority; only the heading in French is mandatory (art. 4). The apostille itself does not translate the text of the document. What Ukrainian authorities say:

    • Power of attorney. The Ministry of Justice of Ukraine (explanation of 23 October 2025): a power of attorney from a foreign notary is translated into Ukrainian once in Ukraine, and the translation is notarised; after that it can be used.
    • Documents for amending a civil status record at the civil registry office (DRACS). According to the explanation of the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine, documents in a foreign language submitted to amend a civil status record are submitted together with a translation into Ukrainian certified in the prescribed manner; the translation remains in the file.

    Before ordering the translation, we check with the receiving party where and by whom it must be certified for that particular authority — DRACS, a consulate, a bank, a notary — and whether the apostille itself needs translating: the requirements of DRACS, a consulate and a Ukrainian notary may differ.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where in Spain can I get an apostille on a child's birth certificate for Ukraine?
    Since 30 April 2021, a Registro Civil certificate has been apostilled as an administrative document: this is done by the Ministry of Justice citizen information service, a territorial office of the Ministry of Justice, the secretariat of the regional High Court of Justice or the notarial association (RD 1497/2011, art. 1). It does not matter in which Spanish city the certificate was issued: any of these authorities apostilles it regardless of the place of issue.
    Can a power of attorney be apostilled at the Spanish Ministry of Justice?
    No. For notarial documents — powers of attorney, consents, authenticated signatures — the apostille is issued only by the notarial association: its dean or a notary to whom the dean has delegated this (RD 1497/2011, art. 3). Such an apostille is issued only on the paper original (Orden JUS/1207/2011, art. 11), so the document itself is sent to Ukraine, not a scan.
    How do I check a Spanish apostille online?
    In the Electronic Register of Apostilles on the Ministry of Justice sede: you enter the CSV code, the apostille number and the date of issue. The register works around the clock, and an apostille can be checked for 25 years from the date of issue (Orden JUS/1207/2011, art. 4). The check is available to anyone to whom the document is presented, including a Ukrainian notary or authority.
    Does a Spanish document with an apostille need a translation into Ukrainian?
    For a power of attorney, yes: according to the explanation of the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine, a power of attorney from a foreign notary is translated into Ukrainian once in Ukraine, and the translation is notarised. Documents in a foreign language submitted to DRACS to amend a civil status record are also submitted with a translation certified in the prescribed manner. Where exactly to certify the translation and whether to translate the apostille itself is best checked with the authority that will accept the document.
    Does the document need to be legalised at the Ukrainian consulate after the apostille?
    No. Spain and Ukraine are parties to the 1961 Hague Convention, and under it the apostille is the only formality instead of legalisation (art. 3). The signature and seal on the apostille itself are not certified further (art. 5).
    Can a consent handwritten without a notary be apostilled?
    No. Private documents cannot be apostilled (RD 1497/2011, art. 6). First a Spanish notary authenticates the signature or draws up the consent, and then the notarial association issues the apostille on the paper original.
