    Arraigo socioformativo: residence through study and which courses qualify

    Krepko · 24 November 2026 · ~8 min read
    In short. Arraigo socioformativo is granted to people who have lived in Spain without interruption for at least two years (absences of no more than 90 days), have no criminal record and are enrolled on, or studying, one of the programmes named in Article 127.d of the Regulation: post-compulsory secondary education, including FP grado medio, a full course leading to a certificado profesional at levels 1–3, in-person compulsory education for adults, or a public employment service course for a shortage occupation. You also need an integration report (informe de integración). The permit is issued for a year and is also renewed for a year, and you may work as an employee for up to 30 hours a week (art. 131.b). University and language courses are not on this list. For holders of temporary protection, time spent under it counts towards the two years, according to instruction SEM 2/2026.

    Arraigo socioformativo is a residence permit for people who have already been living in Spain for two years and are studying, or are ready to study, on a programme recognised by the Regulation. It is the former arraigo para la formación: since 2025 it has had a new name and is open not only to those who undertake to enrol on a course, but also to those who are already studying.

    Below we look at the requirements set by the Reglamento de Extranjería (Immigration Regulation, RD 1155/2024 as amended by RD 316/2026), which courses count and which do not, how much you can work, how to renew the permit and what changes for holders of temporary protection. The rules were checked on 24 November 2026 against the BOE and the website of the Ministry of Inclusion, Social Security and Migration.

    Arraigo socioformativo is one of the five types of arraigo (residence on the grounds of ties to Spain) in Article 125 of the Regulation, alongside segunda oportunidad, sociolaboral, social and familiar. Instead of an employment contract or relatives with a residence permit, the basis here is study: you show that you are building your life in Spain around a profession you are gaining here. We handle this route from start to finish — details on the service page arraigo socioformativo.

    Who can apply for arraigo socioformativo

    The general requirements for all types of arraigo are set out in Article 126 of RD 1155/2024. They must all be met at the same time:

    • two years in Spain without interruption up to the day you apply. According to the ministry's information sheet (hoja 30), absences from Spain during these two years must not exceed 90 days. Time spent waiting for a decision on an application for international protection (asylum) does not count towards the two years;
    • at the time of applying and while the application is being processed, you are not an applicant for international protection;
    • no criminal record in Spain or in the countries where you lived for the five years before entering Spain, for acts that are also offences under Spanish law;
    • you are not a threat to public order, security or public health, you are not on an entry-ban list in countries with which Spain has an agreement, and you are not within a period during which you have undertaken not to return to Spain;
    • you do not hold another stay or residence permit and have no pending procedure for such a permit (point h, added by RD 316/2026). If you are studying on a student stay (estancia por estudios), as a general rule arraigo is not open to you;
    • the fee (tasa) has been paid: Modelo 790, code 052, section 2.3.1. According to hoja 30, it falls due when the application is filed, and it must be paid within 10 working days.

    The criminal record certificate from your country of previous residence is submitted with an apostille and a sworn translation into Spanish. It is not required if you have lived in Spain without interruption for five years before applying, or have already proved a clean record in another application within those five years and have not left Spain since (art. 130.2). Extranjería (the immigration office) obtains the Spanish criminal record information itself. How to obtain the certificate from Ukraine is covered in the article on the criminal record certificate for Spain.

    Which training counts and which does not

    The specific requirement for socioformativo is Article 127.d: you must be enrolled on, or already studying, one of the programmes it refers to. The list is closed:

    Programme What the Regulation requires
    Post-compulsory secondary education (art. 52.1.b) In-person or blended, with at least 50% of the programme in person; a full study load; the centre is on the state or regional register of educational institutions; the outcome is a recognised qualification. FP grado medio cycles and Especialista de Formación Profesional programmes are expressly included
    Certificado profesional, grado C (art. 52.1.e, 5.º) A full course, not individual modules or part of a programme, at an authorised centre; at least 50% in person. Levels 1, 2 and 3 qualify for arraigo (under LO 3/2022, grado C leads to a certificado profesional at these levels)
    Compulsory education for adults In-person provision only (oferta presencial) within the educación de personas adultas system
    Public employment service course (Servicios Públicos de Empleo) Training for an occupation in the catalogue of shortage occupations (Catálogo de ocupaciones de difícil cobertura, art. 75.1); when applying, you undertake to complete it

    What is not on the list: higher education, that is, university and master's degrees (art. 52.1.a), language courses at EOI schools and Instituto Cervantes centres (art. 52.1.e, 2.º), and online courses with no in-person part. A paid course “for a residence permit” at a centre that is not on the official registers will not qualify either: the Regulation requires an authorised or accredited centre.

    When to apply and when to show proof of enrolment

    This is the main pitfall of this route. If the course has an official enrolment period (matrícula), the arraigo application is filed within the two months before that period begins. Proof of enrolment is taken to Extranjería within three months of notification of approval. If you do not bring it in time, the permit is terminated.

    If your plans change, for a justified reason you can show enrolment on a different programme, but only one from the same list. The rule is the same for public employment service courses: if you do not confirm that you have completed the training, the permit will be terminated.

    Which documents are needed and how to apply

    According to hoja 30, the set is as follows:

    • form EX-10, signed by you;
    • a copy of all pages of your passport (or cédula de inscripción, or a recognised travel document);
    • proof of the two years in Spain — preferably issued by Spanish authorities: municipal registration (empadronamiento), records of appointments in the public health system, documents from the town hall, the region or the state;
    • a criminal record certificate from the countries where you lived for the five years before entering;
    • an integration report from the autonomous community or the town hall where you live. Under Article 127.c it is issued within a month of the request, and if it has not been issued, you need to prove this and demonstrate your integration by any other means;
    • proof of enrolment, admission to the programme or a document showing that you are already studying, and for public employment service courses — a written undertaking to complete the training.

    Foreign documents are submitted with a sworn translation into Spanish and an apostille or legalisation. The application is filed by the person themselves, in person, at the Oficina de Extranjería of their province; for a minor, by their legal representative. A decision is due within three months, and if there is no answer, the application is deemed refused (silencio negativo). Once it is approved, you must apply in person for the TIE card (foreigner identity card) at the police within a month.

    How much you can work, how to renew and what to switch to

    Work. Unlike other types of arraigo, which give a full right to work, socioformativo only allows work as an employee — up to 30 hours a week in total across all employers, with pay no lower than the minimum wage (SMI) or the collective agreement rate in proportion to the hours (art. 131.b). Self-employment (autónomo) is not covered by this permit.

    Renewal. The permit and each renewal are valid for a year (art. 132.1). For FP grado básico and grado medio cycles, renewal depends on a certificate from the school confirming progression to the second year. If the course ended before the end of the year, you need the diploma or certificate, registration with the employment service and an active job search (art. 132.2.b). You can apply for renewal within the two months before the permit expires or within three months after — in that case the permit remains valid until a decision is made, but a fine is possible for the delay (art. 132.3).

    Switching. From temporary residence, under Article 191, you can switch to a residence and work permit without a visa if the requirements of the new type are met. If you have less than a year of residence behind you, all the requirements of Article 74 are checked, including an employment contract, and the new permit is issued for a year. If you have lived on a residence permit for at least a year and your current permit already allowed you to work — and socioformativo does — you need to meet the requirements of Article 80 (employment) or Article 86 (self-employment), and the new permit is issued for four years. More on changing status — on the change of status page.

    How socioformativo differs from arraigo social

    The required time in Spain and the criminal record requirements are the same for both — the general ones from Article 126. The difference lies in the basis and the rights:

    • arraigo social (art. 127.c) — close relatives with a residence permit and funds of at least 100% of IPREM, or an integration report; the permit gives a full right to work as an employee and as self-employed, and it is renewed on the basis of an active job search with registration at the employment service;
    • arraigo socioformativo (art. 127.d) — study on a programme from the list and an integration report; Article 127.d sets no separate requirement for funds, you can only work as an employee for up to 30 hours, and renewal depends on your progress in your studies.

    More on social — in the article on arraigo social. If you already have an employment contract, compare the route with arraigo sociolaboral (formerly arraigo laboral): see the article on arraigo laboral.

    What changes if you have temporary protection

    The Regulation itself contains no direct rule on counting time under temporary protection. There is additional provision 19 (as amended by RD 316/2026): holders of temporary protection under EU Decision 2022/382 may apply for permits that are processed from within Spain, including arraigo under Article 125.

    The procedure was clarified in instructions SEM 2/2026 of 22 June 2026 issued by the Secretaría de Estado de Migraciones (State Secretariat for Migration):

    • the bar in point 126.h does not apply to holders of temporary protection — you can apply for arraigo without giving up your card in advance (instruction segunda.1);
    • time under temporary protection counts towards the two years of residence (segunda.2);
    • if you are studying and can switch under Article 191, you must apply for a change of status (cuarta.1). If you cannot, you apply for arraigo socioformativo, but instead of the requirements of Article 127.d you prove any study or activity from Article 52.1 (this includes university), and for renewal — that you are continuing your studies (cuarta.2);
    • once you have the new permit, you must give up temporary protection, and when applying for the TIE you show the receipt for your application to give it up (séptima).

    Which to choose — a change of status or arraigo — is covered in the article on the change of status (modificación) from temporary protection.

    The one-off 2026 regularisation (arraigo extraordinario, additional provision 21) is a separate procedure: applications under it were accepted until 30 June 2026. Ordinary arraigo socioformativo has no general application deadline; the only restriction is the application window before the course enrolment period mentioned above. What to do if you applied for regularisation — on the Regularisation 2026 page.

    Frequently asked questions

    How long do you need to have lived in Spain for arraigo socioformativo?
    Two years without interruption up to the day you apply (art. 126.b RD 1155/2024). According to the ministry's information sheet, absences from Spain during these two years must not exceed 90 days. Time spent waiting for a decision on an asylum application does not count towards these two years.
    Can you get arraigo socioformativo through university or Spanish courses?
    As a general rule, no. Article 127.d only recognises post-compulsory secondary education (including FP grado medio), a full course leading to a certificado profesional at levels 1–3, in-person compulsory education for adults and public employment service courses for shortage occupations. The exception is holders of temporary protection who are studying and cannot switch to work under Article 191: under instruction SEM 2/2026, any study from Article 52.1 counts for them, including university.
    How many hours can you work with arraigo socioformativo?
    Up to 30 hours a week as an employee, in total across all employers, with pay no lower than the minimum wage or the collective agreement rate in proportion to the hours (art. 131.b). This permit does not allow you to work for yourself as an autónomo.
    When should you apply if enrolment for the course takes place on set dates?
    If the programme has an official enrolment period, the arraigo application is filed within the two months before it begins. After approval, you have three months from notification to bring proof of enrolment to Extranjería; otherwise the permit is terminated.
    Does time under temporary protection count for arraigo socioformativo?
    Yes. Under instruction SEM 2/2026 of the Secretaría de Estado de Migraciones of 22 June 2026 (segunda.2), time under temporary protection counts towards the two years of residence. You can apply without giving up your card in advance; temporary protection is given up only after approval, and when applying for the TIE you show the receipt for your application to give it up.
    What happens after a year on arraigo socioformativo?
    The permit is renewed for another year: for FP grado básico and grado medio you need a certificate from the school confirming progression to the second year, and if your studies have ended — the diploma, registration with the employment service and an active job search. Alternatively, under Article 191 you can switch to a residence and work permit; after a year of residence with the right to work, the new permit is issued for four years.
