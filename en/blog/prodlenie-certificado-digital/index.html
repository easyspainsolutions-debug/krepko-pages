    How to renew an FNMT certificado digital online and what to do if you no longer can

    Krepko · 11 November 2026 · ~7 min read
    In short. An FNMT certificate for an individual (Certificado de Ciudadano) is renewed online at sede.fnmt.gob.es in the last 60 days before its expiry date, while it is still valid and has not been revoked. The request is submitted from the browser where the valid certificate is installed, and, according to FNMT, the renewed one is downloaded about an hour later on the same computer using the code from the email. Online renewal is possible only once, and a certificate obtained with a DNIe, another electronic certificate or video identification cannot be renewed online: in these cases FNMT issues a new one only after a new identity check, and for a foreigner with an NIE that check requires a visit to an accreditation office. An expired certificate cannot be renewed; a new one is obtained instead. Conditions checked against sede.fnmt.gob.es on 11 November 2026.

    The FNMT certificate you use to log in to the Hacienda (tax office) and Seguridad Social (Social Security) online accounts is valid until the date written in the certificate itself. It can be renewed online, without visiting an office, only in the last 60 days before that date and only once, and an expired certificate can no longer be renewed, so it is worth checking the date in advance.

    Below we explain how to find out the expiry date, when online renewal is available, how it works step by step and how long the renewed certificate will be valid. If it cannot be renewed online, we handle the new certificate from start to finish; see the digital certificate page for details.

    When the certificate can be renewed online

    On the FNMT website this certificate is called Certificado de Ciudadano (the “Cert. Electrónico Ciudadano” section); it is the same certificado de persona física (individual's certificate) that is issued with an NIE or DNI. Renewal at FNMT means issuing a new certificate with new keys, but without a new identity check: you are identified by your valid certificate, which you use to log in to the renewal page and to sign the request. That is where the conditions come from.

    Your case Online renewal What to do
    The certificate was obtained by appearing in person at an office or consulate, has not been renewed yet, and 60 days or less remain until it expires Available Renew at sede.fnmt.gob.es, steps below
    More than 60 days remain until it expires Not yet Wait for the renewal window. A new certificate can be obtained earlier, but the old one will be revoked when it is issued
    The certificate has already been renewed online No New certificate with an identity check
    The certificate was obtained with a DNIe, another electronic certificate or video identification No New certificate with an identity check
    It has expired No New certificate
    The certificate has been revoked No New certificate: a revoked one cannot be reinstated

    The limit of one renewal is set by the FNMT certificate issuance rules (Políticas y Prácticas de Certificación, para. 109): after it, to keep using a certificate, you obtain a new one and verify your identity. The same rules say that online renewal is possible only while no more than five years have passed since you appeared in person and your documents were checked (para. 113), with a reference to Article 7.6 of Ley 6/2020 on electronic trust services.

    How to find out when the certificate expires

    The expiry date is written in the certificate itself and can be seen in the browser where it is installed. FNMT describes the path as follows; in your version of the browser the menu items may be named slightly differently:

    • Microsoft Edge: the “…” menu (or Alt+F) → “Settings”, search the settings for “Certificates” → “Manage certificates” → the “Personal” tab → double-click the certificate;
    • Firefox: menu → “Settings” → “Privacy & Security” → “Certificates” → “View Certificates” → the “Your Certificates” tab → double-click the certificate: the validity field shows the start and expiry dates.

    The same details show the issuer. Certificates that AC FNMT Usuarios issues now are valid until 31 December 2028 (FAQ 1146 on the FNMT website), while those issued earlier may have a different term, so go by the date in your own certificate. If yours says 31 December 2028 and the certificate meets the conditions in the table above, you will be able to renew it online in November–December 2028, provided FNMT's rules have not changed by then.

    If the browser says the certificate has expired or is not yet valid, first check the date and time on your computer: FNMT names an incorrect clock as one of the two reasons for this message. If the clock is correct, the certificate really has expired.

    Renewal step by step

    The whole renewal takes place at sede.fnmt.gob.es, in the “Cert. Electrónico Ciudadano” → “Renovar Certificado” section. It must be done from the same computer and under the same system user, and the computer must not be formatted between the request and the download.

    1
    Install Configurador FNMT-RCM and Autofirma
    Configurador is FNMT's program for generating keys, and Autofirma is an electronic signature program. At the “Configuración previa” step FNMT names both as the programs for renewal and asks you to install them before the request. Browser: the latest version of Firefox, Chrome, Edge, Opera or Safari.
    2
    Submit the request from the browser with the valid certificate
    At the “Solicitar Renovación” step you log in with your current certificate and sign the request with it. The program will ask you to set a new password: you will need it for the download, it cannot be recovered, and if you forget it, you will have to submit the request again. At the end FNMT will send a request code (código de solicitud) to the email address you enter in the request form.
    3
    Download the renewed certificate
    According to FNMT, it can be downloaded about an hour after the request, at the “Descargar Certificado” step. You will need your document number, first surname, the code from the email and the password set during the request. It is downloaded on the same computer and under the same user as the request, and after installation FNMT recommends making a backup. If the download fails, what to check is explained in the article on why an FNMT certificate will not download.

    What changes after renewal

    The old certificate is not revoked immediately on renewal: it remains valid until its own expiry date (FNMT rules, para. 112). But if you request revocation during that time, FNMT will revoke both certificates. A new certificate obtained other than through renewal works differently: when it is issued, the old one is revoked immediately, because as a general rule a person has only one valid FNMT certificate (para. 132 of the same rules, FAQ 1053 on the FNMT website).

    The renewed certificate is a new one, with new keys, so a copy of the old one on your phone or a second computer will not update by itself. Make a copy of the new one and install it on your other devices: how to do this is explained in the article on transferring a certificado digital to another computer.

    How long the renewed certificate will be valid. This is written in the certificate itself, so check the date right after downloading it. Certificates with RSA keys that the AC FNMT Usuarios chain issues now are valid until 31 December 2028 at the latest. Under the plan FNMT published on 9 December 2025, during 2026 it is introducing certificates with elliptic curve keys (ECC) and will issue both types for a while, but certificates with RSA keys only until 31 December 2026. Renewal means issuing a new certificate, so if the plan does not change, a certificate renewed from 2027 onwards will already be ECC. Under FNMT's rules such certificates are issued by the AC Usuarios G2 chain, and they are valid for no more than four years. According to FNMT, most commonly used programs, for example Adobe Acrobat and Autofirma from version 1.9, already support signatures with such certificates.

    If it cannot be renewed online

    Then a new certificate is obtained, just as the first time: a request at sede.fnmt.gob.es, an identity check and the download. A foreigner with an NIE needs to visit an accreditation office for this, with the request code from the FNMT email, the document with the NIE and a passport: FNMT video identification so far works only with a valid Spanish DNI. Where to verify your identity and how to book an appointment is explained in the article on identity verification for a certificado digital, and the whole route to getting one in the article on the certificado digital.

    If the certificate has already expired and you are registered with Cl@ve, some online procedures can be done through it while the new certificate is being obtained. How to register with Cl@ve with an NIE is explained in the article on Cl@ve for foreigners.

    We handle the new certificate from start to finish: we submit the request, prepare the documents, accompany you to the short office visit and interpret, install the certificate on your device and make a backup. Certificates for several family members are arranged in a single visit. The overall timeframe depends on when there is a free appointment at the office in your city. What the work includes is described on the digital certificate (firma digital) page.

    Frequently asked questions

    My FNMT certificate has already expired. Can it be renewed?
    No. FNMT renews only a valid certificate; an expired one cannot be renewed (FAQ 1158 on the FNMT website). A new one is obtained: a request, an identity check (with an NIE, at an accreditation office) and the download. If the browser says the certificate has expired, first check the date and time on your computer: an incorrect clock produces the same message (FAQ 1075).
    I already renewed my certificate online last time. Can I do it again?
    No. A certificate is renewed online once; after that FNMT issues a new one only with an identity check (FNMT certificate issuance rules, para. 109). With an NIE this means a visit to an accreditation office with the request code, the document with your NIE and your passport. We handle such a new certificate from start to finish, from the request to installation on your device.
    I have renewed my certificate. Does the old one still work?
    Yes, until its own expiry date: on renewal the previous certificate is not revoked immediately (FNMT certificate issuance rules, para. 112). But if you request revocation during that time, FNMT will revoke both. Replace the copies on your other devices with a copy of the new certificate: how to transfer the certificate.
    The certificate is installed on another computer. Can I renew it from this one?
    The request is submitted from the browser where the valid certificate is installed, and the renewed one is downloaded on the same computer and under the same user as the request. So first install a copy of the certificate in the browser you will renew from: one certificate can be installed in several browsers by exporting and importing it (FAQ 1052 on the FNMT website).
    Can I give a new email address when renewing?
    For the request code, yes: according to FNMT's renewal guide, the email address is entered in the renewal request form and the code is sent to that address. So give a mailbox you definitely have access to. But FNMT does not change the email address already recorded in an issued certificate (FAQ 1355 on the FNMT website).
    How much does it cost to renew an FNMT certificate?
    FNMT does not charge for certificates for individuals, including renewed ones: the only thing it sometimes charges for is video identification (FNMT certificate issuance rules, para. 300). If we handle the new certificate, we calculate the cost per person or per family and tell you after reviewing your case.
