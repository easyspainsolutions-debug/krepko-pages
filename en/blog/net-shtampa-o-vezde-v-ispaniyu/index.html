    No entry stamp for Spain: the EES and how to prove your date of entry

    Krepko · updated 13 November 2026 · ~8 min read
    In short. Since 10 April 2026 Schengen has not been stamping the passports of third-country nationals on short-stay entry: entries and exits are recorded by the EES system. The Ukrainian border stamps the passports of Ukrainian citizens only at their request, and this has been the case for many years (Law No. 1710-VI, Art. 12; Order No. 714 of the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine, MVS). If you came to Spain from another Schengen country without a Spanish residence permit, you must file an entry declaration with the police within three days (Art. 13 RD 1155/2024); whether applicants for temporary protection are required to file it has not been clarified. The date you left Ukraine is confirmed by a DPSU certificate: it is free if you request it yourself, and the timeframe is up to 30 calendar days. For a lawyer's request the law gives 5 working days, extendable to 20; in practice it currently takes 5 to 20 working days.

    Your passport has neither a Ukrainian exit stamp nor a Schengen entry stamp, and it is unclear how you can now prove the date you arrived in Spain. This is not a mistake by the border guards. Ukraine has long put the border crossing stamp («штамп про перетин кордону») in its citizens' passports only on request, and since 10 April 2026 the Schengen countries have been recording entries in the EES system instead of stamping passports.

    Below we look at what the Spanish authorities see, when you need an entry declaration (declaración de entrada), whether you can request your data from the EES and where a certificate from the State Border Guard Service of Ukraine (DPSU) is useful, in Ukrainian «довідка про перетин кордону» (dovidka pro peretyn kordonu). We checked the rules against primary sources on 1 October 2026.

    Why does Ukraine not stamp passports on exit («штамп про перетин кордону»)?

    The Law of Ukraine “On Border Control” («Про прикордонний контроль») No. 1710-VI (Art. 12, part 3, item 1) provides that no border crossing mark is placed in the passport of a Ukrainian citizen, with one exception: if the person asks the border guard for it. The rule was already in the original text of the 2009 law, and since 1 January 2020 the procedure for marks has been set by MVS Order No. 714. Foreign nationals and stateless persons always receive a mark — in their passport or, at their request, on an immigration card (Art. 12, parts 1 and 4).

    A stamp will not be added retroactively: this was explained on 21 August 2026 by a DPSU spokesperson in a comment to RBC-Ukraine (as reported by hromadske). If there is no mark, what remains is the border crossing certificate. In its clarifications of 18 August 2026 the European Commission also acknowledges that the passports of Ukrainian citizens are systematically not stamped. On your next trip, ask the border guard to stamp your passport: on 20 August 2026 the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Ukraine advised asking for a stamp when leaving for the EU.

    Why is there no Schengen entry stamp in the passport either?

    Since 10 April 2026 the European Entry/Exit System (EES) has been fully operational. It was rolled out in stages from 12 October 2025, and since April border guards at the external borders of Schengen have not been stamping the passports of third-country nationals on short-stay entry: entries and exits are recorded in an electronic database. The Spanish Immigration Regulation (RD 1155/2024, Art. 12.4, 21.2 and 207) states explicitly that the stamp has been replaced by registration in the EES and that the stay of a visa-exempt foreign national is now proven by the system's data.

    As a result, a Ukrainian who entered the Schengen area after 10 April 2026 and did not ask for a mark at the Ukrainian border has neither a Ukrainian nor a Schengen stamp in their passport. In itself this is not a violation but the new way border control works. The Spanish immigration authorities have access to EES records under Art. 26 of Regulation (EU) 2017/2226, on which the system is based.

    Who is not recorded in the EES, and can you find out your own data?

    Holders of a residence permit are not recorded in the EES: they are excluded by Art. 2.3.c of Regulation 2017/2226. In Spain these are holders of the TIE card. The system does not record their trips, and now only the Spanish border can give them a Schengen stamp. If an authority asks you to prove your absences, tickets and boarding passes will be useful; for long-term residence, the immigration office (Extranjería) checks the periods of residence itself (Art. 184.4 RD 1155/2024).

    Those who are recorded in the EES have the right under the regulation to request their data: under Art. 52 the response must arrive within 45 days. This way you can find out which date of entry into Schengen the system recorded. But the EES works only at the external border. If you entered through Poland and travelled to Spain within Schengen, the database will hold the date of the Polish border, not the date you arrived in Spain. Whether a Spanish authority will accept the response to such a request as a document has not been officially clarified.

    Do you need to file an entry declaration (declaración de entrada)?

    Under Art. 13 RD 1155/2024, an entry declaration (declaración de entrada) must be filed in person with the National Police (Policía Nacional) by a third-country national who entered Spain from another Schengen country and does not hold a Spanish residence permit. If this was not done on entry, there are three days to file it, and any National Police station (comisaría) accepts it. The website of the Spanish Ministry of the Interior says the same.

    The obligation is based on Art. 13 RD 1155/2024 and Art. 22 of the Schengen Convention; Art. 22 of the Immigration Act (LOEX) has nothing to do with it: that article is about free legal aid. The Spanish regulation simply says “tres días”, while the Schengen Convention specifies three working days (tres días hábiles). The Administrative Procedure Act, Ley 39/2015 (Art. 30.2), also counts days as working days by default.

    The declaration records your arrival with the Spanish police when there was no border control on entry into Spain. What the rules do not say: neither the regulation nor the Ministry of the Interior website names a penalty for not filing it or clarifies whether those who came to apply for temporary protection are required to file the declaration.

    How can you prove your date of entry if there is no stamp?

    The date is usually proven by several records, and which of them you have depends on your route:

    • Entry into Schengen after 10 April 2026. The date at the external border is recorded in the EES, and the Spanish authorities can see it. You can request your data; the response takes up to 45 days.
    • Arrival in Spain from another Schengen country. The Spanish date is recorded by the entry declaration if you filed it within three days.
    • Departure from Ukraine. The date, time, border checkpoint and direction are shown in the DPSU certificate. It confirms your departure, not your arrival in Spain, but it shows when your journey began.
    • Tickets and boarding passes. Keep them: they are indirect proof, and their weight is assessed by the authority to which you submit your documents.

    Where is a DPSU certificate («довідка про перетин кордону») useful?

    The certificate is issued by the DPSU Main Centre for Processing Special Information (in Ukrainian «Головний центр обробки спеціальної інформації», GTsOSI) in Kyiv, not by border checkpoints. According to descriptions by the DPSU in the media and by lawyers, the certificate states your full name, date of birth, citizenship, passport details, the date and time of each crossing, the border checkpoint and the direction. The grounds for leaving, for example a deferment or exemption from service, are not mentioned in descriptions of the response. So a DPSU certificate proves the fact of departure, but not that you have fulfilled your military obligations.

    The main scenario in Spain is temporary protection. Since 5 August 2026, Council of the EU Decision 2026/1912 (Art. 2) requires new applicants to prove that they have fulfilled their military obligations in Ukraine. Recital (18) names as proof a lawful border crossing on departure or an exit stamp. If departure cannot be proven this way, an easily verifiable official document is needed, for example from the Reserve+ app. The burden of proof is on the applicant.

    The new condition does not apply to those who were under temporary protection in Spain on 4 August 2026 and have kept it without interruption. Nor does it apply to those who applied for protection in Spain before 5 August 2026 but have not yet received it (European Commission clarifications, section IV). It applies to new applicants and to those who move to Spain from another EU country, for example from Poland or Germany, and apply again (ibid., section III).

    There is no guarantee of acceptance. According to the European Commission's clarifications, in countries that do not border Ukraine (and therefore in Spain), at the first level of checks a Ukrainian exit stamp is sufficient, as a rule no older than 90 days before the application. The Commission does not explicitly name the DPSU certificate: each country accepts “other documents” at its own discretion. The 90-day rule is not in the text of the decision itself, and whether Spain applies it has not been confirmed. Spain has not published a list of documents: the Embassy of Ukraine requested it on 12 August 2026; as of 1 October no response had been published. The certificate does not replace Reserve+ or documents on a deferment or exemption.

    Do you need the certificate for a residence permit, arraigo or permanent residence?

    No, the rules do not require it. The immigration authority's instruction SEM 2/2026 of 22 June 2026 on switching from temporary protection does not ask for proof of border crossings. A change of residence status (modificación) under Art. 191 RD 1155/2024 does not require proof of entry or exit either. For arraigo (residence on the grounds of ties to Spain), time under protection counts towards the two years under Art. 126.b, and for long-term residence (larga duración nacional) it counts in full. That is why we do not offer the DPSU certificate for these procedures.

    There is only a hypothesis. For long-term residence, continuity is not broken by absences of up to 6 months in a row and up to 10 months in total over five years (Art. 183.2), and TIE holders are not recorded in the EES. In theory, a DPSU certificate could show the dates of trips to Ukraine. But the rules do not require it, we have found no such practice, and the certificate may also show an absence longer than allowed. How to count your period of residence and your trips is explained on the page on long-term residence.

    How to get a DPSU certificate: on your own or through a lawyer?

    You can request the certificate yourself, free of charge. You need a written application to the GTsOSI DPSU and a scan of the first page of the passport you used to leave. You can submit it in person or by post (26 Volodymyrska Street, Kyiv, 01601), and according to the DPSU page of 18 December 2025 also by e-mail to gcosi-dpsu@dpsu.gov.ua: a scan of the application signed by hand or a file signed with a qualified electronic signature (KEP). A newer DPSU page names only submission in person and by post, but the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Ukraine, according to media reports of 15 September 2026, confirms e-mail. Under the Personal Data Protection Act (No. 2297-VI, Art. 16), a decision is made within up to 10 working days, and the information is provided within up to 30 calendar days.

    By law, the timeframe for a lawyer's request is shorter: 5 working days versus 30 calendar days, but with an extension to 20 working days the difference almost disappears. Art. 24 of the Law on the Bar (No. 5076-VI) requires a response within 5 working days at the latest, and if the authority notifies the lawyer in writing within the first 5 days, the period is extended to 20 working days. This is the general timeframe for responding to a lawyer's request. The law excludes restricted-access data from this obligation, and information on border crossings is confidential personal data. That is why the request is accompanied by your written consent and the lawyer's contract or warrant, and the real timeframe is currently unstable: some people get a response in 5 days, others in 15 or 20.

    We have not found a channel through Diia, a TsNAP (administrative services centre) or a consulate. The DPSU online account has been working since May 2026, but, according to media reports, it shows only temporary restrictions on leaving the country, for example by a decision of a court or the enforcement service. It has no history of crossings and no certificate, so the request to the DPSU is submitted in writing: by yourself or through a lawyer.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why was my passport not stamped when I left Ukraine?
    Because by law the passports of Ukrainian citizens are not stamped. The Law “On Border Control” No. 1710-VI (Art. 12) allows a mark only at a personal request, and since 1 January 2020 the procedure has been set by MVS Order No. 714. A stamp will not be added retroactively. If you need it as proof of departure, request a DPSU border crossing certificate.
    Why is there no Schengen entry stamp in my passport?
    Since 10 April 2026 the passports of third-country nationals have not been stamped at the Schengen external borders on short-stay entry: entries and exits are recorded by the EES system. The Spanish Immigration Regulation (RD 1155/2024) states explicitly that the stamp has been replaced by registration in the EES, and the Spanish immigration authorities can see these records.
    I came to Spain by bus from Poland. Do I need an entry declaration?
    If you do not hold a Spanish residence permit, then under Art. 13 RD 1155/2024, yes. The declaration (declaración de entrada) is filed in person, on entry or within three days, at any National Police station; under the Schengen Convention these are working days. Neither the rule nor the website of the Spanish Ministry of the Interior names a penalty for not filing it, and whether applicants for temporary protection are required to file it has not been clarified.
    I have a TIE card. Does the EES record my trips?
    No. Residence permit holders are excluded from the EES (Art. 2.3.c of Regulation (EU) 2017/2226), and now only the Spanish border can give them a Schengen stamp. If an authority asks you to prove your absences, tickets and boarding passes will be useful. For long-term residence, the immigration office checks the periods of residence itself (Art. 184.4 RD 1155/2024).
    Can I get a DPSU certificate through Diia or a consulate?
    We have not found such a channel, and the DPSU online account shows only restrictions on leaving the country. You request the certificate yourself, free of charge, from the GTsOSI DPSU: in person, by post to Kyiv or, according to the DPSU page of 18 December 2025, by e-mail to gcosi-dpsu@dpsu.gov.ua. The timeframe by law is up to 30 calendar days. For a lawyer's request, the Law on the Bar gives 5 working days with a possible extension to 20, but for confidential data there is no guarantee of this timeframe.
    Will a DPSU certificate be accepted in Spain for temporary protection?
    There is no guarantee. The European Commission does not name the certificate explicitly and leaves “other documents” to the discretion of each country. Spain has not published a list of documents: the Embassy of Ukraine requested it on 12 August 2026; as of 1 October no response had been published. The certificate confirms the fact of departure but does not replace Reserve+ or documents on a deferment or exemption.
    Do I need a DPSU certificate for arraigo, a change of residence status or permanent residence?
    The rules do not require it: neither instruction SEM 2/2026, nor Art. 191 RD 1155/2024, nor the rules on arraigo and long-term residence. Time under temporary protection counts without proof of border crossings. Using the certificate to prove trips for long-term residence is only a hypothesis with no practice behind it, and the certificate may show an absence longer than allowed. We do not offer it for these procedures.
