    Ukrainian border crossing certificate: how to get it from Spain

    Krepko · updated 13 November 2026 · ~9 min read
    In short. The certificate is issued by the DPSU's Main Centre for Processing Special Information (GTsOSI) in Kyiv. The certificate is not available in Diia, and the consulate does not issue it: we had not found such a channel as of 1 October 2026. A request made on your own is free: the application and a scan of your passport are sent by e-mail or post, and by law the response is given within 30 calendar days. A lawyer's request has a shorter deadline by law: a response within 5 working days, but the DPSU is entitled to extend the period to 20 working days; in practice timeframes are currently unpredictable: some people get a response in 5 working days, others in 15 or 20. Such a request is possible only with your written consent. The certificate confirms the date and place of departure, but not its grounds, and Spain has not yet published the list of documents for temporary protection.

    A Ukrainian border crossing certificate, in Ukrainian “довідка про перетин кордону” (dovidka pro peretyn kordonu), is a response from the State Border Guard Service of Ukraine (DPSU) about your entries and exits: dates, border checkpoints, direction. You may need it when there is no exit stamp in your passport (“штамп про перетин кордону”, a border crossing stamp) and, when applying again for temporary protection in the EU, you have to prove a lawful departure.

    Below we look at the two lawful routes from Spain: requesting the certificate yourself and free of charge, or through a lawyer's request. We also cover what it says, why it is not apostilled and what translation is needed. We checked the rules and the authorities' pages on 1 October 2026.

    What is a довідка про перетин кордону (border crossing certificate) and what does it say?

    Information on crossings of the state border is prepared by the Main Centre for Processing Special Information of the State Border Guard Service of Ukraine (GTsOSI DPSU). This is the DPSU's central unit in Kyiv; border checkpoints do not issue certificates. A DPSU certificate, a GPSU certificate, a border guard service certificate, a довідка про перетин кордону (dovidka pro peretyn kordonu) — these are all one and the same document.

    According to DPSU descriptions in the media and the practice of Ukrainian lawyers, the response states:

    • surname, first name, patronymic, date of birth and citizenship;
    • the details of the passport you crossed the border with;
    • the date and time of each recorded crossing;
    • the border checkpoint and direction: entry or exit.
    What the certificate does not contain. The grounds for departure, for example a deferment or exemption from service, are not mentioned in descriptions of the response. The certificate shows that the departure went through a border checkpoint, while it confirms the fulfilment of military obligations only indirectly, and it is the Spanish authority that assesses it. It does not replace documents from the Reserve+ app or documents on a deferment or exemption.

    Why is there no exit stamp from Ukraine in your passport?

    This is not a glitch and not a new rule. Under the Law of Ukraine “On Border Control” No. 1710-VI (Article 12(3)(1)), a border crossing mark is not placed in a Ukrainian citizen's passport unless the person asks for it. The rule is not new, and since 1 January 2020 Order No. 714 of the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine (MVS), which sets out the procedure for marks, has been in force. Foreigners and stateless persons must be given a mark (Article 12(1)), so the certificate is needed above all by Ukrainian citizens.

    On 15 July and 21 August 2026, DPSU representatives publicly confirmed in the media (TSN and hromadske): the stamp is placed on request during the border check, it cannot be placed retroactively, and if there is no stamp, the certificate is what remains. The European Commission, in its clarifications of August 2026, also acknowledges that the passports of Ukrainian citizens are systematically not stamped.

    The European side no longer stamps either: since 10 April 2026 the Entry/Exit System (EES) has been fully operational, and the short-term entry of third-country nationals into the Schengen area is recorded in an electronic database. So those who arrived after that date often have neither a Ukrainian mark nor a Schengen one.

    Why is the certificate needed in Spain in 2026?

    Since 5 August 2026, Council of the EU Decision 2026/1912 (Article 2, with a corrigendum on the dates) has been in force. New applicants for temporary protection must confirm that they have fulfilled their military obligations in Ukraine. The preamble of the decision (recital 18) names as proof a lawful border crossing on departure or an exit stamp. If this cannot be confirmed, an easily verifiable official document is needed, for example from Reserve+. The burden of proof lies with the applicant. There is more on the new condition in the article on temporary protection.

    The condition does not apply to those who were under protection in a particular EU country on 4 August 2026 and keep it without interruption. The certificate may be needed by new applicants in Spain, by those who move here from another EU country and file a new application, and by those who have lost their status. For switching to another residence permit, arraigo (residence on the grounds of ties to Spain) and long-term residence (larga duración) it is not required: neither SEM Instruction 2/2026 nor the Immigration Regulation (Royal Decree 1155/2024) mentions it.

    Spain has not published the list of accepted documents. The Ucrania Urgente portal only says that since August 2026 the fulfilment of military obligations must be confirmed. On 12 August 2026 the Embassy of Ukraine officially asked the Spanish side for the list; as of 1 October 2026 no response has been published. The European Commission does not name the DPSU certificate explicitly: each country accepts “other documents” at its own discretion. So no one can guarantee that the certificate will be accepted, and Krepko does not promise this either.

    About the 90 days. The European Commission's clarifications contain a guideline: the date of the exit stamp should, as a rule, be no earlier than 90 days before the application for protection, taking exceptional circumstances into account. The decision itself does not contain this rule, and whether Spain applies it has not been confirmed. If you left recently, request the certificate straight away. If you left Ukraine long ago, for example in 2022–2025, and are moving to Spain from another EU country, a certificate with an old departure date may not fit this guideline. How Spain handles such cases is unknown, so before ordering we compare the departure date with the application date.

    How do you request the certificate yourself, free of charge?

    The procedure is described on the DPSU page “Як отримати довідку про перетинання державного кордону” (“How to obtain a certificate of crossing the state border”) of 18 December 2025. This is the “запит до прикордонної служби” (request to the border guard service) that can be sent from Spain without travelling. The page mentions no fee for the certificate, and the Law of Ukraine on the protection of personal data No. 2297-VI does not provide for one either.

    1. Fill in a written application to the GTsOSI DPSU. The form and instructions are published on the DPSU website and by the Embassy of Ukraine in Spain.
    2. Sign the application by hand and scan it, or sign the file with a qualified electronic signature (KEP).
    3. Attach a scan of the first page of your passport.
    4. Send everything to gcosi-dpsu@dpsu.gov.ua or by post to: 26 Volodymyrska St, Kyiv, 01601.
    5. In the application, choose how to receive the response: by e-mail, by post or in person.

    Timeframes are set by the Law of Ukraine on the protection of personal data No. 2297-VI (Article 16): up to 10 working days for the decision to issue and up to 30 calendar days for issue from the date the request is received. According to media reports citing the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Ukraine, a request by e-mail is answered with a certificate on letterhead with an electronic signature, and a paper original with a seal is issued if you give a postal address in the application or choose collection in person. Whether the DPSU sends paper outside Ukraine is not officially described.

    A contradiction on the DPSU website. The page of 18 December 2025 explicitly allows filing by e-mail. But the announcement of 10 January 2024 allows only post or filing in person, and the page of 24 March 2026 names only those in its text, although e-mail is listed in the contacts. According to media reports citing the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Ukraine (September 2026) and the instructions of the Embassy of Ukraine in Spain, e-mail works. Post is named on all DPSU pages, so it is the backup channel.

    How do you get the certificate through a lawyer, and why is your consent needed?

    The second route is a lawyer's request under the Law of Ukraine “On the Bar and Practice of Law” No. 5076-VI. Under Article 24, the authority must reply to the lawyer within 5 working days. If there is a lot of information, the period may be extended to 20 working days, but the lawyer must be notified in writing within the first 5 working days. The lawyer's authority is confirmed by a contract or a lawyer's warrant (order) (Article 26).

    Information on border crossings is confidential personal data, and the law excludes restricted-access information from a lawyer's request. The DPSU releases such information to a representative only with the person's written consent, so your signed consent is attached to the request. A lawyer's request gives no access beyond what you have yourself: you are entitled to get the same certificate yourself. What sets it apart is a shorter period by law. In practice, the timeframe is currently unpredictable: some people get a response in 5 working days, others in 15 or 20, and there is no pattern, so we give you a realistic timeframe with a margin.

    Can you get a довідка про перетин кордону in Diia or at the consulate?

    No. People search for “довідка про перетин кордону дія” (border crossing certificate Diia), but we had not found a channel through Diia, a TsNAP (administrative services centre) or a consulate as of 1 October 2026. The DPSU's online account, which has been operating since 19 May 2026, according to media reports only shows restrictions on leaving the country, for example by court decision, not the history of crossings. At the same time, the website of the Embassy of Ukraine in Spain has instructions for making a request to the GTsOSI yourself by e-mail.

    Does a DPSU certificate need an apostille?

    No. An apostille is not placed on a DPSU certificate: it is a response from a state authority, issued electronically with a KEP or on paper with a seal, and it is not legalised with an apostille — this is the experience of lawyers who make such requests. Spanish rules on temporary protection and the European Commission's clarifications do not require an apostille for this document either.

    If an authority in Spain asks for a translation, order a sworn translation from Ukrainian: more in the next section.

    What translation do Spanish authorities need?

    The certificate is issued in Ukrainian. For a document from Reserve+, the European Commission advises a sworn translation into English or the language of the country. There are no separate instructions on translating a DPSU certificate, and which translation the Spanish police and temporary protection offices will accept has not been officially published. There are only four sworn translators (traductores jurados) from Ukrainian in the register of the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs: two in Madrid and one each in Seville and Valencia, so allow time. For more, see the article on the apostille and translation.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much does the border crossing certificate cost if you request it yourself?
    Nothing. The DPSU page states no fee, and the Law of Ukraine on the protection of personal data No. 2297-VI does not provide for one either. You only pay if you order a lawyer's request, delivery of the paper original or a translation.
    Can I send a request to the border guard service by e-mail from Spain?
    Yes. According to the DPSU page of 18 December 2025, a scan of the application with a handwritten signature or a file with a KEP, together with a scan of the first page of your passport, is sent to gcosi-dpsu@dpsu.gov.ua. The DPSU website also has other pages that name only post and filing in person, but according to media reports citing the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Ukraine and the instructions of the embassy in Spain, e-mail works. The backup option is a letter to: 26 Volodymyrska St, Kyiv, 01601.
    How long does it take to get the certificate?
    With a request made on your own — up to 30 calendar days from receipt, with the decision to issue within 10 working days (art. 16 of Law No. 2297-VI). For a lawyer's request the law gives 5 working days, extendable to 20 (art. 24 of Law No. 5076-VI), but in practice timeframes are currently unpredictable: some people receive the certificate in 5 working days, others in 15 or 20. Order with time to spare.
    Is the border crossing certificate (dovidka pro peretyn kordonu) available in Diia?
    No. We did not find such a service in Diia, at a TsNAP or through a consulate. According to media reports, the DPSU online account only shows restrictions on leaving the country, not the history of crossings. The certificate is issued by the GTsOSI DPSU on a written request.
    Does a DPSU certificate replace a document from Reserve+?
    No. The certificate shows the date, time, border checkpoint and direction of the crossing, but the grounds for departure are not mentioned in descriptions of the response. So it confirms the fulfilment of military obligations only indirectly and does not replace documents on military registration, deferment or exemption.
    Will the certificate be accepted in Spain for temporary protection?
    There is no guarantee. EU Decision 2026/1912 names a lawful border crossing or an exit stamp as proof, but Spain has not published the list of accepted documents. The Embassy of Ukraine requested it on 12 August 2026; as of 1 October 2026 no response has been published. The European Commission does not name the DPSU certificate explicitly: each country assesses such documents itself.
    I already have temporary protection in Spain. Do I need the certificate?
    Most likely not. The new condition does not apply to those who were under protection in this country on 4 August 2026 and keep it without interruption. For switching to another residence permit, arraigo and long-term residence the certificate is not required either: SEM Instruction 2/2026 and the Immigration Regulation (Royal Decree 1155/2024) do not mention it.
