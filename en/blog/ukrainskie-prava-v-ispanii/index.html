    Ukrainian driving licence in Spain: how long you can drive on it and how to exchange it without mistakes

    Krepko · updated 11 November 2026 · ~9 min read
    In short. Under temporary protection, a Ukrainian licence is valid for as long as the protection itself is in force: there is no fixed date such as “until the end of the year”. Without temporary protection, a foreign licence has six months from the start of your residence. Exchanging it for a Spanish licence for categories A and B involves no exams; for C and D a practical test in real traffic (prueba de circulación) is scheduled.

    If you have temporary protection, you can drive on a Ukrainian licence in Spain and throughout the European Union for as long as the protection is in force, and you do not have to exchange it. The European Union has extended the protection until 4 March 2028. If you do not have protection, a foreign licence is valid for six months from the start of your residence; after that you cannot drive without exchanging it.

    Below we explain which case is yours, when an exchange is still worth doing, how it works step by step and in which cases a licence will not be exchanged. The rules and figures were checked against the BOE and the DGT website on 1 October 2026.

    With temporary protection: the licence is valid while the protection is

    Holders of temporary protection are covered by a separate European Union rule, Regulation (EU) 2022/1280. Under its Article 3.1, Ukrainian licences are recognised in all EU countries for as long as temporary protection applies to the holder. No exchange is required, and neither a sworn translation nor an international driving permit is needed (art. 3.2). The Directorate-General for Traffic (DGT) page on Ukraine also states the general six-month rule, but for holders of temporary protection it is overridden by the EU regulation: the DGT refers to it at the start of that same page.

    The period is tied to the protection itself, and the European Union has extended it until 4 March 2028 (Decision (EU) 2026/1912). Spain has so far extended the temporary protection TIE cards only until 4 March 2027 (Orden INT/96/2026). The DGT also still relies on the previous extension: the DGT page on Ukraine refers to Decision (EU) 2025/1460, and the date of 4 March 2027 is given in the DGT information note of 4 August 2025. As of 1 October 2026 there is no Spanish order extending the cards to 2028. At the same time, under Article 3.1 of the regulation and under that same DGT note, Ukrainian licences are recognised for as long as temporary protection is in force, without being tied to a specific date. So a new extension of the protection also extends the recognition of licences.

    There are, however, two conditions. First: the licence must be valid. First-time Ukrainian licences issued for two years that expired after 31 December 2021 are considered valid throughout martial law and for one more year after it. Other Ukrainian licences are valid for 30 years (15 years for those issued from 10 July 2026, and 5 years for categories C, D and T), and old ones have no expiry date, so this extension does not concern them. Second: if you lose temporary protection before the general end date, recognition of your licence under the regulation ends for you immediately.

    The DGT's special procedure for Ukraine applies to Ukrainian citizens, as well as stateless persons and citizens of other countries who lived in Ukraine before 24 February 2022, and members of their families. It also covers Ukrainian citizens who lived in Spain before 24 February 2022, including those without documents, and cannot return because of the war.

    When an exchange is worth doing even though it is not mandatory. If you are planning to switch from temporary protection to another residence permit. After the change of status the EU regulation no longer applies to you, and the DGT does not officially say from what date the general six months should be counted in that case. That is why we advise exchanging your licence while the protection is in force. This is our recommendation, not a legal requirement. There is also a practical reason not to delay: with a valid TIE card the application can be filed straight away, and the temporary protection cards have so far been extended only until 4 March 2027.

    Without temporary protection: six months from the start of residence

    If you live in Spain on another basis, the general rule of Royal Decree (RD) 818/2009 applies. A foreign licence is valid for no more than six months from the moment you became normally resident (art. 21.2.c). Normal residence means at least 185 days in a calendar year while lawfully present in the country.

    After six months you cannot drive on a Ukrainian licence until you exchange it (art. 21.3 and 22.1). You can apply for the exchange straight after obtaining residence (art. 22.2) and also later, once the six months have already passed: only driving without an exchange is restricted.

    How to exchange it: six steps

    The exchange is carried out under the agreement between Spain and Ukraine, in force since 31 October 2010 (BOE-A-2010-14841). There are no exams: neither theory nor driving. The exception is categories C1, C, D1, D and their variants with a trailer: for them a practical test in real traffic on public roads (prueba de circulación) is scheduled. The categories in the agreement's table (A1, A, B, C1, C, D1, D and the trailer variants BE, C1E, CE, D1E, DE) become the Spanish categories of the same name. The Ukrainian categories B1 (quad bikes and tricycles) and T (trams and trolleybuses) have no Spanish counterpart in the table, so they cannot be exchanged under the agreement.

    1. Check whether your licence qualifies for exchange
    The licence must be valid and obtained before you became a legal resident of Spain (for licences issued after 31 October 2010). Who cannot exchange is covered below.
    2. A medical check no earlier than 90 days before you apply
    The report on fitness to drive (informe de aptitud psicofísica) is issued by medical centres for drivers. It is valid for 90 days from the day after it is issued (RD 170/2010, art. 15.4), and you must file the application within that time. Categories A and B require group I, C and D group II. The centre itself sends the result and the photo to the DGT. There is no state tariff; when we checked in August 2026, the medical check cost about €50.
    3. Filing: online or by appointment
    Online through the DGT's electronic office, without an appointment. You will need Cl@ve or a digital certificate; you can also apply through a representative entered in the register of powers of representation (Registro de apoderamientos). The service has been running since 20 May 2025. You will come to the DGT office (Jefatura) once, at the end. The DGT does not guarantee a processing time: applications are handled in order of filing. In person by appointment through 060 or on the DGT website, the “canje UA” option. An appointment for an exchange is given only once: if you cancel it, the procedure and the check of your licence with the Ukrainian side start again from the beginning.
    4. The fee
    Tariff 2.3 (€28.87) for an exchange without exams, categories A1, A, B and B+E. Tariff 2.1 (€94.05) for categories C and D with the practical test; it includes two attempts. At the office you can pay only by card. The DGT does not name a separate fee for the temporary authorisation.
    5. Check of the licence with Ukraine
    The DGT checks the licence with the Ukrainian side in every case. The request is sent when you book an appointment or file the application online, so the check runs while you wait for your visit. Under the agreement, Ukraine has 30 calendar days to reply. If there is no reply within that time, under the agreement it is assumed that there are no records of the licence, and the exchange will not go through. No certificate from Ukraine is required from you for this.
    6. The final visit and the card
    The original Ukrainian licence is withdrawn: the DGT sends it to Ukraine and it will not be returned to you (agreement, point 8; RD 818/2009, art. 23.3). In its place you are given a temporary authorisation for six months, which lets you drive only in Spain. The plastic licence arrives by post at your home in roughly a month and a half, according to the DGT's estimate.

    The timeframes do not simply add up one after another. The check with Ukraine, up to 30 days, runs in parallel with waiting for the appointment or for the online application to be processed. For C and D there is also the wait for an appointment for the practical test. The month and a half it takes to produce the card does not stop you driving: at the final visit you are given a temporary authorisation straight away, and you can drive on it in Spain. There are no official statistics by province, so we do not give specific numbers of weeks.

    Documents: what you need and what is unnecessary

    • the application: the online form or the DGT form Mod. 03 “Trámites de conductores”. In it you declare that you have not been deprived of the right to drive by a court decision and do not hold an EU country licence of the same category;
    • the original identity document: passport, DNI or residence card;
    • proof of residence: under temporary protection this is the receipt for the application (resguardo) or the decision granting protection together with the TIE card. If you submit only the receipt or the decision, six months must have passed since they were issued or since your confirmed entry. Those who lived in Spain before 24 February 2022 prove residence with a valid TIE card;
    • proof that you were not living in Spain when you obtained the licence: a TIE card or a certificado de registro is suitable for this;
    • the original valid Ukrainian licence. If you fall under the DGT's special procedure for Ukraine (who it covers is described above) and have reported the theft or loss of the licence, you do not have to bring the original;
    • the medical report: the centre sends it;
    • payment of the fee.

    You do not need to bring a municipal registration certificate separately: form Mod. 03 states explicitly that the DGT itself checks identity, municipal registration and lawful residence. If the check fails, you will be asked to send the document later. A sworn translation of the licence, a certificate from the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine (MVS) with an apostille and utility bills are not on the DGT list. Separate photos are usually not needed either: the medical centre sends them.

    When an exchange will not be possible

    • The licence is not valid. The agreement and the DGT require the licence to be in force. There is no window for licences that expired “no more than ten years ago”.
    • The licence was obtained in Ukraine when you were already legally living in Spain, if this happened after 31 October 2010 (agreement, point 3).
    • The Ukrainian licence was itself obtained in exchange for a licence from another country (agreement, point 11). In the DGT leaflet this restriction is worded more narrowly, only for licences from countries without an agreement with Spain, so it is worth checking such a case with the DGT before applying.
    • Licences from Russia, Kazakhstan or Armenia. Spain has no exchange agreement with these countries: after six months of residence you have to take the exams for a Spanish licence. With Georgia and Moldova there are agreements.

    If your licence has expired, there are two options: renew it in Ukraine or take the exams in Spain. In December 2023 the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine added Spain to the list of countries to which licences exchanged online through the “Кабінет водія” (Driver's Account) service can be delivered. Whether the service works now, check with a service centre of the Ministry of Internal Affairs.

    Before renewing your licence in Ukraine from Spain. A licence obtained while resident in Spain cannot be exchanged. How the DGT treats licences that were renewed or replaced in Ukraine after moving has not been officially clarified. Ask at your DGT office before renewing the licence, otherwise you risk ending up with a licence whose exchange will be in question.

    Mistakes that cost months

    • A medical check “in advance”. The report lasts 90 days. If your appointment falls later than that, you will have to take the medical check and pay for it again.
    • A cancelled appointment. An appointment for an exchange is given only once. After cancelling, everything starts again, including the check of the licence with Ukraine.
    • An exchange right before a trip abroad. The original is withdrawn, and the temporary authorisation is valid only in Spain. To drive abroad before the card arrives, you need an international driving permit (Permiso Internacional).
    • A change of status without a plan for your licence. Recognition under the EU regulation ends together with temporary protection.
    • Unnecessary documents instead of the required ones. A translation, a certificate from the Ministry of Internal Affairs and electricity bills are not needed. But proof of residence and the medical report are essential.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I drive on a Ukrainian licence in 2027?
    Under temporary protection, yes, for as long as it is in force: the licence is tied to the protection, not to a calendar date. The European Union has extended the protection until 4 March 2028. Spanish temporary protection TIE cards have so far been extended until 4 March 2027, and the DGT still relies on the previous extension to that date (note of 4 August 2025). But under Article 3.1 of Regulation (EU) 2022/1280 and under that same note, Ukrainian licences are recognised for as long as temporary protection is in force, without being tied to a specific date, so a new extension of the protection also extends the recognition of licences. Without temporary protection, a Ukrainian licence is valid for six months from the start of your residence.
    How long is the medical certificate for a licence exchange valid?
    90 days from the day after it is issued (RD 170/2010, art. 15.4). You must file the exchange application within that time. If you miss it, you take the medical check again, so there is no point doing it long before the filing date.
    Do I need a translation of the licence or a certificate from the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine?
    No. The DGT list of documents for an exchange includes neither a translation nor a certificate from Ukraine. The DGT itself checks the licence with the Ukrainian side, which has 30 calendar days to reply. To drive on a Ukrainian licence under temporary protection, a translation and an international permit are not needed either (Regulation (EU) 2022/1280, art. 3.2).
    Will my Ukrainian licence be returned to me after the exchange?
    No. The original is withdrawn and sent to Ukraine through diplomatic channels (agreement between Spain and Ukraine, point 8; RD 818/2009, art. 23.3). In its place you are given a temporary authorisation for six months, which lets you drive only in Spain. The plastic licence arrives by post in roughly a month and a half, according to the DGT's estimate.
    How much does a licence exchange cost in 2026?
    The DGT fee: €28.87 for categories A and B (tariff 2.3) or €94.05 for C and D with the practical test (tariff 2.1, two attempts). You pay by card; cash is not accepted at the office. The medical check is paid separately: there is no state tariff, and when we checked in August 2026, it cost about €50.
    I got my Ukrainian licence when I was already living in Spain. Will it be exchanged?
    If the licence was issued after 31 October 2010, when you were already a legal resident of Spain, no: the agreement only allows the exchange of licences obtained in the country where you were living at the time. The only option left is to take the exams in Spain. For licences renewed or replaced in Ukraine after moving there is no official clarification; this question is worth asking at the DGT office.
    For how long is a Spanish licence issued after an exchange?
    Categories A and B for 10 years, after the age of 65 for 5 years. Categories C and D for 5 years, after the age of 65 for 3 years (RD 818/2009, art. 12).
