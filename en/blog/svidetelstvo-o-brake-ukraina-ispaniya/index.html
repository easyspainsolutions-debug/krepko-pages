    Marriage certificate from Ukraine without leaving Spain: duplicate, extract (vytiah), apostille

    Krepko · 5 November 2026 · ~8 min read
    In short. A Ukrainian marriage certificate is requested in Spain for family reunification, for a residence permit for the spouse of a digital nomad or of a Spanish citizen, for Spanish nationality, for the IMV benefit (ingreso mínimo vital), for registering a child and for a divorce. If you do not have the original, a reissued certificate (a duplicate) can be obtained without leaving Spain through a representative holding a power of attorney or a lawyer in Ukraine, or at the Embassy of Ukraine in Madrid; it has the same force as the first one, but it is not issued after a divorce — instead you get a marriage extract (vytiah). A document issued in Ukraine is apostilled for Spain, but an apostille is not placed on a Soviet form, so you need a reissued document of the modern type. Spanish rules set no single period of validity: for family reunification no period is stated, while for nationality the document must be valid on the date of application. Rules checked on 5 November 2026.

    What a Ukrainian marriage document is needed for in Spain

    Spanish authorities verify a marriage registered in Ukraine using the Ukrainian document. The table shows the procedures in which it is requested and what to order. If the modern-type original is intact and kept by relatives in Ukraine, a reissued one is usually not needed: they hand it over to our team, and we arrange the apostille. If there is no original or no one to hand it over, the Krepko team in Ukraine requests a reissued certificate or an extract without you having to travel — marriage certificate from Ukraine.

    What it is for in Spain Which document from Ukraine Apostille, translation, validity
    Family reunification (reagrupación) Marriage certificate; if it is lost, a reissued one: documents proving the family relationship are attached to the application (art. 68.3 RD 1155/2024). If the marriage is not your first, you also need to prove the dissolution of the previous one and the situation of the former spouse and children Apostille and a translation by a sworn translator. The Ministry of Inclusion information sheet (Hoja 8) states a validity period only for the housing report — six months; there is none for documents proving kinship
    Residence permit for the family of a digital nomad Marriage certificate: the UGE guidance note for family members names it explicitly. More in the article on the nomad's family Apostille; a translation by a sworn translator or a consular translation legalised at the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The guidance note sets no validity period
    Residence permit for the spouse of a Spanish citizen A document proving the marriage (art. 96 RD 1155/2024) Apostille and a translation into Spanish; more in the article on nationality through marriage
    Spouse of a long-term resident Marriage certificate for reunification. The spouse's first permit is valid until your card expires, but for at least a year, and the next one will be long-term (art. 68.9 RD 1155/2024) As for family reunification
    Nationality by residence, if you are married Marriage certificate from the country where the marriage was concluded (Orden JUS/1625/2016, annex, para. 1.a.7.º) Apostille and translation; the document must be valid on the date of application (para. 4 of the annex)
    Nationality through marriage to a Spanish citizen Marriage extract from the Spanish Civil Registry (para. 2.d of the annex) — in addition to the general list, which also includes the marriage certificate of the country where the marriage was concluded (para. 1.a.7.º).     IMV benefit Household composition is proven with the family book (libro de familia), a Civil Registry document and municipal registration (empadronamiento) data (art. 21.4 of Law 19/2021). More in the article on benefits Check the form of the Ukrainian document with the INSS before ordering
    Registering a child born in Spain The parents' marriage certificate: if the marriage to the mother is proven by a document, the father is registered as her husband (art. 44.4.a of Law 20/2011).     New surname or marital status with a residence permit Marriage certificate; after a divorce — a document on the dissolution and an extract confirming the pre-marriage surname Notify the police within a month, attaching the documents (art. 213 RD 1155/2024); relatives of Spanish citizens notify Extranjería (the immigration office) or the police within two months (art. 98.2)
    Divorce in a Spanish court Marriage certificate: a document on the registration of the marriage is attached to the petition (arts. 770 and 777 of the Civil Procedure Act, LEC) Apostille (art. 323 LEC) and a translation into Spanish (art. 144 LEC)

    To obtain long-term residence (larga duración nacional), a marriage certificate is not needed: as a general rule, each person earns the right to it with their own five years of legal and continuous residence (art. 183.1 RD 1155/2024), and it is not on the list of documents (art. 184.3). Years under temporary protection count towards the 5 years for larga duración nacional; you cannot apply for larga duración-UE from a temporary protection card; for nationality the question has not been officially resolved. More in the articles on larga duración and on family reunification.

    Reissued certificate or extract: what to order

    A reissued marriage certificate is issued by the civil registry office (DRACS) on the basis of the civil status record, marked «Повторно» (Reissued) (Civil Status Registration Rules, Order No. 52/5 of the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine, section IV, para. 5); the Embassy of Ukraine in Spain states plainly that it has the same force as the one issued the first time. If the original is lost, stolen or damaged, you can apply to any DRACS office, wherever the marriage was registered (ibid., para. 2). The routes from Spain are the same as for the birth certificate.

    The main difference in the case of marriage is divorce. Once a marriage has been dissolved, the marriage certificate is not reissued: upon a written application you get a marriage extract that confirms the pre-marriage surname (ibid., section IV, para. 4). If the Spanish authority needs the whole history of the marriage together with its dissolution, the closest to that is a full extract of the marriage record: it lists all changes in order (Instruction on maintaining the register, Order No. 1269/5, section III, para. 3.4). Whether a particular authority will accept an extract instead of a certificate has not been officially determined, so it is better to show it a sample before ordering.

    If the marriage was dissolved by a court after the 2010 changes to the Family Code, there is usually no divorce certificate: the divorce is proven by the court decision, and a note is entered in the marriage record. Which document to order in your case is explained on the page divorce certificate from Ukraine.

    How to get the document while you are in Spain

    • Through a representative — with a power of attorney certified under the Law of Ukraine “On Notaries”, or through a lawyer (Rules No. 52/5, section IV, para. 4). A power of attorney for this can also be executed before a notary in Spain; why we check its text against DRACS requirements before signing is explained in the section on the power of attorney.
    • At the Embassy of Ukraine in Madrid: since 15 July 2024 it has been issuing reissued certificates and extracts itself. A Ukrainian apostille is not placed on an embassy document, and whether a Spanish authority will accept it is not officially clear (more).
    • Through your parents — you should not count on this: regardless of the child's age, the rules explicitly allow parents to obtain only the child's birth certificate.

    Men aged 18–60 currently need a valid electronic military registration document for consular services, including at the embassy (art. 20 of the Law of Ukraine on the legal regime of martial law; the verification procedure is set by Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 981 of 29 July 2026). A Spanish notary does not ask for such a document. Which routes remain without it is covered in the article on marital status.

    A Soviet form is not apostilled. The Rules for affixing the apostille of 17 March 2023 do not accept originals of documents issued by authorities of the former Soviet republics of the USSR, except documents on education and academic titles of the Ukrainian SSR (para. 4). If the certificate was issued on a Soviet form, a reissued certificate of the modern type is ordered for Spain. If the old original is intact, state in the application why you need the document, for example for an apostille: the “any office” rule is tied to loss or damage, and the office may refer you to the place where the record is kept (more).

    Translation and names in Latin script

    The translation is done after the apostille, so that the apostille is translated too. For family reunification, the Ministry of Inclusion information sheet requires a translation by a sworn translator (traductor jurado), while the UGE guidance note for a nomad's family also allows a consular translation followed by legalisation at the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs. A translation made in Ukraine can be legalised, but the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs warns that some Spanish authorities may not accept it.

    A marriage certificate names both spouses, often also the wife's pre-marriage surname, and all of it in Cyrillic: the Latin spelling comes from the translator. We have not found a Spanish rule on how Ukrainian names should be rendered, and in practice authorities check the translation against the passport and TIE. So before the translation, collect the spelling of all names as it appears in the passports and cards (more about names).

    Frequently asked questions

    My marriage certificate is still in Ukraine. How can I get it without travelling?
    If the modern-type original is intact and kept by relatives, a reissued one is usually not needed: they hand it over to our team in Ukraine, and we arrange the apostille. If there is no original or no one to hand it over, a reissued certificate or an extract is obtained by a representative holding a power of attorney or by a lawyer in Ukraine (Rules No. 52/5, section IV, para. 4). The Embassy of Ukraine in Madrid issues them itself, but to men aged 18–60 it currently does so only with a valid electronic military registration document; a Ukrainian apostille is not placed on an embassy document, and whether a Spanish authority will accept it is not officially clear. In our experience, a reissued certificate with an apostille is ready in 3–4 weeks including delivery, longer in some regions.
    We are divorced. Can I get a reissued marriage certificate?
    No. After a divorce the marriage certificate is not reissued: upon application you get a marriage extract that confirms the pre-marriage surname (Rules No. 52/5, section IV, para. 4). If the authority needs the whole history of the marriage, a full extract of the marriage record is ordered: it also shows the note of dissolution. Which document proves the divorce itself is explained on the page divorce certificate.
    Is a marriage certificate needed for larga duración?
    For larga duración nacional — no: as a general rule, each person earns the right to it with their own five years of legal and continuous residence, and the certificate is not on the list of documents (arts. 183.1 and 184.3 RD 1155/2024). It is needed if you are already a long-term resident and are bringing your husband or wife through reunification: documents on the marriage are attached to the application, and the spouse's next permit after the first one will be long-term (art. 68 RD 1155/2024).
    Must the certificate be no more than three months old?
    There is no general rule. The Ministry of Inclusion information sheet on family reunification and the UGE guidance note set no validity period for the marriage certificate, while for nationality the document must be valid on the date of application (Orden JUS/1625/2016, annex, para. 4). A particular authority may set its own period, so ask it before ordering.
    I got married and changed my surname. Do I need to report this for my TIE?
    Yes. A foreign national authorised to be in Spain reports a change in marital status to the police within a month and attaches supporting documents (art. 213 RD 1155/2024); relatives of Spanish citizens report it to Extranjería or the police within two months (art. 98.2). The link between the pre-marriage and the new surname is proven by the marriage certificate. Check with your office in what form it will be accepted, and check the spelling of names against your passport in advance.
    My husband is a Spanish citizen, and we married in Ukraine. What is needed for nationality after a year?
    For nationality through marriage, the ministry asks for a marriage extract from the Spanish Registro Civil (Orden JUS/1625/2016, annex, para. 2.d). This is in addition to the general list, which for married applicants also includes the certificate of the country where the marriage was concluded (para. 1.a.7.º). A marriage concluded in Ukraine is first entered in the Spanish register on the basis of the Ukrainian certificate with an apostille and translation (arts. 59.2 and 98 of Law 20/2011). Timeframes and conditions are in the article on nationality through marriage.
    The certificate was issued back in the USSR. What should I do with it?
    For Spain the certificate needs an apostille, and in Ukraine apostilles are not placed on original Soviet documents (Rules for affixing the apostille of 17.03.2023, para. 4). So a reissued certificate of the modern type is ordered, or, if the marriage has been dissolved, an extract. You can get the document without travelling: through a representative holding a power of attorney or through our team in Ukraine.
