    Divorce certificate from Ukraine without leaving Spain: which document to order and what the Registro Civil accepts

    Krepko · 24 November 2026 · ~8 min read
    In short. If the marriage was dissolved by DRACS, the divorce is proven by a divorce certificate; it can be reissued. If a court dissolved the marriage after 27 July 2010, there is no certificate: the divorce document is the court decision that has entered into force, and a note is entered in the marriage record. For the marriage file, the civil registries of Madrid and Valencia ask for a full record of the previous marriage with a note of the divorce, and Valencia does not accept a court decision; the closest to this in Ukraine is a full extract (vytiah) of the marriage record. DRACS and court documents for Spain are apostilled by the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine, and then a sworn translator translates them.

    Most often a divorce document from Ukraine is needed in Spain before a new marriage: the Registro Civil (civil registry) opens a marriage file (expediente matrimonial) and wants to see that the previous marriage has been dissolved. The original, meanwhile, has often stayed in Ukraine, and which document exactly to order depends on how the marriage was dissolved: at the civil registry office (DRACS) or through a court.

    Below: what divorce documents exist in Ukraine, how to obtain them without leaving Spain, what the civil registries of Madrid and Valencia write in their lists and what Spanish law says about recognising a foreign divorce. We checked the rules and official pages on 24 November 2026.

    Which document proves a divorce in Ukraine?

    The Family Code of Ukraine provides for two ways of dissolving a marriage, and each leaves its own document. The Ministry of Justice of Ukraine describes them as follows in its explanation of 13 May 2026.

    How the marriage was dissolved Which document remains What to order if you do not have it
    At DRACS: couples without children on a joint application, and also on the application of one spouse if the other has been declared missing or legally incapable (arts. 106–107 of the Family Code) Divorce certificate; the marriage ends on the day the divorce is registered (art. 114) A reissued divorce certificate or a divorce extract
    Through a court before 27 July 2010 Court decision; the divorce was then registered at DRACS on the spouses' application and a certificate was issued If the divorce was registered at DRACS — a reissued certificate. If not, according to the Ministry of Justice explanation, such a dissolution is subject to registration
    Through a court after 27 July 2010 The court decision that has entered into force is itself the divorce document (art. 115); the court sends it to DRACS for a note in the marriage record. The marriage ends on the day the decision enters into force (art. 114) A copy of the decision from the court that issued it, and a full extract of the marriage record: in this case DRACS does not draw up a divorce record

    A full extract from the State Register of Civil Status Acts contains all the details of the record, the changes and other information entered in it; a standard extract contains only selected details (this is how the Ministry of Justice describes them, referring to Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 1064). That is why, for the history of a marriage with a divorce, it is a full extract of the marriage record that is ordered. And to confirm the pre-marriage surname after a court divorce, the Diia portal recommends a marriage extract.

    After a divorce, a reissued marriage certificate usually no longer helps: what is issued instead is covered in the article on the marriage certificate from Ukraine.

    How to get the document without leaving Spain?

    A reissued certificate is issued by DRACS on the basis of the civil status record. It has the same force as the one issued the first time (Ministry of Justice explanation of 24 June 2026, referring to art. 19 of the Law on State Registration of Civil Status Acts and Rules No. 52/5, section IV). The routes from Spain are:

    • A representative in Ukraine. A reissued certificate and an extract are issued to a third party holding a power of attorney certified under the Law of Ukraine “On Notaries”, or to a lawyer with a warrant (order) and a copy of the legal aid agreement. Under martial law you can apply to any DRACS office in government-controlled territory, and for an extract — regardless of where the marriage was registered. A power of attorney for this can also be executed before a Spanish notary — how this works is explained in the article on a power of attorney from Spain for Ukraine.
    • The consulate of Ukraine. The Ministry of Justice states that for citizens living abroad, DRACS documents are requested through diplomatic missions and consular posts of Ukraine. For men aged 18–60, consular services come with their own conditions: we covered them in the article on consular services for men.
    • Diia. A reissued certificate and a divorce extract are ordered there online with a Ukrainian electronic signature (KEP or Diia.Signature), and the finished document is sent by Ukrposhta (Ukrainian Post). On the day we checked, both services were marked as temporarily unavailable.

    With a court decision the route is different: for a copy, Diia refers you to the court that heard the case. For Spain, it matters that the document shows that the decision has entered into force: more on this below.

    What does the Registro Civil require for a new marriage?

    The lists of documents for the marriage file are published by the civil registries themselves, and they differ from city to city. As of 24 November 2026:

    • Madrid (page updated on 8 October 2026): if one of the future spouses is divorced, a full record of the previous marriage (certificado literal) with a marginal note of the divorce is required. As a general rule, foreign nationals are also asked for a document on marital status, a translation by a sworn translator and legalisation of documents (for Ukrainian ones — an apostille).
    • Valencia: the same full record of the previous marriage with a note of the divorce, and it states explicitly that a court decision is not accepted. The birth certificate must have been issued no more than a year earlier, and foreign documents must carry an apostille and a translation by a sworn translator.

    The Ukrainian document closest to such a record is a full extract of the marriage record: it shows both the marriage and the note of its dissolution. A court decision or a divorce certificate also proves the divorce, but the Spanish authority may ask specifically for the marriage record. Whether your civil registry will accept a full extract as a certificado literal is not officially established anywhere, so it is better to agree the sample and the wording of the requirement before ordering.

    A divorce document only states that a specific marriage has ended. It does not prove that you have not entered into a new one: for that you need a separate document, which we covered in the article on proving marital status.

    How does Spain recognise a foreign divorce?

    A foreign divorce can be entered in the Spanish register under Law 20/2011 on the Civil Registry. In brief, without procedural details:

    • Court decision (art. 96). Only decisions that have become final (firmeza) are entered in the register. There are two routes: exequatur (exequátur) in court, or a decision by the civil registrar (Encargado), who checks the authenticity of the documents, that the court of the country of origin based its jurisdiction on criteria equivalent to Spanish ones, that the parties were duly notified, and that there is no manifest conflict with Spanish public policy. The same regime applies to decisions of non-judicial authorities on matters that in Spain fall to the courts.
    • A document of a non-judicial authority (art. 97) may serve as the basis for an entry if the authority was competent under the law of its country, performed functions equivalent to those of Spanish authorities, the act is valid under the rules of Spanish private international law, and the entry is not manifestly contrary to Spanish public policy.
    • An extract from a foreign register (art. 98). If it merely reflects a court decision, the basis for the entry is the decision itself, and it is recognised under art. 96. By the logic of this provision, this also covers an extract in which the divorce is noted on the basis of a court decision.

    Exequatur is heard by a court of first instance; the parties in these proceedings are represented by a procurador (court representative) and a lawyer, and the application is accompanied by the decision with an apostille, proof that it has entered into force and a translation (Law 29/2015 on international legal cooperation in civil matters, arts. 52 and 54).

    Law 20/2011 does not say explicitly whether a Ukrainian divorce must be recognised before the marriage file, and the Madrid and Valencia lists do not mention exequatur. The marriage file is handled by the civil registry or a notary (art. 58 of the same law), and it is they who set the document requirements, so this question is worth asking at the first visit. How the marriage file itself works is described on the page about getting married in Spain.

    Which apostille and translation do you need?

    Spain and Ukraine are both parties to the Hague Apostille Convention (Spain since 1978, Ukraine since 22 December 2003), so a Ukrainian document for Spain needs an apostille, not consular legalisation. On documents of justice authorities, including DRACS, and of courts, it is affixed by the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine (Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 61). The apostille is placed on the original, so you first obtain the document and then have it apostilled. Since 1 February 2026 this has been done under a new procedure (Ministry of Justice Order No. 3177/5) through the Electronic Register of Apostilles.

    The translation is done after the apostille, so that the apostille is translated too. The Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs writes that a translation by a sworn translator recognised by the ministry is valid in Spain in its own right, while some Spanish authorities may not accept a translation made abroad. We arrange the sworn translation; more on the order of steps in the article on the apostille and translation.

    Check the names before the translation. In a divorce document the names of both former spouses are written in Cyrillic, and the wife's pre-marriage surname often appears too. The Latin spelling comes from the translator, and the civil registry checks it against the passport and TIE, so collect in advance the spelling of the names as it appears in your documents.

    In what order should you proceed?

    1. Ask the civil registry or the notary where you are opening the marriage file which document on the previous marriage they need, and ask for the wording in writing.
    2. Determine how the marriage was dissolved: at DRACS, through a court before 27 July 2010 or after. This determines what to order.
    3. Order the document in Ukraine through a representative holding a power of attorney or through the consulate, or entrust it to us; for the history of the marriage — a full extract of the marriage record.
    4. Have the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine apostille the original, then order the sworn translation.
    5. Mind the time limits: in Valencia, for example, the birth certificate is accepted only if issued no more than a year earlier, so it is better to order documents with a specific submission date in mind.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I get a divorce certificate from Ukraine if I live in Spain?
    A reissued divorce certificate or an extract is issued to a representative holding a notarial power of attorney or to a lawyer; under martial law, at any DRACS office in government-controlled territory. The Ministry of Justice of Ukraine also states that for citizens abroad, DRACS documents are requested through consular posts. The power of attorney can be executed before a Spanish notary. For Spain, the finished document is apostilled by the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine.
    We divorced through a court, and I have no divorce certificate. Is that normal?
    Yes, if the court dissolved the marriage after 27 July 2010. Since then the document proving a court divorce is the court decision itself once it has entered into force, and DRACS only enters a note in the marriage record (art. 115 of the Family Code of Ukraine, according to the Ministry of Justice explanation). In this case no separate divorce record is drawn up. For Spain you usually need a copy of the decision and a full extract of the marriage record.
    Will a Spanish civil registry accept a Ukrainian court decision on divorce for a new marriage?
    It depends on the civil registry. Madrid and Valencia ask for a full record of the previous marriage with a note of the divorce, and Valencia states explicitly that it does not accept a court decision. The closest to such a record in Ukraine is a full extract of the marriage record. It is better to agree the format with your civil registry before ordering.
    Is exequatur of a Ukrainian divorce needed to marry in Spain?
    Law 20/2011 does not say so explicitly, and the Madrid and Valencia document lists do not mention exequatur. To enter a foreign divorce decision in the Spanish register, you need either exequatur in court or a check by the civil registrar under the conditions of art. 96 of that law. Whether this is needed in your case is decided by whoever handles the marriage file (the civil registry or a notary), so ask at the first visit.
    Does a Ukrainian divorce certificate need an apostille for Spain?
    Yes. Both countries are parties to the Hague Convention, and on DRACS and court documents the apostille is affixed by the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine. After the apostille, the document is translated by a sworn translator: the civil registries of Madrid and Valencia require exactly this kind of translation.
    Can I order a divorce extract through Diia from Spain?
    The service exists, but on the day we checked, 24 November 2026, Diia marked it as temporarily unavailable. In addition, ordering online requires a Ukrainian electronic signature (KEP or Diia.Signature), and the finished document is sent by Ukrposhta. That is why from Spain people more often act through a representative holding a power of attorney.
