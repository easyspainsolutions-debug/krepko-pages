    Income certificate from Ukraine for Spain: which one to order and where

    Krepko · 24 November 2026 · ~7 min read
    In short. Official income in Ukraine is confirmed by information from the State Register of Individuals issued by the State Tax Service of Ukraine (DPS): it is issued free of charge, within three working days, including to a representative acting under a notarised power of attorney. The Pension Fund issues OK-5 and OK-7 (insurance record and salary) and a pension amount certificate. The tax service itself apostilles DPS information; the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Ukraine apostilles Pension Fund documents. For immigration procedures the document is submitted with a translation into Spanish; a translation by a sworn translator is considered official.

    A Spanish procedure asks you to show your income, and your income is Ukrainian: a pension, a salary or savings built up over years of work. In Ukraine, “income certificate” is the name given to several different documents, and they are issued by different authorities: the tax service, the Pension Fund, your employer.

    Below: what each document shows, which one is suitable for a residence permit, a bank and the tax office, who affixes the apostille to it and how to obtain it without leaving Spain. We checked the rules on 24 November 2026; the sources are at the end of the article.

    Which Ukrainian documents confirm income?

    Document Issued by What it shows
    Information from the State Register of Individuals – Taxpayers (commonly called “VDRFO”) Tax service (DPS) Sources and amounts of accrued income, tax and military levy withheld and paid
    OK-5 Pension Fund of Ukraine (PFU) Insurance record and the salary that will be taken into account when the pension is awarded, for periods from 2000 (sometimes from 1998)
    OK-7 Pension Fund Salary (income) actually accrued and insurance record from 2011
    Pension amount certificate Pension Fund Amount of the pension awarded
    Certificate from your place of work Employer Position and salary for a period: whatever the employer has chosen to state

    DPS information and PFU documents are drawn from state registers. Under the Regulation on the State Register of Individuals, DPS information certifies whether or not the register contains data about a person: if income did not go through official reporting, it will not appear in the document. An employer's certificate is a company document, not one issued by a public authority, and an apostille is not affixed to it directly (more on this below).

    Which document do you need for a residence permit, a bank and the tax office?

    Residence permit without the right to work (residencia no lucrativa). The Immigration Regulation requires means of at least 400% of IPREM a month for the applicant and 100% of IPREM for each family member, or a regular source of income (Article 62 of RD 1155/2024). They can be proven by any means permitted by law. For foreign accounts, the Regulation lists what the document must contain: the bank's name and address, the account details, the balance on 31 December of the previous year and the average balance over the last year. A Ukrainian pension is exactly that kind of regular income, and it is confirmed by the PFU pension amount certificate. This permit is granted for living in Spain without working (Article 61), so a salary you continue to receive from a Ukrainian employer does not fit this ground. The same documents on means will also be needed when renewing (Article 64).

    Remote work. For this there is the digital nomad residence permit, with its own income threshold and its own set of evidence. We covered it separately in the article on income for the digital nomad visa; DPS information can supplement this set as confirmation that the income is official.

    Bank. A Spanish bank is required to establish the nature of your activity and to monitor transactions, including the source of funds (Ley 10/2010, Articles 5 and 6). If savings from Ukraine arrive in your account, DPS information for your years of work and OK-7 show that the sum was built up from an official salary. Account statements for the same period are usually added to them. The whole chain of documents is in the article on the source of funds for the bank.

    The tax office (Agencia Tributaria). A Spanish tax resident pays personal income tax (IRPF) on all income, wherever it was obtained (Ley 35/2006, Article 2). If the income has already been taxed in Ukraine, the lower of two amounts is deducted from the Spanish tax: the equivalent income tax paid abroad, or the tax calculated at the average Spanish rate on that part of the income (Article 80). DPS information shows both the income and the tax withheld, so for this deduction it is more convenient than an employer's certificate. Whether you are required to file a tax return is covered in the article on the IRPF tax return.

    Who affixes the apostille to the certificate?

    Spain (since 1978) and Ukraine (since 22 December 2003) are parties to the 1961 Hague Convention, so a Ukrainian public document for Spain is not legalised at a consulate but apostilled. Spanish authorities say exactly this in their information sheets: a foreign public document is accepted with legalisation or with an apostille from the country that issued it. Who affixes the apostille in Ukraine is determined by Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 61:

    • DPS information — the tax service itself: since 2023 it has been apostilling documents issued by the DPS and its territorial bodies.
    • OK-5, OK-7, pension amount certificate — the Pension Fund is not named in the list of authorities, and all other documents are apostilled by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Ukraine.
    • Employer's certificate — a company document, and such papers are not among the public documents listed in the Convention (Article 1). The solution is a notarised copy: a notary in Ukraine certifies that a copy of a document issued by a legal entity is true (Law of Ukraine “On Notaries”, Article 75), and notaries' documents are apostilled by the Ministry of Justice.

    An apostille confirms the signature and seal, not the content of the document. Whether a specific authority requires an apostille on a particular certificate and whether it will accept an electronic copy, we check before ordering. More about the procedure on the page “Apostille for a Ukrainian document”.

    Do you need a sworn translation?

    Documents from other countries are submitted for immigration procedures with a translation into Spanish — this is stated in the information sheets of the Ministry of Inclusion. The law considers official a translation by a sworn translator (traductor-intérprete jurado) appointed by the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs and a translation certified by the Ministry's Translation Office (Ley 2/2014, additional provision 16). A bank or the tax office may also accept a different translation, but that is their decision, so we check the requirements in advance. We arrange the sworn translation: we give the timeframe and cost once the document is in hand and its length is clear.

    How do you obtain the certificate without leaving Spain?

    DPS information. An application on form No. 10DR can be filed with any tax authority, not necessarily the one where you are registered. This can be done by a representative. They attach a notarised power of attorney to obtain information from the register, their own identity document and a clear copy of your passport. The information is prepared within three working days and issued free of charge (Regulation No. 822, Section X).

    Pension Fund documents. OK-5 and OK-7 based on PFU data are issued to the insured person themselves or to a representative whose authority has been formalised in accordance with the law. The pension amount certificate is obtained by a representative at a PFU service centre on presentation of their own identity document, the document confirming their authority and certified copies of the documents by which the fund will establish your identity.

    Power of attorney from Spain. It is drawn up before a Spanish notary with an apostille or at the Ukrainian consulate. How to choose the route and what to include in the text is covered in the article on a power of attorney from Spain to Ukraine.

    With a qualified electronic signature or a login via id.gov.ua, you can order DPS information in the tax service's Electronic Cabinet, and PFU documents on the PFU web portal.

    Where do people most often slip up?

    • The wrong document. An employer's certificate instead of information from the register, or OK-5 where the pension amount is needed. Start by asking who will accept the document and what it has to prove.
    • The wrong period. For a residence permit without the right to work, foreign accounts require the balance on 31 December of the previous year and the average balance over the last year (Article 62), while for a bank you need data for the years when the money was being saved.
    • Unofficial income. It is not in the register, and no authority will issue such a certificate. In that case other evidence is relied on: statements, contracts, documents on the sale of property.
    • How recent it is. The Regulation does not set a validity period for an income certificate, but amounts are assessed at the time of application (Article 62), and the receiving party may ask for a recent document. Time the certificate to your application date, not six months before it.
    • Name in the Latin alphabet. In the translation, your name must match your passport and TIE (foreigner identity card): a discrepancy of a single letter leads to unnecessary questions.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which income certificate from Ukraine do you need for a residence permit in Spain?
    It depends on the grounds. For a residence permit without the right to work, you show means of at least 400% of IPREM a month or a regular income (Article 62 of RD 1155/2024): a pension is confirmed by the Pension Fund's pension amount certificate, savings by bank documents. For remote work there is the digital nomad residence permit with its own set of evidence, and information from the tax service confirms that the income is official.
    Where can I get an income certificate from the Ukrainian tax service if I live in Spain?
    Information from the State Register of Individuals is issued by any tax authority in Ukraine upon application on form No. 10DR. It can be collected by a representative with a notarised power of attorney, their own identity document and a copy of your passport. It takes three working days and is free of charge (Regulation No. 822, Section X). The power of attorney is drawn up before a Spanish notary with an apostille or at the Ukrainian consulate.
    How do OK-5 and OK-7 differ?
    Both certificates are issued by the Pension Fund. OK-5 shows your insurance record and the salary that will be taken into account when your pension is awarded, for periods from 2000 (in some cases from 1998). OK-7 shows the salary actually accrued and your insurance record from 2011 onwards.
    Who affixes the apostille to an income certificate from Ukraine?
    For tax service information, the State Tax Service itself: since 2023 it has been apostilling the documents it issues. Pension Fund documents are apostilled by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Ukraine, as the PFU is not named separately in Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 61. For an employer's certificate, a notary in Ukraine makes a certified copy, and the Ministry of Justice apostilles it.
    Does an income certificate need a sworn translation?
    For immigration procedures, foreign documents are submitted with a translation into Spanish. In Spain, a translation by a sworn translator appointed by the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs is considered official (Ley 2/2014, additional provision 16). A bank or the tax office may set their own conditions, so it is best to find out the translation requirements before ordering.
    Will a certificate be issued if I worked unofficially?
    No. Tax service information shows only what is in the State Register, that is, income for which reports were filed. Unofficial earnings are confirmed by other documents: account statements, contracts, documents on the sale of property. Which of these will be accepted depends on who is asking for them.
