    Renouncing temporary protection: how to file it and what to do first

    Krepko · 1 November 2026 · ~8 min read
    In short. Temporary protection is renounced after your new residence permit has been approved and before you apply for the new TIE card (foreigner identity card): this is what instruction SEM 2/2026 requires. The renunciation is filed with the Ministry of the Interior, at the Directorate General for International Protection, using the form “Desistimiento o renuncia PT”: through the electronic registry with the subject “RENUNCIA PT” and the case number, or on paper at a registry office. The filing receipt is shown when applying for the new card. The renunciation is irreversible: protection ends automatically, and with it the recognition of Ukrainian driving licences under the EU regulation, so we advise exchanging your licence in advance.

    When your new residence permit is approved, temporary protection does not end by itself: you have to renounce it explicitly and at the right moment. Not before the approval, or you will be left without a status, and not after applying for the new card, because the renunciation receipt is shown at that stage. The renunciation is irreversible, and together with protection, the special regime for Ukrainian driving licences also ends.

    Below we cover when and where to file the renunciation, how to do it on behalf of a child and what to take care of in advance, starting with your driving licence. We checked the rules on 1 November 2026 against the BOE, the instruction of the State Secretariat for Migration and the Ministry of the Interior's pages on temporary protection.

    When to file the renunciation: after approval, before the new card

    The order is set by the seventh point of instruction SEM 2/2026 of 22 June 2026. Anyone who has been granted a new residence permit must explicitly renounce temporary protection (renunciar expresamente), and when applying for the TIE card under the new permit, show the receipt confirming that the renunciation has been filed. The rule is the same for all routes: change of status (modificación), arraigo (residence on the grounds of ties to Spain), residence for a relative of a Spanish citizen, the digital nomad visa and long-term residence.

    Renouncing before the approval is risky. Under art. 24.1.d RD 1325/2003, with an explicit renunciation protection ends automatically, and if the new permit has not yet been approved, you will be left without a status.

    Putting it off will not work either. The card under the new permit is applied for in person within a month from the day the permit was approved or came into force (art. 209.1 RD 1155/2024), and the renunciation is confirmed when you make that application. Only weeks remain between the approval and the renunciation, so everything that depends on protection is prepared while the new application is still being processed.

    First of all, this means your Ukrainian driving licence: its recognition in the EU is tied to protection. We handle the exchange of Ukrainian driving licences for Spanish ones, and it is better to start while protection is still in force. Dates matter here: the medical certificate for the exchange is valid for 90 days (art. 15.4 RD 170/2010), and an appointment for the exchange is given only once, so the exchange is planned in advance.

    Where and how to file the renunciation

    The renunciation is filed not with Extranjería (the immigration office), but with the Ministry of the Interior: at the Directorate General for International Protection (Dirección General de Protección Internacional), which also handles temporary protection cases. Its portal “Asilo y Refugio” has the form “Desistimiento o renuncia PT”. The same form is used both to withdraw an application that is still being processed (desistimiento) and to renounce protection already granted (renuncia). So state clearly in the form that this is a renunciation of protection. If the decision on protection has not yet been served on you, the renunciation, as the form's page says, will be processed only if the corresponding box is ticked in it.

    The Ministry of the Interior's guidance note on submitting documents requires the application to contain your first name and surname exactly as in your application for protection, your NIE without hyphens, your current address and a signature. It can be filed in two ways.

    • Electronically. Through the Registro Electrónico General of the public administration, logging in with a digital certificate or Cl@ve. In the subject field (asunto) you write “RENUNCIA PT” and the protection case number; the recipient is unit EA0054388 (Subdirección General de Instrucción de Protección Internacional y Apatridia). After submission, the registry issues a receipt with the entry number, date and time.
    • On paper. At a public administration registry office (oficina de asistencia en materia de registros): there they return the original and issue a receipt with the date and time of filing; visits are sometimes by appointment only. Another option is the Correos post office: the envelope is handed in open, so that the application is date-stamped and you are given a copy, and it is addressed to the Registro General del Ministerio del Interior, Calle Amador de los Ríos 7, 28010 Madrid. Filing documents through these channels is allowed by art. 16.4 of Law 39/2015.

    Keep the receipt: it is shown when applying for the new card. The temporary protection card must be handed in after the renunciation, as Order INT/96/2026 points out.

    How to renounce on behalf of a child

    The form's page says that for a minor the application is signed by the father, the mother or the guardian. This is written about withdrawing an application (desistimiento), but the form for withdrawal and renunciation is the same, and there is no separate rule there for renouncing on behalf of a child. The page says nothing about the second parent's signature. The application gives the child's details: first name and surname as in the child's application for protection, and the child's NIE.

    Everyone who applies for a card under the new residence permit needs a receipt, including a child: the seventh point of instruction SEM 2/2026 applies to all holders of protection who are granted a new permit. If the new residence permit was not approved for the whole family at once, file the renunciation only for those who already have approval, otherwise the others will be left without a status.

    Why the renunciation is irreversible

    With an explicit renunciation, protection ends automatically (art. 24.1.d RD 1325/2003). It can be obtained again only through a new application, on the conditions for new applicants.

    Since 5 August 2026 these conditions have changed. Under art. 2 of Council Decision (EU) 2026/1912 (as amended by the corrigendum of 5 August), protection is granted only to those who have fulfilled their military obligations in Ukraine; proof is presented when it is required. An exception is made for those whose protection in the same EU country was in force up to and including 4 August 2026 and continues without interruption. A renunciation is exactly such an interruption.

    What the renunciation does not take away: years under protection count towards the five years for larga duración nacional (national long-term residence) and are added to the time on the new permit; this is the sixth point of the same instruction SEM 2/2026. You cannot apply for larga duración-UE (EU long-term residence) from a protection card, and for citizenship the question has not been officially settled.

    What to do before the renunciation

    1. Exchange your driving licence. Ukrainian driving licences are recognised in the EU while temporary protection applies to you (art. 3.1 of EU Regulation 2022/1280), and the DGT repeats the same in its note of 4 August 2025. The renunciation ends protection, and with it this recognition. After that, the general rule applies: a foreign licence is valid for six months from the start of normal residence (art. 21.2.c RD 818/2009), but from which date this period is counted when your status changes, the DGT does not say. So we advise exchanging your licence while protection is still in force. This is our recommendation, not a legal requirement. It particularly concerns first Ukrainian licences issued for two years that expired after 31 December 2021: according to the DGT note, they are valid throughout martial law and for one more year after it, while art. 5 of the regulation recognises such an extension only for the purposes of Articles 3, 4 and 6, which concern holders of protection. How the DGT will treat such licences after a renunciation has not been officially clarified, and only a valid licence can be exchanged. How the exchange works is covered in the article on Ukrainian driving licences in Spain.
    2. Prepare a signature for the electronic registry. To file the renunciation from home, you need a digital certificate or Cl@ve. An FNMT certificate is issued to any adult with an NIE, and it will be useful later, for example for the online licence exchange with the DGT and for the tax office. We arrange a digital certificate and install it on your device. Without a signature, the remaining option is filing on paper at a registry office.
    3. Check your passport. To apply for a new residence permit you need a valid document: a copy of all pages of the passport is attached to the application. In guidance sheet Hoja 49 on long-term residence, for example, instead of a copy of the passport you can attach a copy of a travel document or a cédula de inscripción, and the document must be valid. If it is about to expire, the passport can be replaced in Spain. What you can sign before a notary while your passport has expired is covered in the article on an expired passport and the notary.
    4. Find your protection case number. It goes in the subject of the renunciation application, so find it in your protection documents in advance.
    5. Find out the deadline for your car. If you drive a car with Ukrainian number plates, the deadline by which it must be registered in Spain depends on your status and the dates in your documents. Find it out before the renunciation. On the page on customs clearance we describe how an imported car is registered, and we work out the deadline based on your documents.

    If you are still choosing which residence permit to switch to, the routes are collected on the page on switching from temporary protection. We will review your case; the application is handled by an immigration lawyer.

    Frequently asked questions

    When should I renounce temporary protection?
    After your new residence permit has been approved and before you apply for the TIE card under it: when applying for the card, you show the receipt confirming that the renunciation has been filed (the seventh point of instruction SEM 2/2026). The card is applied for within a month from the day the permit was approved or came into force (art. 209.1 RD 1155/2024), so only weeks remain for the renunciation. Renouncing before the approval is risky: with an explicit renunciation, protection ends automatically, and you will be left without a status until a decision on the new residence permit.
    How do I renounce temporary protection on behalf of a child?
    Using the same form “Desistimiento o renuncia PT” on the Ministry of the Interior's “Asilo y Refugio” portal. The form's page says that for a minor the application is signed by the father, the mother or the guardian. This is written about withdrawing an application, but the form for withdrawal and renunciation is the same, and it says nothing about the second parent's signature. The application gives the child's first name and surname as in the child's application for protection, and the child's NIE. You will need the receipt when you apply for the child's card under the new residence permit.
    Can I go back to temporary protection later?
    The renunciation is irreversible: with an explicit renunciation, protection ends automatically (art. 24.1.d RD 1325/2003). It can be obtained again only as a new applicant. Since 5 August 2026, protection is granted only to those who have fulfilled their military obligations in Ukraine, and proof is presented when it is required (art. 2 of EU Decision 2026/1912). The exception covers those whose protection in the same country was in force up to and including 4 August 2026 and continues without interruption, and a renunciation creates exactly such an interruption.
    What happens to my Ukrainian driving licence after renouncing temporary protection?
    Recognition of Ukrainian driving licences under EU Regulation 2022/1280 applies while you have temporary protection, and ends after the renunciation. After that, the general rule applies: a foreign licence is valid for six months from the start of normal residence, but from which date this period is counted when your status changes, the DGT does not say. So we advise exchanging your licence before the renunciation. This is our recommendation, not a legal requirement.
    Where do I get the renunciation receipt for the TIE card?
    It is issued when you file. The electronic registry issues a receipt with the entry number, date and time. At a public administration registry office, you will be given back the original and a receipt with the date and time of filing. When sending through Correos, the application is date-stamped and you are given a copy.
    Do I have to hand in my temporary protection card after the renunciation?
    Yes. Order INT/96/2026, which extended temporary protection cards until 4 March 2027, specifically states that for those who have renounced protection, the obligation to hand in the card remains.
