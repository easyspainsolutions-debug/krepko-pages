    Ukrainian diploma in Spain: apostille, translation, homologation or equivalence

    Krepko · 24 November 2026 · ~8 min read
    In short. A foreign university degree is recognised in Spain under Real Decreto 889/2022 through one of two procedures. Homologation (homologación) is needed for a regulated profession — doctor, nurse, architect, teacher and some thirty others from the annex to the decree. Equivalence (declaración de equivalencia) confirms the Grado or Máster level, but does not give the right to practise a regulated profession. Both require the diploma and the supplement with an apostille from the Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine (MON), an official translation into Spanish and an electronic application to the ministry; the fee, paid with Modelo 790-107, is €166.50 as of 24 November 2026.

    You graduated from a university in Ukraine and want to work in Spain in your field, enrol in a master's programme or take part in a competitive examination for the civil service. On its own, a Ukrainian diploma is a foreign piece of paper for the Spanish authorities: it has to be authenticated with an apostille, translated and, if your purpose requires it, recognised by the ministry.

    Below: how homologation differs from equivalence, what the requirements are for the apostille on the diploma and its supplement, which translation will be accepted and how the application works. We checked the rules and the amount of the fee on 24 November 2026 against the BOE and the ministries' websites; the addresses are in the list of sources.

    Homologation or equivalence: which do you need?

    The choice depends on your purpose, not on a wish to get the “strongest” recognition. Under the decree, homologation can only be requested to a Spanish título (official degree) that gives access to a regulated profession (art. 8.1). If your profession is not on that list, homologation is not possible — the ministry explicitly refers such applicants to equivalence.

    Homologation (homologación) Equivalence (equivalencia)
    What it gives you The same rights as the Spanish título, including working in a regulated profession (art. 5.1) The academic and administrative effects of the Grado or Máster level throughout Spain (art. 5.2)
    What it is for Doctor, nurse, pharmacist, psicólogo general sanitario (general health psychologist), architect, engineer in a number of specialities, teacher and other professions from the annex to RD 889/2022 Continuing your studies, if the university requires it; a competitive examination for the civil service; showing an employer what your diploma corresponds to
    Minimum for the diploma The programme must contain the knowledge and competences that are compulsory for the Spanish título (art. 9.3) Grado — at least three years of full-time study (180 ECTS) and access to a master's; Máster — at least one year (60 ECTS) and access to a doctorate
    Spanish language Proof of level B2 or higher: DELE, an EOI certificate, etc. (art. 13.2) Not required
    Possible outcome Recognition, refusal or conditional recognition: a placement, an exam, a project or courses at a Spanish university — four years are given for this (art. 19) Recognition or refusal

    The regulated professions are medical (doctor, dentist, nurse, pharmacist, physiotherapist, veterinarian, psicólogo general sanitario, etc.), technical (architect and engineering specialities) and teaching (primary school teacher, ESO and Bachillerato teacher, etc.). According to a note from the ministry, the professions of lawyer (abogado) and procurador are not subject to homologation, while medical specialities (residency) are recognised under separate rules (first additional provision). If in Ukraine another document or condition is required to work in the profession, it will also have to be proved (art. 11.3).

    What the decree does not do: it does not recognise universities' own (unofficial) diplomas and professional development courses, and it does not re-examine an identical application on which a decision has already been made (art. 4). Equivalence of a doctoral degree (Doctor) is granted by the universities themselves, not by the ministry.

    Which apostille do the diploma and supplement need?

    The decree requires the diploma and the academic transcript to be legalised or apostilled (art. 13.1). The ministry accepts an academic transcript or the European diploma supplement; for a Ukrainian graduate this is usually the diploma supplement. It must show the length of study, the subjects and the hours or ECTS credits. Without the supplement, the ministry has nothing to compare the programmes against.

    In Ukraine, the apostille on educational documents is affixed by the Ministry of Education and Science (Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 61 of 18 January 2003); before the apostille, the document is checked by the state enterprise “Інформаційно-іміджевий центр” (Information and Image Centre). What is important to know in advance:

    • Original only. The MON affixes the apostille to the original or to a duplicate: a duplicate is treated as equal to the original. A notarised copy will not be accepted. The Spanish ministry, for its part, does not accept an apostilled certified copy (compulsa) instead of an apostille on the document itself.
    • The supplement is a separate document. The apostille is affixed to the diploma and to the supplement separately, and the supplement is not apostilled without an apostille on the diploma. For Spain you need both.
    • Check against the database. If the diploma details do not match the Unified State Electronic Database on Education (EDEBO) or the fact that it was issued is not confirmed, the apostille will be refused. The same applies if there are corrections or damage, or if the form does not match the template in force on the date of issue.
    • An affidavit is not a substitute. The MON centre warns: a notarial statement about the diploma with an apostille from the Ministry of Justice does not replace the MON apostille on the educational document itself.
    • Validity. An apostille has none, and the Spanish ministry also states that legalisation does not expire. An electronic apostille is accepted if its QR code or CSV works.

    If the diploma is lost or damaged, you will have to start with a duplicate: Ukrainian law provides for one, including when the university's archive is unavailable. Only then comes the apostille.

    Which translation will the ministry accept?

    Documents not in Spanish are submitted with an official translation (art. 13.1). According to the ministry's guidance, in Spain this means a translation by a sworn translator (traductor-intérprete jurado) appointed by the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs — their register is kept at exteriores.gob.es. A translation by an official translator from an EU country is also accepted. If the translation was made by an official translator outside the EU, their signature must be legalised.

    • The whole document is translated, together with the apostille. So the order is: first the apostille, then the translation, not the other way round.
    • If the entire document is drawn up in two languages, only one of them is translated into Spanish.
    • A translation, like an apostille, has no expiry date: a translation ordered once will also be useful for later procedures.

    We arrange the sworn translation; the timeframe depends on the length of the supplement and is discussed once the documents are in hand. How it differs from a notarised translation is explained in the article on the apostille and sworn translation, and the service is on the page “Sworn translation”.

    How do you apply and how much is the fee?

    The recognition of university degrees is handled by the Ministry of Science, Innovation and Universities. Everything is done only electronically, through its sede (online office): the application, responses to requests, appeals; notifications also arrive electronically (art. 7). A representative may submit the application (art. 12.3). The application is accompanied by (art. 13):

    • a document proving identity and nationality;
    • the diploma and the supplement with an apostille and a translation;
    • proof of payment of the fee;
    • a power of attorney, if a representative is applying;
    • a declaración responsable (sworn statement) that the details are correct and that you hold the originals: the ministry may request them at any time;
    • for homologation — proof of Spanish at level B2.

    The fee for homologation or equivalence is €166.50 (according to the ministry's website as of 24 November 2026). You pay by card or from an account while filling in the application (you need a NIF or NIE and electronic identification), using form 790-107 at a bank that accepts such payments (entidad colaboradora), or by transfer from abroad. If a representative pays online on your behalf, the power of attorney must be registered in the electronic register of powers of attorney (Registro Electrónico de Apoderamientos). If the application is not accepted, lapses or is refused, the fee is not refunded.

    If the ministry asks you to provide an additional document, you are given 10 working days to do so (art. 14.1). For those who find it difficult to obtain documents, the time limit is extended on request to 35 working days. Missing the deadline means the application is treated as withdrawn — so it is better to prepare the apostille and translation before applying, not after.

    What changes if you have temporary protection?

    The decree has a separate rule for beneficiaries of temporary protection (second transitional provision). They can start the procedure with a declaration instead of the documents, but they will still have to submit the documents before the decision. The fee is not required from them until the examination of the case (instrucción) is completed, and the time limits for providing documents do not apply until the draft decision. In addition, under the ministry's instruction of 13 April 2022, they may ask for the examination to be expedited: after submitting the application, they write to titulos.protecciontemporalucrania@ciencia.gob.es, stating their situation from the list in the instruction (points a–g), their name and case number. Since 1 January 2025, Spain has also taken part in the Council of Europe's European Qualifications Passport for Refugees (EQPR) programme.

    What if you need a school-leaving certificate recognised?

    RD 889/2022 only concerns higher education (art. 1). A school-leaving certificate for admission to a Spanish university is recognised under a different rule and a different procedure: see the page “Homologation of the Bachiller certificate”.

    Where do people most often lose time and the fee?

    1. An apostille on the diploma only. Without the supplement with the subjects and hours, the ministry has nothing to compare the programme with.
    2. Translation before the apostille. The apostille must be translated too, and the translation will have to be redone.
    3. Homologation “just in case”. If the profession is not regulated, homologation will not be granted and the fee will not be refunded. What you need is equivalence.
    4. A copy instead of the original. Neither the MON nor the Spanish ministry works with an apostilled copy.
    5. Discrepancies in the name. The spelling of the name in Latin letters in the translation should be checked against your passport and TIE before ordering the translation.

    Frequently asked questions

    Do you need homologation of a Ukrainian diploma to work in Spain?
    Only for a regulated profession from the annex to Real Decreto 889/2022: doctor, nurse, pharmacist, architect, engineer in a number of specialities, teacher, etc. For other professions homologation is not possible: the ministry offers such applicants equivalence, which confirms the Grado or Máster level. It is useful for a competitive examination for the civil service, for continuing your studies or for an employer.
    How does homologation differ from equivalence?
    Homologation makes the diploma equivalent to a specific Spanish título and gives the right to work in a regulated profession; it requires Spanish at level B2, and recognition can be conditional — with a placement or an exam at a Spanish university. Equivalence only confirms the Grado or Máster level, with academic and administrative effects. Both are submitted to the Ministry of Science, Innovation and Universities, and the fee is the same — €166.50 as of 24.11.2026.
    Does the diploma supplement need to be apostilled?
    Yes. Under RD 889/2022, an academic transcript with the subjects, hours or credits is submitted together with the diploma, and it must also be apostilled. For a Ukrainian diploma, this is the supplement. The MON affixes the apostille to the diploma and to the supplement separately, and does not apostille the supplement without an apostille on the diploma.
    Can an apostille be affixed to a notarised copy of the diploma?
    No. The MON of Ukraine affixes the apostille only to the original educational document or its duplicate. The Spanish ministry also does not accept an apostilled certified copy instead of an apostille on the document itself. According to a warning from the MON centre, an affidavit with an apostille from the Ministry of Justice does not replace the apostille on the diploma.
    Who can translate a diploma for the ministry?
    A sworn translator appointed by the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs, or an official translator from an EU country. If the translator is outside the EU, their signature must be legalised. The whole document is translated, together with the apostille, so the apostille is affixed first, and only then is the translation ordered.
    Are there any concessions for Ukrainians with temporary protection?
    Yes. Under RD 889/2022, beneficiaries of temporary protection can start the procedure with a declaration instead of the documents (the documents will still be needed before the decision), and the fee is not required from them until the examination of the case (instrucción) is completed. You can ask for the examination to be expedited by writing to titulos.protecciontemporalucrania@ciencia.gob.es after submitting the application, with your case number.
