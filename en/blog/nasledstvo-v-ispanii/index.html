    Inheritance in Spain: what to do in the first months

    Krepko · updated 11 November 2026 · ~10 min read
    In short. The certificate from the register of wills (certificado de actos de última voluntad) is requested no earlier than 15 working days after the death, and every heir without a DNI needs a NIE. Being there in person is not required: the heir grants a power of attorney before a notary of the country where they are, followed by an apostille and a translation. Checked on 24 October 2026.     When someone close to you dies, dealing with procedures is the last thing you want to do. But in Spain an inheritance case has a deadline that runs on its own: six months from the day of death to file the inheritance tax. Missing it costs more than it seems.

    Below is a calm walk-through of the process: which documents are gathered and in what order, what a certificado de últimas voluntades is and why it cannot be requested straight away, what an heir who lives in another country should do, and what to do if the inheritance is in Ukraine. No rush, but with dates.
    The key point about deadlines. Inheritance tax is filed within six months of the day of death. An extension for another six months is possible, but the application is filed in the first five months — after that, this option is no longer available. The extension removes penalties, but not late-payment interest.

    First steps

    A death that occurs in Spain is registered by the Spanish civil registration authorities — the Registro Civil of the place where the death occurred, regardless of the person's citizenship. The same office issues the certificado de defunción, the death certificate. Everything else starts with it.

    A certificate issued by a Spanish authority is recognised in Ukraine — this requires an “Apostille” stamp and a notarised translation into Ukrainian. The embassy does not register the death again.

    A pause of 15 working days. The certificado de actos de última voluntad — the certificate from the register of wills — can be requested only after 15 working days have passed since the day of death. This is not a notary's formality but a condition of the procedure. Use these days to gather everything else.

    Which documents are gathered

    • Certificado de defunción — the death certificate from the Registro Civil;
    • Certificado de actos de última voluntad — shows whether the deceased left a will and with which notary. It is requested with Modelo 790; the fee (tasa) is small, about €4;
    • Certificado de contratos de seguros de cobertura de fallecimiento — the certificate of life insurance contracts. It is requested in the same way and often reveals payouts the family did not know about;
    • the will — a copy from the notary who certified it, or, if there is no will, a declaración de herederos — the deed declaring the heirs;
    • the NIE of each heir who does not have a Spanish DNI. Without it the case does not move;
    • documents for the property: a nota simple from the register, account statements, details of the car.

    Inheritance tax: who pays and where

    The Impuesto sobre Sucesiones y Donaciones is calculated differently depending on where the heir lives.

    An heir who is resident in Spain pays under “personal liability”: all inherited property is declared regardless of the country in which it is located.

    A non-resident heir pays under “real liability”: only for property and rights located in Spain.

    Rates and allowances vary greatly between autonomous communities — Valencia, Madrid and Andalusia calculate differently. Heirs living abroad, as a rule, pay through the central authority in Madrid.

    The six months run whether or not you have gathered the documents. If you can see that you will not make it, the application for an extension is filed in the first five months. The extension is considered granted if the tax office has not replied within a month. Late-payment interest is still charged, but there will be no penalties or surcharges.

    If the heir lives in another country

    Being there in person is not required at every stage. The heir can grant a power of attorney to a representative — and from then on documents are signed without them having to fly. This is standard practice, not an exception.

    The power of attorney is drawn up before a notary of the country where the heir is. If they are in Spain and the inheritance is in Ukraine, the same mechanism works in reverse: a Spanish notary draws up a power of attorney to handle the inheritance case as a notarial record (acta) under Article 207 of the Reglamento Notarial, followed by an apostille and a notarised translation into Ukrainian.

    The Ministry of Justice of Ukraine explicitly names drawing it up before a notary of the country of residence as one of the two permitted ways — on a par with the consular one. So the route through a Spanish notary is not a fallback but a full-fledged one.

    Accept or renounce

    An inheritance is accepted together with its debts. If the deceased had loans or obligations, they pass to the heirs together with the property. So before accepting, it makes sense to understand what exactly is being inherited.

    There are three options: accept, accept “with the benefit of inventory” (a beneficio de inventario — you are liable for debts only up to the value of the inheritance) or renounce. A renunciation is made before a notary, and it cannot be withdrawn — it needs to be thought through before signing, not after.

    If the inheritance is in Ukraine

    The procedure is different, and so are the deadlines. The case can be handled through a representative under a power of attorney that you draw up here, in Spain — except for the statement accepting or renouncing the inheritance: you sign such a statement yourself (this can be done before a Spanish notary); it is not accepted by power of attorney. Through our team in Ukraine we handle requests for documents — civil registry certificates, other certificates, archive extracts — and, if necessary, work with a Ukrainian notary or lawyer.

    If kinship needs to be proved, the documents for this are prepared too: birth and marriage certificates for each link in the family chain, with an apostille and a translation.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much time is there to deal with an inheritance in Spain?
    Inheritance tax is filed within six months of the day of death. An extension for another six months is possible if the application is filed in the first five months. The extension spares you penalties and surcharges, but late-payment interest is charged.
    Can I deal with an inheritance without coming to Spain?
    Yes, through a representative under a power of attorney. The power of attorney is drawn up before a notary of the country where you are, followed by an apostille and a translation. Being there in person is not required at every stage.
    Why can't the certificate of wills be requested straight away?
    The request for the certificado de actos de última voluntad is filed no earlier than 15 working days from the day of death. This is a condition of the procedure itself.
    What if there was no will?
    A declaración de herederos — the deed declaring the heirs — is drawn up. This is a notarial procedure both for the closest relatives and for brothers, sisters and other collateral relatives.
    The inheritance is in Ukraine, and I live in Spain. How do I handle the case?
    Through a representative under a power of attorney drawn up before a Spanish notary — except for the statement accepting or renouncing the inheritance: you sign such a statement yourself (this can be done before the same notary); it is not accepted by power of attorney. The Ministry of Justice of Ukraine names drawing it up before a notary of the country of residence as one of the two permitted ways. Conditions: an apostille and a notarially certified translation into Ukrainian.
    I am a non-resident. Do I pay tax in Spain?
    A non-resident pays under real liability — only for property and rights located in Spain. A resident declares all inherited property regardless of the country where it is located.
    The deceased had debts. Do I have to accept the inheritance?
    No. You can accept it in full, accept it a beneficio de inventario — then you are liable for debts up to the value of the estate — or renounce it. A renunciation is made before a notary, and it cannot be withdrawn.
    Does an heir need a NIE?
    Yes, every heir without a Spanish DNI. Without a NIE neither the inheritance case nor the tax part can be completed.
