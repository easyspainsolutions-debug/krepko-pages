    Spanish citizenship exams in 2027: CCSE and DELE A2 dates and how to register

    Krepko · 18 November 2026 · ~8 min read
    In short. For Spanish nationality by residence, an adult applicant from Ukraine or Russia takes two Instituto Cervantes exams: DELE at level A2 or higher and CCSE (art. 6 RD 1004/2015). Registration for the 2027 DELE is open from 18 November 2026, for CCSE from 23 November. The first CCSE session is on 28 January, the first DELE A2 on 5 March 2027. The CCSE certificate is valid for 4 years (art. 10.4 Orden JUS/1625/2016), and the DELE diploma does not expire. A criminal record certificate with no stated validity period is valid for 6 months, so it is ordered to match the application date. Checked on 18 November 2026.

    CCSE calendar for 2027

    CCSE is a test of knowledge of the Spanish Constitution and of life in Spain. The Instituto Cervantes opens registration for the 2027 exams on 23 November 2026. The calendar has ten sessions, from January to November; there is no exam in August or December. Each session has three dates: the registration deadline, the period of scheduled dates and the official date.

    Session Registration closes Period of scheduled dates Official date
    January7 January14–28 January28 January
    February4 February11–25 February25 February
    March25 February4–18 March18 March
    April8 April15–29 April29 April
    May6 May13–27 May27 May
    June3 June10–24 June24 June
    July8 July15–29 July29 July
    September9 September16–30 September30 September
    October7 October14–28 October28 October
    November4 November11–25 November25 November

    All dates refer to 2027. The Instituto's page does not explain how the day and time are assigned within the period; under the Instituto's agreement with one of the exam centres (BOE, March 2025), the candidate chooses the date and place when registering online. If you miss the registration deadline, you can take the next session; after the July session, the next one is not until September.

    The content of the test is set by regulation: 60% of the questions are about the Constitution and the administrative and territorial organisation of Spain, 40% about culture, history and society, and all questions are closed-ended (art. 6.3 RD 1004/2015). On the pages of Instituto centres that describe previous sessions, the test looks like this: 25 questions in five tasks, up to 45 minutes, and you need 15 correct answers to pass. Candidates prepare with the Instituto's manual (300 questions in five tasks) and its free app. As of 18 November 2026, the 2026 manual is the one published: before your session, check whether a new one has come out.

    DELE A2 calendar for 2027

    DELE is a diploma in Spanish as a foreign language; for nationality you need level A2 or higher. Registration is open from 18 November 2026, and level A2 is available in each of the seven 2027 sessions:

    Written part Registration closes Levels of the general exam
    5 March, Friday20 JanuaryA2
    2 April, Friday10 FebruaryA1, A2, B1, B2, C1
    22 May, Saturday7 AprilA1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2
    9 July, Friday12 MayA2, B1, B2, C1
    10 September, Friday14 JulyA2
    15 October, Friday1 SeptemberA2, B1, B2
    13 November, Saturday29 SeptemberA1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2

    In May and November, the Friday is reserved for school exams, and the general exam takes place on the Saturday. In practice, DELE results arrive about three months after the exam, as stated on the pages of Instituto Cervantes centres, so plan your application from the date of the results.

    How to register and what to bring to the exam

    You register and pay online, in the Instituto Cervantes shop; in your personal account you can see your marks and download certificates. The Instituto is currently redesigning its exams website: the temporary page has the dates, but neither prices nor the number of attempts per payment. The price of the CCSE is approved by the Instituto's board for each calendar year (agreement between the Instituto and one of the centres, BOE, March 2025). Check the price and conditions for 2027 in the shop before paying.

    • Courses are not compulsory (art. 10.1 Orden JUS/1625/2016).
    • The exams are taken in person, at centres recognised by the Instituto; remotely only in cases that the Instituto itself determines (art. 6.2–6.3 RD 1004/2015).
    • Bring your passport and your residence card (TIE) to the exam: your identity is checked against both documents, or, as an exception, against one of them in the original (art. 10.4 Orden JUS/1625/2016).
    • You do not have to attach the certificates: in your nationality application you authorise the ministry to check your results in the Instituto Cervantes database (annex to Orden JUS/1625/2016, point 3).

    Who is exempt from the exams

    The exams are required by law (Ley 19/2015). Citizens of Ukraine and Russia need both; the exceptions are as follows:

    Who DELE CCSE Where it is set out
    Under 18; people whose legal capacity has been modified by a court Do not take it Do not take it art. 10.6 Orden JUS/1625/2016
    Citizens of the 20 Spanish-speaking countries and territories on the list Do not take it Take it art. 6.5 RD 1004/2015
    Holders of a DELE diploma at level A2 or higher Do not take it again Take it art. 6.5 RD 1004/2015
    Holders of an official A2, B1 or B2 certificate in Spanish issued by the Spanish education authorities or a state language school (Escuela Oficial de Idiomas) The certificate replaces the exam if it is entered in the register Take it art. 10.3 Orden JUS/1625/2016
    Former pupils of a Spanish school who have completed compulsory secondary education (ESO) May be exempted May be exempted art. 10.5 Orden JUS/1625/2016
    People who cannot read or write or who have learning difficulties By decision of the ministry By decision of the ministry art. 10.5 Orden JUS/1625/2016

    An exemption by decision of the Ministry of Justice (dispensa) is requested before the nationality application; the request can be submitted electronically. Under the order, the request may take up to six months to be decided, and silence means refusal (art. 10.5): in that case you will need DELE and CCSE certificates to apply. Until there is a decision, the nationality application is not submitted: if it is submitted at the same time as the request, the ministry closes it. Former pupils of a Spanish school who have completed ESO do not have to submit a separate request: their education documents are attached to the application. According to the ministry's guidance, those who have completed upper secondary school, vocational training or university in Spain may be exempted from DELE, but not from CCSE; studies outside Spain and at schools with a foreign curriculum do not count. On children, see the article on citizenship for a child.

    How long certificates and documents are valid

    All documents must be valid at the time the application is submitted (annex to Orden JUS/1625/2016, point 4). Their validity periods differ:

    Document Validity Where it is set out
    CCSE certificate 4 years from the date of the certificate art. 10.4 Orden JUS/1625/2016
    DELE diploma No expiry Instituto Cervantes answers to frequently asked questions
    Criminal record certificate from the country of origin The period stated on the certificate; if none is stated, 6 months from the date of issue Annex to Orden JUS/1625/2016, point 4
    Birth certificate The only certificate that may be more than 6 months old at the time of application Ministry of Justice answers to frequently asked questions
    Passport from the country of origin Complete and valid on the day of application Annex to Orden JUS/1625/2016, point 1

    Since the birth certificate is the only certificate that may be older than six months, the other certificates, such as a marriage certificate, are in practice ordered so that they are no more than six months old when you apply. If a document states a shorter period, that period applies. On the validity of certificates for different authorities, see the article on the “no older than 3 months” certificate.

    What to prepare in parallel with the exams

    1. Straight after registering, the birth certificate. It may be more than six months old at the time of application, so it is ordered first. At the same time, check your passport's expiry date and the spelling of names in your documents and on your residence card.
    2. To match the application date, the criminal record certificate. Its six months must cover the apostille, the translation and the application; the order date is calculated from the expected exam results.
    3. After the results, the application. It is submitted electronically to the Ministry of Justice once the results of both exams are known.
    Example. You take the CCSE in the January session and DELE A2 on 5 March 2027. If the DELE results arrive in early June, the criminal record certificate is ordered in spring: issued in April with no stated validity period, it is valid until October. The birth certificate can be ordered right now, and the CCSE certificate will be valid until early 2031.

    Frequently asked questions

    When does registration for the 2027 CCSE open?
    On 23 November 2026. The 2027 calendar has ten sessions. The official date of the first one is 28 January, and registration for it closes on 7 January. There is no exam in August or December. You register online, in the Instituto Cervantes shop.
    When is the next DELE A2 exam and what is the registration deadline?
    The next one is on 5 March 2027, and registration for it closes on 20 January. The following dates with level A2 are 2 April, 22 May, 9 July, 10 September, 15 October and 13 November 2027. Registration for all 2027 sessions is open from 18 November 2026.
    How long is the CCSE certificate valid and do I need to retake DELE?
    The CCSE certificate is valid for 4 years from the date of the certificate (art. 10.4 Orden JUS/1625/2016), and this period must not have expired on the day you submit your application. The DELE diploma does not expire: according to the Instituto Cervantes, it certifies your language level permanently. The regulation exempts those who already hold a diploma at level A2 or higher from the exam (art. 6.5 RD 1004/2015).
    I am from Ukraine. Do I need to take both exams?
    Yes. Citizens of the 20 Spanish-speaking countries and territories listed in art. 6.5 RD 1004/2015 are exempt from DELE, but they take the CCSE too. Minors take neither exam. If you went to school in Spain and completed compulsory secondary education, you may be exempted from the exams: your education documents are attached to the nationality application (art. 10.5 Orden JUS/1625/2016).
    How much does the CCSE exam cost in 2027?
    As of 18 November 2026, the 2027 price has not been published: the temporary Instituto Cervantes exams page shows neither the price nor the number of attempts per payment. You register and pay in the Instituto's shop. The price of the CCSE is approved by the Instituto's board for each calendar year (agreement between the Instituto and one of the exam centres, BOE, March 2025). Check the price and conditions in the shop before paying.
    Which documents should I order in advance and which just before applying?
    In advance, the birth certificate: according to the Ministry of Justice, it is the only document that may be more than six months old at the time of application. The criminal record certificate is ordered to match the application date: if no validity period is stated on it, it is valid for six months from the date of issue (annex to Orden JUS/1625/2016, point 4).
