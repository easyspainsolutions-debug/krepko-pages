    How to divorce in Ukraine while living in Spain: DRACS, court and power of attorney

    Krepko · 24 November 2026 · ~7 min read
    In short. With no children and with both spouses' consent, the marriage is dissolved by DRACS (Family Code of Ukraine, Art. 106); if there are children or one of the spouses objects, only by a court (Arts 109–110). For DRACS, the spouse in Spain signs the application before a Spanish notary, and it is filed by the other spouse in Ukraine. For a court, a power of attorney for a lawyer is drawn up in Spain: another person can be represented in court by a lawyer or a legal representative. A document from a Spanish notary needs an apostille from the College of Notaries and a translation into Ukrainian, which is certified in Ukraine itself.

    The marriage was registered in Ukraine, but you now live in Spain, and you do not want to or cannot travel there for a divorce. Ukrainian law offers two routes: through the civil registry office (DRACS) and through a court. Both can be done without travelling, provided the documents are properly drawn up here, before a Spanish notary.

    Below: which route suits you, what to sign in Spain, how to prepare the document for Ukraine and what you will receive at the end. We checked the Ukrainian rules against the Family Code as amended in 2026 and the explanations of the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine, and the Spanish rules against the BOE, on 24 November 2026.

    Which route is yours: DRACS or a court?

    The Family Code of Ukraine draws a clear line between the cases (Art. 105). Which route is yours depends on two questions: whether you have children together and whether both spouses agree.

    Situation Who dissolves the marriage Document at the end
    No children, both agree DRACS, on a joint application (Art. 106) Certificate of dissolution of marriage (Art. 115)
    Children, both agree A court, on a joint application with a written agreement about the children (Art. 109) A court decision that has entered into legal force
    One of the spouses objects A court, on a claim by one spouse (Art. 110) A court decision that has entered into legal force

    A dispute over property does not change the route: the marriage is dissolved regardless of it (Art. 106, part 3). DRACS can also dissolve a marriage on the application of one spouse, but only if a court has declared the other spouse missing or legally incapable (Art. 107).

    How to divorce through DRACS if there are no children and both agree?

    The application is filed by the couple. If one of the spouses, for a valid reason, cannot file it in person, their application, attested by a notary or by an equivalent procedure, is filed on their behalf by the other spouse (Art. 106, part 1). A spouse who lives abroad can also do this. According to the explanations of the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine, the scheme for the spouse in Spain is as follows:

    1. You sign the application for dissolution of marriage before a Spanish notary, who attests your signature.
    2. In the application you state that you ask for the marriage to be dissolved in your absence, and you name the DRACS office to which the certificate should be sent. If you cannot attend the registration, you need written consent to it with a notarially attested signature (Ministry of Justice, May 2026).
    3. You send the original with the apostille by post to the other spouse. They have the translation certified by a notary in Ukraine and file the applications with DRACS.

    The civil status record of the divorce is drawn up one month after filing, if the application has not been withdrawn (Art. 106, part 2), and the marriage ends on the day of registration (Art. 114).

    If you are both in Spain, bear in mind: in wartime, Ukrainian missions abroad provide DRACS services under Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 66, and in the Ministry of Justice explanation of August 2026 divorce registration is not listed among them — the list covers birth, death, marriage, change of name, amendments to civil status records, duplicate certificates and extracts. So before counting on the Ukrainian consulate in Spain, check with it whether it accepts divorce applications.

    What to sign before a Spanish notary?

    For the DRACS application, the notary performs a legitimación de firma — attests that the signature is genuine. Documents expressing a person's will are attested by the notary only when you have signed them or acknowledged the signature in their presence (Reglamento Notarial, Art. 259), and the notary is not responsible for the content of the text (Art. 256). Ukrainian law takes the same view: such attestation confirms who signed, not the facts in the text (Law “On Notaries”, Art. 78). So the text must be ready and checked before the visit.

    What to check in the application:

    • the full names of both spouses as in their Ukrainian passports, and the passport details;
    • the details of the marriage record: date, DRACS office, civil status record number;
    • that there are no children together;
    • the request to register the divorce in your absence and the address of the DRACS office for the certificate;
    • your surname after the divorce: you are entitled to keep your married surname or return to your pre-marriage surname (Art. 113).

    Next, the document is prepared for Ukraine. Spain and Ukraine are parties to the Hague Apostille Convention (Spain since 1978, Ukraine since 22 December 2003), so instead of the legalisation that Ukrainian law requires for foreign documents (Law “On Private International Law”, Art. 13), an apostille is affixed. The apostille on a notarial document is affixed by the College of Notaries (Real Decreto 1497/2011, Art. 3). According to the explanations of the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine, the translation into Ukrainian is done and notarially certified in Ukraine itself.

    How to divorce through a court if there are children or no agreement?

    With children, the couple files a joint application with the court together with a written agreement: who the children will live with and how the other parent takes part in their lives (Art. 109, part 1). An agreement on the amount of maintenance must be notarially certified (part 2). The court checks that the application reflects the genuine will of both and does not violate the children's rights, and issues a decision no earlier than one month after filing (parts 3–4). If there is no agreement, the claim is filed by one spouse (Art. 110).

    Where to file. As a general rule, at the defendant's place of residence. For a divorce claim, the court at the claimant's registered place of residence or stay is also possible: if minor children are dependent on the claimant, if for health or another valid reason the claimant cannot travel to the defendant, and, by agreement of the spouses, at such a place of either of them (this is how jurisdiction is described by Ukraine's free legal aid system).

    How to take part from Spain. The claim can be filed in person, through the “Electronic Court” system or through a representative — under a power of attorney or under an agreement with a lawyer (a Ministry of Justice explanation as set out by a court, April 2026). A representative in court can be a lawyer or a legal representative (Civil Procedure Code of Ukraine, Art. 60, part 1). A power of attorney from an individual must be notarially certified, and a lawyer's authority is confirmed by a power of attorney or a warrant (order) (Art. 62). The Ministry of Justice of Ukraine recognises a power of attorney from a Spanish notary as one of two lawful options, alongside a consular one — with an apostille and a translation certified in Ukraine. For a consular power of attorney, men aged 18–60 need a military registration document (Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 981) — more in the article on consular services for men.

    What is usually included in a power of attorney for a divorce through a court:

    • the details of the principal and the lawyer, and the details of the marriage record;
    • the right to file and sign a claim or a joint application for dissolution of marriage, including through the “Electronic Court” system;
    • the right to represent you in court, receive the decision and other documents, and file applications and appeals;
    • the right to obtain an extract on the dissolution of marriage from DRACS.

    We agree the exact list with the lawyer handling the case: they know which actions will be needed. How a Ukrainian power of attorney from Spain works in general is covered in the article on a power of attorney from Spain for Ukraine.

    Which document will you receive?

    • A divorce through DRACS is confirmed by a certificate of dissolution of marriage (Art. 115, part 3). It is sent to the DRACS office that you named in the application or in the written consent to registration without you.
    • A divorce through a court is confirmed by a court decision that has entered into legal force (Art. 115, part 3). The court itself sends it to DRACS, where a note is made in the civil status record of the marriage (part 2).
    • An extract on the state registration of the dissolution of marriage: according to the Ministry of Justice explanation, it is issued by any DRACS office and any TsNAP (administrative services centre), and the application can also be filed on the Diia portal.

    For the document to be accepted in Spain — for example, for a new marriage — it is apostilled by the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine: the ministry apostilles documents of justice bodies and courts. A sworn translation is added if the Spanish authority requires it. Which document your authority needs and how to obtain it without travelling is described on the page “Divorce certificate from Ukraine”.

    What about divorcing in Spain?

    A Spanish notarial divorce is not for everyone: both spouses must appear in person with a lawyer, three months must have passed since the marriage was registered, and there must be no non-emancipated minor children and no adult children for whom a court has ordered support measures to be provided through the parents (Código Civil, Arts 81, 82 and 87). The conditions and recognition in Ukraine are covered in the article on divorce before a notary in Spain.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I get divorced in Ukraine without going there?
    Yes. If there are no children and both agree, the marriage is dissolved by DRACS: the spouse in Spain signs the application before a Spanish notary, and it is filed by the other spouse in Ukraine (Family Code of Ukraine, Art. 106). If there are children or there is no agreement, the divorce goes through a court, and the case can be handled from Spain by a lawyer under a power of attorney or a warrant (order).
    Will DRACS accept a divorce application signed before a Spanish notary?
    The Family Code allows a notarially attested application from an absent spouse, filed by the other spouse (Art. 106). According to the explanation of the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine, it can be attested by a notary abroad and sent by post, and the translation certified by a notary in Ukraine. A document from a Spanish notary is apostilled by the College of Notaries.
    Can we divorce through DRACS if we both live in Spain?
    The application to a DRACS office in Ukraine is filed by at least one of the spouses: the absent spouse's notarially attested application is submitted by the other (Family Code of Ukraine, Art. 106). If neither of you is in Ukraine, this route is inconvenient. In the Ministry of Justice explanation of August 2026, divorce registration is not listed among the DRACS services that Ukrainian missions abroad provide in wartime, so check in advance whether the consulate accepts such an application.
    Who can represent me in a Ukrainian divorce court case?
    A representative in court can be a lawyer or a legal representative (Civil Procedure Code of Ukraine, Art. 60). A lawyer's authority is confirmed by a power of attorney or a warrant (order), and a power of attorney from an individual must be notarial (Art. 62). The power of attorney can be drawn up before a Spanish notary with an apostille and a translation certified in Ukraine.
    Which document will confirm a divorce granted in Ukraine?
    A divorce through DRACS is confirmed by a certificate of dissolution of marriage, and a divorce through a court by a court decision that has entered into legal force (Family Code of Ukraine, Art. 115). An extract on the registration of the dissolution of marriage is issued by DRACS and TsNAP, and the application can also be filed on the Diia portal. For Spain, the document is apostilled by the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine.
    Can I get divorced before a notary in Spain instead?
    Only under three conditions: both spouses agree and come to the notary in person with a lawyer, three months have passed since the marriage was registered, and there are no non-emancipated minor children or adult children with support measures ordered by a court (Código Civil, Arts 81, 82 and 87). Spanish consuls do not handle such a divorce. More about the conditions in our article on divorce before a notary in Spain.
