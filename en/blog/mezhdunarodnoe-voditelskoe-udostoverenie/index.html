    International driving permit in Spain: who gets one and where you need it

    Krepko · 11 November 2026 · ~8 min read
    In short. DGT issues the Spanish international driving permit (permiso internacional de conducción) to people who normally live in Spain and hold a valid Spanish driving licence or an EU or EEA licence entered in the DGT register (art. 32 RD 818/2009), so it is not issued against a Ukrainian licence: that licence is first exchanged for a Spanish one. The permit is valid for one year with no renewal, abroad it works only together with your Spanish licence, and in Spain itself it is not valid; the fee (tasa 4.5) is €10.51. In EU and EEA countries and in countries with a bilateral agreement on the DGT list, for example the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Turkey, Morocco and Ukraine, a Spanish licence is enough, while in Japan and all other countries you need an international permit. The conditions were checked against the DGT website and the BOE on 11 November 2026.

    You will need an international driving permit if you are going to drive outside the European Union, in a country where a Spanish licence alone is not enough. In Spain it is issued by the Directorate-General for Traffic (DGT), but only against a Spanish licence or an EU or EEA licence entered in the DGT register. So with a Ukrainian licence the path to it starts with exchanging your licence for a Spanish one. And after your visit to the DGT office (Jefatura) and until the plastic card arrives, what you hold is a temporary authorisation, which is not valid abroad.

    Below we explain where a Spanish licence is enough, who DGT issues an international permit to and how, whether you need one for a foreign licence in Spain itself and how to combine a licence exchange with a trip. The conditions were checked against the DGT website and the BOE on 11 November 2026.

    What this document is and where it is valid

    The international permit follows the model of the 1949 Geneva Convention on Road Traffic. In Spain it is issued by DGT offices (Jefatura Provincial de Tráfico), and it entitles you to drive temporarily in the countries of the convention, except the one that issued it (art. 2 and 31 RD 818/2009). It is a grey 16-page booklet: the holder's details and licence details are written in it in eight languages, including Spanish, English, French and Russian. In Spain itself you cannot drive on a Spanish international permit; here you need the licence itself.

    The permit has no force of its own. Abroad it is shown together with your passport and your valid Spanish licence, and if the Spanish licence has expired, the international permit is not valid either. The licence in the miDGT app is valid for driving in Spain, but for trips abroad DGT advises taking the licence card itself with you. The international permit is valid for one year, and it cannot be renewed: when you need it again, you get a new one.

    Hence the key point for those who have moved here: a Spanish international permit is not issued against a Ukrainian licence, so you first need a driving licence exchange; we went through how the exchange works step by step in our article on Ukrainian driving licences in Spain.

    Where a Spanish licence is enough and where you need an international permit

    The answer depends on the country you are travelling to, and agreements, as DGT warns, may change: it is best to check the list again before your trip.

    Where you are going Is a Spanish licence enough?
    EU countries, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway Yes, as long as the licence is valid
    Countries with a bilateral agreement on the DGT list, for example Andorra, Algeria, the United Kingdom, Morocco, Monaco, North Macedonia, Serbia, Tunisia, Turkey, Ukraine, Switzerland, a number of Latin American countries, South Korea, the Philippines, New Zealand Yes, but on dgt.es DGT still advises taking an international permit: agreements may change
    Japan No: there is an agreement, but you need your Spanish licence together with an international permit
    All other countries No, you need an international permit together with your Spanish licence

    The Spanish international permit is drawn up under the Geneva Convention, and DGT says that it is valid for driving in the countries that signed it. If the country you are travelling to has no agreement with Spain and has not signed the convention, DGT advises asking about the rules at its consulate or tourist office.

    Licence exchange and travel are different lists. Among the countries where a Spanish licence is valid without an international permit, DGT does not name, for example, Georgia and Moldova, even though their licences are exchanged for Spanish ones in Spain under agreements. For DGT these fall under “all other countries”: you need an international permit there, and the details are best checked with that country's consulate.

    Who DGT issues an international permit to

    The conditions are listed in art. 32 of the Reglamento General de Conductores (General Drivers Regulation, RD 818/2009):

    • you normally live in Spain (residencia normal);
    • you hold a valid Spanish licence for the category you need the international permit for, or a licence from another EU or EEA country entered in advance in the DGT drivers' register;
    • you have not been banned from driving by a court, and your licence has not been suspended or withdrawn by an administrative decision.

    Licences from countries outside the EU and the EEA are not on this list, so a Spanish international permit will not be issued against a Ukrainian, Russian or, for example, Georgian licence. If the licence is exchanged under an agreement, as Ukrainian ones are, you first need the exchange. If there is no agreement (the DGT list does not include, for example, Russia, Kazakhstan or Armenia), a Spanish licence is obtained by taking the exams. EU and EEA licences follow their own procedure: they are first entered in the DGT register (inscripción), and only then do you apply for the international permit.

    How to apply: online or by appointment

    Online. The application is filed through the DGT electronic register (Registro Electrónico) with a digital certificate, an electronic DNI or Cl@ve; we explained how to register for Cl@ve with an NIE in a separate article. In the form you choose the office where you will collect the permit, and pay the fee with the “Pagar” button or enter the number of a tasa paid in advance (12 digits on the last page of the receipt); after payment the application is sent with the “Enviar” button. According to DGT, the permit is ready in two days. It is collected in person and without an appointment: with your passport, DNI or NIE and a colour photo measuring 32×26 mm. If you cannot come, another person can collect it with your authorisation on the DGT form “Otorgamiento de representación” and the original or a certified copy of your DNI or NIE.

    In person. The application is filed at any Tráfico office by appointment through the DGT website or by phone on 060. You need to bring:

    • an application on form Mod. 03 “Trámites de conductores” with “Solicitud de permiso internacional” marked;
    • the originals of valid documents proving your identity and residence: for nationals of non-EU countries, a residence permit (autorización de residencia); for Spanish nationals, a DNI or passport; what EU citizens should bring is stated on the DGT page;
    • a colour photo measuring 32×26 mm on a light plain background, without headwear or dark glasses;
    • a photocopy of your valid Spanish licence and the licence itself: the original is returned after it is checked against the copy (this is what the DGT information sheet and Annex III to RD 818/2009 say);
    • fee (tasa 4.5): €10.51; at the office it is accepted by bank card only, not in cash; you can also bring a receipt for payment made in advance.

    A representative can also file the application: through the DGT register of representations (Registro de apoderamientos, category “Conductores”, procedure “Permiso internacional de conducción”) or in person with the DGT form “Otorgamiento de representación” signed by you. When booking the appointment, the document numbers of both people are given.

    Do you need an international permit to drive in Spain?

    Ukrainian licences under temporary protection. No, you do not. As long as temporary protection applies to you, a valid Ukrainian licence is recognised throughout the European Union, and EU countries require neither a sworn translation nor an international permit for it, but they may ask for your passport, a temporary residence document or another document proving your identity (art. 3 of Regulation (EU) 2022/1280). Outside the EU the regulation does not apply, and it is best to check the rules there with the consulate of the country you are travelling to. We explained how long this rule applies and when it is still worth exchanging your licence in our article on Ukrainian driving licences.

    Licences from countries outside the EU and the EEA without temporary protection. In Spain they are valid if they follow the model of the Geneva or Vienna Convention, are written in Spanish or come with an official translation; an international permit issued abroad is also acceptable (art. 21.1 RD 818/2009) together with your passport and national licence, and DGT advises getting such a permit at home before you arrive. But all this works for no longer than six months from the start of normal residence in Spain, and without residence, six months from entry, unless the agreement with the country that issued the licence provides otherwise (art. 21.2). After that, neither the licence nor the international permit is valid for driving in Spain (art. 21.3): you need a Spanish licence obtained through exchange, if there is an agreement with the country, or by taking the exams.

    EU and EEA licences are valid in Spain without an international permit until they expire. If you live here, that validity period is calculated under Spanish rules, and licences with no expiry date are renewed after two years of residence (art. 15.4 RD 818/2009).

    Licence exchange and travelling abroad

    When you exchange a Ukrainian licence, the original is withdrawn at the Jefatura appointment and returned to the Ukrainian authorities, and you are given a temporary authorisation instead. According to DGT, it is valid for six months and only in Spain, and to drive abroad you need to get an international permit at any Tráfico office by appointment. DGT sends the plastic licence by post, by its own estimate in about a month and a half.

    For holders of temporary protection this is a noticeable change. Before the appointment you can drive throughout the European Union on your Ukrainian licence without an international permit, but from the appointment until the card arrives, to drive abroad you will need a Spanish international permit.

    That is why we plan the start of the exchange around your trips. If a car trip is already scheduled, it is better for the card to arrive before it: then the card alone will be enough in the EU, and for countries where a Spanish licence is not enough, you will get an international permit in advance. If the trip is very soon and you have temporary protection, it is better to start the exchange after it. It is best not to cancel an exchange appointment you have already booked because of a trip: DGT gives it only once, and after cancellation the procedure and the check of your licence with Ukraine start again. We handle the driving licence exchange from checking your licence to the arrival of the card.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I get an international driving permit in Spain if I have a Ukrainian licence?
    No. DGT issues an international permit only against a Spanish licence or an EU or EEA licence entered in its register (art. 32 RD 818/2009). A Ukrainian licence is first exchanged for a Spanish one: categories A and B without exams, under the agreement between Spain and Ukraine. The international permit is then issued against the Spanish licence. We handle the driving licence exchange and will tell you when to start the exchange with your trips in mind.
    Do I need an international permit to drive in Spain on a Ukrainian licence?
    If you have temporary protection, no: under art. 3 of Regulation (EU) 2022/1280, EU countries do not require either a sworn translation or an international permit for a valid Ukrainian licence, but they may ask for your passport or another document proving your identity. Without temporary protection an international permit is not compulsory either, although DGT advises getting one at home before you arrive: a Ukrainian licence is recognised under the agreement between Spain and Ukraine, but for no longer than six months from the start of normal residence, and after that neither the licence nor an international permit issued abroad is valid (art. 21 RD 818/2009).
    How much does an international permit from DGT cost and how long is it valid?
    The fee (tasa 4.5) is €10.51 according to the DGT online office (sede) as of 11 November 2026. The permit is valid for one year and is not renewed: if you need it again, you get a new one. For an online application, DGT says it prepares the permit in two days, and it is collected in person, with an identity document and a 32×26 mm photo.
    Can I drive to France or Portugal on the temporary authorisation after exchanging my licence?
    No. According to DGT, the temporary authorisation is valid for six months and only in Spain, and to drive abroad you need to get an international permit at a Tráfico office by appointment. DGT sends the plastic licence by post, by its own estimate in about a month and a half. So if a trip is already scheduled, it is better to plan the exchange so that the card arrives before it: in the EU the card will be enough without an international permit.
    Which countries can I drive in with a Spanish licence without an international permit?
    All EU countries, Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway, and also countries with a bilateral agreement on the DGT list: among them Andorra, the United Kingdom, Morocco, Serbia, Turkey, Ukraine and Switzerland. In Japan, despite the agreement, you need an international permit, as in all other countries. For trips outside the EU, DGT advises taking an international permit just in case even to countries with an agreement, or checking the rules in advance with the country's consulate or tourist office.
    Is a Spanish international permit valid in Spain itself?
    No: an international permit is not valid in the country that issued it (art. 31 RD 818/2009); in Spain you drive on your Spanish licence. Abroad it is only valid together with your Spanish licence, so if that licence has expired, the international permit will not help either.
