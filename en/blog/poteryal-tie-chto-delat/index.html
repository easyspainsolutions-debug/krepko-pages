    Lost or stolen TIE: the duplicate and whether you can leave Spain without the card

    Krepko · 24 November 2026 · ~8 min read
    In short. Report the loss or theft to the police straight away (denuncia) and book an appointment for a duplicate: the application on form EX-17 marked “duplicado” can only be submitted in person to the Policía Nacional. The new card is not a renewal: it is valid for as long as the old one had left (art. 209.9 RD 1155/2024). To leave and return before the duplicate is ready, you need an autorización de regreso (return authorisation): it can be obtained by the holder of a valid TIE who has already applied for the duplicate, for a period of no more than 90 days, and with it you can enter only through Spanish border crossing points (art. 5). The fee for it on form 790-012 as of 24 November 2026 is €10.72.

    Your TIE card has gone missing — on the metro, together with your wallet or on a trip. Your resident status does not disappear because of this: the residence permit remains valid; only the document that proves it is gone. But until you have a new card, travel becomes harder, and so does anything that requires you to show your TIE.

    Below we go through it in order: why you should report it to the police, how the duplicate is issued and how much the fee is, whether you can leave Spain and return without the card, what to do if the card went missing abroad, and what if you have temporary protection. We checked the rules on 24 November 2026 against the Immigration Regulation (RD 1155/2024 as amended by RD 316/2026), the BOE and the websites of the police, the Ministry of Inclusion and the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

    What should you do in the first days after losing your TIE?

    1. Report to the police
    Report the loss or theft of the card at a National Police station and keep a copy of the report (denuncia): it records the date and the circumstances.
    2. Check what else is missing
    If your passport went missing together with the card, it is replaced separately, at the consulate or passport service of your country: without a passport you cannot leave even with the new card in hand.
    3. Appointment and application for a duplicate
    The appointment (cita previa) for issuing the TIE is booked online; the application is form EX-17 marked “duplicado por pérdida, sustracción, deterioro o cambio de datos”.
    4. Fee 790-012
    The fee is paid on form 790, code 012, on the police website; the amount depends on the type of card.
    5. Travel only with an autorización de regreso
    If you need to leave before the card is ready, after applying for the duplicate you request a return authorisation.

    Why report it to the police?

    The Regulation describes the TIE as a personal document that may not be handed to others, and makes keeping it safe and in good condition the holder's obligation (art. 209.3 RD 1155/2024). Breaches of the obligations relating to the card fall under the penalty regime of the Immigration Act (LO 4/2000, art. 209.4). A police report officially records when and in what circumstances the card left your hands.

    The report will be useful later. Spanish consulates that issue a return visa to people who lost their TIE abroad explicitly include in their list of documents a police report on the loss or theft of the card, as, for example, does the list of the Consulate General in Casablanca, updated in January 2025. In Spain itself, for the autorización de regreso the Regulation requires something else: proof that you have already applied for a duplicate (art. 5.1). The denuncia does not replace this, so once you have reported it to the police, do not put off booking the appointment for the duplicate.

    How do you get a duplicate TIE and how much is the fee?

    Loss, destruction or damage of the card is a ground for issuing a new one at the holder's request (art. 209.9). Two details from the same article matter for your plans:

    • It is not a renewal. The new card is valid for exactly as long as the old one had left. If there are a few months left until expiry, you will have to renew in a few months.
    • It coincides with the renewal window. If the application for the new card is submitted within the period for renewing the permit, both procedures are handled together.

    The application is made on form EX-17: in the section on the type of document you tick “duplicado por pérdida, sustracción, deterioro o cambio de datos”. The form itself states that it can only be submitted in person at a National Police office, not online and not through public registries; you bring the original and a copy. The form is free. The appointment for issuing the TIE is booked online in the cita previa system.

    The fee is paid on form 790, code 012, on the police website (sede.policia.gob.es). There is no separate “duplicate” line on the form: the lines are divided by type of card. The amounts the form showed on 24 November 2026:

    Line on form 790-012 Amount
    TIE with a first temporary residence or stay authorisation €16.08
    TIE on renewal of a temporary residence authorisation €19.30
    Long-term residence TIE (larga duración, larga duración-UE) €21.87
    Card for a family member of a Spanish or EU citizen, EU resident certificate €12.00
    Autorización de regreso €10.72

    The amounts were approved by Orden PJC/617/2025, and under its Article 1.2 they may rise at the start of the year by the indexation set in the State Budget Act, so check them on the form on the day you pay. The card is issued at the police station, and when you collect it you confirm that the card is yours and that the fee has been paid (art. 209.11 of the Regulation).

    Can you leave Spain and return without the card?

    The issue is not leaving but coming back. Without a visa, Spain can be entered by holders of a valid TIE or an autorización de regreso issued by the Spanish authorities (art. 7.3 of the Regulation). If you have no card and you have not obtained a return authorisation, at the border you will have no Spanish document that exempts you from a visa. That is why you need to obtain the autorización de regreso before your trip. If citizens of your country do not need a visa for short trips to the Schengen area, the general rule also applies: no visa for up to 90 days in any 180 (art. 7.2). But it is designed for short stays, not for a resident's return.

    What Article 5 of the Regulation says about it:

    • Who. The holder of a TIE that was valid at the time of the loss, in the event of theft, loss, destruction or damage of the card, provided that they prove they have applied for a duplicate (art. 5.1). The same authorisation is used by those whose residence permit is in the process of renewal, if the renewal was filed on time.
    • Period. No more than 90 days (art. 5.2). During this period the number of exits and entries is not limited (art. 5.5).
    • Urgent trip. If the trip is driven by necessity, the application is dealt with as a priority (art. 5.2).
    • Who issues it. The Government Delegate or Sub-delegate, the Commissioner General for Immigration and Borders or the heads of National Police stations and border posts (art. 5.4).
    • Where to enter. Under EU law the authorisation is valid for entry only through Spanish border crossing points (art. 5.6). Plan your return route so that you pass border control in Spain, not in another Schengen country.
    • Fee. €10.72 on form 790-012 as of 24 November 2026 (annex to Orden PJC/617/2025, item 5.10).
    The order matters. The autorización de regreso is granted only after you have applied for the duplicate. If you fly out first and then try to sort it out, the remaining route is through a Spanish consulate abroad, and the Consulate General in Casablanca, for example, warns that the visa may take several weeks (see below).

    What should you do if the card went missing abroad?

    Then the solution lies with the Spanish consulate in the country where you are. Consulates issue a return visa for such cases, and each has its own list of documents. One example is the Consulate General in Casablanca (list updated in January 2025): an application form with a photo, a passport valid for at least four months, proof of the date you last left Spain or the Schengen area, copies of documents on your status in Spain where possible, and a police report on the loss or theft of the card. The consulate specifically warns that the visa is issued after a request to the central authorities in Spain, and even the minimum wait may take several weeks. You must collect the visa in person, within a month of being notified.

    Before applying, check the page of the consulate for your district: each consulate sets its own requirements, appointments and fees. In Casablanca, for example, documents not in Spanish are accepted only with a translation by a sworn translator (traductor jurado).

    What should you do if you have temporary protection?

    TIE cards issued under temporary protection in connection with the war in Ukraine have been extended by Orden INT/96/2026 until 4 March 2027, and the protection itself has been extended by EU Council Decision 2025/1460 until the same date. More on the timeframes in our article on temporary protection.

    The answers to frequently asked questions on temporary protection on the Ministry of Inclusion website (Ucrania Urgente section) also cover the case of having no card. A temporary protection card is not a travel document and does not replace a passport. If you have no TIE and need to travel to Ukraine, the ministry advises contacting the Oficina de Extranjería (the immigration office) or the police station in your province before the trip to find out whether an autorización de regreso or another procedure is needed. The key thing is being able to prove that you hold temporary protection: the authorisation granted by the resolution of the Oficina de Asilo y Refugio (OAR, the Office of Asylum and Refuge) remains fully valid. Keep a copy of this resolution with you.

    If your Ukrainian passport went missing together with the card, we have explained how to replace it in Spain in our article on passport replacement.

    Frequently asked questions

    My TIE was stolen. Can I fly out of Spain and come back?
    Yes, if you obtain an autorización de regreso before the trip. Under art. 5 RD 1155/2024 it can be obtained by the holder of a valid TIE in the event of theft, loss or damage of the card, if they have already applied for a duplicate. The authorisation is valid for no more than 90 days, you can leave and enter with it as many times as you like, but entry is only through Spanish border crossing points.
    How much does a duplicate TIE cost?
    There is no separate “duplicate” line on fee form 790-012: the amounts are divided by type of card. As of 24 November 2026 they are €16.08 (card with a first temporary residence or stay authorisation), €19.30 (renewal), €21.87 (long-term residence) and €12.00 (family member of a Spanish or EU citizen). At the start of the year the amounts may rise by the indexation set in the State Budget Act, so check them on the form on the day you pay.
    How long will the replacement for a lost card be valid?
    For the period the old one had left. Art. 209.9 of the Regulation states explicitly that a new card after loss, destruction or damage is not a renewal. If the application coincides with the period for renewing the permit, the duplicate and the renewal are processed together.
    I lost my TIE on holiday abroad. How do I get back to Spain?
    Through the Spanish consulate in the country where you are: consulates issue a return visa for such cases. The Consulate General in Casablanca, for example, asks for a passport, a police report on the loss or theft of the card and proof of the date you left Spain, and warns that the wait may be several weeks. The exact list is on the page of the consulate for your district.
    I have temporary protection and my TIE is missing. Can I travel to Ukraine?
    In its answers on temporary protection, the Ministry of Inclusion advises contacting the Oficina de Extranjería or the police station in your province before the trip to find out whether an autorización de regreso or another procedure is needed. It is important to have proof of protection: the resolution of the Oficina de Asilo y Refugio, which is fully valid. Temporary protection cards have been extended until 4 March 2027.
    Can I apply for a duplicate TIE online?
    No. Form EX-17 states that it can only be submitted in person at a National Police office, not online and not through public registries. Only the appointment is booked online. We can prepare the application, pay the fee and come with you to the appointment; more on the page NIE and TIE.
