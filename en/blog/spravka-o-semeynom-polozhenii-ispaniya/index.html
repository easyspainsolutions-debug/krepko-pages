    Marriage in Spain: which Ukrainian marital status document the civil registry needs

    Krepko · 1 October 2026 · ~9 min read
    In short. The DRACS does not issue a marital status certificate. For marriage in Spain, the Embassy of Ukraine specifies a statement of marital status: you write that you have never been married or that your marriage has ended, and a consul or a notary in Ukraine witnesses your signature. Next you need a translation into Spanish, and a document from a Ukrainian notary also needs an apostille. Civil registries have different lists, so check the exact list with the authority where you are opening your file.

    In its list of documents for marriage, the Madrid civil registry (Registro Civil) states plainly: a foreign national needs a certificate confirming that they are not married. A Ukrainian goes to order it and finds out that no such certificate exists in Ukraine. It is not about queues or “another department”: among the extracts issued by the civil registry office (DRACS), there is simply no form for marital status.

    There is a replacement for it. Below: which document actually confirms a Ukrainian's marital status, how a register extract differs from the statement known as an affidavit, what men aged 18–60 should do after the August changes at consulates, and what to add if you have been married before. We do not cover the marriage procedure itself here, only the document from Ukraine.

    Why there is no certificate and what to use instead

    The Ukrainian DRACS issues information only in the form of extracts (vytiahy) from the State Register of Civil Status Acts. This is set out in Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 1064 as amended on 12 March 2008. Among the extracts a person can obtain themselves are extracts on marriage, on divorce and on the absence of a civil status record, but there is no extract on marital status (Instruction on maintaining the register, para. 3.2). The Kyiv Interregional Office of the Ministry of Justice puts it briefly: «Довідка про сімейний стан відділами ДРАЦС не видається» (“DRACS offices do not issue a marital status certificate”).

    For marriage abroad, the same office points to a different document: a statement of marital status with the signature witnessed by a notary or a consul. This is the document the Embassy of Ukraine in Spain specifies for marriage before Spanish authorities, and it is the one commonly called an affidavit. Of the Spanish civil registries, Catalonia is the most direct: a foreign national may use a marital status document issued by a consulate, embassy or competent authority of their country.

    DRACS extract or statement: what is the difference

    DRACS extract Statement (affidavit)
    What it confirms The register entry: the marriage was registered, dissolved or no record was found Your status on the date of signing, based on your own statement
    Who issues it The DRACS in Ukraine, for a small fee A Ukrainian consul or a notary in Ukraine
    Your presence Not needed: it can be done through a representative with a power of attorney Needed: you sign in person
    Who needs it for marriage Above all, divorced people: it shows the marriage history Everyone: the main document, as specified by the embassy

    An extract on the absence of a civil status record seems suitable, but it is the result of a search for a specific record in the Ukrainian register, not a certificate of status. Whether a Spanish civil registry will accept it as proof that you are not married is not stated officially anywhere. The marriage extract («Витяг про шлюб») in Diia is also about something else: it confirms that a marriage was registered. Moreover, it can only be obtained through Ukrposhta and must be collected in person.

    A Ukrainian affidavit is not legally an oath. The notary does not verify the facts stated; they confirm that it was you who signed (Article 78 of the Law of Ukraine “On Notaries”). The law expressly allows a notary to witness the signature on a statement about facts such as marriage if the document is going to the authorities of another state. You sign in person, in the presence of the notary.

    Statement at the Ukrainian consulate

    According to the Embassy of Ukraine in Spain, for marriage before Spanish authorities the statement is submitted in one of three versions: never married, marriage ended by divorce, marriage ended by the death of a spouse. Templates for all three are on the embassy's page.

    • appointment via go.mfa.gov.ua, attendance in person;
    • a passport, international or internal, with copies, an application for a notarial act and a draft of your statement in Word format;
    • for divorced people, the divorce certificate or court decision on the divorce with a copy; for widowed people, the spouse's death certificate with a copy;
    • the fee according to the embassy's page as of 1 October 2026 is €55: €18 for the signature and €37 for the translation into Spanish. Payment is by card, and the document is ready on the day of submission.
    About the consulate's translation. The Civil Registry Regulation (Article 86) allows a translation made by a consul. But the official pages of the civil registries of Madrid, Valencia and Catalonia require a translation by a sworn translator (traductor jurado). Before booking at the consulate, ask your civil registry whether they will accept the consular translation: if not, you will also need a sworn one.

    Men aged 18–60: the consulate only with a military registration document

    Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 981 of 29 July 2026 has been in force since 8 August. It does not prohibit consular services for men aged 18–60, but it sets a condition: a valid military registration document in electronic form, including one printed from the Reserve+ app. The consul checks it through the e-Consul system or by QR code. If there is no document, if it is invalid or if it lacks confirmation that the data has been updated, the consular act will be refused (para. 11 of the Procedure). The embassy has expressly included the electronic military registration document (e-VOD) in the list of documents for the statement of marital status.

    There are three exceptions, and marriage is not among them: a certificate for return to Ukraine, acts concerning children whose other parent is a foreign national, and statements by people under arrest. From around February 2027, paragraphs 12–13 will come into effect: it will be possible to obtain a consular act without the document as well, by updating your data through consular registration. How this will work in practice is not yet known. A detailed analysis is in the article on consulates refusing men aged 18–60.

    If you do not have an e-VOD, there are two routes without the consulate:

    • A statement before a Spanish notary. The document is in Spanish from the outset, and for a Spanish civil registry it does not need an apostille (Article 87 of the Civil Registry Regulation). The same regulation (Article 363) allows single, widowed or divorced status to be confirmed by a sworn statement. But whether this rule applies to a foreign national in a marriage file has not been officially confirmed, and Madrid, as a general rule, asks for a certificate from the country of citizenship. So this route is first agreed with the specific civil registry or notary. How the document itself works is explained on the page about the affidavit before a notary in Spain.
    • Extracts from Ukraine through a representative. A representative with a power of attorney can obtain an extract from the DRACS (Resolution No. 1064, para. 13). The Ministry of Justice of Ukraine allows a power of attorney from abroad to be made before a local notary as well, in which case it needs an apostille and a translation into Ukrainian. For divorced people, this is the main source of documents about the previous marriage.

    If you have been married before

    A divorce certificate does not replace confirmation of status. It says that a specific marriage ended, but not that there was no new one after it. So divorced people usually need both documents: proof of the divorce and the statement “marriage ended”.

    In Ukraine, a divorce is formalised in two ways. If the marriage was dissolved at the DRACS (couples without children on a joint application, and also on the application of one spouse if the other has been declared missing or legally incapable), a divorce certificate is issued. If it went through a court, the document is the decision that has entered into force: the court itself sends it to the DRACS, and a note is made there in the marriage record (Family Code of Ukraine, Articles 106–107, 114–116).

    The civil registries of Madrid and Valencia ask for the full record of the previous marriage with a note of the divorce (certificado literal). Valencia states plainly that it will not accept the court divorce decision on its own. Catalonia also allows another option if your country's law works that way: the full record of the previous marriage plus the divorce certificate or a copy of the court decision with a note that it has entered into force. The closest thing to such a record in Ukraine is the full extract of the marriage record: it lists all changes in order, including the divorce. It is issued to the person themselves or to a representative. Whether a specific civil registry will recognise it as equivalent is not officially stated, so it is better to show a sample before ordering.

    Recognition of the divorce in Spain. A foreign decision is entered in the Spanish register after exequatur or by decision of a civil registry officer (Article 96 of Law 20/2011). If the divorced person is a Spanish citizen or the previous marriage is already entered in the Spanish register, in practice civil registries usually first recognise the divorce and only then allow a new marriage. If both former spouses are foreign nationals and the previous marriage was not registered in Spain, there is no direct rule. Ask about this at the civil registry on your very first visit: recognition is a separate procedure with its own timeframes.

    Apostille and translation

    Ukraine and Spain are parties to the Hague Convention, so documents from Ukrainian DRACS offices, courts and notaries need an apostille for Spain, not consular legalisation. They are apostilled by the Ministry of Justice: up to three working days from the registration of the application in the electronic register of apostilles, for a small state fee.

    A statement from the Ukrainian consulate does not need legalisation: Ukraine and Spain are parties to the 1968 London Convention abolishing the legalisation of documents executed by diplomatic agents or consular officers. For Ukraine it has been in force since 6 April 2023. If the civil registry nevertheless asks you to legalise the consular statement, refer to this convention.

    The translation into Spanish is done after the apostille, so that the stamp is translated too. The civil registries of Madrid, Valencia and Catalonia require a translation by a sworn translator, and there are few such translators from Ukrainian in Spain (only a handful in the register of the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs). A statement signed before a Spanish notary needs neither an apostille nor a translation for a Spanish civil registry.

    How long the document lasts and when to order it

    Neither Spanish nor Ukrainian law sets a single period of validity for a marital status document: each authority sets its own. The civil registries of Catalonia accept it if it is no older than six months, unless the document itself states a different period. Madrid and Valencia do not give a period for it on their official pages. The time limits there apply to other papers: a birth certificate no older than one year, a municipal registration certificate (padrón) one month in Valencia and three months at notaries in Madrid and Andalusia. And the “six months” in Article 252 of the Civil Registry Regulation is the time limit for a different document: the Spanish certificate of capacity to marry abroad.

    In practice, authorities often want a recent document, especially the statement: it speaks to your situation on the date of signing. So the order is as follows: first find out where and when you are opening your file and what time limit they set, then sign the statement and order the extracts. We start precisely with a question to the authority, and we launch the order in Ukraine after the reply. The authorisation also has a time limit: Valencia states that it is valid for one year without extension, and under Article 58.5 of Law 20/2011, if the marriage is not concluded within a year after the public notices or the steps replacing them, they are carried out again.

    Law 20/2011 does not set a time limit for processing a marriage file, either for the civil registry or for a notary: it depends on the specific authority. What the procedure itself consists of is on the Getting married page.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I simply order a marital status certificate from the DRACS?
    No. The DRACS only issues register extracts, and there is no marital status form among them; the Kyiv Interregional Office of the Ministry of Justice confirms this directly. For marriage abroad, instead of a certificate you submit a statement of marital status with a witnessed signature: before a Ukrainian consul or a notary in Ukraine.
    I have a divorce certificate. Is that enough?
    Usually not. The certificate says that a specific marriage ended, but not that there was no new one after it. You need both the statement of marital status and a document about the previous marriage in the form your civil registry asks for: Madrid and Valencia, for example, want the full marriage record with a note of the divorce.
    I am 34 and have no e-VOD. How do I prove I am not married?
    The consulate will refuse without a valid military registration document: this is how Resolution No. 981 has worked since 8 August 2026. What remains is a statement before a Spanish notary, if your civil registry or notary accepts it, and extracts from Ukraine through a representative with a power of attorney. Agree with the authority on what exactly will work before ordering anything.
    Who can obtain a DRACS extract on my behalf?
    A representative with a power of attorney: this right is expressly set out in Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 1064. A power of attorney from Spain is made before a consul or a Spanish notary; in the second case it needs an apostille and a translation into Ukrainian. For men aged 18–60 without a valid e-VOD, only before a Spanish notary.
    How long before submission should I make the statement?
    The law does not set a time limit, but authorities often want a recent document, and the statement speaks to your status on the date of signing. First find out the date and requirements of your civil registry or notary, then sign the statement and order the extracts. It is not worth doing it in advance “to have it ready”.
    The consulate translates the statement into Spanish. Do I still need a sworn translation?
    It depends on the civil registry. The Civil Registry Regulation allows a consul's translation, but the official pages of the civil registries of Madrid, Valencia and Catalonia require a translation by a sworn translator. Check with your authority before booking at the consulate.
    My marriage certificate is lost, and the marriage was dissolved long ago. What should I do?
    After a divorce, a marriage certificate is not reissued; instead, on application, a marriage extract is issued to confirm the premarital surname. If the civil registry asks for the full record with a note of the divorce, order the full extract of the marriage record: it lists all the changes. A representative with a power of attorney can obtain it.
