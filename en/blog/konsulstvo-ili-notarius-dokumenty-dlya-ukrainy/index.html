    Consulate or Spanish notary: where to arrange a document for Ukraine if you are in Spain

    Krepko · 20 November 2026 · ~7 min read
    In short. A power of attorney, a consent and a statement for Ukraine are arranged in Spain before a Spanish notary or before a Ukrainian consul. A document from a Spanish notary is valid in Ukraine with an apostille from the College of Notaries, and a power of attorney, according to the explanation of the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine, is translated into Ukrainian and the translation is then certified in Ukraine. A consular document is Ukrainian from the start, with no apostille or translation, but the consul sees people in person, by appointment, and establishes identity from a Ukrainian passport. Where passports are replaced, notarial documents are not arranged, and a consul does not certify a contract for the sale of a flat in Ukraine.

    You need to sell a flat in Ukraine, give consent to a transaction by your husband or wife, or send a notary a statement in an inheritance case, but you live in Spain. You can arrange such a document here before a Spanish notary or at the Ukrainian consulate. The passport service, where passports are replaced, is not suitable for this: notarial acts are performed by notaries and consuls.

    How does the route through a Spanish notary work?

    The Ministry of Justice of Ukraine names it as one of two lawful ways: the document is drawn up under the law of the country where you are, and then confirmed for Ukraine. Spain and Ukraine are parties to the Hague Apostille Convention (Spain since 1978, Ukraine since 22 December 2003), so an apostille is affixed instead of consular legalisation. On notarial documents it is affixed by the College of Notaries, and only on the paper original (RD 1497/2011, Article 3; Orden JUS/1207/2011, Article 11).

    • Identity. A foreign national who lives in Spain proves it with a passport or a Spanish residence card (Reglamento Notarial, Article 161). So you can sign a document with a valid TIE (foreigner identity card) if your Ukrainian passport has expired. What to do with a temporary protection card that has the old expiry date printed on it is covered in the article on an expired passport.
    • Language. At your request, the notary draws up the document in two columns: in Spanish and in your language (Article 150).
    • Translation. According to the explanation of the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine, a power of attorney from a foreign notary is translated into Ukrainian and the translation is certified by a notary later, in Ukraine. How much to translate is decided in the end by the notary, bank or authority that accepts the document.
    • Checks in Ukraine. A Ukrainian notary accepts a document from abroad with an apostille (Law “On Notaries”, Article 100), and does not check a foreign power of attorney against the Unified Register of Powers of Attorney (Procedure for Performing Notarial Acts, Section I, Chapter 4, point 4).

    Why a flat specifically needs a notarial power of attorney: its form must match the form of the transaction (Civil Code of Ukraine, Article 245), and a contract for the sale of housing is certified by a notary (Article 657). What to include in the text is covered in the article on a power of attorney from Spain for Ukraine.

    When is the consulate more convenient?

    A consular power of attorney is a Ukrainian document from the start: according to the explanation of the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine, it does not require legalisation or translation and is valid from the moment it is received. It is, however, entered in the Unified Register of Powers of Attorney: if the consulate has no access to the register, the power of attorney is registered by a notary in Ukraine at the request of the principal. A consul certifies transactions, including powers of attorney and wills, and witnesses signatures (Law “On Notaries”, Article 38). This route has its own conditions:

    • an appointment through e-Consul and a draft of the document in electronic form;
    • a personal visit: the documents are signed in the presence of the consul;
    • a Ukrainian citizen's passport, either the international passport or the internal one, as well as the taxpayer identification number (RNOKPP), a document showing legal residence and a copy of the representative's passport;
    • a consular fee, and paperwork in Ukrainian only.

    Men aged 18–60 also need a military registration document for consular acts (Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 981): we covered what follows from this in the article on consular services for men. This requirement does not apply to anyone else.

    Why not the passport service?

    The passport service of DP “Dokument” issues international passports, international passports for children under 14 and ID cards (according to the list of services of the centre in Valencia). Under the law, notarial acts are performed by notaries, and abroad by Ukrainian consular institutions (Law “On Notaries”, Articles 1 and 38), so a power of attorney or a consent cannot be arranged there. How to replace a passport and where to go is described in the article on replacing a passport in Spain.

    When can neither a notary nor a consul help?

    • An action that can only be done in person. A representative cannot perform a transaction which, by its content, can only be performed by the person themselves (Civil Code of Ukraine, Article 238). A statement accepting an inheritance is submitted by the heir in person (Article 1269): a notary does not accept statements accepting or renouncing an inheritance from a representative acting under a power of attorney (Procedure, Section II, Chapter 10, point 3.6). The signature on such a statement is witnessed by a notary or a consul, and it can be sent by post: if it was sent before the end of the six months, the deadline has been met (ibid., point 3.21). More detail in the article on inheritance in Ukraine.
    • A property contract in Ukraine. A consul does not certify contracts for the sale or mortgage of housing in Ukraine (Article 38). The usual route is a power of attorney from Spain, with the contract signed by the representative.
    • Facts confirmed by a public authority. Ukrainian law does not allow birth, marriage, death or property ownership to be confirmed by a statement with an authenticated signature (Article 78 of the Law “On Notaries”). For this you need a certificate or an extract from a register.

    How to choose the route: three questions

    1. Do you have a valid Ukrainian passport? Without a valid passport, the option left is a Spanish notary: a valid TIE is enough there.
    2. Is there a deadline? The Ministry of Justice of Ukraine itself notes that you sometimes have to wait a long time for free consulate slots and travel hundreds of kilometres. If the document is needed urgently, a foreign notary is, in the Ministry's own assessment, more convenient.
    3. Who will accept the document in Ukraine? A notary, a bank or a service centre of the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine (MVS) may look at the text, the apostille and the translation in their own way. It is better to find out their requirements before signing.

    If the flat transaction is within a marriage, you will also need the consent of the other spouse: how to arrange it in Spain is covered in the article on spouse's consent to a sale.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is a Spanish notary suitable for a consent and a statement, not just for a power of attorney?
    Yes. A Spanish notary arranges the consent of a husband or wife to the sale of a flat as a full notarial document: an authenticated signature alone is not enough for such a consent (Family Code of Ukraine, Article 65). On a statement, the notary witnesses the signature: it confirms who signed, not the facts in the text (Law “On Notaries”, Article 78). Both documents receive an apostille from the College of Notaries, and only on the paper original.
    Can I arrange a document for Ukraine if my Ukrainian passport has expired?
    Before a Spanish notary, yes: a foreign national who lives in Spain proves their identity with a passport or a Spanish residence card (Reglamento Notarial, Article 161), so a valid TIE will do. If it is a temporary protection card with the old expiry date printed on it, we covered what to take to the notary in the article on an expired passport. A Ukrainian consul establishes a citizen's identity from a Ukrainian passport, either the international or the internal one.
    Can I arrange a power of attorney at the same place where passports are replaced?
    No. The passport service issues international passports and ID cards: this is how it appears in the list of services of the centre in Valencia. Under the law, notarial acts are performed by notaries, and abroad by Ukrainian consular institutions (Law “On Notaries”, Articles 1 and 38). A power of attorney, consent or statement for Ukraine is arranged before a Spanish notary or a consul.
    Can I sign a contract for the sale of a flat in Ukraine at the consulate?
    No. A consul does not certify contracts for the sale or mortgage of housing and other property in Ukraine (Law “On Notaries”, Article 38). Usually a power of attorney to sell is arranged from Spain, and the representative signs the contract before a notary in Ukraine.
    Who will authenticate the signature on an inheritance statement: a Spanish notary or a consul?
    Either: the signature can be witnessed by a Spanish notary, in which case the statement receives an apostille, or by a Ukrainian consul (Law “On Notaries”, Article 38). Such a statement cannot be submitted under a power of attorney (Civil Code of Ukraine, Article 1269; Procedure for Performing Notarial Acts, Section II, Chapter 10, point 3.6). A statement sent by post before the end of the six months (Civil Code of Ukraine, Article 1270) is considered sent on time (Procedure, point 3.21). The step-by-step procedure is in the article on inheritance in Ukraine.
    Is a translation needed if the document was arranged at the consulate?
    No. According to the explanation of the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine, a power of attorney certified at the consulate does not require legalisation or translation and is valid in Ukraine from the moment it is received: the consul draws it up in Ukrainian. It is, however, entered in the Unified Register of Powers of Attorney: if the consulate has no access to the register, this is done by a notary in Ukraine at the request of the principal. Translation and an apostille are needed for a document from a Spanish notary.
    Does the military registration document requirement apply to all Ukrainians?
    No. Under Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 981, the military registration document is submitted for consular acts by men who are Ukrainian citizens aged 18 to 60. It does not apply to women, or to men under 18 or over 60.
