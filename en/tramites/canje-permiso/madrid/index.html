    Local specifics in Madrid

    In Madrid, driving licences are exchanged by the Jefatura Provincial de Tráfico (the provincial traffic office). In person, licences from countries with an agreement are exchanged there only by appointment; the other route is an online application via the DGT sede (online portal). There are many medical centres in the city for the psychophysical aptitude report — we choose the one closest to your home.

    The temporary permit issued at the visit is valid only in Spain, so we plan the visit with your trips in mind. We prepare the filing and keep track of the card until it arrives.

    Besides the Jefatura in Madrid itself, the DGT has two more public offices in the region — local branches in Alcorcón, for the south of the region, and in Alcalá de Henares, for the east; both are part of the Madrid Jefatura. Before the visit we check which of the three offices your appointment is at, so that you do not go to the wrong one.

    What we need from you

    • a foreign driving licence that is valid on the date of the exchange;
    • your passport and NIE or TIE card;
    • a document showing your status: the temporary protection decision or a residence permit;
    • a photo: when Ukrainian licences are exchanged, the medical centre sends it to the DGT together with the report; for EEA licences one 32×26 mm photo is needed.

    How the process works

    1. ReviewWe look at the country and category of your licence and your status in Spain, and tell you which exchange procedure applies to you.
    2. Medical checkWe take you through the psychophysical report at an accredited centre with an interpreter (it is not needed for EEA licences).
    3. FilingWe fully prepare your filing: the application, the fee, the set of documents for the DGT — and accompany you to the visit at the Jefatura.
    4. CardAt the visit you are given a temporary permit; after that we follow the licence check and the issue of the plastic card.

    Timeframes and payment

    The timeframe is made up of three parts: waiting for a date at the DGT (depends on the province), the check of the licence with the issuing country — Ukraine replies within 30 days — and the production of the card: the DGT sends the final licence by post in about a month and a half. The temporary permit is issued straight away, on the day of the visit to the Jefatura.

    The government fee is tasa 2.3 (€28.87) for a motorcycle and a car, and tasa 2.1 (€94.05) for lorries and buses. We quote the cost of our work after reviewing your case.

    We agree the terms and price before work begins, with no hidden extras. The review is free.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I go to the Jefatura without an appointment?
    In person — by appointment: the Jefatura does not exchange licences from countries with an agreement, including Ukrainian ones, without an appointment; the DGT's exception is people aged 65 and over and people with reduced mobility, and only for their own matters. The other route is an online application via the DGT sede: then you go to the Jefatura once, when the DGT invites you.
    Can I travel abroad on the temporary permit?
    No: the temporary permit issued at the visit is valid for six months and only in Spain. If you have a trip coming up, we plan the visit so that the plastic card has time to arrive — usually about a month and a half.
    What if my Ukrainian licence has already expired?
    Only a valid licence can be exchanged. If it has expired, the exchange will not work — we look at what is possible in your case: restoring a valid licence or obtaining a Spanish one.
    Can I apply for the exchange through a representative?
    Yes. The online procedure with the DGT can be handled by a representative registered in the Registro de apoderamientos (register of authorised representatives). You come in person only once — to hand in the original licence and receive the temporary permit.
    Do I need a sworn translation of my Ukrainian licence?
    For the Ukrainian exchange procedure the DGT requires neither a sworn translation of the licence nor an international driving permit. For licences from other countries a translation is usually needed — we check based on the issuing country.
