    Identification for the PFU — by 31 December 2026

    If you do not complete it, payments will be stopped from 1 January. The PFU schedules a video call within a month of the request, so we submit the request no later than the end of November.

    A Ukrainian pension paid abroad follows one rule: once a year, by 31 December, you have to confirm to the Pension Fund of Ukraine (PFU) that you are who you say you are. If you do not, payments are stopped from 1 January. The procedure itself is free and can take as little as fifteen minutes — if you know which route to take and what to prepare. That is what we take care of.

    There is no need to travel to Ukraine. In most cases, there is no need to go to the consulate either.

    Timeframes and payment

    The identification itself is a free state procedure, and we say so plainly. Our work is help and setup: the cost depends on the route and is discussed after the review.

    Everyone who starts in time completes it by 31 December. The online route can take as little as fifteen minutes; the video call involves up to a month of waiting for the session, which is why we submit the request no later than November.

    We agree the terms and price before work begins, with no hidden extras.

    Who this applies to

    Everyone who receives a Ukrainian pension or insurance payments and is abroad for more than 183 days a year. The procedure has applied since 2025 (Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 299): identification is completed every year, by 31 December.

    An important detail that not everyone knows: identification is valid for a calendar year. If you completed it at the end of 2025, for payments in 2027 you need to complete it again by 31 December 2026.

    Three methods from Spain

    Diia.Signature — fifteen minutes, if you have a biometric document

    The fastest route: logging in to your personal account on the PFU portal with Diia.Signature counts as identification — immediately, with no applications and no waiting. The most convenient way is through the “Pension Fund of Ukraine” app, right from your phone.

    There is one pitfall: Diia.Signature can only be activated with a biometric document — a passport with a chip or an ID card — via NFC on a smartphone. The old paper booklet without a chip will not work. The second point of failure is the photo check: for older people it often fails on the first attempt. We guide you step by step: lighting, background, how to hold the phone — until it works.

    A video call with the Pension Fund

    If you have no biometric document, we prepare your request for a video conference with a PFU officer: it is submitted without any electronic signature, the session takes place through a familiar messenger of your choice, it lasts about twenty minutes, and you show your passport during it.

    The key thing here is timing: the session is scheduled within a month of the request being submitted. Submitting in December risks not making it before the 31st. Our rule: the request goes in no later than the end of November.

    The consulate — when the first two do not work

    The consulate certifies that the person is alive, and this document, together with an application, is sent to the PFU. The route works, but it is the hardest: getting an appointment at the consulate is a challenge in itself, and from Alicante the nearest consulate offering this service is in Barcelona. We use this option as a fallback and prepare a complete set of documents for it.

    If you have neither a biometric passport nor the possibility of getting to the consulate, this can be solved too: an urgent biometric passport is issued at the passport service in Spain, and after that the online route opens up. We will review your case and work out what is faster.

    If the deadline has already been missed

    Payments are stopped from 1 January, but they are not lost: complete the identification within a year, and your pension will be resumed from the date it was stopped, with back payment for all the months missed. We prepare the application for resumption and guide you through the identification.

    Your pension should keep arriving in January too

    Write to us: when you last completed identification, and what passport and phone you have. We will tell you which route is yours and go through it together. The review is free.

    Frequently asked questions

    I completed identification in December last year. Do I have to do it again?
    Yes. Identification is valid for a calendar year: one completed in 2025 covers 2026, and to receive your pension in 2027 you need to complete it by 31 December 2026.
    I have a booklet passport without a chip. Does that mean I cannot do it online?
    Not through Diia.Signature: it can only be activated with a biometric document. But there is still the video call with the PFU — with no electronic signature at all. And if you want the online route for the future, an urgent biometric passport can be issued in Spain, without travelling to Ukraine.
    My mother only has a basic push-button phone. What should we do?
    The video call can be made from any device with a camera — even your own smartphone or tablet, next to your mother. She herself must be present at the session with her passport; we will organise the rest.
    Can I complete identification on behalf of a relative?
    No — the Fund has to see the person themselves: in person, by video or through their own Diia.Signature. But you can be next to them and guide them step by step — that is exactly what we do.
    My pension has already been stopped. Is everything lost?
    No. Complete the identification within a year of the date it was stopped, and payments will be resumed retroactively, with back payment for the months missed. We prepare the application and guide you through the identification itself.
    How can I make sure the identification has been counted?
    In your personal account on the PFU portal there is a “My Identification” (Моя ідентифікація) service, where the mark is shown. We check it together after you complete it — this is part of the work.
