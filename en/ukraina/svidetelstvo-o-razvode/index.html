    How the process works

    1. You tell us how and when the divorce was processed and where you are submitting the document.
    2. We identify the document — a certificate, a full extract of the marriage record or a court decision — and check your authority's requirements.
    3. We agree the scope of work and the cost before we start.
    4. The Krepko team in Ukraine orders the document from the DRACS or the court.
    5. We have it apostilled by the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine.
    6. We arrange a sworn translation, if needed, and check the spelling of names.
    7. We send it by courier to Spain.

    Timeframes and payment

    A duplicate certificate or a DRACS extract with an apostille usually takes 3–4 weeks including delivery. In some regions it takes longer, for example in the Kharkiv, Dnipropetrovsk and Zaporizhzhia regions. A copy of a court decision takes up to 25 working days to be issued, followed by the apostille and delivery in 5–7 working days. We tell you the timeframe for your region before we start.

    Which document confirms a divorce

    It depends on how the marriage was dissolved (Family Code of Ukraine, arts. 106–107, 114–115):

    • through the DRACS — couples without children on a joint application, and also on the application of one spouse if the other has been declared missing or legally incapacitated. The document is a divorce certificate;
    • through a court — the document is the court decision that has entered into force. Until 2010, on the basis of a court decision the DRACS also issued a certificate; since 2010 there is no certificate, and a note of the divorce is added to the marriage record.

    There is also a third document — a full extract of the marriage record. It shows the whole history of the record, including the note of the divorce. This is the closest Ukrainian equivalent of the Spanish certificado literal with marginal notes.

    After a divorce, a duplicate marriage certificate is not issued: instead, the DRACS issues a marriage extract, which confirms the maiden name.

    What is requested for a marriage in Spain

    The Registro Civil in Madrid and in the Valencian Community (Alicante, Valencia) asks divorced people for a certificado literal of the previous marriage with a note of the divorce. Valencia states explicitly that it does not accept a court divorce decision. The closest thing to such a document is a full extract of the marriage record, but whether your civil registry will accept it is not officially set out. So we first check the requirements of your Registro Civil or notary, and then order.

    A divorce document does not say that you have not entered into a new marriage. If the authority asks you to confirm your current status as well, that is a separate document — confirmation of marital status.

    Apostille and translation

    The apostille on DRACS and court documents is placed by the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine, and only in Ukraine: the consulate in Spain does not place it. Under the Ministry of Justice procedure (Order No. 3177/5, since 1 February 2026), the apostille is placed or refused within three working days. There is an electronic version of the apostille, but whether Spanish authorities will accept it has not been officially confirmed, so for Spain we prepare a paper document with an apostille. More on the apostille for a Ukrainian document page.

    The Registro Civil and Extranjería accept a sworn translation, but not a translation made in Ukraine. We arrange a sworn translation and check the spelling of names against your passport and TIE.

    Frequently asked questions

    We divorced through a court. Why is there no divorce certificate?
    Since 2010, no certificate has been issued after a court divorce: the court decision itself serves as the document, and a note is added to the marriage record. We will order a copy of the court decision or a full extract of the marriage record — whichever your authority will accept.
    I have no divorce papers left. Can I get them again?
    Yes. The DRACS issues a duplicate certificate and extracts based on the register data, not on your copy; a copy of the decision is issued by the court that made it. You do not need to travel: the Krepko team in Ukraine orders the document.
    I want to get married in Spain. Which divorce documents do I need?
    Usually a document on the end of the previous marriage and confirmation that you are not currently married. Madrid and Valencia ask for the record of the previous marriage with a note of the divorce; Valencia does not accept a court decision. We check the requirements of your civil registry or notary before you order.
    Is an apostille needed?
    Yes, for Spanish authorities the divorce document is needed with an apostille. It is placed by the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine and is included in the process.
    The marriage has been dissolved, but I need to confirm my maiden name. What should I order?
    A marriage extract: after a divorce, a duplicate marriage certificate is not issued, but the extract confirms the surname before the marriage. We will order it with an apostille.
    Can I order it together with other documents?
    Yes, and it is better value: a birth certificate, a divorce document and extracts travel in one shipment. Send us the full list — we will price it all together.
