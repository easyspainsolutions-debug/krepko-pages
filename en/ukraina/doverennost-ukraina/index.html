    It can also be in Ukrainian — choose the language that suits you and your representative.

    In short. You can sell a flat in Ukraine, re-register a car, handle an inheritance or deal with a bank without leaving Spain, using a power of attorney executed before a Spanish notary. We draft its text straight away in the language you need and to the requirements of the Ukrainian side, support you at the signing, arrange the apostille at the College of Notaries and send the paper original by courier to your representative in Ukraine. Spain and Ukraine are both parties to the Hague Convention: for the Ukrainian side, an apostille is enough for such a power of attorney, consular legalisation is not needed, and foreign powers of attorney are not entered in the Unified Register of Powers of Attorney of Ukraine.

    What matters in Ukraine require a power of attorney from Spain?

    • selling or buying a flat, house or land — your representative signs the contract before a Ukrainian notary and, if you have authorised this, receives the money;
    • a car — sale, re-registration, deregistration;
    • an inheritance — collecting documents, the certificate of inheritance, registering the property;
    • a bank — account transactions, closing an account, statements and certificates;
    • documents — certificates and extracts from Ukrainian registers;
    • representation — before public authorities, at an administrative services centre (TsNAP), before a notary and in court.

    One power of attorney can cover several matters at once, provided all the powers are listed in advance: nothing can be added to a finished power of attorney — you would need a new one.

    Inheritance: one application must be made in person. Ukrainian law requires the application to accept an inheritance to be signed by the heir personally; it cannot be submitted under a power of attorney. The signature on it can be witnessed by a Spanish notary or at the consulate — we check in advance which option the notary in Ukraine who is handling the case will accept, and send the apostilled document to them. The deadline for accepting an inheritance is six months, so do not put this document off. Everything else in the inheritance case your representative handles under the power of attorney.

    What will we check in the text for you?

    A power of attorney works only as well as its wording is precise. If the action you need is not in the text, your representative goes to the notary or the bank — and the matter comes to a halt. That is why, before the signing, we check:

    • the date and place of execution — without a date, the power of attorney is invalid;
    • your details and those of your representative — full names as in the passport, dates of birth, documents and taxpayer identification numbers (RNOKPP), if you have them;
    • specific actions — not “manage the property”, but exactly what: sell the flat at such-and-such an address, sign the contract, receive the money, submit an application to such-and-such an authority;
    • details of the asset — the address and cadastral number of the property, the car's VIN, the account or case number;
    • the term — we write it out in words so that there is no doubt about when it expires;
    • delegation — whether your representative can pass the powers on to another person: under Ukrainian law this is possible, as a rule, only if the power of attorney expressly allows it.

    If it is already known who in Ukraine will work with the power of attorney — a notary, a bank, a lawyer — we agree the wording with them before the signing. That way the document will not have to be redone.

    What if I execute the power of attorney at the Consulate of Ukraine?

    That is a lawful alternative: a power of attorney executed at the consulate is a Ukrainian notarial document, and it needs neither an apostille nor a translation. But this route has its own conditions:

    • travel — residents of Alicante and Valencia are served by the Consulate General in Barcelona, and residents of Murcia by the consulate in Málaga. Notarial acts can be performed at any consulate, but only in person;
    • an appointment — you need a free slot in the electronic queue, and the draft power of attorney is agreed with the consulate in advance;
    • Ukrainian documents only — the consul establishes your identity from your Ukrainian passport or ID card; Spanish TIE and NIE documents are not accepted;
    • men aged 18–60 currently need a valid electronic military registration document with verified data for consular services.

    A Spanish notary has none of these conditions: the signing takes place in your city, on an agreed date. A Spanish notary does not ask for a military registration document, and the Ukrainian rules on notarial practice contain no such requirement for the person granting the power of attorney; we check the requirements of a particular bank or public authority in advance.

    What is important to know in advance?

    • An apostille does not replace a translation. It certifies the notary's signature and seal. If the receiving party in Ukraine asks for a translation into Ukrainian, it is done in Ukraine and certified by a Ukrainian notary — this is what the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine recommends. If the power of attorney is in Ukrainian from the start, there is usually less to translate.
    • Requirements differ from place to place. A notary, a bank, a TsNAP and an MVS service centre may each view a power of attorney in their own way. That is why, before the signing, we check who will work with the document and draft the text accordingly.
    • How recent the document is. An apostille has no expiry date, and the term of the power of attorney is stated in the document itself. But an individual notary or bank may ask for a more recent power of attorney — we check this in advance.
    • A car. The Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine (MVS) explains that a representative cannot re-register a car in their own name. If you need flexibility, you can name two representatives — this is our recommendation, not an MVS requirement.
    • Revocation. A power of attorney can be revoked before any Spanish notary. Ukrainian notaries cannot see Spanish notarial databases, so the revocation must be communicated to your representative and to anyone with whom they might conclude a transaction.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I have a bilingual power of attorney?
    Yes — we draft the power of attorney straight away in the language you need: Russian or Ukrainian. You read the text in advance and sign, before a Spanish notary, a document in which you understand every clause. If a notary or bank in Ukraine will work with the power of attorney, Ukrainian usually reduces the amount of translation — we will tell you which option is more convenient in your case.
    I need to sell a flat in Ukraine, but I am in Spain. Do I have to travel?
    No. You execute a power of attorney before a Spanish notary in favour of a person in Ukraine, and they sign the contract for you before a Ukrainian notary. We draft the text for this transaction — with the flat's address and cadastral number and the right to sign the contract and receive the money — support you at the signing, arrange the apostille and send the original to your representative. If the flat is jointly owned by spouses, the other spouse's consent will also be needed: we prepare that too.
    Will a Spanish power of attorney be accepted in Ukraine?
    Spain and Ukraine are both parties to the Hague Convention, so for the Ukrainian side an apostille is enough for a Spanish notarial power of attorney, and consular legalisation is not needed. The document is handled by a particular notary, bank or authority, and each has its own requirements for the text and the translation. That is why we check them before the signing, not after.
    Do I need a translation into Ukrainian?
    If the receiving party in Ukraine asks for one — yes, and it is done in Ukraine: the translation is certified by a Ukrainian notary, as the Ministry of Justice of Ukraine recommends. We will tell you exactly what needs translating and, if necessary, arrange the translation through the Krepko team in Ukraine. If the power of attorney is in Ukrainian from the start, usually only the notary's certification and the apostille are translated; the final scope of the translation is decided by the notary or bank that accepts the document.
    Can an inheritance in Ukraine be handled under a power of attorney?
    Almost all of it — yes: collecting documents, obtaining the certificate of inheritance, registering the property. There is one exception: the law requires the application to accept an inheritance to be signed in person; it cannot be submitted under a power of attorney. The signature on it can be witnessed by a Spanish notary or at the consulate — we check in advance which option the notary handling the case will accept. The deadline for accepting an inheritance is six months, so start with this.
    I am 18–60, and the consulate will not serve me without a military registration document. How can I execute a power of attorney?
    Before a Spanish notary: they do not ask for a military registration document, and the Ukrainian rules on notarial practice contain no such requirement for the person granting the power of attorney. If the power of attorney is needed for a bank or a public authority, we check their requirements in advance.
    Does my representative need to come to Spain?
    No. Only their details are needed: full name as in the passport, date of birth, passport details and taxpayer identification number. Only you sign the power of attorney.
    Can a power of attorney be revoked?
    Yes, at any time — before any Spanish notary. Ukrainian notaries cannot see Spanish notarial databases, so the revocation must be communicated to your representative and to anyone with whom they might conclude a transaction. For a Ukrainian notary, the revocation document is, as a rule, needed with an apostille and a translation. More on the page on revoking a power of attorney.
