    Política de privacidad / Privacy policy

    In force since 2026-04-29 · Última actualización: 26 octubre 2026
    In brief. Krepko collects the minimum of data (name, contact details, subject of the enquiry) solely in order to contact you about your request. It does not share data with third parties, except partners involved in a specific service (notary, bank, gestor). It deletes data on request. It does not send spam.

    1. Data controller (Responsable del tratamiento)

    WEONERENT, S.L., trading name Krepko: administrative support with documents and immigration. It does not provide legal services — for legal matters it works with partner lawyers.

    • Activity: administrative support with notarial, banking and immigration procedures in Spain. Translation, logistics, coordination of partners.
    • Address: Avinguda de la Costa Blanca 93, 03540 Alicante, España
    • Contact: hello@krepko.es · +34 624 309 985 · Telegram +34 624 309 985
    • CIF: B22809552

    2. What data Krepko collects (Datos personales que tratamos)

    Through the website krepko.es, and through WhatsApp and Telegram on +34 624 309 985, Krepko may collect:

    • Name (how to address you)
    • Contact details — Telegram username, WhatsApp number or email
    • City in Spain
    • Subject of the enquiry — notary, bank, residence permit, documents, etc.
    • A brief description of your situation that you provide voluntarily
    • Technical data when you visit the website — Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity (anonymised IP address, traffic source, behaviour on the page, clicks and scrolling; only after you consent to cookies) — for traffic analytics
    • Anonymised statistics without cookies — which page was opened, where the visit came from, how far the page was scrolled, which buttons and questions were clicked (more details below)

    Statistics without cookies. To understand which sections help and which ones confuse people, the website collects anonymised statistics: which page was opened, where the visit came from, how far the page was scrolled, which buttons and questions were clicked. We do not store the IP address or browser data and do not write anything to your device, so visits cannot be linked to one another or to a specific person. The data is stored with Cloudflare (EU/USA, standard contractual clauses) for up to 14 months. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).

    Krepko does not request or store passport details, NIE, account numbers, photographs of documents or any other sensitive data through the website/bot. Such data is provided only when a specific service contract is concluded — through a separate channel, with the signing of the corresponding agreement.

    3. Purposes of processing (Finalidad)

    1. To contact you in response to your request (call, message, consultation)
    2. To prepare a cost estimate and an action plan for your request
    3. To conclude a contract for the service, if you agree
    4. Website traffic analytics (Google Analytics 4, Microsoft Clarity) — to improve the content and usability of the website
    5. Statistics without cookies — to understand which sections help and which ones confuse people

    Krepko does not use data for marketing mailings without your separate consent. It does not sell data or share it with third parties for advertising purposes.

    4. Legal basis (Base legal)

    • Consent (Art. 6.1.a GDPR) — for contact details and messages that you voluntarily send through the bot, the form or WhatsApp
    • Performance of a contract (Art. 6.1.b GDPR) — for data needed to provide the service after it has been ordered
    • Legitimate interests (Art. 6.1.f GDPR) — for basic traffic analytics (aggregated, non-personal data)

    5. Recipients of data (Destinatarios)

    Your data may be disclosed to:

    • Partners involved in the service — notary, bank, immigration lawyer, gestor — only to the extent needed to fulfil your request, and only with your consent
    • Technical service providers — Google (Analytics, Sheets), Microsoft (Clarity), Telegram, WhatsApp Business, Cloudflare. They act as encargados del tratamiento (data processors) and are bound by the corresponding agreements
    • Public authorities — where there is a lawful request (court, tax office)

    6. International data transfers (Transferencia internacional)

    Some technical providers (Google, Telegram) may store data in the USA. These transfers are governed by:

    • EU-US Data Privacy Framework for Google and Microsoft
    • Standard contractual clauses (SCC) for the others

    7. Retention period (Plazo de conservación)

    • Contact details with no subsequent transaction: 12 months from the last contact, then deletion
    • Data of clients with a concluded contract: the limitation period under Spanish law (usually 5-10 years) for documentation
    • GA4 analytics: 14 months (Google’s standard retention)
    • Microsoft Clarity: session recordings — 30 days, aggregate statistics — 13 months (Microsoft’s standard retention)
    • Statistics without cookies: 14 months, then automatic deletion

    8. Your rights (Derechos ARSOPL)

    Under the GDPR (RGPD) and the LOPDGDD you have the following rights:

    • Acceso (access) — to find out what data about you Krepko holds
    • Rectificación (rectification) — to ask for inaccurate data to be corrected
    • Supresión (erasure, “right to be forgotten”) — to ask for your data to be deleted
    • Oposición (objection) — to object to processing based on legitimate interests
    • Portabilidad (portability) — to receive your data in a machine-readable format
    • Limitación (restriction) — to ask for processing to be restricted in disputed cases
    • Retirada de consentimiento — to withdraw your consent at any time (for future processing)

    To exercise your rights, write to hello@krepko.es or on WhatsApp marked “Solicitud RGPD”. Krepko will reply within 30 days.

    If you believe your rights have been infringed, you may lodge a complaint with the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD): www.aepd.es

    9. Cookies

    The website uses the analytics cookies of Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity — only after your consent in the banner. Details are in the cookie policy. No advertising or tracking cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings — the website will continue to work.

    10. Data security

    • HTTPS across the entire website (TLS 1.3, GitHub Pages)
    • Two-factor authentication on all accounts (Google, Railway, Telegram)
    • Data minimisation — Krepko collects only what is needed for the service
    • Backups, encrypted
    • Access logs — there are no public endpoints with direct access to personal data

    11. Changes to the policy

    Krepko may update this policy. Material changes will be clearly indicated with a new date. The change history is kept in the website’s git repository (open source).

    12. Privacy contact

    If you have questions about data processing, wish to exercise your rights or withdraw your consent:

    • Email: hello@krepko.es
    • WhatsApp: +34 624 309 985
    • Telegram: +34 624 309 985

    Given the small scale of data processing, a DPO (delegado de protección de datos) is not required. If the scale of processing changes, we will appoint a DPO and update this page.
