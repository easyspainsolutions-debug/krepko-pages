    Krepko: documents and paperwork in Spain, in Russian and Ukrainian

    Krepko helps Russian- and Ukrainian-speaking people living in Spain with official paperwork: we prepare the documents and translations and accompany you at every step. The notary authenticates, the authorities decide and, if needed, a lawyer steps in. Since 2020 we have completed 1,187 cases.

    Full version in Russian and Ukrainian: По-русски · Українською

    Message us on WhatsApp

    What we do

    • Signing before a notary. Powers of attorney, consents, notarial records and signature authentication: we prepare the draft, review it with the notary's office and translate every clause for you at the signing.
    • Documents from Ukraine and Russia. Criminal record certificates, birth and marriage certificates, with an apostille and without you travelling. Those from Ukraine are handled by the Krepko team in Ukraine.
    • Immigration (extranjería). Switching from temporary protection to another residence permit, arraigo and family reunification: we gather the documents and the application is filed by an immigration specialist.
    • Administrative procedures. Municipal registration (empadronamiento), digital certificate and driving licence exchange: we prepare the application and accompany you to the office.
    • Apostille and translation. We arrange the apostille for Spanish documents and organise the translation each authority accepts; sworn translations are done by a sworn translator.
    • Taxes and bank accounts. Non-Resident Income Tax (form 210), personal income tax (IRPF) and form 720: calculated and filed by our tax specialist. We accompany you to the bank to open an account and, if it gets blocked, we prepare what the bank asks for.
    • Notices from a court, the police or the tax office. We translate them and explain what is being asked of you and by when. Tax office notices are handled with our tax specialist; if you need a lawyer, we put you in touch and you hire them yourself.

    Can't find your case? Write to us and we will explain how we would approach it.

    Where we work

    We work throughout Spain: in the main cities and their surroundings. Krepko has a permanent presence in six cities: Alicante, Valencia, Murcia, Madrid, Barcelona and Málaga.

    How we work

    1. 1

      Write to us on WhatsApp. A specialist replies within 15 minutes, in Russian, Ukrainian, Spanish or English.

    2. 2

      We study your case and, before starting, tell you what is needed, how long it usually takes and how much it costs.

    3. 3

      We prepare the documents and accompany you to the notary or the relevant public office.

    4. 4

      We keep you informed at every step, without you having to ask.

    For notaries, gestorías and public authorities

    When the client is not fluent in Spanish, we prepare their documentation, explain beforehand what they are going to sign and, at the signing, our specialist acts as interpreter. Contact for professionals: hello@krepko.es
